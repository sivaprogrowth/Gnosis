# A celebration need not last long to be beautiful

Expecting a short life, the painter asks whether length makes a celebration more beautiful, and sees her life as a short, intense celebration.

> I know that I shall not live very long. But I wonder, is that
> sad? Is a celebration more beautiful because it lasts longer? And
> my life is a celebration, a short, intense celebration.
>
> — Paula Modersohn-Becker, Excerpt of her journal (1897); as quoted in Voicing our Visions: Writings by Women Artists
