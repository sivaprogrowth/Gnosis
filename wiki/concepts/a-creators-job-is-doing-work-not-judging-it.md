# A creator's job is doing work, not judging it

We can't predict how valuable our work will be to the world. Our job is to make it, put ourselves into it, and master our craft, not to judge it.

> We cannot predict the value our work will provide to the world.
> That’s fine. It is not our job to judge our own work. It is our
> job to create it, to pour ourselves into it, and to master our
> craft as best we can.
>
> — James Clear

> The responsibility of any creator is to do the work, not judge
> it.
>
> Your job is to fall in love with the process, not grade the
> outcome.
>
> — James Clear
