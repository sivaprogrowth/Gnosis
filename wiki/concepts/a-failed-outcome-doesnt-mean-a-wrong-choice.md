# A failed outcome doesn't mean a wrong choice

Because the world runs on probabilities rather than certainties, a bad result doesn't prove the decision was bad. Find situations where the odds favor you and keep trying.

> Just because it didn’t work doesn’t mean it was the wrong
> choice.
>
> The world is full of probabilities, not certainties.
>
> Find a game where the probabilities favor you and keep taking
> shots.
>
> — James Clear
