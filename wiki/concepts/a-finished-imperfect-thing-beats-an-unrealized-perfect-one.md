# A finished imperfect thing beats an unrealized perfect one

Completing something imperfect is more valuable than pursuing perfection on a project you never complete.

> The imperfect project you actually complete is worth more than
> the perfect project you never finish.
>
> — James Clear

> An imperfect solution that exists outperforms a perfect one that
> doesn't.
>
> Facebook wasn't the first social network - it was just the one
> that kept iterating and showing up. The Wright brothers weren't
> the most educated aviation pioneers, but they were the ones who
> got a plane off the ground.
>
> What you build matters more than what you could have built.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
