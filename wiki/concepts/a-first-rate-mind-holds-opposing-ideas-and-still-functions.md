# A first-rate mind holds opposing ideas and still functions

The mark of a brilliant intellect is being able to keep two contradictory ideas in mind at once without becoming paralyzed.

> The test of a first-rate intellect is the ability to hold two
> opposing ideas in your head at the same time, and still retain
> the ability to function.
>
> — F. Scott Fitzgerald, The Crack-Up
