# A fulfilled life needs both learning and enjoyment

Learning and enjoyment together make a fulfilling life. Learning without enjoyment exhausts you, and enjoyment without learning makes you dull.

> Learning and enjoyment are the secret to a fulfilled life. Learning without enjoyment wears you down, enjoyment without learning dulls you.
>
> — Richard David Precht, Who Am I? And If So, How Many?
