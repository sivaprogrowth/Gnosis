# A good idea becomes dogma when overapplied

Learning from only one teacher risks stretching a good idea beyond where it is useful until it becomes dogma. Stay open and learn from many teachers.

> Beware the student of one teacher. A good idea spirals into dogma when it gets applied to everything and stretched beyond the areas where it is useful. Remain open and embrace a lot of teachers.
>
> — James Clear
