# A great product is worthless unless you sell it

Staying on top is harder than getting there, and even the finest product has no value if you don't go out and sell it.

> “It’s easier to get to the top than to stay there. You can have
> the finest product in the world, but if you don’t go to sell it
> it’s worth nothing.”
>
> — Estee Lauder
