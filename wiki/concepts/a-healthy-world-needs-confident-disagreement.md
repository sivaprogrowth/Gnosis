# A healthy world needs confident disagreement

Mary Beard does not want universal agreement. She wants people to feel secure enough to disagree, and she is curious about why people think as they do.

> “I’m very pleased to think that there are people with whom I
> agree on some issues and not others. I don’t want a world in
> which we all agree. I want a world in which people feel that they
> have the standing and confidence to feel that they can disagree.
> I’m interested in why people think as they do.”
>
> — Mary Beard
