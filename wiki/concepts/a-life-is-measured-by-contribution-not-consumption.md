# A life is measured by contribution, not consumption

The worth of a life comes from what you give and create rather than what you own or take. Shift time from consuming toward giving, creating, and making.

> The measure of a life is not what you consume, but what you contribute.
>
> Spend less time owning, consuming, taking. Spend more time giving, creating, making.
>
> — James Clear
