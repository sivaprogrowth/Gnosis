# A little action beats lots of thinking

Taking even a small action is more valuable than endless thinking about it.

> A small act is worth a million thoughts.
>
> — Ai Weiwei, Meet the Most Interesting Person in China

> The first minute of action is worth more than a year of perfect planning.
>
> — James Clear

> “Theorizing is not nearly as effective as trying.”
>
> — Charles F. Kettering

> An ounce of action is worth a ton of theory.
>
> — Friedrich Engels
