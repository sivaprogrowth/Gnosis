# A mind that cannot adapt becomes a relic

A living mind is flexible and can update its beliefs. A fixed mind cannot absorb new ideas, and once it loses the ability to adapt it becomes outdated.

> "The mind is alive when it is flexible and adaptable. It can be
> updated, take new form, endure change.
>
> The mind is dead when it is fixed and immovable. It cannot absorb
> new ideas or thoughts. It is stiff and brittle.
>
> When we lose our ability to adapt, to move with the environment,
> to tolerate new beliefs and reshape our old ones, our mind is at
> risk of becoming outdated. The fixed mind becomes a relic."
>
> — James Clear
