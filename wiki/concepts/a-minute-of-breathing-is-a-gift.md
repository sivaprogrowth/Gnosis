# A minute of breathing is a gift

You can give yourself a simple gift right now by stopping, closing your eyes, and just breathing for a minute.

> A gift you can give yourself right now:
>
> Stop what you're doing. Close your eyes.
>
> For the next 60 seconds, just breathe.
>
> — James Clear
