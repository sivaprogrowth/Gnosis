# A newly planted tree needs time before fruit

Expecting immediate results from something just started is unreasonable; it must be allowed to grow first.

> Some people think that as soon as you plant a tree, it must bear
> fruit. We must allow it to grow a bit.
>
> — Tunku Abdul Rahman, Leaders of New Nations
