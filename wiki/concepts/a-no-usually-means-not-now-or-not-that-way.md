# A no usually means not now or not that way

When people decline a request, it is often about competing priorities rather than you. Don't take it personally; ask again later or ask in a different way.

> When someone says no to a request, they usually mean “not right
> now” or “not in that way.”
>
> Most people want to help others, but there are many priorities
> competing for our time.
>
> Don’t take it personally. Ask again later. Ask differently.
>
> — James Clear
