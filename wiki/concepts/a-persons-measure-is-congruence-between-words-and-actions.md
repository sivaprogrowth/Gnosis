# A person's measure is congruence between words and actions

Admirable people are measured by how well their actions match their words, plus their kindness, confidence, and decisiveness about who they are and intend to remain.

> “The measure of a person is the congruence between their words
> and actions, their kindness, their confidence, and their
> decisiveness about who they are in the world and who they intend
> to remain.”
>
> — Unknown, Letter to Peter Cundill
