# A pleasant attitude makes you luckier

Opportunities come through people, and people bring them to those they like, so being pleasant increases your luck while a bad attitude adds friction everywhere.

> Being pleasant and having a good attitude is a simple way to
> become luckier.
>
> Opportunities come through people, and people are more likely to
> bring opportunities to people they like.
>
> It's hard to win if your attitude adds friction to every
> interpersonal experience.
>
> — James Clear
