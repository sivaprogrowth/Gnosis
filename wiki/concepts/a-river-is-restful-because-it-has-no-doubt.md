# A river is restful because it has no doubt

Boyle suggests a river calms people because it is certain of its direction and wants to go nowhere else. Certainty of purpose has a restful quality.

> What makes a river so
> restful to people is that it doesn’t have any doubt—it is sure to
> get where it is going, and it doesn’t want to go anywhere else.
>
> — Hal Boyle, Help, Help! Another Day!
