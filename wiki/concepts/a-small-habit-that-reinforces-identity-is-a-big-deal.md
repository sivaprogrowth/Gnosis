# A small habit that reinforces identity is a big deal

The value of a habit lies in whether it supports the identity you want, not in its size. If it does, it is meaningful and therefore important.

> If a small habit reinforces your desired identity, then it is meaningful. And if it is meaningful, then it is actually a big deal.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
