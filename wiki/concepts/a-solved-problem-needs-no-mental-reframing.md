# A solved problem needs no mental reframing

Reframing a bad experience can help, but life still requires dealing with real things. Taking action can lessen a burden as much as a healthy perspective, and a solved problem doesn't need reframing at all.

> Sure, at some level, every experience in life boils down to your
> interpretation of it. And if you can shift your mindset, then
> perhaps you can turn a negative experience into a positive one.
>
> But life is not a hallucination. You still have to deal with
> things. A healthy perspective can lessen the burden, but so can
> taking action. A problem that is solved is one that you don't
> need to mentally reframe.
>
> — James Clear
