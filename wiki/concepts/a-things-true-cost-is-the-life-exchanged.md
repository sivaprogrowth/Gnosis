# A thing's true cost is the life exchanged

The real price of anything is the amount of your life you must give up to get it, whether now or over time.

> The cost of a thing is the amount of ... life which is required
> to be exchanged for it, immediately or in the long run.
>
> — Henry David Thoreau, Walden
