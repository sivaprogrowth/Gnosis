# A vision gives purpose that prevents drifting

Having a vision gives meaning to daily effort and a reason to get up and act. Without that purpose, people tend to drift.

> A vision gives a purpose to daily pursuits. A reason to push
> yourself out of bed each morning and do something. Without this
> purpose, it's easy to drift.
>
> — Bob Bowman
