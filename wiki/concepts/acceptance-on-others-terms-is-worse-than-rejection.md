# Acceptance on others' terms is worse than rejection

Being accepted only by compromising yourself to someone else's conditions costs more than being rejected as your full self.

> Acceptance, under someone else's terms, is worse than
> rejection.
>
> — Mary Cassatt, Mary Cassatt: Painting the Modern Woman
