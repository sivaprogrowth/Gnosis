# Accomplishment comes gradually, then all at once

Achievements build slowly through steady progress until results suddenly become visible. Every accomplishment follows this pattern.

> "How did you accomplish that?"
>
> "Same as everything else. Gradually, then all at once."
>
> — James Clear
