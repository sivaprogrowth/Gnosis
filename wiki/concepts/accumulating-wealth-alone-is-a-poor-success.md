# Accumulating wealth alone is a poor success

Being wealthy is not an achievement in itself. A life whose only accomplishment is accumulating money is a poor success.

> “Wealth isn't a distinction. If I have no other achievement to my
> credit than the accumulation of wealth, then I have made a poor
> success of my life.”
>
> — John D. Rockefeller
