# Act in ways that cancel out others' cruelty

Set a high standard for yourself by behaving in a way that counterbalances the cruel or stupid behavior of others.

> Always aspire to act in a way that cancels out someone else's
> cruel or stupid behavior.
>
> — Carl Hiaasen, Assume the Worst
