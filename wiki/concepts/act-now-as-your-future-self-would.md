# Act now as your future self would

A prompt to align today's actions with the identity you aspire to.

> How would the person I wish to be act today?
>
> — James Clear

> If you want to change the future, start living as if you're
> already there.
>
> — Lynn Conway, Surfing the waves of techno-social evolution

> “Don’t act like the person you used to be. Act like the person you’re going to become.”
>
> — Ryan Serhant
