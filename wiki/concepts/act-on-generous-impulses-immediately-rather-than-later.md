# Act on generous impulses immediately rather than later

When you feel the urge to give or praise someone, do it right away. Delaying to do it perfectly usually means it never happens, and an imperfect act beats none.

> Whenever a generous impulse arises in your mind – to give money,
> check in on a friend, send an email praising someone's work – act
> on the impulse right away, rather than putting it off until
> later.
>
> — Oliver Burkeman, Four Thousand Weeks
