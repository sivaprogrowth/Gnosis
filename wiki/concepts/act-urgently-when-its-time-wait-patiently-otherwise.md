# Act urgently when it's time, wait patiently otherwise

Act with urgency when it is time to act, and be calm and patient when it is time to wait.

> Move like you're late and wait like you're early.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Mix
> extreme patience with extreme decisiveness.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Outliers episode: The Witch of Wall Street
