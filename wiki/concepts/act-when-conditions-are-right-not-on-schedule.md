# Act when conditions are right, not on schedule

A serious surfer goes when the waves, wind, and tide are right rather than at a pre-planned time. Some things can't be forced and depend on responding to conditions.

> A serious surfer doesn’t plan to go surfing next Tuesday at 2
> o’clock. You go surfing when there are waves and wind and the
> tide is right.
>
> — Yvon Chouinard
