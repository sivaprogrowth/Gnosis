# Act your spirit, not your age

Age can stop you from dancing, starting over, or trying new things, but your spirit wants you to pursue what feels aligned at any age. Acting your spirit makes you stand out and feel more alive.

> The advice shouldn't be to act your age. It should be to act
> your spirit. Your age may try to prohibit you from dancing like
> that, or starting over, or trying something new. But your spirit
> would never do such a thing. If something feels aligned, your
> spirit wants you to go for it, whether you're 15 or 85. Acting
> your age makes you fit in more, while acting your spirit will
> indeed cause you to stand out—in a bad way to people who act
> their age, but in an inspiring way to those who act their spirit.
> Try acting your spirit from time to time, and you can see for
> yourself which path makes you feel more alive.
>
> — Light Watkins, Newsletter by Light Watkins
