# Acting first beats asking permission

Kletz found that asking permission led to refusal about half the time, while simply acting drew no objection 19 times out of 20. Acting without asking often moves things forward with little pushback.

> If one asked permission there was a 50% chance it would be
> refused. If one just got on with it, 19 times out of 20 nothing
> was said.
>
> — Trevor Kletz, By Accident: A Life Preventing Them in Industry

> It's easier to ask forgiveness than it is to get permission.
>
> — [[grace-hopper|Grace Hopper]], Only the Limits of Our Imagination
