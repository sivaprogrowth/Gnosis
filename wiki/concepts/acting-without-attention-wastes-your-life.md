# Acting without attention wastes your life

Doing things without paying attention is a way of wasting life. Attention matters equally whether the task is mundane or a masterpiece.

> Anyone who acts without paying attention to what he is doing is
> wasting his life. I'd go so far as to say life is denied by lack
> of attention, whether it be to cleaning windows or trying to
> write a masterpiece.
>
> — Nadia Boulanger, Mademoiselle: Conversations with Nadia Boulanger
