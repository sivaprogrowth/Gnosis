# Action forces prioritization when you are stuck deciding

Because you can only act on one thing at a time, acting forces you to choose a top priority. Even a wrong choice teaches you something.

> Action forces prioritization.
>
> If you're stuck deciding between options, force yourself to act.
> You can only act on one thing at a time, which means you will
> have to make something the top priority.
>
> Even if you pick wrong, you'll learn something.
>
> — James Clear
