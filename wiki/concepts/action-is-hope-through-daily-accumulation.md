# Action is hope through daily accumulation

Doing the work each day, regardless of quality, brings satisfaction and adds up over weeks and a year into something meaningful.

> Action is hope. At the end of each day, when you’ve done your
> work, you lie there and think, Well, I’ll be damned, I did this
> today. It doesn’t matter how good it is, or how bad—you did it.
> At the end of the week you’ll have a certain amount of
> accumulation. At the end of a year, you look back and say, I’ll
> be damned, it’s been a good year.
>
> — Ray Bradbury, The Art of Fiction No. 203
