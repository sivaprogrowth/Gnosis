# Action makes people come alive like meteors

A rock is dull on the ground but becomes a blazing meteor when moving fast. People likewise feel lifeless without activity and come alive when taking action.

> On the ground, a rock is just a rock. But when moving at high
> speed through the atmosphere, a rock becomes a meteor—alive with
> fire and burning bright.
>
> People are not so different. Without activity, we are lifeless
> and dull. When moving fast and taking action, we come alive.
>
> — James Clear
