# Action produces the information inaction awaits

Acting on a partially correct answer fills in the gaps faster than waiting for a perfect plan, because answers emerge as you move.

> Questions are answered as you move, not before you move. Moving
> forward with an answer that is partially correct will usually
> fill in the gaps faster than waiting until you come up with a
> plan that is perfectly correct.
>
> — James Clear

> Action produces information. If you're unsure of what to do,
> just do anything, even if it's the wrong thing. This will give
> you information about what you should actually be doing.
>
> Sounds simple on the surface - the hard part is making it part of
> your every day working process.
>
> — Brian Armstrong, Twitter

> Action produces the very information inaction waits for.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
