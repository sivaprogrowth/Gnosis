# Action reduces stress when you know what to do

Once you know what needs doing, delaying increases stress. Doing what's within your control to move toward your goal lowers it.

> "When you know what needs to be done, inaction increases stress.
> You feel a lot less stress when you do the things within your
> control that move you closer to your objective. Action reduces
> stress."
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
