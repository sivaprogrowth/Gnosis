# Activation energy is an upfront cost, not permanent obstacle

Any change requires an initial burst of effort to overcome inertia, like breaking habits or launching ideas. Once started, momentum makes the process self-sustaining.

> The key is recognizing activation energy for what it is: a
> necessary upfront cost, not a permanent obstacle. Once things are
> moving, momentum takes over. Once the reaction starts, it becomes
> self-sustaining.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
