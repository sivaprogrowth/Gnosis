# Actively seek ideas to learn faster

Most people learn passively and wait for insights, but you can learn faster by actively searching for useful ideas. One way is to ask top performers what they do and adopt their best practices as your baseline.

> Almost anything in life can be learned faster.
>
> Most people learn passively. They wait for insights to come to
> them.
>
> But you can speed things up by actively searching for useful
> ideas.
>
> One strategy is to ask the top people what they do. Make their
> best practices your baseline.
>
> — James Clear
