# Admit mistakes early to build trust

Admitting a mistake early solves problems and earns respect for honesty, while hiding errors and admitting them late creates problems and loses trust.

> There are two times to admit mistakes: early and late. Early
> admissions solve problems. Late admissions create them.
>
> A person who proactively tells another they made a mistake gains
> trust and respect for their honesty. In contrast, one who tries
> to hide errors quickly loses trust.
>
> Swift admissions build bridges. Slow ones burn them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> “It is important to admit your mistakes, and to do so before you
> are charged with them. Many clients are surrounded by
> buck-passers who make a fine art of blaming the agency for their
> own failures. I seize the earliest opportunity to assume the
> blame.”
>
> — David Ogilvy
