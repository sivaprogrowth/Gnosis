# Adulthood means letting go of other possible lives

Maturity is giving up alternative existences and committing to the one you have. Which life you pick matters less than living it well.

> "The goal of adulthood is to let go of the other possible
> existences and to make the best of the one. A successful adult is
> one who understands that it doesn't matter which life you
> ultimately pick, only that you live it well."
>
> — Chris Ballas (The Last Psychiatrist), A Surprising Number Of Teens Think They'll Die Young, Or Live Forever, Whichever Comes First
