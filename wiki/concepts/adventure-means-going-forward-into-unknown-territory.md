# Adventure means going forward into unknown territory

Enjoying life requires grasping and maintaining an adventurous mindset, which at its core means moving into the unknown.

> To enjoy life, the adventurous state of mind must be grasped and
> maintained. The essential feature of adventure is that it is a
> going forward into unknown territory.
>
> — Agnes Martin, Interview with the Guggenheim Museum
