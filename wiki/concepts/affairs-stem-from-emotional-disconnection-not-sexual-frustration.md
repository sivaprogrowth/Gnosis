# Affairs stem from emotional disconnection, not sexual frustration

Affairs usually happen when people feel alone, disconnected, and rejected by their partner.

> Affairs rarely happen because of sexual frustration: they occur
> when people feel emotionally disconnected, alone, and rejected by
> their partner.
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
