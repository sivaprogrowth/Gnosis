# Against social norms, lean on the other three laws

When you can't change your social environment, make the habit obvious, easy, and satisfying to stick with it even against the group's norm.

> When this is not possible, you’ll need to leverage the remaining
> three Laws of Behavior Change. If you can manage to make a habit
> obvious, easy, and satisfying, then it’s possible you’ll be able
> to stick with it—even if it runs against the social norm.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
