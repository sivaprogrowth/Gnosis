# Age is measured in days, thoughts, and contributions

Biological age counts days lived, psychological age counts thoughts entertained, and sociological age counts contributions made. Each is a different way of measuring a life.

> Your biological age is the number of days you've lived.
>
> Your psychological age is the number of thoughts you've
> entertained.
>
> Your sociological age is the number of contributions you've
> made.
>
> — James Clear
