# Agreeing with your last book means you haven't read enough

If whatever book you read most recently always convinces you, you probably haven't read widely. Broad reading shows that even the best arguments have limits.

> "One sign you haven’t done enough reading is if you find yourself
> agreeing with whatever book you read last. At first, it’s easy to
> be swayed by any reasonable argument. Once you’ve read a lot, you
> can see that even the best arguments have limitations."
>
> — James Clear
