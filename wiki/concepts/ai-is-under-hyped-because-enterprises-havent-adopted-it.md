# AI is under-hyped because enterprises haven't adopted it

Gil argues AI is under-hyped since most enterprises haven't yet used it, and enterprises are where the money, change, and impact lie.

> “I think AI is dramatically under-hyped because most enterprises
> have not done anything in it—and that’s where all the money is,
> all the changes, all the impact, all the jobs, everything.”
>
> — Elad Gil, The Knowledge Project podcast
