# Aim to become the person, not get results

Identity-based habits start with the person you wish to become rather than the outcome you want to achieve.

> The alternative is to build what I call “identity-based habits”
> and start by focusing on who we wish to become, not what we want
> to achieve.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> The goal is not to get straight A’s, the goal is to become a
> person who studies every day.
>
> — James Clear
