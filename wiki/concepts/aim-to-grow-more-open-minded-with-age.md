# Aim to grow more open-minded with age

People tend to become less open-minded, resilient, and flexible as they age. The goal is to grow in the opposite direction and keep improving your mindset each day.

> One of my hopes is to age into a better mindset.
>
> The general trend seems to become less open minded, less
> resilient, less capable of handling change with ease and
> flexibility as the years roll by.
>
> I hope to grow in the opposite direction. How can my mindset be
> better today than it was yesterday?
>
> — James Clear
