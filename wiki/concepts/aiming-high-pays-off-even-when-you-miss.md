# Aiming high pays off even when you miss

Aiming high produces significant results even if you fall short, while aiming low yields nothing even when you succeed.

> “If you have really high ambitions, you achieve great things
> even if you fail. If you have low ambitions, you achieve nothing
> even if you succeed."
>
> — Nicolai Tangen, The Knowledge Project

> “If you aim for perfection and miss, you're still pretty good,
> but if you aim for mediocre and miss?”
>
> — Bill Walsh
