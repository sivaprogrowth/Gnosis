# Aligned incentives mean worker, risk-bearer, and beneficiary coincide

Incentives are aligned when the same person does the work, bears the consequences, and reaps the rewards.

> Who does the work? Who bears the consequences? Who reaps the
> rewards? When the incentives are aligned, it's the same person.
>
> — James Clear
