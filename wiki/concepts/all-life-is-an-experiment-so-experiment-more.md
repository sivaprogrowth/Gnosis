# All life is an experiment, so experiment more

Don't be timid about acting; life is a series of experiments and more is better. Failing and getting knocked down makes you less afraid of falling next time.

> Do not be too timid and squeamish about your actions. All life
> is an experiment. The more experiments you make the better. What
> if they are a little coarse, and you may get your coat soiled or
> torn? What if you do fail, and get fairly rolled in the dirt once
> or twice. Up again, you shall never be so afraid of a tumble.
>
> — Ralph Waldo Emerson, Journal entry (November 1842)
