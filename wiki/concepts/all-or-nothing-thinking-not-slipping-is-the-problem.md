# All-or-nothing thinking, not slipping, is the problem

Missing a habit isn't the real problem. The real problem is believing that if you can't do something perfectly, you shouldn't do it at all.

> Too often, we fall into an all-or-nothing cycle with our habits.
>
> The problem is not slipping up; the problem is thinking that if
> you can’t do something perfectly, then you shouldn’t do it at
> all.
>
> — James Clear
