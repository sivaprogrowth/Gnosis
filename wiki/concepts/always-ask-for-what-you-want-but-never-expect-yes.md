# Always ask for what you want, but never expect yes

Make direct, specific requests because many people will help and remarkable things become possible. But don't expect a yes, since others have their own priorities; when refused, move on lightly.

> Always ask, but never expect.
>
> Always ask for what you want. Many people are happy to help—if
> the request is direct and specific. In a surprising number of
> cases, something remarkable is possible if you have the courage
> to ask.
>
> Never expect people to say yes. Everyone is busy and balancing
> multiple priorities. Your request is not their responsibility.
> When you're told no, move on lightly and freely. The world is
> full of opportunity.
>
> — James Clear
