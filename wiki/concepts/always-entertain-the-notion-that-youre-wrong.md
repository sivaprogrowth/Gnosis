# Always entertain the notion that you're wrong

Bourdain kept open the possibility of being wrong and revising his views, accepting that this is usually pleasant but sometimes painful and embarrassing.

> “I always entertain the notion that I'm wrong, or that I'll have
> to revise my opinion. Most of the time that feels good; sometimes
> it really hurts and is embarrassing.”
>
> — Anthony Bourdain
