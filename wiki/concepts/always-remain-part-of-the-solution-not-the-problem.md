# Always remain part of the solution, not the problem

Much of living well is learning to keep directing your attention and effort toward solving problems rather than adding to them.

> Much of life is in learning to always remain part of the solution rather than allowing ourselves to become part of the problem.
>
> — Jim Rohn, The Seasons of Life
