# Am I creating the conditions for success?

Stop fixating on outcomes and focus on what comes before them. Ask whether you are putting yourself in good positions that make success likely.

> Forget about the outcome and focus on what precedes it.
>
> Am I putting myself in good positions and creating the conditions
> for success?
>
> — James Clear
