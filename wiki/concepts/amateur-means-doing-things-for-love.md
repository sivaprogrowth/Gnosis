# Amateur means doing things for love

The word amateur comes from the Latin amare, to love, so being an amateur means doing something for the love of it rather than something shameful.

> You say 'amateur' as if it was a dirty word. 'Amateur' comes
> from the Latin word 'amare', which means to love. To do things
> for the love of it.
>
> — Mozart in the Jungle, Mozart in the Jungle
