# Ambition closes your own gap; entitlement expects others to

Ambition means expecting yourself to bridge the gap between what you have and what you want. Entitlement means expecting other people to bridge it for you.

> Ambition is when you expect yourself to close the gap between
> what you have and what you want.
>
> Entitlement is when you expect others to close the gap between
> what you have and what you want.
>
> — James Clear
