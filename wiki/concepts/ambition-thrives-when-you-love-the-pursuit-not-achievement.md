# Ambition thrives when you love the pursuit, not achievement

Ambition becomes powerful once you stop expecting accomplishments to bring satisfaction and instead enjoy the work of pursuing them.

> Ambition becomes a superpower when you stop expecting achievement to satisfy you and learn to love the pursuit itself.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
