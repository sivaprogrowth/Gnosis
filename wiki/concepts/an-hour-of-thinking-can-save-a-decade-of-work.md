# An hour of thinking can save a decade of work

Many people never define what they want, so their effort is slightly misdirected and they end up with a life that isn't quite what they hoped for. Taking time to clarify your goals saves years of wasted work.

> Life is easier when you know what you want—but most people don't
> take the time to figure out what they want.
>
> It's not that we are completely lost, but our efforts are often
> slightly misdirected. People will work for years and ultimately
> achieve a lifestyle that isn't quite what they were hoping
> for—often, simply, because they never clearly defined what they
> wanted.
>
> An hour of thinking can save you a decade of work.
>
> — James Clear
