# Anger at opposing opinions signals weak reasons

If a contrary opinion makes you angry, it suggests you lack good reasons for your own view. The fiercest arguments happen where there is no good evidence either way.

> If an opinion contrary to your own makes you angry, that is a
> sign that you are subconsciously aware of having no good reason
> for thinking as you do.
>
> — [[bertrand-russell|Bertrand Russell]], On Avoiding Foolish Opinions
