# Anger can cleanse but bitterness weighs you down

Anger, like fire, can burn away what is worthless and leave something positive. Bitterness, however, is a weight that drags you down, so forgiveness is essential.

> I believe in anger. Anger’s like fire, it can burn out all the
> dross and leave some positive things. But what I don’t believe in
> is bitterness. Forgiveness is imperative because you don’t want
> to carry that weight around, who needs to? And it will throw you
> down. It doesn’t help you to live life. I don’t make myself
> vulnerable if I can help it.
>
> — Maya Angelou, Voices of Powerful Women
