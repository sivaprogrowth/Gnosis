# Anyone's reputation would suffer from a full list of faults

No one's reputation would survive a complete cataloguing of their mistakes, so we should be humble when judging others.

> “Let’s keep in mind that there is not one of us whose reputation
> would not be grievously dimmed by a thorough cataloguing of our
> faults and errors.”
>
> — Carl Braun
