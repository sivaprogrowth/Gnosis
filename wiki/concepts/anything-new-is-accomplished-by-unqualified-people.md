# Anything new is accomplished by unqualified people

You can only be qualified for what you've already done or trained for. So everyone doing something new feels unqualified, and that shouldn't stop you.

> Don’t feel qualified? Nobody does.
>
> You can only be qualified to do that which you have already
> accomplished or trained for.
>
> Anything new is accomplished by unqualified people.
>
> — James Clear
