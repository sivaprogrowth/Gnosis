# Apathy is the greatest danger; small differences add up

Everyone affects the world daily, and small individual contributions combine into real change. The real question is what kind of impact you choose to make.

> The greatest danger to our future is apathy. We can't all save
> the world in a dramatic way, but we can each make our small
> difference, and together those small differences add up. Every
> single person makes an impact on the planet every single day. The
> question is: What kind of impact do you want to make?
>
> — Jane Goodall, Reason for Hope (paraphrased)
