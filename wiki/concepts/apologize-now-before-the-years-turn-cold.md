# Apologize now before the years turn cold

If you value the relationship more than winning the argument, end the conflict. Apologies lose their warmth when delayed, so make them promptly.

> If you love them more than the fight, end the fight. An apology
> loses its warmth if it waits too long. Do it now, before the
> years turn cold.
>
> — James Clear
