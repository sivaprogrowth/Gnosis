# Apply the Pareto Principle across your whole life

Identify the few people, actions, and information sources with the biggest positive impact and give them more attention. Identify the few sources causing most of your stress and eliminate them.

> Live the Pareto Principle lifestyle:
>
> Relationships. Who are the few people that have the most positive
> impact on my life? Spend more time with them.
>
> Priorities. What are the few actions that have the most positive
> impact on my day? Prioritize them.
>
> Learning. What are the few information sources I learn the most
> from? Focus on them.
>
> Stress. What are the few sources that cause most of the stress
> and friction in my life? Eliminate them.
>
> — James Clear
