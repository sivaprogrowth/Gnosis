# Appreciating little things brings frequent pleasant surprises

Making a habit of noticing small joys prevents constant disappointment and reveals that the world gives back more than expected.

> I make a point to appreciate all the little things in my life,
> because I learned early that if you don't, you get disappointed a
> lot. If you do, you might be pleasantly surprised quite often.
>
> I go out and smell the air after a good, hard rain. I re-read
> passages from my favorite books. I hold the little treasures that
> somebody special gave me. By keeping my eyes open for unexpected
> joys, I find the world gives back more than we sometimes think.
>
> — Dolly Parton, Dream More
