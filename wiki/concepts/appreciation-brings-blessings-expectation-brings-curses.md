# Appreciation brings blessings, expectation brings curses

People who expect everything are constantly disappointed, while people who appreciate everything find many things to be grateful for.

> For those who expect everything, there are many curses.
>
> For those who appreciate everything, there are many blessings.
>
> — James Clear
