# Are you being too passive or too aggressive?

Sometimes it helps to let life unfold without force, and sometimes it helps to actively shape the world. The question asks which imbalance you have right now.

> Sometimes it benefits us to be passive: to allow life to come to
> us and unfold without force.
>
> Other times it benefits us to be aggressive: to bend the world to
> our will and actively shape the life we want.
>
> Are you being too passive or too aggressive right now?
>
> — James Clear
