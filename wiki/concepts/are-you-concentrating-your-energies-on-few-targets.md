# Are you concentrating your energies on few targets?

Great outcomes are hard when time, money, and attention are spread thin. The question asks whether you are focusing your resources on a small number of goals.

> It's hard to get a great outcome when your resources — time,
> money, attention — are spread thin. Are you concentrating your
> energies on a small set of targets?
>
> — James Clear
