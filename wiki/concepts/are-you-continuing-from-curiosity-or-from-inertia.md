# Are you continuing from curiosity or from inertia?

Check whether you keep doing something because it makes you feel curious and alive, or just because inertia makes it easy and letting go feels hard.

> Are you doing this because it makes you feel curious and alive,
> or because inertia makes it easy to continue doing and you're
> hesitant to let go?
>
> — James Clear
