# Are you doing what is required or seeking shortcuts?

Drawing on Churchill's line that doing our best is sometimes not enough, the reader is asked whether their efforts toward this year's goals meet what is truly required or look for shortcuts.

> Winston Churchill once said, "It is not enough that we do our
> best; sometimes we must do what is required."
>
> Think about what you are working toward this year. Are you doing
> what is required or are you looking for a shortcut?
>
> — James Clear
