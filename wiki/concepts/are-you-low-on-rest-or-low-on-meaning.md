# Are you low on rest or low on meaning?

Feeling tired and feeling empty are different. Tiredness may need sleep, while emptiness may need purpose.

> There's a difference between feeling tired and feeling empty. One
> probably needs sleep. The other may need purpose.
>
> Are you low on rest or low on meaning?
>
> — James Clear
