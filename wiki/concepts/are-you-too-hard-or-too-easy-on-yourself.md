# Are you too hard or too easy on yourself?

We can err by harshly criticizing our mistakes or by letting excuses run our lives. Notice which way you lean now and how to return to center.

> Sometimes we are too hard on ourselves and criticize our mistakes
> to an unhelpful degree. Sometimes we are too easy on ourselves
> and let excuses run our lives.
>
> Which way are you leaning right now? How can you pull yourself
> back to center?
>
> — James Clear
