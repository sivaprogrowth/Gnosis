# Are you working on important problems?

Identify the most important problems in your field. If you're not working on them, ask yourself why not.

> What are the important problems in your field? And if you're not
> working on them, why not?
>
> — Richard Hamming, You and Your Research

> "If you do not work on important problems, how can you expect to
> do important work?"
>
> — Richard Hamming
