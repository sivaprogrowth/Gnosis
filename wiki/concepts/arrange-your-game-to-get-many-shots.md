# Arrange your game to get many shots

With only one attempt everything must go right, but with many attempts success becomes likely. Structure your approach so you get lots of tries.

> "The person who gets 1 shot needs everything to go right.
>
> The person who gets 1000 shots is going to score at some point.
>
> Find a way to play the game that ensures you get a lot of shots."
>
> — James Clear
