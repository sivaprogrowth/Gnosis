# Arrogant partners must find new partners every deal

Partnerships are repeated games, so someone who acts as if others are lucky to work with them burns relationships and has to keep starting over.

> Every partnership is a repeated game.
>
> The person who treats others like they're lucky to have them has to find a new partner for every deal.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
