# Art renews memories of moments of perfection

Art's purpose is to bring back rare, fleeting experiences of clarity, inner stillness, and harmony.

> The function of art is the renewal of memories of moments of
> perfection.
>
> — Agnes Martin, "On the Perfection Underlying Life" (1973 lecture at the Institute of Contemporary Art)
