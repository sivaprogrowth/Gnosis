# Ask for a 0-10 score to get honest feedback

When people hesitate to give honest feedback, ask them to rate your work from 0 to 10, then ask how to get closer to a 10. This turns them into coaches and makes you more coachable.

> When people hesitate to give honest feedback on an idea, draft,
> or performance, I ask for a 0-10 score.
>
> No one ever says 10. Then I ask how I can get closer to a 10.
>
> It motivates them to start coaching me – and motivates me to be
> coachable. I want to learn how to close the gap.
>
> — Adam Grant, Twitter
