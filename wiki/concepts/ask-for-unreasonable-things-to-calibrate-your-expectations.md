# Ask for unreasonable things to calibrate your expectations

Making requests that feel unreasonable tests whether your sense of what is reasonable is accurate. If every request gets a yes, you are aiming too low, though you should avoid being a jerk about it.

> Ask for things. Ask for things that feel unreasonable, to make
> sure your intuitions about what's reasonable are accurate (of
> course, try not to be a jerk in the process). If you're only
> asking for things you get, you're not aiming high enough.
>
> — Cate Hall, How to be More Agentic
