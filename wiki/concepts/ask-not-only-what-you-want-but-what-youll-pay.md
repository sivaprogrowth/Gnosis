# Ask not only what you want but what you'll pay

Setting goals is easy because everyone wants the glamorous result. What matters is whether you want the costs that come with it, such as money, time, and tradeoffs.

> Writing down your goals is easy. Everybody wants the glamorous
> result. What really matters is whether you want the costs
> associated with it: the money, the time, the tradeoffs.
>
> Don't merely ask yourself what do I want to achieve, but also
> what am I willing to pay?
>
> — James Clear
