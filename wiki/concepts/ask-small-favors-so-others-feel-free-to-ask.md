# Ask small favors so others feel free to ask

Asking a struggling neighbor for something small makes them feel needed too, which makes it easier for them to ask you for help.

> They don't have much money and they sometimes ask us for things. So I asked for something small that wouldn't burden them. I want them to feel as if we needed them too. That way, it will be much easier for them to ask us for anything they need.
>
> — Unknown
