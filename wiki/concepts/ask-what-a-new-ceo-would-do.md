# Ask what a new CEO would do

Andy Grove saved Intel by asking what a newly hired CEO would do if the current leaders were fired, then acting on that answer himself. Staying paranoid even during success helps you spot threats early.

> he asked: What would a new CEO do if we got
> fired? The answer was brutal: exit memories. So Grove fired
> himself and became the new CEO.
>
> The lesson: only the paranoid survive.
>
> — Andy Grove, The Knowledge Project
