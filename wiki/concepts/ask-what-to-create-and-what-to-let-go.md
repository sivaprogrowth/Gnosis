# Ask what to create and what to let go

At each year's end, ask what you want to create in the coming year and, perhaps more importantly, what you want to let go of.

> At the end of each year, I ask myself two questions:
>
> 1) What do I want to create in this New Year?
>
> And, perhaps even more importantly,
>
> 2) What do I want to let go of?
>
> — Patricia Digh, Life is a Verb
