# Ask what you need repeatedly to find the first movement

Break a broad life goal down by repeatedly asking what you need to achieve it until you reach a behavior that takes two minutes or less.

> And so on
> until you get to a behavior that takes two minutes or less –
> until you discover the first movement.
>
> — James Clear
