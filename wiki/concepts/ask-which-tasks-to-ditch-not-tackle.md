# Ask which tasks to ditch, not tackle

Rather than asking how many tasks fit into your working hours, ask how many you can drop given what you need to do to excel. Exceptional work comes from doing less, better.

> Instead of asking how many tasks you can tackle given your
> working hours, ask how many you can ditch given what you must do
> to excel.
>
> — Morten Hansen, Great at Work
