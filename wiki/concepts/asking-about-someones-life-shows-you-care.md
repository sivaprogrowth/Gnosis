# Asking about someone's life shows you care

Asking people what they are excited about, working on, or hoping for, and listening thoughtfully, is a generous act. It gives them the gift of attention.

> One easy way to show you care about others is to ask them
> questions about their life.
>
> * What are they excited about?
> * What are they working on?
> * What are they hoping for?
>
> Simply asking the question and listening thoughtfully is an act
> of generosity. You're giving them the gift of attention.
>
> — James Clear
