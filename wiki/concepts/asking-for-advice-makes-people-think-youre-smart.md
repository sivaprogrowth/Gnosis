# Asking for advice makes people think you're smart

When you ask for advice, people see you as smart because you recognized how clever they are.

> Asking for advice makes people think you’re smart because you
> are clever enough to recognize how clever they are.
>
> — Nicolai Tangen, The Knowledge Project
