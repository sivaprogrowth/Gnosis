# Asking for help is a brave superpower

Anyone can ask for help but few do; doing so takes courage and is the first step toward finding a mentor who can change your life.

> Asking for help is a superpower anyone can have but only some
> people use. It is brave to ask for help. Asking for help is the
> first step toward finding a mentor. Mentors can help us change
> our lives if we let them.
>
> — Alexi Pappas, Bravey
