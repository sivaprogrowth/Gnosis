# Asking makes you a fool briefly, not forever

Asking a question may make you look foolish for a moment, but not asking keeps you ignorant permanently.

> "The person who asks is a fool for five minutes, but the person
> who does not ask remains a fool forever."
>
> — Georg Christoph Lichtenberg (attributed; often called a Chinese proverb)
