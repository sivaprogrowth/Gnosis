# Asking successful people how they succeeded is cheap but valuable

Asking a successful person how they got there costs nothing and can teach you more than a huge amount of solo hard work. People don't owe you their secrets, but sincere, direct, thoughtful questions often get surprisingly useful answers.

> It costs nothing to ask a successful person how they succeeded, but it may deliver more value than 1,000 hours of hard work. Others are under no obligation to tell you their secrets, but it is surprising how much you can learn from sincere, direct, and thoughtful questions.
>
> — James Clear
