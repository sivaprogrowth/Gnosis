# Assertiveness meets your needs while respecting others

Being assertive means getting your needs met, standing up for your beliefs, owning your actions, and protecting yourself, all while valuing others and not needing them to lose for you to win. At root it is self-attention, self-affection, self-approval, and self-acceptance.

> "Being assertive means being able to have your needs met while
> still interacting with great sensitivity to those around you.
>
> — Greg Harden
