# Assume you're on a winning path until proven otherwise

Enter each situation with baseline optimism: expect a good day, expect to like new people, and expect hard things to work out. Deal with problems as they come, but start from an optimistic default.

> When I go into a situation, I try to keep a mindset of baseline
> optimism.
>
> * When starting the day: It's going to be a good day.
> * When meeting someone new: I'm going to like them.
> * When trying something challenging: It's going to work out.
>
> Problems will inevitably arise and I'll deal with them as the
> situation demands, but my preference is to assume I'm on a
> winning path until proven otherwise.
>
> — James Clear
