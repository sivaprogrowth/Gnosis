# Assuming what we see is everything is hubris

Each time physics looked beyond familiar scales, it found unexpected things like quarks. Assuming our current view captures all that exists is arrogant.

> In the history of physics, every time we've looked beyond the
> scales and energies we were familiar with, we've found things
> that we wouldn't have thought were there. You look inside the
> atom and eventually you discover quarks. Who would have thought
> that? It's hubris to think that the way we see things is
> everything there is.
>
> — Lisa Randall, The Discover Interview: Lisa Randall
