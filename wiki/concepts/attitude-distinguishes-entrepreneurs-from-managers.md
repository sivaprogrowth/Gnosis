# Attitude distinguishes entrepreneurs from managers

What mainly distinguishes an entrepreneur from a manager is mindset, not skills or position.

> The main difference between the entrepreneur and the manager is
> attitude.
>
> — Harrison McCain, The Knowledge Project episode on Harrison McCain

> "The entrepreneur
> keeps himself operating on the threshold of excellence because he
> fears mediocrity. The entrepreneur has learned to dig for facts.
> The first explanation given does not include all the facts. Once
> the facts are found, the necessary action is clear. The
> entrepreneur has a sixth sense of what will work and what will
> not work – by adjusting experience and knowledge. The
> entrepreneur tenaciously grasps every opportunity to meet goals
> using the art of human relations (some say it is a science), the
> laws of the marketplace, and excellent management of manpower,
> money, and machines or resources. The entrepreneur knows that he
> must delegate responsibility, but he never sacrifices his
> knowledge of the details. The main difference between the
> entrepreneur and the manager is attitude!"
>
> — Harrison McCain
