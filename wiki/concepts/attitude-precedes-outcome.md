# Attitude precedes outcome

We want solutions, but what we need is the right attitude: training rather than abs, curiosity rather than answers, perseverance rather than an easier life. The outcomes follow from the attitude.

> "We want solutions, but what we really need are attitudes.
>
> You don't need abs, but rather an attitude of training. You don't
> need the answer, but rather an attitude of curiosity. You don't
> need an easier life, but rather an attitude of perseverance.
>
> Attitude precedes outcome."
>
> — James Clear
