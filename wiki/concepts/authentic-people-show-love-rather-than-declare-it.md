# Authentic people show love rather than declare it

Authentic people prefer deep conversations with a few, align words and actions even unobserved, make others feel seen, and don't depend on external validation.

> “There’s a distinctive signature to authentic people: they prefer
> meaningful conversations with a few over shallow exchanges with
> many. Their love is demonstrated rather than declared. Their
> words and actions align, even when no one’s watching. They bring
> a quality of presence that makes you feel truly seen, and carry a
> sense of peace and joy that doesn’t depend on external
> validation.”
>
> — Vex King
