# Autumn's beauty comes from knowing life's limits

Lin Yutang prefers autumn because, like approaching age, it carries mellowness and wisdom. It accepts life's limitations and is content, and richness emerges from that acceptance.

> I like spring, but it is too young. I like summer, but it is too
> proud. So I like best of all autumn, because its leaves are a
> little yellow, its tone mellower, its colours richer, and it is
> tinged a little with sorrow and a premonition of death. Its
> golden richness speaks not of the innocence of spring, nor of the
> power of summer, but of the mellowness and kindly wisdom of
> approaching age. It knows the limitations of life and is content.
> From a knowledge of those limitations and its richness of
> experience emerges a symphony of colours, richer than all, its
> green speaking of life and strength, its orange speaking of
> golden content and its purple of resignation and death.
>
> — Lin Yutang, My Country and My People
