# Avoid talking with those who write more than they read

Johnson did not want to talk with people who produce more than they take in. People worth listening to have learned more than they express.

> I never desire to converse with a man who has written more than
> he has read.
>
> — Samuel Johnson, Apophthegms, Sentiments, Opinions and Occasional Reflections
