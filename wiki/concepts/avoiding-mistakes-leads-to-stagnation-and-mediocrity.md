# Avoiding mistakes leads to stagnation and mediocrity

To be active you must accept making mistakes; trying to be good by merely doing no harm leads to mediocrity. The blank canvas fears the passionate person who dares to step in and do something.

> I tell you, if one wants to be active, one mustn’t be afraid to
> do something wrong sometimes, not afraid to lapse into some
> mistakes. To be good — many people think that they’ll achieve it
> by doing no harm — and that’s a lie, and you said yourself in the
> past that it was a lie. That leads to stagnation, to
> mediocrity...
>
> — Vincent van Gogh, Ever Yours: The Essential Letters
