# Avoiding stupidity beats trying to be brilliant

It is easier and more reliable to avoid dumb mistakes than to try to be brilliant.

> Avoiding stupidity is easier than seeking brilliance.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], All I Want To Know Is Where I'm Going To Die So I'll Never Go There

> “It is remarkable how much long-term advantage people like us
> have gotten by trying to be consistently not stupid, instead of
> trying to be very intelligent.”
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]

> If you don't know what will work, eliminate what won't.
>
> Often what looks like correct judgment in hindsight was avoiding obvious stupidity in the moment.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
