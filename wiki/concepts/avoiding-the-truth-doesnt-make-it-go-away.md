# Avoiding the truth doesn't make it go away

People think they can escape reality by not thinking about it, but consequences are inevitable, so it's better to be honest with yourself early.

> “I have spoken of the necessity of being frank and honest with
> oneself about one's own affairs: many people assume that they can
> get away from the truth by avoiding thinking about it, but the
> natural law is inevitable, and the sooner it is recognized, the
> better.”
>
> — John D. Rockefeller
