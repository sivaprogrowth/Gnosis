# Avoiding waste matters more than appearances

Despite being a billionaire, Bragg reuses scuffed golf balls because waste bothers him more than how it looks.

> At 85 and worth multiple billions, Bragg still uses scuffed
> golf balls and retrieves lost ones from water hazards because
> waste offends him more than appearances matter. When asked about
> this, he says, 'I find they go as far as the new ones.'
>
> — John Bragg, The Knowledge Project
