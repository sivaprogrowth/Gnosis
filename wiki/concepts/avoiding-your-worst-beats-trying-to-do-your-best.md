# Avoiding your worst beats trying to do your best

Preventing mistakes and bad days is an underrated way to improve, because it is easier to fend off a bad day than to achieve a perfect one.

> Avoiding mistakes is an underrated way to improve. It's easier
> to fend off a bad day than achieve a perfect day. Rather than do
> your best, avoid your worst.
>
> — James Clear
