# Bad days matter more than good days

Doing the habit when you don't feel like it is what keeps the habit alive. Once the habit is maintained, time does the rest.

> The bad days are more important than the good days.
>
> If you write or exercise or meditate or cook when you don't feel
> like it, then you maintain the habit.
>
> And if you maintain the habit, then all you need is time.
>
> — James Clear
