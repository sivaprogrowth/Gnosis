# Bad workouts matter because they reaffirm identity

Showing up when you don't feel like it is crucial, even if you do less than you hoped. A short session may not change your body, but it reinforces who you are.

> The “bad” workouts are often the most important ones.
>
> It's easy to train when you feel good, but it’s crucial to show
> up when you don’t feel like it—even if you do less than you hope.
>
> Going to gym for 5 minutes might not transform your body, but it
> does reaffirm your identity.
>
> — James Clear
