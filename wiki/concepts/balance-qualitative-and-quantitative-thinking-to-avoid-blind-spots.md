# Balance qualitative and quantitative thinking to avoid blind spots

Being too qualitative makes you miss important details, and being too quantitative makes you miss qualitative details, so good thinking balances both.

> “I think if you're too qualitative, you miss a lot of details
> that are important, and if you're too quantitative, you miss a
> lot of the details that are qualitative.”
>
> — Vince Hanks
