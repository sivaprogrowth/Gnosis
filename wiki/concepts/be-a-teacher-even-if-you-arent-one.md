# Be a teacher even if you aren't one

Everyone should share their ideas and what they learn. Don't take your education for granted; enjoy learning and spread it widely.

> Even if you're not a teacher, be a teacher. Share your ideas.
> Don't take for granted your education. Rejoice in what you learn
> and spray it.
>
> — Tim Minchin, 9 Life Lessons
