# Be able to drop ideas once they prove wrong

We constantly learn, modify, or destroy ideas, and being able to quickly abandon an idea when it's time is extremely valuable. You must make yourself consider the opposing arguments.

> We all are learning, modifying, or destroying ideas all the
> time. Rapid destruction of your ideas when the time is right is
> one of the most valuable qualities you can acquire. You must
> force yourself to consider arguments on the other side.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]], The Work Required to Have an Opinion

> Hold your ideas with the right grip. Let go of incorrect
> ideas.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project episode on Steve Wozniak
