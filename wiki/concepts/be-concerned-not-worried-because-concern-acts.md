# Be concerned, not worried, because concern acts

Concern implies you can do something about a problem, while worry assumes you are already helpless. Choose concern over worry.

> Never be worried. Be concerned.
>
> When you're concerned, you can do something about it. Worry is something you do when you've already decided you're helpless.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
