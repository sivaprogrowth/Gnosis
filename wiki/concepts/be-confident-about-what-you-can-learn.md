# Be confident about what you can learn

Confidence that you have everything figured out is arrogant and closed. Confidence that you can figure things out is humble and open, so be humble about what you know and confident about what you can learn.

> One version of confidence is: I've got this figured out.
>
> Another version is: I can figure this out.
>
> The first is arrogant and close-minded. The second is humble and
> open-minded.
>
> Be humble about what you know, but confident about what you can
> learn.
>
> — James Clear
