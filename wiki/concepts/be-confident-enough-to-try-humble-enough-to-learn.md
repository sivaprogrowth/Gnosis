# Be confident enough to try, humble enough to learn

Expertise comes from staying confident enough to keep attempting things while staying humble enough to keep learning, sustained over many years.

> An expert is someone who, over many years, manages to remain
> confident enough to keep trying and humble enough to keep
> learning.
>
> — James Clear

> "The mindset:
>
> Confident enough to start. Humble enough to keep learning.
>
> The trap:
>
> Too humble to start. Or too confident to keep learning."
>
> — James Clear
