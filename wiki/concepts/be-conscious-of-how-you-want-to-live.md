# Be conscious of how you want to live

The point isn't to work more or less or to indulge more. It is to be deliberate about how you want to live and what you spend your time on.

> The point isn't to work more or less, to eat more ice cream or
> less beans, it's to be conscious of how you want to live your
> life and what you want to spend your time on.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
