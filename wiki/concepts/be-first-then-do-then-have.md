# Be first, then do, then have

People often try to get things and money so they can do what they want and be happy. The real order is reversed: be who you truly are, do what you need to do, and then you will have what you want.

> Often people attempt to live their lives backwards: they try to
> have more things, or more money, in order to do more of what they
> want so that they will be happier.
>
> The way it actually works is the reverse. You must first be who
> you really are, then, do what you need to do, in order to have
> what you want.
>
> — Shakti Gawain, Creative Visualization
