# Be grateful for unwanted things you lack

When you don't get everything you want, remember the things you didn't get that you never wanted.

> If you don’t get everything you want, think of the things you
> don’t get that you don’t want.
>
> — Oscar Wilde, Don't Forget to Sing in the Lifeboats

> "Even if you don't have all the things you want, be grateful for
> the things you don't have that you don't want."
>
> — Bob Dylan, Chronicles, Volume One
