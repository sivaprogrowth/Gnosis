# Be impatient with effort, patient with results

Taking action daily teaches patience because you see how small improvements compound over time. Push hard on what you do each day, but wait calmly for the outcomes.

> Live with a bias toward action.
>
> Ironically, this will teach you patience. When you take action
> each day, you learn the value of accumulating small improvements
> over time. You understand how daily habits compound.
>
> Be impatient with your actions. Be patient with your results.
>
> — James Clear

> When you're impatient with results, every day feels expensive.
> When you're impatient with effort, every day feels like progress.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
