# Be kind, ask, and follow up

Three simple ways to get more of what you want are to be kind and pleasant to others, ask for what you want, and follow up.

> Three simple ways to get more of what you want in life:
>
> 1) Be kind and pleasant to others.
>
> 2) Ask for what you want.
>
> 3) Follow up.
>
> — James Clear
