# Be kind because power shifts over time

Kindness is worth it simply for decency, but even a purely self-interested person should be kind because you never know where people will end up years from now.

> Be kind. You should do it simply to be decent, but do it even if
> you’re a ruthless mercenary because you have no idea where people
> will end up in 10 years. The balance of power is always shifting.
> People will surprise you.
>
> — James Clear
