# Be pleasant, ask questions, engage daily to find opportunities

Being warm, curious, and engaged in your industry every day makes it very likely you will run into interesting opportunities.

> A simple recipe for finding opportunities:
>
> * Be pleasant
> * Ask questions
> * Engage daily
>
> It's hard for a warm and pleasant person who is asking a lot of
> questions and engaging in their industry daily to not come across
> interesting opportunities.
>
> — James Clear
