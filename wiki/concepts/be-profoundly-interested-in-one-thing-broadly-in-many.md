# Be profoundly interested in one thing, broadly in many

The good life combines deep specialist interest in one area with genuine interest in many other things.

> The whole secret of life is to be interested in one thing
> profoundly and a thousand other things well.
>
> — Hugh Walpole, Reading: An Essay
