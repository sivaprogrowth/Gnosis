# Be radically proactive about behaviors with ten-year payoffs

Behaviors whose benefits arrive a decade later deserve aggressive, deliberate investment now. Long-term payoffs justify going well beyond ordinary effort.

> Be radically proactive about any behavior that pays off in 10
> years.
>
> — James Clear
