# Be selective before trying to be productive

Productivity matters most for tasks you don't want to do. Instead of shortening unwanted work, choose work you'd be delighted to spend time on even if it went slowly, and focus on starting it.

> Productivity is most important for things you don't want to be
> doing. Most people want to increase productivity so they can
> spend less time on the task.
>
> But before you worry about being more productive, think about
> being more selective. Rather than focusing on increasing
> productivity, it may be worth asking, "What would I be delighted
> to spend time on, even if it went slowly?"
>
> Direct your energy toward figuring out how to start what you want
> to do rather than thinking about how to shorten what you don't
> want to do.
>
> — James Clear
