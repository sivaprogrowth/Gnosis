# Be sensible on small decisions, take risks on life-changing ones

The sensible choice only works well for small decisions. For decisions that could change your life, you have to take the risk.

> "I have noticed that doing the sensible thing is only a good idea
> when the decision is quite small. For the life-changing things,
> you must risk it."
>
> — Jeanette Winterson, Why Be Happy When You Could Be Normal?
