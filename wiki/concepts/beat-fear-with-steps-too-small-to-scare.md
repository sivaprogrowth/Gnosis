# Beat fear with steps too small to scare

You can overcome fear by moving toward it in steps small enough that they don't frighten you. As you get closer, you find there was little there to fear.

> One way to beat fear is with steps so small they don't scare you.
>
> As you get closer to fear, you realize there was never anything
> there to be afraid of.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
