# Beating the odds is easy because odds reflect averages

The odds are calculated from average people, so simply showing up and raising your effort puts you ahead of them.

> “It's a lot easier than you think to 'beat the odds' because the
> odds are based on average people. Show up and elevate.”
>
> — Dr. Julie Gurner
