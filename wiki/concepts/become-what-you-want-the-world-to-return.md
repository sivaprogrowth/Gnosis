# Become what you want the world to return

To get good treatment, offer it first: be a great partner, be kind, listen. The best way to achieve success is to deserve it.

> Become what you want to see in the world, and the world will
> return it to you. If you want an amazing relationship with your
> partner, be an amazing partner. If you want people to be
> thoughtful and kind to you, be thoughtful and kind to them. If
> you want people to listen to you, listen to them. The best way to
> achieve success is to deserve success.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
