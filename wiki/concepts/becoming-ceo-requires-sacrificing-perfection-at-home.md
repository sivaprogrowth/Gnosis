# Becoming CEO requires sacrificing perfection at home

Reaching the top means you can't be a perfect parent or spouse; you do your best and accept hard sacrifices.

> “You don’t get to be CEO by being the perfect Mom,
> the perfect wife. You don’t. You do the best you can. It’s hard
> to make those sacrifices.”
>
> — Indra Nooyi, The Knowledge Project podcast
