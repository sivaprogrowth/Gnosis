# Becoming exceptional requires saying no

Exceptional work needs focus, and much time is wasted optimizing things that should not be done at all. Being great at saying no is essential.

> You need focus to become exceptional at anything.
>
> Massive amounts of time and energy are wasted optimizing things
> that should be left undone.
>
> You have to be great at saying no.
>
> — James Clear
