# Becoming yourself is a continual process

Selfhood is never complete; you keep becoming more fully yourself over time through the choices you make.

> "I am Me, and I hope to become Me more and more."
>
> — Paula Modersohn-Becker, Letter to Rainer Maria Rilke (February 17, 1906)
