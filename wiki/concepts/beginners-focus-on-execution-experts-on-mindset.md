# Beginners focus on execution, experts on mindset

What deserves focus changes with skill level. Beginners should work on execution, intermediates on strategy, and experts on mindset.

> "Where to focus:
>
> For the beginner, execution.
>
> For the intermediate, strategy.
>
> For the expert, mindset."
>
> — James Clear
