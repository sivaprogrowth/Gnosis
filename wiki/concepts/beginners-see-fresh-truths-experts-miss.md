# Beginners see fresh truths experts miss

Long experience in a field biases you toward refining what you already do rather than exploring. Applying your expertise to something new brings back the fresh eyes of a beginner.

> The gift of a beginner is fresh eyes.
>
> The longer you're in a field, the harder it is to perceive new
> truths. Your mind is biased toward refining what you're already
> doing instead of exploring fresh terrain.
>
> Take your expertise and apply it to something new.
>
> — James Clear
