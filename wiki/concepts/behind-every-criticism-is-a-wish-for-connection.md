# Behind every criticism is a wish for connection

When partners complain or act passive-aggressively, they are really asking whether they matter to you.

> Behind every criticism is a wish: when partners complain or
> become passive-aggressive, they're asking, "Where are you? Do I
> matter to you?"
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
