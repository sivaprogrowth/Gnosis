# Being a fan beats being a critic

Building things up is more satisfying and enjoyable than tearing them down. Choose to spend your life supporting rather than criticizing.

> It's more fun to be a fan than a critic. I'm not looking to spend my life tearing things down, when it can be so satisfying to build things up.
>
> — James Clear
