# Being a merchant means sensing what will sell

Drexler argues retail success comes from instinct about what will sell rather than from crunching numbers, and he trusts that instinct as non-negotiable.

> “To be a merchant is having a sense of what’s going to sell. I
> always had an instinct on what I loved, and I learned later on—if
> I think it, it is. Non-negotiable.”
>
> — Mickey Drexler, The Knowledge Project Podcast
