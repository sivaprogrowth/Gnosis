# Being a tiny but useful nail is enough

Even a very small contribution has value if it is useful. Mary Oliver wishes to be a tiny but useful part of the larger whole.

> May I be the tiniest nail in the house of the universe, tiny but
> useful.
>
> — Mary Oliver, Upstream
