# Being alive today makes you rich in time

Of the 108 billion people who have ever lived, 93% are dead, so anyone alive is rich in time. Today is something every past ruler or celebrity would trade all their wealth for.

> You are richer than 93% of people.
>
> Not in money, but in time. 108 billion people have lived
> throughout history. 93% of them are dead.
>
> You have what every king and queen, every pharaoh and ruler,
> every CEO and celebrity of the past would give all their wealth
> for:
>
> Today.
>
> — James Clear
