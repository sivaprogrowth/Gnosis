# Being counted out is an advantage for the underdog

Being underestimated means opponents misread you and you push yourself harder. Digging in when others expect you to quit also earns you support.

> There's nothing better than being the underdog. The more people count me out, the more I count myself in. I don’t like to show all my cards too early, and that gives me two distinct advantages: my opponents often get the wrong read on me, and I push myself longer and harder.
>
> — T. Boone Pickens
