# Being easy to live with can destroy you

Making yourself agreeable and accommodating for others can come at the cost of the person you actually are, who still has to live your life.

> The cost of being easy to live with is destroying the person who has to do the living.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
