# Being good doesn't guarantee being noticed or chosen

Skill alone won't make people notice your work, trust you, or pick you; getting noticed, earning trust, and asking well are separate skills.

> Being good at what you do doesn’t guarantee that people will notice your work, trust you, or choose you.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project (episode with Ryan Serhant)
