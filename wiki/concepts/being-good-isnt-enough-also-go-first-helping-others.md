# Being good isn't enough; also go first helping others

Being a good person is only one key to opportunity; the second is proactively helping others first, and only both together unlock hidden opportunities.

> Most people are brought up believing that if they are a good
> person, good things will happen to them. While not entirely
> wrong, it’s not enough to unlock your potential. It's like
> walking up to a door with two locks and only one key. If you want
> to go inside, you need the other key. The second key is going
> positive and going first—proactively helping others. When both
> keys are used together, the door unlocks, and a previously hidden
> world of opportunities reveals itself.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
