# Being in the game beats having the best view

Watching from the sidelines may give you the clearest view, but the real action happens on the field. You have to choose between comfort as an observer and participation as a player.

> The best view of the game is probably from the stands. But that's not where the action is. And so you have to decide, do you want a nice view or do you want to be in the thick of it and playing the game?
>
> — James Clear
