# Being industrious is not enough; what are you industrious about?

Busyness alone is not a virtue, since ants are busy too; what matters is directing effort toward something worthwhile.

> It is not enough to be industrious; so are the ants. What are
> you industrious about?
>
> — Henry David Thoreau, Letter to H.G.O. Blake (November 16, 1857)
