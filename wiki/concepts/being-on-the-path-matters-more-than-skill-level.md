# Being on the path matters more than skill level

Whether you are a beginner or an expert matters less than whether you are consistently practicing. A beginner on the path just needs time, while an expert who stops will lose their expertise.

> "It doesn’t matter if you’re a beginner or an expert as long as
> you’re on the path.
>
> If a beginner is on the path, all they need is time.
>
> If an expert is off the path, they won’t be an expert for long."
>
> — James Clear
