# Being wrong is fine; thinking you're right isn't

Take full responsibility for your work whether it goes right or wrong. The real problem is not doing it wrong but doing it wrong and believing you did it right.

> [T]his is my work. I have to take full responsibility for doing
> it right as well as doing it wrong. Doing it wrong isn’t bad, but
> doing it wrong and thinking you’ve done it right is.
>
> — Toni Morrison
