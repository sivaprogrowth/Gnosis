# Being your own boss enables speed and conviction

Without outside investors demanding justification, a business can act fast on strong convictions, and speed, flexibility, and decision authority are vital when ideas must be implemented quickly.

> "If we had to please investors, we'd have stringent financial
> controls, we'd have to explain and justify every decision. That
> would be bad business for people who rely on strong feelings
> about what's right rather than on black and white "facts," which
> often turn out to be false. If you're your own boss, you can move
> fast. When ideas have to be implemented overnight, speed,
> flexibility, and authority to make decisions without consulting
> shareholders are of utmost importance."
>
> — Estée Lauder, Estée: A Success Story
