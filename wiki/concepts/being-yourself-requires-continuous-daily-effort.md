# Being yourself requires continuous daily effort

Outside expectations and social pressures constantly pull you toward what others want. Staying true to yourself is an ongoing battle, not a one-time choice.

> Being yourself is a continuous effort.
>
> There is always another expectation placed upon you, another
> person pulling you toward their preferences, another nudge from
> society to act a certain way.
>
> It's a daily battle to be yourself, not merely what the world
> wants you to be.
>
> — James Clear
