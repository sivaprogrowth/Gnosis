# Best practices guarantee average performance

Best practices are other people's prescriptions that reduce variation and bring you to a baseline. Following them keeps you working harder just to keep up, so being the best requires doing what no one can prescribe.

> Best practices guarantee average performance. They are literally the prescriptions of others that limit variation and get you to a baseline of performance. Following them virtually ensures that you will be working harder and harder just to "keep up." To be the best, you have to do things that no one can prescribe.
