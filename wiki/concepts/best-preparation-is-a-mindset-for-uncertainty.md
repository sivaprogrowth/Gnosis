# Best preparation is a mindset for uncertainty

Rather than planning for one specific scenario, the strongest preparation is developing a mindset capable of dealing with whatever uncertain situation arises.

> The ultimate form of preparation is not planning for a specific scenario, but a mindset that can handle uncertainty.
>
> — James Clear
