# Bet on your instinct and own your mistakes

Founders should trust their own instincts and accept the right to be wrong on their own terms, so they control their destiny.

> "You owe it to yourself to bet on your instinct. You owe it to yourself to lose because of yourself. It's your right to be wrong. You, as a founder, owe it to yourself to control your own destiny."
>
> — Mark Pincus, The Knowledge Project
