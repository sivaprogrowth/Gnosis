# Better to rest than climb the wrong mountain

Being alone beats spending time with toxic people, and doing nothing beats working on what doesn't matter. Rest is better than effort spent in the wrong direction.

> It's better to be alone than to spend time with toxic people.
>
> It's better to do nothing than to work on something that doesn't
> matter.
>
> It's better to rest than to climb the wrong mountain.
>
> — James Clear
