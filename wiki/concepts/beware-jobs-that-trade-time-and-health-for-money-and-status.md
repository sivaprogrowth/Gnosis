# Beware jobs that trade time and health for money and status

Wealth comes in at least four forms: financial, social, time, and physical. Be careful with jobs that offer money and status but take away your freedom and health.

> There are at least 4 types of wealth:
>
> * Financial wealth (money)
> * Social wealth (status)
> * Time wealth (freedom)
> * Physical wealth (health)
>
> Be wary of jobs that lure you in with 1 and 2, but rob you of 3
> and 4.
>
> — James Clear
