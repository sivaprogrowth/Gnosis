# Big achievements are built through consistent small daily work

Great achievements take a long time, but they are built through steady, continuous effort. Rome took years, yet the work happened every hour.

> Rome wasn’t built in a day, but they were laying bricks every hour.
>
> — James Clear

> "Rome wasn’t built in a day, but they were laying bricks every
> hour.
>
> You don’t have to do it all today. Just lay a brick."
>
> — James Clear
