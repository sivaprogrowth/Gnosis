# Biographies give pattern recognition for future problems

Reading biographies shows you how others handled situations you will later face. It's like having someone who solved your problem whisper the answer to you.

> Reading gives you pattern recognition for problems you haven't
> faced yet.
>
> Having someone who solved the same problem you're facing
> whispering the answer in your ear is basically a cheat code.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
