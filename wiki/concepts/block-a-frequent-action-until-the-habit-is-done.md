# Block a frequent action until the habit is done

Put a note on a door or phone you use often, and do not let yourself open or use it until you finish the habit. This turns an everyday action into a reminder and a gate.

> When you leave
> your bedroom in the morning, close the door and put a Post-It
> Note directly at eye level that says, "Write 500 words." At
> bedtime, you are not allowed to open the door until those words
> are written.
>
> — James Clear
