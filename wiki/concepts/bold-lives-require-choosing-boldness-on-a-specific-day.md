# Bold lives require choosing boldness on a specific day

There is never a perfect time to do something bold. Changing your trajectory requires one particular day when you simply make the choice.

> If you'd like to do something bold with your life, you will have
> to choose to do something bold on a specific day.
>
> There is no perfect day. There is no right time. For the
> trajectory to change, there has to be one day when you simply
> make the choice.
>
> — James Clear
