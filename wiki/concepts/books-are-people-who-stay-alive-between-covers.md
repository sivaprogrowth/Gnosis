# Books are people who stay alive between covers

Books hold most of the world's thoughts and offer comfort, answers, and company in sad and happy times. Reading is a private meeting with the author, who lives on through the book.

> Books are
> good company, in sad times and happy times, for books are
> people—people who have managed to stay alive by hiding between
> the covers of a book.
>
> — E.B. White, Letters to the Children of Troy
