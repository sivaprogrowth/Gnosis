# Books change minds better than conversations do

In conversation we are distracted by social worries about how we look and whether we'll lose friends. Books let you consider an idea without that social risk.

> Books are more likely to change minds than conversations.
>
> There is too much happening internally during conversation:
>
> Did that sound stupid?What do they think of me?Will I lose the
> friendship over this opinion?
>
> Books can let you chew on an idea without social risk.
>
> — James Clear
