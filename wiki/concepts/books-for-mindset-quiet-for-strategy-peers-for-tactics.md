# Books for mindset, quiet for strategy, peers for tactics

Different sources serve different purposes. Reading shapes your mindset, quiet thinking time produces strategy, and talking with successful peers gives you tactics.

> "Books for mindset.
>
> Quiet time to think for strategy.
>
> Conversations with successful peers for tactics."
>
> — James Clear
