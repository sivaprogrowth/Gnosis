# Boredom signals it is time to scale up a habit

When a habit becomes routine and boring, that is a sign to graduate it to the next level. You scale up when what was challenging is now normal.

> Once you know
> what to expect, habits tend to be less interesting. Sometimes
> they even become boring. This can be one of the first signals
> that it’s time to graduate your habit to the next level. You
> scale up when what was previously challenging is now the new
> normal.
>
> — James Clear
