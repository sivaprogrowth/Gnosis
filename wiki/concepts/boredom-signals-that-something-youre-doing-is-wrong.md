# Boredom signals that something you're doing is wrong

Boredom drains your energy and should be heeded as a signal that your work isn't quite right. The fix may be a small adjustment or a drastic change.

> Boredom is a tremendous indicator. Your energy goes; it caves in
> on you; you can’t do anything. That’s very frightening but you
> must listen because you are being told that whatever it is you
> are doing is not quite right. It may only need a small adjustment
> or it may need more drastic treatment.
>
> — Bridget Riley, The Eye’s Mind: Bridget Riley, Collected Writings, 1965–2009
