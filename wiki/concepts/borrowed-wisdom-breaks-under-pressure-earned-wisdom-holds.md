# Borrowed wisdom breaks under pressure; earned wisdom holds

Advice you adopt from others is a compression you don't understand, so it fails when tested. Wisdom from your own experience tells you when it works, why, and when to bend or ignore it.

> Borrowed wisdom breaks under pressure because you haven't earned
> it.
>
> You're trusting someone else's compression without knowing what
> created it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
