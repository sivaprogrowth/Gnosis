# Braving life's storms beats merely existing safely

Thompson asks whether the person who takes risks and truly lives is happier than the one who stays safe and merely exists, implying the bold life is better.

> Who is the happier man, he who has braved the storm of life and lived, or he who has stayed securely on shore and merely existed?
>
> — Hunter S. Thompson, Security (1955)
