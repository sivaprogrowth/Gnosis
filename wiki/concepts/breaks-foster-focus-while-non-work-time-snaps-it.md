# Breaks foster focus while non-work time snaps it

A deliberate break restores and supports focus, whereas simply spending time not working breaks concentration.

> A break and time spent not working are very different. One
> fosters focus, and the other snaps it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Maker vs. Manager
