# Brilliance comes from doing your best, not looking good

The sword glitters as a byproduct of the swordsman fighting hard, not because he tries to make it shine. What looks best comes from focusing on the work rather than appearances.

> The sword glitters not because the swordsman set out to make it
> glitter but because he is fighting for his life and therefore
> moving it very quickly.
>
> — C.S. Lewis, Surprised by Joy
