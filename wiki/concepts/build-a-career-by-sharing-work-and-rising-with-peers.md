# Build a career by sharing work and rising with peers

A seven-step career framework: do great work, share it publicly, reach out to people slightly ahead of you, trade ideas, meet in person, become friends, and rise together.

> How to build a career in 7 steps:
>
> 1. Do great work.
>
> 2. Share it publicly.
>
> 3. Cold email people 2 steps ahead of you.
>
> 4. Talk about your work and trade ideas.
>
> 5. Host events and meet in-person.
>
> 6. Become friends.
>
> 7. Rise together.
>
> — James Clear
