# Build a career starting from fascinating people

A great career has four qualities: enjoyment, competence, good pay, and fascinating people. Pursue them in reverse order: find fascinating people, see how to make money with them, then pick what you're good at and enjoy.

> The 4 qualities of a great career:
>
> 1) I enjoy it2) I’m good at it3) I make good money 4) I’m around
> fascinating people
>
> Answer in reverse order:
>
> 1) Where are fascinating people?2) In what ways can I make money
> with them?3) Which ones am I good at?4) Which ones do I enjoy?
>
> — James Clear
