# Build consistency first, then increase intensity

Start small and focus on showing up every day to build a new identity. Only after that should you increase the intensity.

> Consistency before intensity.
>
> Start small and become the kind of person who shows up every day.
> Build a new identity.
>
> Then increase the intensity.
>
> — James Clear
