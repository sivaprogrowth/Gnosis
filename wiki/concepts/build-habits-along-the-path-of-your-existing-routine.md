# Build habits along the path of your existing routine

Choose places for new habits that already lie on your daily path, because going out of your way adds friction.

> When deciding where to practice a new habit, it is best to choose
> a place that is already along the path of your daily routine.
> Habits are easier to build when they fit into the flow of your
> life.
>
> — James Clear
