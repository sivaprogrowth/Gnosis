# Build judgment by studying bad judgment

Good judgment can be developed by collecting examples of poor decisions and thinking about how to avoid those outcomes.

> “I sought good judgment mostly by collecting instances of bad
> judgment, then pondering ways to avoid such outcomes.”
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]
