# Build momentum in the right direction so inertia helps

Once something moves it is hard to stop, so the key is choosing the right direction and building momentum. Small wins then compound, as in the business flywheel.

> The key is to pick the right direction and build momentum so inertia works to your advantage and carries you forward.
>
