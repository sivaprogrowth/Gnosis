# Build on past masters with slight improvements

Carlsen sees his task as absorbing the knowledge uncovered by earlier chess masters and making small, continual improvements on it.

> “I have the utmost respect for -- for the masters of the past and
> they've -- you know, they've uncovered many of the secrets in
> chess so I think for me my main task is just to try and make
> slight improvements on that, you know, to amass all the knowledge
> and try to all the time use that and improve a little bit.”
>
> — Magnus Carlsen
