# Build strategy on what does not change

Asking what will stay the same over ten years matters more than asking what will change, because stable truths let you invest heavily with confidence the effort will keep paying off.

> "I almost never get the question: 'What's not going to change in
> the next 10 years?' And I submit to you that that second question
> is actually the more important of the two — because you can build
> a business strategy around the things that are stable in time…
>
> — Jeff Bezos, Jeff Bezos at reInvent, November 2012

> Because when everything changes, the real edge is
> knowing what doesn’t.
>
> — Bruce Flatt, The Knowledge Project podcast
