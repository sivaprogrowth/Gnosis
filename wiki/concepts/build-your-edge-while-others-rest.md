# Build your edge while others rest

Teenage Schwab ran his paper route before dawn and eventually owned every route in town; working when others rest builds an edge.

> Every morning before dawn, teenage Les ran his
> paper route. Not biked, ran. For two months, he sprinted through
> dark streets on foot, saving enough to buy a bicycle. While his
> classmates slept, he earned. By senior year, Les owned all nine
> routes in town. When your competition sleeps, you can build your
> lead.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project [Outliers]

> You win in the dark, when everyone else is partying or
> sleeping.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project episode on Steve Wozniak
