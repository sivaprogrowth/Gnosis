# Building one habit means building several habits

Focusing on one habit at a time is best partly because any single habit actually involves a cluster of supporting habits. Eating healthier, for instance, requires shopping, meal planning, prepping, and cleaning habits.

> My answer is nearly always to
> focus on one habit. Not only is this simpler, but it also
> addresses an often overlooked aspect of habit formation: when
> you’re building one habit, you’re often building multiple habits.
>
> — James Clear
