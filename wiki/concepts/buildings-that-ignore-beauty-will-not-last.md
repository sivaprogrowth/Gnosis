# Buildings that ignore beauty will not last

People have a deep need for beauty, and architecture that ignores it fails to endure because people never feel at home in it.

> There is a deep human need for beauty, and if you ignore that
> need in architecture, your buildings will not last, since people
> will never feel at home in them.
>
> — Roger Scruton, The Modern Cult of Ugliness
