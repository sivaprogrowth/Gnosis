# Bundled rewards condition you to do unwanted tasks

Even if you don't want to do a chore, you become conditioned to do it when it means getting something you really want along the way.

> even if you don’t really want to fold laundry or
> work on your book or workout or meal prep, you’ll become
> conditioned to do it if it means you get to do something you
> really want to do along the way.
>
> — James Clear
