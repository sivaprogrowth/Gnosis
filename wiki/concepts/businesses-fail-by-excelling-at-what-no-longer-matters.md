# Businesses fail by excelling at what no longer matters

Major business failures often come from continuing to be great at things that have lost their relevance, not from poor execution.

> The greatest business failures often come not from playing the
> game poorly, but from continuing to excel at things that no
> longer matter.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
