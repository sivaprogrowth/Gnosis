# Busyness replaces working smarter

When we can't find smarter ways to work, we default to working harder and cramming more in, valuing the appearance of busyness over actually getting things done.

> If we can’t work smarter, we resort to working harder. We cut
> out lunch, skip the gym, and run from one meeting to another so
> we can fit more in. Being busy seems to matter more than getting
> stuff done.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Much of What You’re Going to Do or Say Today is Not Essential
