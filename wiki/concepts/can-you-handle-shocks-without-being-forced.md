# Can you handle shocks without being forced?

Before following the math, ask whether you could face events like a pandemic, a market crash, or rising rates with equanimity, or whether circumstances would force you into choices you don't want.

> What if we have a pandemic? What if the stock market drops 20% or
> 30%? What if mortgage rates rise 10%? Can you handle these events
> with equanimity, or will circumstances force you to do something
> you don't want to do?
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
