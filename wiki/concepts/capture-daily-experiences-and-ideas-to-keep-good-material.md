# Capture daily experiences and ideas to keep good material

Most good stories and ideas are forgotten unless you record them. Writing down one interesting thing or idea each day and filtering over time leaves you with the best ones; great storytellers and thinkers simply have this capture habit and the patience to filter.

> Most good material is forgotten.
>
> Write down the funniest or most interesting thing that happens to
> you each day. Most days will be boring, but if you write
> something each day, then you'll have 5 to 10 entertaining stories
> within a year or two. People are sitting on more funny stories
> than they realize because they do not have a habit of capturing
> humorous things as they happen.
>
> Write down one idea each day. Most ideas will be simple and
> uninteresting, but within a year you'll have a handful that are
> compelling and useful. Little bits of genius are bubbling up all
> the time, but you need a habit of recording your ideas in order
> to capture the good ones.
>
> It's a myth that great storytellers only think of great stories
> or that insightful people only have brilliant ideas. But they do
> have a habit of capturing their experiences and insights, and the
> patience to filter the majority until the best remain.
>
> — James Clear
