# Care deeply about a small set of things

Caring about everything and caring about nothing are both disasters. Put your care into the few things that truly matter to you.

> Caring about everything is a disaster.
>
> Caring about nothing is also a disaster.
>
> Nurture the small pocket of things that truly matter to you.
>
> — James Clear
