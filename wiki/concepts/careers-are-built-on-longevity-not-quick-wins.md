# Careers are built on longevity, not quick wins

Success in big goals comes from enduring over the long term through ups and downs; hacks and shortcuts may yield quick wins but not longevity.

> I think
> success in any large goal comes down to your ability to endure
> over the long term. Perseverance. Resilience. People always talk
> about hacks and quick wins and “How can you shorten the
> timeline?” And ultimately I think that leads to maybe a quick win
> here and there, but not longevity. In business and in sport,
> careers are built on longevity, and that really comes down to a
> mindset
>
> — Rob Fraser, The Knowledge Project podcast
