# Careers progress from wanting meetings to avoiding them

As people advance, they first want to attend meetings, then to run them, and finally to avoid them altogether.

> The three stages of career development are:
>
> 1. I want to be in the meeting
>
> 2. I want to run the meeting
>
> 3. I want to avoid meetings.
>
> — Jay Ferro, Twitter
