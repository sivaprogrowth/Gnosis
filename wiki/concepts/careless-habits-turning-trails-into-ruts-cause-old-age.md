# Careless habits turning trails into ruts cause old age

Wharton argues that old age is produced by repeating the same things in the same way out of carelessness and later inertia. One stays alive longer by being unafraid of change, curious, interested in big things, and happy in small ways.

> "The producer of old age is habit: the deathly process of doing
> the same thing in the same way at the same hour day after day,
> first from carelessness, then from inclination, at last from
> cowardice or inertia.
>
> Habit is necessary; but it is the habit of having careless
> habits, of turning a trail into a rut, that must be incessantly
> fought against if one is to remain alive... one can remain alive
> long past the usual date of disintegration if one is unafraid of
> change, insatiable in intellectual curiosity, interested in big
> things, and happy in small ways."
>
> — Edith Wharton, A Backward Glance
