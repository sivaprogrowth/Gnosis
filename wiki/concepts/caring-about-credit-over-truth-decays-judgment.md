# Caring about credit over truth decays judgment

Chasing recognition distorts what you see, and recognition tends to come most reliably to people who ignore it and focus on seeing clearly.

> When you start caring more about credit than truth, your judgment
> decays.
>
> The scientist racing to publish first overlooks crucial evidence
> that doesn't fit their story. The writer chasing popularity loses
> what made their voice worth hearing.
>
> The strange thing is that recognition comes most reliably to
> those who forget about it and focus simply on seeing clearly.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
