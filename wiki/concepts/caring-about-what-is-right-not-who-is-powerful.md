# Caring about what is right, not who, is powerful

Bill Gates was formidable because he didn't mind admitting he was wrong; he cared about getting the right answer rather than being the one who was right.

> “Now, most people hate to admit they're wrong, but it didn't bother Bill one bit. All he cared about was what was right, not who was right. That's what makes Bill very, very dangerous.”
>
> — Larry Ellison
