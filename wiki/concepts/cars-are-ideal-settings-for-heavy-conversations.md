# Cars are ideal settings for heavy conversations

Sitting side by side in a car, looking at the road without eye contact, can make difficult conversations easier.

> "Sometimes being in a car, looking at the road, not having to
> make eye contact, is the ideal setting for heavy conversation."
>
> — Francine Prose, A Changed Man
