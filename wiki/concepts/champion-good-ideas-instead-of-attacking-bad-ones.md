# Champion good ideas instead of attacking bad ones

Your effort is better spent sharing and promoting good ideas than criticizing bad ones. Bad ideas are best left to be forgotten.

> Your time is better spent championing good ideas than tearing
> down bad ones.
>
> The best thing that can happen to a bad idea is that it is
> forgotten. The best thing that can happen to a good idea is that
> it is shared.
>
> Feed the good ideas and let bad ideas die of starvation.
>
> — James Clear
