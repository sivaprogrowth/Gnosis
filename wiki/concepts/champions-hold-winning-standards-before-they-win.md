# Champions hold winning standards before they win

Standards come first and results follow. People become winners by behaving with a winning standard of performance before they have the results.

> Champions behave like champions before they're champions; they
> have a winning standard of performance before they are winners.
>
> — Bill Walsh, The Score Takes Care of Itself
