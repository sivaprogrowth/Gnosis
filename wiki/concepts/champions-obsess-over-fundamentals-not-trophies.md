# Champions obsess over fundamentals, not trophies

Outliers focus on what they can control today and on perfecting the basics, rather than on outcomes like revenue or titles. Excellence comes from mastering the process, not from worrying about results.

> Champions obsess over fundamentals, not trophies.
>
> While others chase revenue and fancy titles, outliers focus on
> what they can control today: reading one more book, making one
> more phone call, and reviewing that email one final time. They
> know that excellence comes from perfecting the basics.
>
> Stop worrying about where the arrow lands. Master the art of
> drawing the bow.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
