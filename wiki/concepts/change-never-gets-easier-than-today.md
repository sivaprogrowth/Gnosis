# Change never gets easier than today

The longer you wait to act, the more your habits, beliefs, and comfort lock you into your current life. Acting now may be the easiest chance you will get.

> Today might be the best chance you have to take action.
>
> The longer you wait, the more deeply embedded you get in your
> current lifestyle.
>
> Your habits solidify. Your beliefs harden. You get comfortable.
>
> It will never be easy, but it may also never be easier than it is
> right now.
>
> — James Clear
