# Change one meal, not a lifelong habit

Rather than vowing to change forever, focus on changing the single choice in front of you. Repeated small choices become new habits.

> "Instead of imagining all the meals ahead of you, focus on the
> meal in front of you. Don't give up burgers for the rest of your
> life. Just order something different one time. It's hard to
> change lifelong habits, but it's not that hard to change a meal.
> Over time, those meals become your new habits."
>
> — Jonathan Safran Foer, We Are the Weather
