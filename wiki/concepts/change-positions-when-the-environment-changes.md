# Change positions when the environment changes

Adjusting direction in response to a changed environment is not inconsistency; being dogmatic about strategy when conditions shift is the real mistake.

> “Sometimes people say, “Oh, you flip-flop.” No, the environment
> changed. ... You cannot be dogmatic about your strategic
> direction. When the environment changes around you, you’ve got to
> zag a little bit."
>
> — Indra Nooyi

> “I believe in maximum flexibility, so I reserve the right to change my position on any subject when the external environment relating to any topic changes too.”
>
> — Henry Singleton
