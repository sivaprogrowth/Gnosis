# Change your life by mastering one habit

Trying to change everything at once pulls you back into old patterns. Focusing on one habit until it becomes normal changes your life naturally as a side effect.

> The most reliable way to change your life is by not changing
> your entire life.
>
> If you try to change everything all at once, you will quickly
> find yourself pulled back into the same patterns as before. But
> if you merely focus on changing one specific habit and work on it
> until it becomes part of your normal day, you will find your life
> changes naturally as a side effect.
>
> Improve the whole by mastering one thing.
>
> — James Clear
