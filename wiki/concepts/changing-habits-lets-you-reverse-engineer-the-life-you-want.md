# Changing habits lets you reverse-engineer the life you want

Knowing how to change your habits lets you own your days and focus on the behaviors with the most impact. That lets you work backward from the life you want.

> Knowing how to change your habits means knowing how to
> confidently own and manage your days, focus on the behaviors that
> have the highest impact, and reverse-engineer the life you want.
>
> — James Clear
