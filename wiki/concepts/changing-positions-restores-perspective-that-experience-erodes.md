# Changing positions restores perspective that experience erodes

The longer you hold a role, the more you forget what it is like to be on the other side of it. Switching positions gives you a new perspective that can improve your methods.

> "The longer you're a teacher, the less you remember what it is
> like to be a student.
>
> The longer you're a doctor, the less you remember what it is like
> to be a patient.
>
> The longer you're a coach, the less you remember what it is like
> to be a player.
>
> Change positions. A new perspective can improve your old
> methods."
>
> — James Clear
