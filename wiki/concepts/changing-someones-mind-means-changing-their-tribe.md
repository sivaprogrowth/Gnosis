# Changing someone's mind means changing their tribe

People resist abandoning beliefs because it risks losing their social ties. To persuade someone, you must give them a community to go to so they don't end up lonely.

> Convincing someone to change their mind is really the process of
> convincing someone to change their tribe. If they abandon their
> beliefs, they run the risk of losing social ties. You can’t
> expect someone to change their mind if you take away their
> community too. You have to give them somewhere to go.
>
> Nobody wants their worldview torn apart if loneliness is the
> outcome.
>
> — James Clear
