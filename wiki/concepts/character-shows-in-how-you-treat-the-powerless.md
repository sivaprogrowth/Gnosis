# Character shows in how you treat the powerless

A person's character is best revealed by how they treat people who can't benefit them and people who can't fight back.

> The best index to a person's character is (a) how he treats
> people who can't do him any good, and (b) how he treats people
> who can't fight back.
>
> — Abigail Van Buren, Dear Abby column (May 16, 1974)
