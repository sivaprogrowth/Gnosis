# Charity can be a daily lifestyle, not just a gift

Charity can be practiced every day. You can read authors generously, give people your full attention when listening, and share your expertise freely at work.

> "Charity can be a lifestyle, not merely a gift.
>
> Read charitably. Give the author your most favorable
> interpretation.
>
> Listen charitably. Donate your undivided attention.
>
> Work charitably. Be generous with your expertise.
>
> In this way, you make charity a daily habit."
>
> — James Clear
