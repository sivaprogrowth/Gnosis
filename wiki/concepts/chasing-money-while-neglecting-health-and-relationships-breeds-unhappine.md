# Chasing money while neglecting health and relationships breeds unhappiness

Spending your time accumulating wealth while letting your health and relationships decline is a reliable path to being unhappy.

> If you want a recipe for unhappiness, spend your time accumulating a lot of money and let your health and relationships deteriorate.
>
> — James Clear
