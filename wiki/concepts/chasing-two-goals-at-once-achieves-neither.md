# Chasing two goals at once achieves neither

Splitting your effort between two directions at the same time stops you from making progress on either one.

> The person attempting to travel two roads at once will get
> nowhere.
>
> — Xun Zi, Iron Sharpens Iron: Wisdom of the Ages

> He who chases two hares catches neither.
>
> — Desiderius Erasmus, Adagia
