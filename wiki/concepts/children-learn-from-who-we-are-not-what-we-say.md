# Children learn from who we are, not what we say

Our example teaches children far more than our words, so we must embody what we want them to become. This applies to leaders as well.

> What we are teaches the child far more than what we say, so we
> must be what we want our children to become.
>
> — Joseph Chilton Pearce, Teaching Children to Love
