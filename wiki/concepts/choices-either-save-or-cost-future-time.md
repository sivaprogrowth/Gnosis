# Choices either save or cost future time

A simple filter for daily decisions is to ask whether a choice will cost you time later or save you time later.

> Will this cost me time in the future or save me time in the
> future?
>
> — James Clear

> "Time assets vs. Time debts.
>
> Time assets are choices that save you time in the future. Think:
> saying no to a meeting, automating a task, working on something
> that persists and compounds.
>
> Time debts are choices that must be repaid and cost you time in
> the future. Think: saying yes to a meeting, doing sloppy work
> that will need to be revised, etc.
>
> Time assets are an investment. Time debts are an expense."
>
> — James Clear
