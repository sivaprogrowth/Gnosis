# Choose a beautiful life over an easy one

Hard work that most people would dismiss as not worth the effort can be worth it when the goal is a beautiful life rather than an easy one.

> ... looking at the work done and the work still to do, most
> people would have written the whole thing off as too much sweat
> for too little gain. But I didn't want an easy life. I wanted a
> beautiful one.
>
> — Ben Short, Burn
