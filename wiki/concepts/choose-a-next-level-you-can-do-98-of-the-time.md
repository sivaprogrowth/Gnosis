# Choose a next level you can do 98% of the time

A useful metric for graduating a habit is picking a level exciting enough to end boredom but easy enough to complete nearly always.

> One of the metrics I like to keep in
> mind is choosing a new level that is exciting enough that I’m no
> longer bored, but easy enough that I know I’ll be able to do it
> 98% of the time.
>
> — James Clear
