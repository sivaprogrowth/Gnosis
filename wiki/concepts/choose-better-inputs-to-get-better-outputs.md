# Choose better inputs to get better outputs

Actions come from thoughts, thoughts come from what you consume, and today consumption is shaped largely by your social media feed. Improving your inputs improves your results.

> Your actions are a consequence of your thoughts.
>
> Your thoughts are a consequence of what you consume.
>
> And in the modern age, what you consume is largely a consequence
> of how you select and refine your social media feed.
>
> Choose better inputs. Get better outputs.
>
> — James Clear
