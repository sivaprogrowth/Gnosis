# Choose carefully what you will be ignorant about

No one can learn everything, so ignorance is unavoidable. The real choice is which topics deserve your limited attention.

> The world contains far more information than any single person
> can learn in their lifetime.
>
> The question is not whether you are ignorant, but what you choose
> to be ignorant about.
>
> Few topics are worth your precious time. Choose what you pay
> attention to with great care.
>
> — James Clear
