# Choose rewards that align with your desired identity

External rewards can become the thing you chase, so pick rewards that reinforce who you want to be. A new jacket conflicts with a saving habit and ice cream conflicts with an exercise habit, casting votes for opposing identities.

> The key is to not lose sight of your desired
> identity, and whenever possible, to choose an external reward
> that reinforces the type of person you wish to be. You want to
> avoid rewards that conflict with your desired identity.
>
> — James Clear
