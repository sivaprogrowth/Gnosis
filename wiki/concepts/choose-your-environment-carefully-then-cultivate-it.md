# Choose your environment carefully, then cultivate it

Environment multiplies or divides your effort. First select well (spouse, business, city), then nurture what you chose into the best it can be.

> Environment can multiply (or divide) your effort.
>
> Before you plant the garden, study the soil. After you plant the
> garden, tend the weeds.
>
> First, selection. Think carefully about the right person to marry
> or business to pursue or city to live in.
>
> Then, cultivation. Once you choose, care for it lovingly and
> nurture it into the best it can be.
>
> — James Clear
