# Choosing the right habit is the most important step

The most important decision is which habit to build; picking the right one makes progress easy, while the wrong one makes life a struggle.

> Pick the right habit and progress is easy. Pick the wrong habit
> and life is a struggle. It is much more important to work on the
> right habit than it is to work really hard.
>
> — James Clear

> The most important habit is choosing the right habit to work
> on.
>
> — James Clear
