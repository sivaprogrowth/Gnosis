# Choosing your attitude is the last freedom

Everything can be taken from a person except the freedom to choose their attitude and their own way in any circumstances.

> Everything can be taken from a person but one thing: the last of
> the human freedoms—to choose one's attitude in any given set of
> circumstances, to choose one's own way.
>
> — Viktor Frankl, Man's Search For Meaning
