# Chosen talent is underrated compared to natural talent

Talent comes in two forms: what you're born with and what you earn through mindset, choices, and work. The earned kind matters more than people think.

> There are two types of talent: the natural talent you're born with and the chosen talent you earn with your mindset, choices, and work.
>
> Natural talent is overrated. Chosen talent is underrated.
>
