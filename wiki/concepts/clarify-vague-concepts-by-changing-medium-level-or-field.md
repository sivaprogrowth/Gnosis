# Clarify vague concepts by changing medium, level, or field

When you can't articulate a concept, try expressing it in a different medium, explaining it at a bigger-picture or finer level, or imagining it in a different field.

> "How to clarify a concept you can't articulate: 1. Change
> mediums. Draw it. Photograph it. Sing it.2. Change levels.
> Explain what is one level up (bigger picture) or one level down
> (finer details).3. Change fields. What would this concept look
> like in different fields?"
>
> — James Clear
