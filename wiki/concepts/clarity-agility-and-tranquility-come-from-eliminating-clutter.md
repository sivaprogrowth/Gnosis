# Clarity, agility, and tranquility come from eliminating clutter

Removing mental clutter brings clarity, removing physical clutter brings agility, and removing spiritual clutter brings tranquility.

> "Clarity is the elimination of mental clutter.
>
> Agility is the elimination of physical clutter.
>
> Tranquility is the elimination of spiritual clutter."
>
> — James Clear
