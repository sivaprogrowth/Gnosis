# Clarity is kind, not cruel

Nice people soften rejection with false hope, while kind people say no clearly so you can move on. Clear answers are a valuable gift.

> Nice
> people soften rejection with false hope. Kind people say no and
> let you move on. Clarity isn't cruel. It's the most expensive
> gift you can give.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project (Anna Wintour episode)
