# Clear thinking rests on three pillars

Clear thinking means positioning yourself to play on easy mode, managing the circumstances that tend to think for you, and thinking independently.

> * Position yourself to play on easy mode
> * Manage the circumstances that tend to think for you
> * Think independently
>
