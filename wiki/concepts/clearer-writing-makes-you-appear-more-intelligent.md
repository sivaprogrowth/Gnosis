# Clearer writing makes you appear more intelligent

You can't quickly increase your intelligence, but you can spend time making your writing clearer. Ideas that are easy to understand make you seem smarter.

> You can't double your intelligence in one hour, but you can use
> one hour to write something twice as clear. And ideas that are
> easy to read and easy to understand will make you seem smarter.
> The better you communicate, the more intelligent you appear.
>
> — James Clear
