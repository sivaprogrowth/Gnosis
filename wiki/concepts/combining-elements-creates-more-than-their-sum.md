# Combining elements creates more than their sum

Like metal alloys, mixing the right skills, people, or ideas in the right proportions produces something stronger and rarer than any pure ingredient; too much or too little of one makes it brittle.

> Alloying is the art of mixing elements to create something
> greater than the sum of its parts. While our intuition tells us
> that pure substances are best, alloying shows this is not always
> true. One plus one can equal ten.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
