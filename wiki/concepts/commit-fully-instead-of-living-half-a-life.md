# Commit fully instead of living half a life

Avoid half-hearted relationships and half-hearted living; give full commitment to love, friendship, and life itself.

> “Do not love half lovers. Do not entertain half friends. Do not live a half life...and do not die a half death.”
>
> — Kahlil Gibran
