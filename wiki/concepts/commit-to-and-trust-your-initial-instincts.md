# Commit to and trust your initial instincts

In creative work, you must trust your first instincts and commit firmly to what you are making and why.

> “The only thing you can do is trust your initial instincts. You
> just have to say, 'This is what I’m making. This is what I’m
> doing. This is why I wrote this script. It is going to work. Just
> trust it.'”
>
> — Christopher Nolan
