# Commit to just one set and momentum follows

When you don't feel like working out, promise yourself only a small start. Doing a little usually leads to doing the whole thing.

> I can't tell you how many times I don't feel like training and
> tell myself, "I'll just do one set and see how I feel."
>
> You would think it would stop working at some point, but I almost
> always end up doing the whole workout.
>
> Just start. Do a little and see what happens.
>
> — James Clear
