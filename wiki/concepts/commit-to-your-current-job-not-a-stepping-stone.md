# Commit to your current job, not a stepping stone

Commit fully to your current job instead of viewing it only as a path to the next one.

> Don't treat your job as a
> stepping stone.
>
> — Barry Diller, The Knowledge Project

> When you stop treating your current opportunity as a stepping
> stone to something else and start treating it as the only one
> that matters, opportunity finds you.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
