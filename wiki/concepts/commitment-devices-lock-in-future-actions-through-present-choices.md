# Commitment devices lock in future actions through present choices

A commitment device is a choice made now that constrains what you do later, like a no-cancellation class policy, website blockers, or automatic savings.

> A commitment device is a choice you make in the present that
> locks in your actions in the future.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 14
