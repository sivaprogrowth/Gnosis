# Committed people do what interested people won't

Being interested in something differs greatly from being committed to it. Commitment shows in willingness to do what mere interest avoids.

> There is a big difference between being interested in something
> and being committed to something.
>
> Committed people do what interested people won’t.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Most people are interested. Few are truly committed.
>
> Interested people act when it's convenient; committed people act
> no matter what. Interested people do the minimum; committed
> people push beyond limits. Interested people wait to be told;
> committed people take the initiative.
>
> Fully committing is the key to accomplishment.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
