# Committing to nothing makes everything look worth doing

Without a firm commitment, you have no filter for choices, so every option seems appealing. Commitment is what lets you say no.

> If you commit to nothing, everything looks worth doing.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
