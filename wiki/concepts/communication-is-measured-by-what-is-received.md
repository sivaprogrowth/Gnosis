# Communication is measured by what is received

What matters in communication is what the other person actually hears, not what you meant. If there is a gap, you need to find a different way to say it.

> Communication is about what is received, not what is intended.
> If there is a gap between what you are saying and what they are
> hearing, you have to find a new way to say it.
>
> — James Clear
