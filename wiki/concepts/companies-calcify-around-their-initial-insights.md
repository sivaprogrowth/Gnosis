# Companies calcify around their initial insights

Companies start out solving problems in unconventional ways. Over time they become rigid around the insights that first made them succeed.

> Companies follow a similar arc. They begin by solving problems in
> unconventional ways, then gradually calcify around their initial
> insights.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project
