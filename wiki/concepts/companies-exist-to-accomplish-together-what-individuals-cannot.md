# Companies exist to accomplish together what individuals cannot

Making money is a result of a company, not its purpose. A company is a group of people who join to accomplish something collectively they couldn't do separately and to contribute to society.

> I think many people assume, wrongly, that a company exists
> simply to make money. While this is an important result of a
> company’s existence, we have to go deeper and find the real
> reasons for our being. As we investigate this, we inevitably come
> to the conclusion that a group of people get together and exist
> as an institution that we call a company so they are able to
> accomplish something collectively which they could not accomplish
> separately. They are able to do something worthwhile— they make a
> contribution to society (a phrase which sounds trite but is
> fundamental).
>
> — David Packard, The HP Way
