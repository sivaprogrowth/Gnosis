# Companies fail by drifting one comfortable lie at a time

Failure is rarely a single decision. Organizations slide toward it gradually through small, comfortable self-deceptions.

> No company decides to fail. It drifts there one comfortable lie at a time.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project, conversation with Kaz Nejatian
