# Compare every choice to your best opportunity

Think in terms of opportunity cost: judge any option against the best alternative available to you.

> “All intelligent people should think primarily in terms of
> opportunity cost. When deciding whether to do something compare
> it with the best opportunity you have.”
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]
