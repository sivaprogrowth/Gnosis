# Compensation follows autonomy, not performance

People who need to be managed face a cap on earnings, while those who create value without being told have no cap. The gap is structural and driven by autonomy.

> Compensation follows autonomy.
>
> When you need to be managed, there's a cap on what you can earn.
> When you know how to create value and do it without being told,
> there's no cap. This is structural, not personal.
>
> The compensation gap isn't about performance; it's about
> autonomy.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
