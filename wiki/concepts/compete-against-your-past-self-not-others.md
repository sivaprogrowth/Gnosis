# Compete against your past self, not others

You can judge yourself by your own standards or against others. Measuring internally drives growth, while comparing externally causes most problems.

> There are two ways to measure how you are doing: internally or externally. Most of our growth comes from internal measurement, while most problems stem from external comparison.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> "Compete externally and you compare.
>
> Compete internally and you improve."
>
> — James Clear

> You have to run your own race. Problems begin the moment you
> start comparing your results to someone who is playing under
> different conditions.
>
> — James Clear

> Improvement is being better than your past self.
>
> It doesn't have to be more complicated than that. Do not compare
> against others, compare against your past self.
>
> Keep the focus internal.
>
> — James Clear
