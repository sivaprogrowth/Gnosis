# Compete fully but remember the game doesn't matter

Give total effort while you are competing, but once you step away, keep perspective that the game is ultimately unimportant.

> When you're on the field, play as if nothing else matters.
>
> When you're off the field, remember that the game doesn't matter
> at all.
>
> — James Clear
