# Competence earns reward but character earns love

Competence is how good you are when there is something to gain, and character is how good you are when there is nothing to gain. People reward competence but love character.

> “Competence is how good you are when there is something to gain.
> Character is how good you are when there is nothing to gain.
> People will reward you for competence. But people will only love
> you for your character.”
>
> — Mark Manson
