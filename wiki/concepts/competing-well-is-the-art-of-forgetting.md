# Competing well is the art of forgetting

To compete, you must forget your limits, doubts, pain, and past rather than carry them into the contest.

> “The art of competing, I'd learn from the track, was the art of forgetting ... You must forget your limits. You must forget your doubts, your pain, your past.”
>
> — Phil Knight
