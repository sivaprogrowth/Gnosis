# Complaining takes time from improving

Time spent complaining about what you think you deserve is time taken away from creating something. Put your attention on what is within your control.

> The more time you spend complaining about what you deserve, the
> less time you have to focus on what you can create. Focus on what
> you can control.
>
> — James Clear

> All complaining comes at the expense of improving.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project episode on Jim Clayton
