# Compromise on small things to save influence

Give way on minor issues but hold firm on important principles. Not sweating the small stuff builds goodwill and preserves your influence for what matters.

> Compromise on small things but stand firm on important principles. By not sweating the small stuff, you build goodwill and save your influence for when it matters.
