# Compromising your standards for admiration creates problems

Trouble begins when we lower the standards we set for ourselves to win others' approval, focusing on how things look to others rather than on reality.

> Problems arise when we start compromising our own standards,
> those we have set for ourselves, in order to earn the admiration
> of others. Problems come when we choose to focus on what others
> think and see versus reality.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Inner Scorecard
