# Concentrated attention is what makes effort powerful

Put all your thoughts on the task in front of you. Like sunlight, which only burns when focused, effort only has real force when concentrated.

> Concentrate all your thoughts upon the work at hand. The sun’s
> rays do not burn until brought to a focus.
>
> — Alexander Graham Bell, How They Succeeded: Life Stories of Successful Men Told By Themselves
