# Concentration builds wealth, diversification preserves it

Big wealth comes from concentrating on a few bets. Diversifying is how you keep wealth once you have it.

> Concentration produces wealth.
>
> Diversification protects wealth.
>
> — James Clear

> “I believe that fortunes are built by focus and preserved through diversification.”
>
> — Strauss Zelnick
