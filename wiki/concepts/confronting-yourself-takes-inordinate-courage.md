# Confronting yourself takes inordinate courage

Introspection and accepting your limitations require great courage, because we seem designed by nature to fool ourselves.

> “It takes inordinate courage to introspect, to confront oneself,
> to accept one’s limitations—scientists are seeing more and more
> evidence that we are specifically designed by mother nature to
> fool ourselves.”
>
> — Nassim Taleb
