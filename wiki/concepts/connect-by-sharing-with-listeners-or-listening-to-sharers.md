# Connect by sharing with listeners or listening to sharers

Connection happens when you match with someone: share with a person who wants to listen, or listen to a person who wants to share.

> "How to connect with others:
>
> Share with someone who wants to listen or listen to someone who
> wants to share."
>
> — James Clear
