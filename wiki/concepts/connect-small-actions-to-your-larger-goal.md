# Connect small actions to your larger goal

A simple way to win is to tie small daily actions to a bigger aim. Short bursts of work toward a meaningful cause accumulate, while most actions evaporate.

> One of the simplest ways to win is to always connect the small
> things you do to the larger thing you hope to accomplish.
>
> Five minutes can be spent working on something trivial or working
> on something life-changing. A brief session of work oriented
> toward a great cause is always time well spent.
>
> Most daily actions evaporate. Some accumulate.
>
> — James Clear
