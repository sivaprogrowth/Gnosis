# Consensus that something will never work signals opportunity

Anna Wintour put Madonna on Vogue's cover after being told it would never happen, and it sold 200,000 extra copies. When everyone agrees something won't work, that's often when it will.

> When everyone agrees something would “never” work,
> that’s precisely when it will. Consensus kills innovation.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project (Anna Wintour episode)
