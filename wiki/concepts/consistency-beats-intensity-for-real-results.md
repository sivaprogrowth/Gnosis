# Consistency beats intensity for real results

Most people benefit more from steady, repeated effort than from dramatic bursts. Intense feats are memorable, but consistent habits are what actually produce progress.

> Most people need consistency more than they need intensity.
>
> Intensity:
> -run a marathon
> -write a book in 30 days
> -silent meditation retreat
>
> Consistency:
> -don't miss a workout for 2 years
> -write every week
> -daily silence
>
> Intensity makes a good story. Consistency makes progress.
>
> — James Clear

> In the short term, you are as good as your intensity. In the long
> term, you are only as good as your consistency.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> “You don't need more intensity; you need more consistency.
>
> Intensity impresses; consistency transforms.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Short-term results come from intensity.
>
> Long-term results come from consistency.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Intensity makes for a good story. Consistency makes
> for good results.
>
> — James Clear, The Knowledge Project
