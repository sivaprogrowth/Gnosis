# Consistency compounds while inconsistency does not

Small, consistent progress on your most important objective compounds, while inconsistent progress is illusory. Make a little progress today and repeat tomorrow.

> "Consistent progress compounds.
>
> Inconsistent progress is a lie.
>
> Make a little progress today on your most important objective.
> Repeat tomorrow."
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Time is the friend of the consistent and the enemy of the
> inconsistent.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Consistency compounds while occasional brilliance fades.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
