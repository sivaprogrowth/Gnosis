# Consistency in practice means adaptability

Consistency in practice comes from adapting your habits to the day rather than rigid determination. When time or energy is short, scale down or do the easy version so you still show up.

> In theory, consistency is about being disciplined, determined,
> and unwavering.
>
> In practice, consistency is about being adaptable. Don't have
> much time? Scale it down. Don't have much energy? Do the easy
> version. Find different ways to show up depending on the
> circumstances. Let your habits change shape to meet the demands
> of the day.
>
> Adaptability is the way of consistency.
>
> — James Clear
