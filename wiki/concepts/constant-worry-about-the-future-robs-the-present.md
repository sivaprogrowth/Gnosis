# Constant worry about the future robs the present

Rumination and the need to control the future take away your ability to enjoy the current moment. Preparation has its place, but constantly thinking ahead means you never enjoy the present.

> Relax. Your rumination, analysis, worry, and need to control the
> future are robbing you of the current moment. Yes, there is a
> time for preparation, but continually thinking of the future
> guarantees you'll never enjoy in the present.
>
> — James Clear
