# Contentment is easier than happiness and enough

Like a cat that returns to the same sunny spot rather than endlessly searching for a better one, a wise person knows it is fine not to climb every mountain. Contentment is easier to reach than happiness, and it is enough.

> A good monk would know that contentment is easier to attain than
> happiness, and that it is enough.
>
> — Ruskin Bond, A Book Of Simple Living
