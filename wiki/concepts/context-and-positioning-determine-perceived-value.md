# Context and positioning determine perceived value

The same product placed in a different context, like cake called a muffin, gets different competitors, prices, and customer assumptions. Positioning shapes how people perceive and value something.

> The product, however, is the same. That thing that you’re eating is a piece of cake, whether it’s in cake form or muffin form. I can choose to position it as a muffin. It’s the same product, but the assumptions about that… The competitors are different; your value is different; the assumptions that customers have about that product are different because I’ve placed it in a different context.
>
> — April Dunford

> Context creates value.
>
> — Rory Sutherland, The Knowledge Project
