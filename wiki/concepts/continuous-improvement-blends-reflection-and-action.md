# Continuous improvement blends reflection and action

Clear's preferred approach to living better combines reflection (listening, reading, questioning) with action (uniting, participating, discussing).

> My preferred approach for continuous improvement is a blend of
> reflection and action, and that combination seems particularly
> relevant right now.
>
> — James Clear
