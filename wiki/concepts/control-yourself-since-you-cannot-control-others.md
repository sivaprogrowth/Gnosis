# Control yourself since you cannot control others

People and events will inevitably hurt and disappoint you, usually without intending to. Since you can't control them, the only solution is to work hardest on your personal growth and your own response.

> Since you cannot control the weather, or the
> traffic, or the one you love, or your neighbors, or your boss,
> then you must learn to control you … the one whose response to
> the difficulties of life really counts.
>
> — Jim Rohn, The Seasons of Life
