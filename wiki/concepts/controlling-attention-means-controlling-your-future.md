# Controlling attention means controlling your future

How well you direct your attention determines how much you can shape where your life goes.

> The more control you have over your attention, the more control
> you have over your future.
>
> — James Clear

> The more control you have over your attention, the more control
> you have over your future. And it starts with having enough
> courage to protect your time.
>
> It's so easy to say yes. We want to be agreeable, helpful, liked.
> That's how time disappears and attention becomes fragmented: not
> in big chunks, but in a thousand small concessions.
>
> What you trade your attention for is what your life becomes.
>
> — James Clear
