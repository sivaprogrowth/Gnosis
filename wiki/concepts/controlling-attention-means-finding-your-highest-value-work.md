# Controlling attention means finding your highest-value work

Controlling your attention is the most important skill, and it goes beyond avoiding distractions. It means figuring out the best use of your time given what matters to you and identifying what truly moves the needle.

> "Arguably the most important skill is controlling your attention.
> This goes beyond merely avoiding distractions. The deeper skill
> is finding the highest and best use for your time, given what is
> important to you. More than anything else, controlling your
> attention is about being able to figure out what you should be
> working on and identifying what truly moves the needle."
>
> — James Clear
