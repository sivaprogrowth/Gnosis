# Controlling your thoughts lets you control yourself

The only real way to be in control of your life is to be in control of your own thoughts.

> Ultimately, the only way to truly be in control of your life is to be in control of your thoughts.
>
> — James Clear

> “I'm a very introspective. I spend a lot of time in my head, and
> it's not a bad place to be. I journal a lot. I break down all of
> my thought processes. I think I apply a very analytical lens to
> my own thinking, and I kind of modify it because it's so
> interesting. You can control what you think. You can control how
> you think, and therefore, you can control who you are."
>
> — Eileen Gu
