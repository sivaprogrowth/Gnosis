# Convenience determines which habits win

People follow the path of least resistance, so the most convenient option usually wins. Making good choices easier and bad choices harder improves behavior naturally.

> Your habits are often a byproduct of convenience. Humans are
> wired to seek the path of least resistance, which means the most
> convenient option is often the one that wins. Make good choices
> more convenient and bad choices less so. Behavior will improve
> naturally.
>
> — James Clear
