# Courage and stupidity look the same

Both courage and stupidity involve knowing something might hurt and doing it anyway. Telling them apart is what makes life hard.

> Courage is knowing it might hurt, and doing it anyway. Stupidity
> is the same. And that's why life is hard.
>
> — Jeremy Goldberg
