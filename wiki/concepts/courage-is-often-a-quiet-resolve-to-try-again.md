# Courage is often a quiet resolve to try again

Courage is not always loud or dramatic; often it is simply deciding at day's end to try again tomorrow.

> "Courage doesn't always roar. Sometimes courage is a quiet voice
> at the end of the day saying, 'I will try again tomorrow.'"
>
> — Mary Anne Radmacher, 1985 poem
