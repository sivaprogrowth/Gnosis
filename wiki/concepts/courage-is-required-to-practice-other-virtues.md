# Courage is required to practice other virtues

Courage is the most important virtue because without it you cannot practice any other virtue reliably.

> “[Courage is] the most important of all the virtues. Without that
> virtue you can’t practice any other virtue with consistency."
>
> — Maya Angelou, The Art of Fiction No. 119

> Without courage we cannot practice any other virtue with consistency. We can't be kind, true, merciful, generous, or honest.
>
> — Maya Angelou
