# Courage starts with willingness to look foolish

Few mistakes are truly unrecoverable; what stops us from starting is fear of embarrassment, not failure itself. Being courageous begins with accepting you might look stupid.

> The list of mistakes you can never recover from is very short.
>
> But you likely realize your life will not be destroyed if your
> book doesn’t sell or if a potential date turns you down or if
> your startup goes bust. It’s not the failed outcome that
> paralyzes us. It’s the possibility of looking stupid, feeling
> humiliated, or dealing with embarrassment that prevents us from
> getting started at all.
>
> The first step to being courageous is being willing to look
> foolish.
>
> — James Clear
