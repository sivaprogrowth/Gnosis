# Courage to act separates success from failure

What often separates successful people from failures is not better abilities or ideas but the courage to bet on their ideas, take a calculated risk, and act.

> Often the difference between a successful person and a failure
> is not one's better abilities or ideas, but the courage that one
> has to bet on one's ideas, to take a calculated risk—and to act.
>
> — André Malraux, Don't Forget to Sing in the Life Boats
