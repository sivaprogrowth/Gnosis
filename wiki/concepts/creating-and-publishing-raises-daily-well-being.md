# Creating and publishing raises daily well-being

Days spent completing and sharing creative work feel significantly better. Creative output and well-being are linked.

> The quality of my life is significantly higher on days that I
> publish an article. The creative process and well-being go
> hand-in-hand.
>
> — James Clear
