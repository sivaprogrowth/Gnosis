# Creating for expected approval kills authentic work

Musicians' first albums come from genuine feeling with no expectations, but after validation they start making what they think the world wants, which is their biggest mistake.

> “Do you know the biggest mistake most musicians make?” He said.
> “Their first album comes from love, heartbreak, passion, or
> depression. They have no expectation of how the world will
> respond. They write it from the heart, and if it catches on,
> they’re validated by the world. But then they start writing their
> second album, and they don’t necessarily write it based on love,
> heartbreak, or passion. They write the album they think the world
> will want."
>
> — Rick Rubin
