# Creation requires questioning every borrowed conclusion

To create something, you must question everything and test others' conclusions against your own reasoning and imagination before accepting them.

> To create, one must first question everything. Never adopt
> someone else's conclusion without putting it to the test of your
> own reasoning and imagination.
>
> — Eileen Gray, Eileen Gray: Her Work and Her World
