# Creative quality approaches the leader's level of taste

The quality of any creative work tends to rise only as high as the taste of the person in charge of it.

> The quality of any creative endeavor tends to approach the level
> of taste of whoever is in charge.
>
> — John Gruber, The Auteur Theory of Design (2009)
