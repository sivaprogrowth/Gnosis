# Creativity follows five steps: discover, collect, generate, combine, refine

The creative process means taking in the world, recording what strikes you, brainstorming from your notes, connecting unrelated ideas, and then editing down to the best.

> The creative process:
>
> * Discover - Read a lot. Observe the world. Notice.
> * Collect - Immediately record anything that strikes you.
> * Generate - Build on your notes to brainstorm lots of ideas.
> * Combine - Connect previously unconnected ideas.
> * Refine - Edit, edit, edit. Select the best.
>
> — James Clear
