# Creators fail by avoiding contact with their users

Founders and makers tend to build elaborate visions alone instead of talking to users, and delay shipping out of embarrassment and fear of feedback, shrinking from the real world.

> What you will get wrong is that you will not pay enough
> attention to your users.
>
> You will make up some idea in your own head that you will call
> your "vision", and you will spend a lot of time thinking about
> your vision. In a cafe. By yourself. And build some elaborate
> thing without going and talking to users, because that's doing
> sales, which is a pain in the ass, and they might say no.
>
> You will not ship fast enough because you're embarrassed to ship
> something unfinished, and you don't want to face the likely
> feedback that you will get from shipping. You will shrink from
> contact with the real world, contact with your users. That's the
> mistake you will make.
>
> — [[paul-graham|Paul Graham]], A Conversation with Paul Graham
