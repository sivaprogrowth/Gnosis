# Credibility is expensive because the bills never stop

Credibility is paid for continuously through private integrity, money left on the table, and saying hard things. It takes years to build before anyone notices and can be lost in an afternoon.

> Credibility is expensive because the bills never stop.
>
> You pay it in conversations no one overhears, in deals where you
> leave money on the table, in credit you give away. You pay for it
> every time you say the hard thing instead of the easy one.
>
> The strange part is that you're paying for years before anyone
> notices, and you can lose it all in an afternoon.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
