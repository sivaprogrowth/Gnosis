# Criticizing is easy; improving or creating tests insight

Tearing down work is fast and easy while making it is slow and hard. Real insight shows in helping improve the work or building something yourself.

> "Criticizing is easy and fast. Creating is difficult and slow.
> The two hours you spent on a book or movie usually took two years
> to produce.
>
> Anyone can tear down someone else’s work. The true test of
> insight is whether you can help them improve it or build
> something of your own."
>
> — Adam Grant, Twitter
