# Criticizing is easy until you must perform

It is easy to criticize others' work from the sidelines, but much harder when you are the one who has to do it.

> Criticizing a musician is easy, but it is more difficult when
> you have a guitar in your hand.
>
> — Chilean saying, Otra cosa es con guitarra
