# Curating close friends adds more healthy years

The strongest way to extend healthy life is to build a close circle of friends you can talk with meaningfully and call on bad days. Such friends do more for you than any drug or supplement.

> I argue that the most powerful thing you can do to add healthy
> years is to curate your immediate social network. In general, you
> want friends with whom you can have a meaningful conversation.
> You can call them on a bad day and they will care. Your group of
> friends are better than any drug or anti-aging supplement, and
> will do more for you than just about anything.
>
> — Dan Buettner, The Power of Positive People
