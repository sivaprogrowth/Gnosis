# Curiosity in the beginning makes people better in the end

There are endless chances to improve every day, and spotting them mostly comes down to curiosity. Those who end up better usually started out curious.

> There are nearly endless opportunities to improve each day and
> finding them largely boils down to being curious.
>
> People who are better in the end are usually curious in the
> beginning.
>
> — James Clear
