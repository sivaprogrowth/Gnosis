# Current results reflect the habits you repeat

Results like net worth, weight, knowledge, and clutter reflect the habits you repeated over time. You get what you repeat.

> Your outcomes are a lagging measure of your habits. Your net
> worth is a lagging measure of your financial habits. Your weight
> is a lagging measure of your eating habits. Your knowledge is a
> lagging measure of your learning habits. Your clutter is a
> lagging measure of your cleaning habits. You get what you
> repeat.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> Your current habits are perfectly designed to deliver your
> current results.
>
> — James Clear
