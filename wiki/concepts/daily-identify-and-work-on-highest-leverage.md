# Daily identify and work on highest leverage

Each day, spend some time identifying your highest leverage activity and some time actually working on it. This simple two-step routine produces exceptional results.

> The 2-step process for exceptional results:
>
> (1) Spend a little time each day thinking about the highest
> leverage activity available to you.
>
> (2) Spend a little time each day working on it.
>
> — James Clear
