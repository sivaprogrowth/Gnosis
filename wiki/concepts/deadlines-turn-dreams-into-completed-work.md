# Deadlines turn dreams into completed work

Deadlines force you to ship imperfect work, and the ingenious decisions made under time pressure are what make the work distinctive.

> Deadlines were the difference between a dream and something that
> you complete. And what happens with deadlines is that you’ve got
> to ship, you have to abandon the project, and it’s not perfect.
> Because it’s not perfect, you kind of have to be ingenious about
> making it a little different.
>
> — Kevin Kelly, The Knowledge Project Podcast
