# Death will not wait until you're less busy

If you want to do something, do it now. Being busy does not protect you from death, and it won't come at a convenient time.

> Do not wait. If there is something you wish to do, go do it.
>
> Death comes for busy people too. It will not pause and return at
> a more convenient time.
>
> — James Clear
