# Debt defines your future and kills hope

Taking on debt commits your future to paying for your past, and a defined future erodes hope.

> Debt defines your future, and when your future is defined, hope
> begins to die. You have committed your life to making money to
> pay for your past.
>
> — Kent Nerburn, Simple Truths: Clear & Gentle Guidance on the Big Issues in Life
