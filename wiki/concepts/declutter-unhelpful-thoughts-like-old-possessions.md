# Declutter unhelpful thoughts like old possessions

Your thoughts and actions are yours, like your belongings. Just as you periodically clear out old clothes, you can let go of unhelpful thoughts and outdated behaviors.

> Your thoughts and actions belong to you just like your
> possessions. Every so often, it helps to declutter—donate old
> clothes or clear out a crowded shelf. Maybe it's time to let go
> of some unhelpful thoughts or outdated actions too?
>
> — James Clear
