# Deep listening means risking being changed

Truly listening requires surrendering preconceptions, quieting your own thoughts, and asking what is at stake for the other person, then returning to your own view and noticing how you've changed.

> Deep listening is an act of surrender. We risk being changed by
> what we hear.
>
> — Valarie Kaur, See No Stranger
