# Defending your limitations makes them yours

A prompt to examine whether your habitual response is to justify what you can't do or to explore what you could do.

> Is your default setting to defend your limitations or expand your
> possibilities?
>
> — James Clear

> Argue for your limitations and, sure enough, they're yours.
>
> — Richard Bach, Illusions
