# Defensiveness blocks learning from feedback

It is hard to learn while feeling defensive. Trusting that you can recover lets you accept feedback without taking it personally and ask what the moment teaches.

> It's hard to learn when you feel defensive.
>
> Relax. You are capable and you can recover from whatever you face.
>
> You can accept feedback without taking it personally. What is this moment teaching you?
>
> — James Clear
