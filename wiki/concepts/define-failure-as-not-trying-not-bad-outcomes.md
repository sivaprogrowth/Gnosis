# Define failure as not trying, not bad outcomes

Sara Blakely's father asked his children weekly what they had failed at, to keep them from fearing failure. This taught her that failure means not trying something you want to do, not missing the right outcome.

> His
> attitude taught me to define failure as not trying something I
> want to do instead of not achieving the right outcome.
>
> — Sara Blakely, Getting There
