# Delay your intuition until all information is in

Most people form a quick impression and then spend their time confirming it. Better judgment comes from gathering all the information before letting intuition decide.

> Delay Your Intuition: Most people form an impression in
> seconds and spend the rest of their time confirming it. The best
> wait for all the information before letting their intuition
> speak.
>
> — Daniel Kahneman, The Knowledge Project
