# Delayed gratification pays more as it grows rarer

As people delay gratification less and less, those who can still delay it gain increasingly large advantages.

> The world seems to be accustomed to delaying gratification less and less, which means the rewards of delaying gratification grow more and more.
>
> — James Clear
