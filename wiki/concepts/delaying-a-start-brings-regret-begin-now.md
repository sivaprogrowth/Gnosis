# Delaying a start brings regret; begin now

Delaying a start brings regret later. The best time to begin is now.

> “A year from now you will wish you had started today.”
>
> — Karen Lamb

> Many people wish they started sooner. Almost nobody wishes they
> started later.
>
> — James Clear
