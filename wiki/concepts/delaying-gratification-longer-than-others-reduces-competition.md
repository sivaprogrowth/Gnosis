# Delaying gratification longer than others reduces competition

Most people optimize only for the near term, so someone willing to plan and delay gratification over several years faces less competition and gains a decisive advantage.

> Most people optimize for the day ahead. A few people optimize
> for 1-2 years ahead. Almost nobody optimizes for 3-4 years ahead
> (or longer).
>
> The person who is willing to delay gratification longer than most
> reduces competition and gains a decisive advantage.
>
> Patience is power.
>
> — James Clear
