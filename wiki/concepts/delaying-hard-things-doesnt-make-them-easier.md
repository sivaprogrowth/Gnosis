# Delaying hard things doesn't make them easier

Waiting to face something unpleasant doesn't change how unpleasant it is, so hesitating only prolongs the dread.

> “The cold water doesn't get warmer if you jump late.”
>
> — Unknown

> If you wait, will it get easier, or will you just be older?
>
> — James Clear

> Delaying a conversation doesn't make it easier.
>
> — James Clear
