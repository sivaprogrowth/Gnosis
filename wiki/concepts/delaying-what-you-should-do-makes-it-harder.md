# Delaying what you should do makes it harder

Putting off something you know you should do makes it harder over time. The easy choice now is often the hard one later, so accept pain today for gain tomorrow.

> The longer you delay that thing you know you should do, the more
> difficult it gets. The easier thing in the short run is often the
> harder thing in the long run. Pain today, gain tomorrow.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
