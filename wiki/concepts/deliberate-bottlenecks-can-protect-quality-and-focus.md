# Deliberate bottlenecks can protect quality and focus

Some bottlenecks, like airport security, serve a necessary purpose. Be intentional about which bottlenecks you keep, since a deliberate one focuses effort while an accidental one just drags.

> The key is to be intentional about your bottlenecks. Choose them
> wisely and make sure they serve a purpose. A deliberate
> bottleneck can be a powerful tool for focusing effort and
> maintaining quality, while an accidental bottleneck is just a
> drag on the system.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v3: Systems and Mathematics
