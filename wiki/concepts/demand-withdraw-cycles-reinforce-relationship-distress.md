# Demand-withdraw cycles reinforce relationship distress

Relationships fall into a predictable pattern where one partner pursues connection and the other pulls away, creating a self-reinforcing negative loop.

> Every relationship follows predictable patterns: one person
> demands connection while the other withdraws, creating a
> self-reinforcing negative cycle.
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
