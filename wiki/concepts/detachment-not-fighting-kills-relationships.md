# Detachment, not fighting, kills relationships

The warning sign of a dying relationship is no longer feeling annoyed and instead feeling nothing toward your partner.

> The warning sign that a relationship is dying isn't fighting,
> but when you stop getting annoyed with your partner and feel
> nothing. Detachment is the true relationship killer.
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
