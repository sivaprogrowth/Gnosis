# Detailed questions expose fakes; real experts know the details

Fake people can relay a conceptual theory but fuzz out when pressed on details, while genuine people have spent years obsessing over the details.

> “You ask increasingly detailed questions and people have trouble
> making things up and things just fuzz into obvious BS, and fake
> founders basically have the same problem. They’re able to relay a
> conceptual theory of what they’re doing… But as they get into the
> details, it just fuzzes out. Whereas the true people that you
> want to back can do it. What you find is they’ve spent 5 or 10 or
> 20 years obsessing over the details of whatever it is they’re
> about to do. And they’re so deep in the details and they know so
> much more about it than you ever will.”
>
> — [[marc-andreessen|Marc Andreessen]]
