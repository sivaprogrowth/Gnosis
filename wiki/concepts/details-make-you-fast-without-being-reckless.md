# Details make you fast without being reckless

Speed alone is cheap; being fast without recklessness comes from deep knowledge of the details, built through slow study. Mastery of details speeds you up rather than slowing you down.

> Anyone can move fast. That's the trap. Speed is cheap, but the
> ability to be fast without being reckless is expensive.
>
> The chess master's lightning moves come from decades of slow
> study. The CEO who is in the weeds knows where the problems lurk.
>
> Details don't slow you down; they speed you up.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
