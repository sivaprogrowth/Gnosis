# Devotion for trivial actions, humor for life and death

Treat small everyday actions with great care and mindfulness. Meet the gravest matters with a sense of humor.

> To the most trivial actions, attach the devotion and mindfulness
> of a hundred monks. To matters of life and death, attach a sense
> of humor.
>
> — Zhuangzi
