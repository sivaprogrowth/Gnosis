# Difficulties and crises contain opportunities

Training yourself to look for the opportunity in every challenge or tragedy lets you take control and bring something good out of it.

> In every challenge or even tragedy, there is an opportunity. And
> if you train yourself to look for the opportunity, you will be
> able to take control of the situation and even turn it into a
> positive or if it can't be turned into something good, at least
> something good could come out of it.
>
> — Menachem Mendel Schneerson, Likutei Sichot: The Collected Writings of the Lubavitcher Rebbe

> In a crisis, don’t mourn the damage. Instead, focus on
> capitalizing on the opportunity that just opened up.
>
> — Connor Teskey, The Knowledge Project

> “Opportunity follows difficulty.”
>
> — Jim Rohn
