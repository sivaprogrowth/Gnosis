# Disappoint others rather than disappoint yourself

Living true to yourself means accepting that you will disappoint as many people as necessary to avoid betraying yourself.

> Your job, throughout your entire life, is to disappoint as many
> people as it takes to avoid disappointing yourself.
>
> — Glennon Doyle, Untamed

> A lesson I need to learn: It's ok to disappoint other people if
> you avoid disappointing yourself.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
