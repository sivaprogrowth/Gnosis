# Discipline fills in when spite and curiosity are absent

Spite drives intensity and curiosity drives endurance, but when you lack both motivations, discipline is what keeps you working.

> Spite makes you work harder. Curiosity makes you work longer. Discipline is what you need when you have neither.
>
