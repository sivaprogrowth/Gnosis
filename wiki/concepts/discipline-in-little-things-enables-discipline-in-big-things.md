# Discipline in little things enables discipline in big things

Buffett refused a lopsided golf bet with tiny downside because discipline is built through small decisions; lax habits in small matters carry over to large ones.

> Warren replied, “If you are not
> disciplined in the little things, you won’t be disciplined in the
> big things.”
>
> — Warren Buffett, Interview with Walter Schloss carried in Outstanding Investor Digest (June 23rd, 1989)
