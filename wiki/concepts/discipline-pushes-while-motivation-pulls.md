# Discipline pushes while motivation pulls

Discipline is a push that is useful for restarting a lapsed routine, but top performers are pulled by internal motivation and obsession rather than slogging through. At our best we are deeply engaged, not forcing ourselves.

> discipline requires a lot of push, right? “I’m very disciplined about my
> workout. I’m really going to push it.” Motivation is a pull. It
> is something that is internal that fuels you, that drives you.
>
> — Julie Gurner, The Knowledge Project Podcast
