# Discipline sustained for months leads to burnout

Discipline is useful for getting through occasional bad days, but relying on it for months at a time is a sign you will burn out.

> Discipline can get you through bad days, but if it's getting you through months, you're going to burn out.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
