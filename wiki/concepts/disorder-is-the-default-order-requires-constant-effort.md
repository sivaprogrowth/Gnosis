# Disorder is the default; order requires constant effort

Entropy affects every part of life, so nothing stays as we leave it. Keeping our health, relationships, careers and possessions in order takes never-ending effort, because order is artificial and temporary.

> We cannot expect anything to stay the way we leave it. To maintain our health, relationships, careers, skills, knowledge, societies, and possessions requires never-ending effort and vigilance. Disorder is not a mistake; it is our default. Order is always artificial and temporary.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Entropy
