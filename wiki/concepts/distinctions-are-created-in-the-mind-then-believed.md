# Distinctions are created in the mind, then believed

Many categories we treat as real, like east and west in the sky, are mental constructs we invent and then mistake for truth.

> “In the sky, there is no distinction between east and west;
> people create distinctions in their own minds and then believe
> them to be true.”
>
> — Gautama Siddhartha
