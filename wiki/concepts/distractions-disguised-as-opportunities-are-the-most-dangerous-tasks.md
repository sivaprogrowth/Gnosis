# Distractions disguised as opportunities are the most dangerous tasks

The riskiest to-do items are those that seem like opportunities but actually pull you away from what matters.

> The most dangerous items on your to-do list are the ones that
> look like opportunities, but are actually distractions.
>
> — James Clear
