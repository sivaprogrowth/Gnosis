# Diverging means losing the map, not just facing criticism

Doing what everyone does gets you what everyone gets, so different results require diverging. Criticism is the easy part; the hard part is defining success yourself while the conventional path's rewards stay visible and yours stay invisible.

> The math is simple: if you do what everyone does, you get what
> everyone gets. If you want different results, you have to
> diverge.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
