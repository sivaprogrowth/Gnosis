# Divide life into ten-minute units and waste few

Ten minutes can achieve a lot, and once spent they never return. Treat your life as ten-minute units and give as few as possible to meaningless activity.

> You can do so much in ten minutes’ time. Ten minutes, once gone,
> are gone for good. Divide your life into 10-minute units and
> sacrifice as few of them as possible in meaningless activity.
>
> — Ingvar Kamprad, Men’s Health (October 2017, page 41)
