# Divided attention prevents building momentum

Spreading your attention across many things makes it difficult to gain momentum on any one of them.

> It's hard to build momentum if you're dividing your attention.
>
> — James Clear

> It's hard to build momentum if you keep dividing your
> attention.
>
> — James Clear
