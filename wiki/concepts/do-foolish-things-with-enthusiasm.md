# Do foolish things with enthusiasm

Mistakes and foolishness are inevitable, so commit to them wholeheartedly rather than half-heartedly.

> You will do foolish things, but do them with enthusiasm.
>
> — Sidonie-Gabrielle Colette, Cheri (unconfirmed)
