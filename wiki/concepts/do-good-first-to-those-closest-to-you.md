# Do good first to those closest to you

Since you cannot help everyone, give special attention to the people circumstance has placed near you.

> Since you cannot do good to all, you are to pay special
> attention to those who, by the accidents of time, or place, or
> circumstances, are brought into closer connection with you.
>
> — Saint Augustine, De Doctrina Christiana
