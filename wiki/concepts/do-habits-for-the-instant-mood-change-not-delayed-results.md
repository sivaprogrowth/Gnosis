# Do habits for the instant mood change, not delayed results

For nearly any habit, focus on how it makes you feel right away rather than on results that arrive later. Mood changes are instant, so they keep you going, while results are delayed.

> Do it for the change
> in mood (which is instant), not the change in results (which is
> delayed).
>
> — James Clear
