# Do humble tasks as though they were great

Keller saw her chief duty as doing ordinary tasks as if they were great and noble, because the world moves forward through the accumulated small efforts of honest workers, not just heroes.

> I long to accomplish a great and noble task; but it is my chief
> duty and joy to accomplish humble tasks as though they were great
> and noble. ... The world is moved along, not only by the mighty
> shoves of its heroes, but also by the aggregate of the tiny
> pushes of each honest worker.
>
> — Helen Keller, Optimism
