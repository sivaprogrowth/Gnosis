# Do the hardest unpleasant task first without dwelling

When facing an unpleasant task, don't stare at it too long. If there are several, tackle the biggest one first.

> “If you have to swallow a frog, don’t look at him too long. If
> you have to swallow two frogs, swallow the big scudder first.”
>
> — Jim Clayton, The Knowledge Project episode on Jim Clayton
