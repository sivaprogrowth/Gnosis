# Do the most important thing first each day

If you tackle your most important task at the start of each day, every day will be productive regardless of what follows.

> Do the most important thing first each day and you'll never have
> an unproductive day.
>
> — James Clear

> I think this is the only productivity tip many people ever need:
> do the most important thing first each day.
>
> — James Clear
