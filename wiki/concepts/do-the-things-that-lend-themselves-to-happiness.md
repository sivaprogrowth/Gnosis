# Do the things that lend themselves to happiness

Happiness comes from habits that support it: exercise, eating well, doing joyful things even when unmotivated, and surrounding yourself with admirable people.

> “Do things that lend themselves to the happiness you desire.
> Exercise. Eat well. Do something that makes you feel joy, even
> when you don’t feel like it. Surround yourself with people you
> admire and who add substance to your life.”
>
> — Jewel
