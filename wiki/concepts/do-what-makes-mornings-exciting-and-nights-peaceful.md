# Do what makes mornings exciting and nights peaceful

All self-help reduces to two tests: does this make you excited to wake up, and does it let you sleep in peace? Anything failing both is noise.

> The entire self-help industry in one sentence: Do what makes
> mornings exciting and nights peaceful.
>
> Will this make me excited to wake up? Will this let me sleep in
> peace?
>
> Everything that fails both tests is noise.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
