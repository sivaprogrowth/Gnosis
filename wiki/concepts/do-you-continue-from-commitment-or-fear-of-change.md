# Do you continue from commitment or fear of change?

Sticking with something can look like commitment but actually be fear of change. Ask whether it still serves you or is just familiar.

> What looks like consistency and commitment can sometimes be fear in disguise. Are you sticking with it because it still serves you or because it's what you already know?
>
> — James Clear

> Are you continuing to do it mostly because it makes you feel
> interested and alive — or are you continuing to do it mostly
> because you know what to expect and you're hesitant to make a
> change?
>
> — James Clear
