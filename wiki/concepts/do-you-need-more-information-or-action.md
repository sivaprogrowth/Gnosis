# Do you need more information or action?

A prompt to check whether seeking more information is really necessary, or whether you already know enough to act.

> Do I actually need more information or do I simply need to act on
> the information I already have?
>
> — James Clear

> Do I need to spend more time searching for better information or
> do I need to spend more time acting on the information I already
> have? Is the bottleneck strategy or execution?
>
> — James Clear

> Think about something you are facing right now. Have you been
> trying to think your way to a solution or act your way to a
> solution? Sometimes you need more action, sometimes you need a
> better strategy.
>
> — James Clear
