# Do your daily habits lead to desired results?

A prompt to check whether the outcomes you hope for are actually consistent with what you do every day.

> Are the results I am expecting aligned with the habits I am
> following each day?
>
> — James Clear

> Can my current habits carry me to my desired future?
>
> — James Clear
