# Do your work and like-minded people find you

However isolated you feel, doing your work truly and conscientiously attracts like-minded people who will seek you out.

> No matter how isolated you are and how lonely you feel, if you do your work truly and conscientiously, unknown friends will come and seek you.
>
> — Carl Jung, Letter to Miguel Serrano (September 14, 1960)

> My father always told me:
>
> 'If you want to go for a run, go for a run, don't look for company. Sooner or later, on your fifth run or your twentieth, like-minded people will find you themselves.'
>
> And only recently have I realized that this principle works everywhere.
>
> — Paul Sharp, Instagram
