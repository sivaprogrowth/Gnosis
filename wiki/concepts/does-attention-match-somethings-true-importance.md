# Does attention match something's true importance?

A prompt to check whether the attention you devote to something is proportional to how much it actually matters.

> Does the amount of attention I’m giving this match its
> importance?
>
> — James Clear

> Ask yourself: Does the attention you give to something truly
> reflect its true importance?
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
