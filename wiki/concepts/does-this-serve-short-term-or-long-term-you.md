# Does this serve short-term or long-term you?

A prompt to check whether a choice serves immediate gratification or your longer-term self.

> Am I doing this for Present Me or Future Me?
>
> — James Clear

> What is best in the short-term? What is best in the long-term?
>
> — James Clear
