# Does your information diet support your good days?

Identify what you spend time on during a good day, then ask whether your daily information sources support or distract from that lifestyle.

> When I'm living a good day, what am I spending my time on?
>
> Do the sources of information I am exposed to each day support
> that type of lifestyle or distract from it?
>
> — James Clear
