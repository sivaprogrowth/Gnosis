# Does your situation need more exploration or persistence?

Exploration finds what works and persistence gets the most out of what already works. The reader is asked which one their current situation calls for.

> Exploration is how you discover what works.
>
> Persistence is how you make the most of what already works.
>
> What does your situation require? More exploration or more
> persistence?
>
> — James Clear
