# Doing hard things builds proof you are capable

Self-image is built from evidence of past abilities, so pushing yourself through hard things makes later challenges feel manageable, while avoiding them makes mild difficulties seem insurmountable.

> The ability to do hard things is perhaps the most useful ability
> you can foster in yourself or your children. And proof that you
> are someone who can do them is one of the most useful assets you
> can have on your life resume.
>
> Our self-image is composed of historical evidence of our
> abilities. The more hard things you push yourself to do, the more
> competent you will see yourself to be.
>
> — Nat Eliason, Twitter
