# Doing it right the first time saves effort

Getting something right the first time is difficult, but having to redo it is even more difficult, so it pays to do it right.

> Doing it right is hard. Doing it over is harder.
>
> — James Clear

> The hard way is the fast way.
>
> Do it right the first time and you won't have to do it over the
> next time.
>
> — James Clear
