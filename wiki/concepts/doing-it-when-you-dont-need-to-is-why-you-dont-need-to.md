# Doing it when you don't need to is why you don't need to

Going to the gym or saving money when it seems unnecessary is exactly what keeps you strong and financially secure. Habits are the source of your strength.

> The fact that you go to the gym even though you don’t “need”
> to... is why you don’t need to.
>
> The fact that you save when you could spend... is why you have
> money to spend.
>
> Your habits create your strength.
>
> — James Clear
