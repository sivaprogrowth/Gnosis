# Doing something publicly makes you take it seriously

Making your work public adds social pressure that pushes you to think, train, and learn harder. Publishing, competing, and presenting all force you to raise your standards.

> If you want to take something more seriously, do it publicly.
>
> Publishing an article pressures you to think clearly. Competing
> in a race pressures you to train consistently. Presenting on any
> topic pressures you to learn it.
>
> Social pressure forces you to up your game.
>
> — James Clear
