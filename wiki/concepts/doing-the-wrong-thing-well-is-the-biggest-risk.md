# Doing the wrong thing well is the biggest risk

Doing the right thing well produces big outcomes, doing the right thing poorly still teaches you something, but executing the wrong thing well is dangerous because it reinforces the wrong direction.

> Big outcome: Do the right thing well.
>
> Big lesson: Do the right thing poorly.
>
> Big risk: Do the wrong thing well.
>
> — James Clear
