# Doing what everyone does yields their results

Greatness requires choices that differ from those around you; if you choose like your friends, you will get the same results they get.

> You can't achieve greatness by doing what everyone else is
> doing.
>
> If your choices resemble those of your friends, you'll get the
> same results they get.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> The price you pay for doing what everyone else does is getting
> what everyone else gets.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
