# Don't add your own obstacles; be your biggest believer

The world already supplies plenty of obstacles, so there's no need to talk yourself out of things. Treat your mind as a friend and believe in yourself.

> It's not my job to tell myself no. The world will offer plenty of obstacles. I don't need to add to them. My mind is my friend. I am my own biggest believer.
>
> — James Clear
