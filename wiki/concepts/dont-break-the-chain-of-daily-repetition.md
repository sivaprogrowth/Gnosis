# Don't break the chain of daily repetition

Once you start tracking, aim never to break the chain of completed days. Unbroken chains of saving, meditating, reading, or practicing compound into wealth, focus, and mastery.

> “Don’t break the chain” is a powerful mantra. Don’t break the
> chain of stashing money away in your savings account every month
> and you’ll build wealth and gain peace of mind.
>
> — James Clear
