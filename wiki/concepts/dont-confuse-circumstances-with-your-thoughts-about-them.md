# Don't confuse circumstances with your thoughts about them

A common self-deception is treating our interpretation of a situation as if it were the situation itself.

> “One of the biggest mistakes we make in our stories is confusing our circumstances with our thoughts about those circumstances.”
>
> — Jim Murphy
