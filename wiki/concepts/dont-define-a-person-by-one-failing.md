# Don't define a person by one failing

A person shouldn't be judged by a single thing that went wrong. Their many good deeds still count even when one thing didn't turn out as expected.

> You cannot define a person on just one thing. You can’t just
> forget all these wonderful and good things that a person has done
> because one thing didn’t come off the way you thought it should
> come off.
>
> — Aretha Franklin, Aretha: Star’s Legacy Lives, Detroit Free Press (February 18, 2012)
