# Don't get deterministic about a single metric

Stay conscious of consequences and avoid fixating on any one metric or variable.

> "You’ve got to be really conscious of the consequence and not get too deterministic about a single metric or a single variable."
>
> — Bill Gurley, The Knowledge Project
