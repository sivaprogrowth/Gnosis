# Don't let a proxy become the target

Metrics like beating the market or reading more books are only stand-ins for the real goals of building wealth and understanding. Optimizing the proxy can pull you away from the actual outcome.

> What is the real goal?
>
> The real goal is not to “beat the market.” The goal is to build
> wealth.
>
> The real goal is not to read more books. The goal is to
> understand what you read.
>
> Don’t let a proxy become the target. Don’t optimize for the wrong
> outcome.
>
> — James Clear
