# Don't let bad examples ruin your faith in the world

Negative examples spread faster and louder than good ones, so you'll always see some. Don't lose faith because of them; keep living well and being a role model.

> The negative examples always spread farther and faster than the
> positive examples. In a world this big, you'll find at least one
> negative example every day.
>
> Don't let the existence of a bad example ruin your faith in the
> world. Good news is always quieter than bad news. Good behavior
> rarely stirs the pot or ignites emotion.
>
> But no matter. We need role models all the same. Continue living
> the best way you know how.
>
> — James Clear
