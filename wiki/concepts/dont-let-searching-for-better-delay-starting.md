# Don't let searching for better delay starting

When you are stuck trying to find the perfect plan, simply starting changes the situation and moves you forward.

> Whenever you are stuck searching for the optimal plan, remember:
>
> Getting started changes everything.
>
> — James Clear

> Two truths that can be at odds:
>
> 1) There are no bonus points in life for doing it the hard way.2)
> There is a lot to be gained in life by acting fast and giving
> your best effort.
>
> Don't let the excuse of searching for a better way prevent you
> from taking action.
>
> — James Clear

> Don't let the hope of finding a better way prevent you from
> starting down the best path you know of right now. This day won't
> come again.
>
> — James Clear
