# Don't lose sleep over critics who don't love you

Criticism from people who don't care about you isn't worth losing sleep over.

> I don’t have any time to stay up all night worrying about what
> someone who doesn’t love me has to say about me.
>
> — Viola Davis, Viola Davis' Battle with Low Self-Esteem
