# Don't merely visit this world; engage fully

Mary Oliver hopes to live a life of amazement and full engagement, rather than passing through life as a mere visitor full of fear or argument.

> When it's over, I want to say: all my life I was a bride married
> to amazement. I was the bridegroom, taking the world into my
> arms.
>
> When it's over, I don't want to wonder if I have made of my life
> something particular, and real. I don't want to find myself
> sighing and frightened, or full of argument.
>
> I don't want to end up simply having visited this world.
>
> — Mary Oliver, When Death Comes
