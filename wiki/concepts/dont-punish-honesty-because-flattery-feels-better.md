# Don't punish honesty because flattery feels better

Flattery is more pleasant to hear than honest feedback. Reacting badly to honesty discourages the very people who help you most.

> Don't punish honesty because flattery feels better.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
