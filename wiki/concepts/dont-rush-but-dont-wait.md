# Don't rush, but don't wait

Act with urgency but without attachment to a specific timeline. Thinking longer term than others lets you think bigger, so if something will take years, start now.

> Don't rush, but don't wait.
>
> Act with urgency, but release yourself from the need to achieve
> it on a particular timeline. When you think longer term than
> most, you can think bigger than most.
>
> If it takes years, start now.
>
> — James Clear
