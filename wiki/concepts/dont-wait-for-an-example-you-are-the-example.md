# Don't wait for an example; you are the example

Waiting until you see someone else doing something keeps you from doing anything interesting. Learn from others, but your path is unique, so act without a perfect model.

> Many people won't attempt something unless they can find an
> example of someone else who is already doing it. Rely on this
> type of thinking too much and you'll never do anything
> interesting.
>
> Your path through life is unique. It is important to extract
> lessons from the experiences of others, but you can't wait for a
> perfect example to take action. You are the example.
>
> — James Clear
