# Dropping unrealistic perfectionism brings happiness

Jacobs became happier once he stopped demanding perfection from himself and everyone around him.

> “'Not beating myself up' has been a hard-learned lesson for me
> and those around me. I became much happier in my middle age when
> I stopped expecting unrealistic levels of perfection from myself
> and my family, my friends, and my co-workers, not to mention
> customers, vendors, and shareholders.”
>
> — Brad Jacobs, How to Make a Few Billion Dollars
