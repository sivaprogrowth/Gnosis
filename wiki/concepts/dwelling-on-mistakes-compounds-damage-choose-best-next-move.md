# Dwelling on mistakes compounds damage; choose best next move

Dwelling on a past mistake compounds the damage. Focus instead on making the best choice from where you are now, a next play mentality.

> A surefire way to make a bad situation worse is to continue
> replaying it in your mind.
>
> The damage is done. The only thing that matters now is making the
> best choice given your current position.
>
> Next play mentality.
>
> — James Clear

> You have to live with your mistakes, but you don't have to
> compound them.
>
> To follow up an error with a foolish reaction is to lose twice.
> Given the reality of where you are right now, what is the best
> next action?
>
> A wise response may not erase a dumb mistake, but it can redeem
> it.
>
> — James Clear
