# Each sunset takes a part of your life

When planning the good you will do each day, remember that every day that passes uses up part of your life, so live with purpose.

> "Every time you wake up and ask yourself, 'What good things am I
> going to do today?' remember that, when the sun goes down at
> sunset, it will take a part of your life with it."
>
> — Indian proverb, Leo Tolstoy, A Calendar of Wisdom
