# Eagerness to learn outweighs natural talent

Being eager and curious can carry you far even without much talent, while lacking curiosity leaves even natural ability falling short.

> "If you're eager to learn—even if you aren't particularly
> talented—then you can make it a long way despite your
> shortcomings.
>
> But if you're not eager and curious, you'll find your natural
> abilities often fall short of what is required."
>
> — James Clear
