# Early feedback beats late criticism

Giving feedback early is better than delaying it or hinting indirectly. Bad news is unpleasant, but people value clarity.

> Early feedback is usually better than late criticism. Delaying
> the conversation or stringing someone along with indirect
> feedback won't make them feel better once the real issue is
> finally addressed. Nobody likes getting bad news, but everyone
> appreciates clarity.
>
> — James Clear
