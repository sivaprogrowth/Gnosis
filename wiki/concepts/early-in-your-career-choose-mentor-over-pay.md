# Early in your career, choose mentor over pay

When starting out, a great mentor is worth far more than higher pay. This is an example of second-order thinking.

> “If you're early on in your career and they give you a choice
> between a great mentor or higher pay, take the mentor every time.
> It's not even close.”
>
> — Stanley Druckenmiller
