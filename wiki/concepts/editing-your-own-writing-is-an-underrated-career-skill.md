# Editing your own writing is an underrated career skill

Great writing comes from rewriting: simplifying, clarifying, and checking for typos. This skill is rarely taught but impresses in any job.

> One of the most underrated career skills that isn’t really taught
> anywhere is editing your own writing.
>
> Great writing is actually re-writing. Simplifying paragraphs.
> Clarifying key points. Double-checking for typos.
>
> Good writing will impress in any job.
>
> — James Clear
