# Education means cultivating the capacity to seek what's significant

Access to information alone gives nothing; success comes from knowing what to look for. Education should cultivate the ability to find what matters and to keep questioning whether you're on the right track.

> You can't expect somebody to become a biologist by giving them
> access to the Harvard University biology library and saying,
> "Just look through it." That will give them nothing. The internet
> is the same, except magnified enormously.
>
> ...
>
> The person who wins the Nobel Prize in biology is not the person
> who read the most journal articles and took the most notes on
> them. It's the person who knew what to look for.
>
> Cultivating that capacity to seek what's significant, always
> willing to question whether you're on the right track, that's
> what education is going to be about, whether it's using computers
> and internet, or pencil and paper and books.
>
> — Noam Chomsky, The Purpose of Education
