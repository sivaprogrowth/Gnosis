# Effort matters before starting, strategy after

Before you start, taking action matters more than finding a better plan. Once you are acting, working on the right thing matters more than working harder.

> If you haven’t started, then taking action is more important than
> finding a better strategy.
>
> If you’re already taking action, then ensuring you’re working on
> the right thing is more important than working harder.
>
> Your effort sets your floor. Your strategy sets your ceiling.
>
> — James Clear

> If you're not working hard, ideas don’t matter. The best idea is
> worthless without execution.
>
> If you're already working hard, ideas are crucial. Most effort is
> wasted on mediocre ideas.
>
> — James Clear

> Being smart helps once you start. Before that, it often just makes your excuses more convincing.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
