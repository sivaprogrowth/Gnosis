# Effortless-looking art comes from constant practice

Like a jazz musician, a writer practices relentlessly so the work can look natural and graceful. Elegance is the result of practice and awareness of form.

> I thought of myself as like the jazz musician: someone who
> practices and practices and practices in order to be able to
> invent and to make his art look effortless and graceful.
>
> — Toni Morrison, The Paris Review
