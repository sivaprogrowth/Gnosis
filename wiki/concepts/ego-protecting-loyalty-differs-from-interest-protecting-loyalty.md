# Ego-protecting loyalty differs from interest-protecting loyalty

Some loyalty only shields your feelings and self-image, while real loyalty looks after what is actually good for you. The two are easy to confuse.

> Loyalty that protects your ego is not the same as loyalty that protects your interests.
>
