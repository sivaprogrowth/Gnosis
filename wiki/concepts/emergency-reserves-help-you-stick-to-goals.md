# Emergency reserves help you stick to goals

Building planned skip days into a goal counters the all-or-nothing purity complex. Accepting that life will interrupt you keeps a missed day from threatening your identity and makes the goal sustainable.

> There is research showing that when we
> introduce what are known as “emergency reserves,” basically “get
> out of jail free” cards, into the process of goal setting, we’re
> much more likely to stay the course and to reach those goals.
>
> — Maya Shankar
