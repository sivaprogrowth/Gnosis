# Emotional responsiveness can be learned without perfect models

Emotional responsiveness means tuning into and feeling your partner's emotions and responding so they know they matter. It can be learned even if your childhood lacked good examples.

> Emotional responsiveness means tuning into your partner's
> emotions, feeling what they feel, and responding in a way that
> shows they matter. This can be learned even without perfect
> childhood models.
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
