# Empathic listening is slower now, faster overall

Taking time to listen empathically costs far less time than fixing misunderstandings, redoing work, and living with unresolved problems later.

> “It’s true that becoming an empathic listener takes
> time, but it doesn’t take any-where near as much time as it takes
> to back up and correct misunderstandings when you’re already
> miles down the road, to redo, and to live with unexpressed and
> unsolved problems.”
>
> — Stephen Covey
