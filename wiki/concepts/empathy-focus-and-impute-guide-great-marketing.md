# Empathy, focus, and impute guide great marketing

Apple's early philosophy was to understand customers better than anyone, cut unimportant opportunities to focus, and recognize that presentation shapes how quality is perceived.

> The Apple Marketing Philosophy:
>
> Empathy. We will truly understand their needs better than any
> other company.
>
> Focus. In order to do a good job of those things we decide to do
> we must eliminate all of the unimportant opportunities.
>
> Impute. People DO judge a book by its cover. We may have the best
> product, the highest quality, the most useful software, etc.; if
> we present them in a slipshod manner, they will be perceived as
> slipshod; if we present them in a creative, professional manner,
> we will impute the desired qualities.
>
> — Mike Markkula, Internal memo (January 3, 1977)
