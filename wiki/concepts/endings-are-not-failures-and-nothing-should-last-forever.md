# Endings are not failures and nothing should last forever

Choosing to end a project or close a business is not a failure. Even the best work has a natural end.

> Endings don't have to be failures, especially when you choose to
> end a project or shut down a business...
>
> Even the best gigs don't last forever. Nor should they.
>
> — Samin Nosrat, Tribe of Mentors
