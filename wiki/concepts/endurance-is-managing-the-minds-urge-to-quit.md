# Endurance is managing the mind's urge to quit

The urge to stop usually comes from the mind well before the body has actually reached its limit.

> 3. Your mind quits long before your body.
>
> — Jim Murphy, The Knowledge Project

> No one tells you running is 10 percent cardio and 90 percent
> negotiating with the voice begging you to quit.
>
> — David Dack, Twitter
