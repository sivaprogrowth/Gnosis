# Engaging actively with opportunities gives them shape

Opportunities slip past passive people, but those who actively engage can gather and shape them into something. The shaping comes from working with the material.

> Opportunities are grains of sand. They slide right past drooping
> fingers, but an active palm can gather whatever is within reach
> and shape it into a little castle. It is the act of engaging with
> the material that gives it shape.
>
> — James Clear
