# Enough comes from inside, not external accomplishment

External achievements will never make you feel okay. Contentment comes from stepping off the treadmill and seeing what you already have.

> “You'll never feel okay by way of external accomplishments.
> Enough comes from the inside. It comes from stepping off the
> train. From seeing what you already have, what you've always
> had.”
>
> — Ryan Holiday
