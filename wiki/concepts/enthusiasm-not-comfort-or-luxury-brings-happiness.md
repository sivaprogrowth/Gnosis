# Enthusiasm, not comfort or luxury, brings happiness

People chase comfort and luxury as if they were essential, but what really makes us happy is having something to be enthusiastic about.

> We act as though comfort and luxury were the chief requirements
> of life, when all that we need to make us happy is something to
> be enthusiastic about.
>
> — Charles Kingsley, Britannica
