# Entrepreneurship is a disguised personal growth engine

Starting and running a business looks like a commercial pursuit, but its deeper effect is forcing the person doing it to grow.

> Entrepreneurship is a personal growth engine disguised as a
> business pursuit.
>
> — James Clear
