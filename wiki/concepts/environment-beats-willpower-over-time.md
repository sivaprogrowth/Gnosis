# Environment beats willpower over time

If your surroundings are set up to support good behavior, you don't need as much personal discipline. Work with your environment rather than against it.

> The more disciplined your environment is, the less disciplined you need to be. Don't swim upstream.
>
> — James Clear

> In the long-run (and often in the short-run),
> your willpower will not beat your environment.
>
> — James Clear

> Consistency cannot be guaranteed, but you can create the
> conditions to make it more likely. Before you try to become a
> more disciplined person, how are you creating a more disciplined
> environment?
>
> — James Clear

> In the long-run (and often in the short-run), your willpower
> will never beat your environment. The more disciplined your
> environment is, the less disciplined you need to be. Don't swim
> upstream.
>
> — James Clear
