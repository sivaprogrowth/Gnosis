# Environment changes make behavior change easier

When your surroundings change dramatically, your behavior changes too. Everyone builds new habits during big disruptions, whether they mean to or not.

> The truth is everyone is building new habits right now. Whenever there is a big change in your environment there will be a big change in your behavior.
>
> — James Clear

> Various research studies have found that it is easier to change
> your behavior when your environment changes. For example,
> students change their television watching habits when they
> transfer schools.
>
> — Wendy Wood and David T. Neal, Healthy through Habit: Interventions for Initiating and Maintaining Health Behavior Change, Behavioral Science and Policy (2016); Changing Circumstances, Disrupting Habits, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
