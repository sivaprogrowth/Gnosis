# Even inventors fail at predicting their own breakthroughs

Wilbur Wright predicted in 1901 that men would not fly for 50 years, then flew two years later. The miss shocked him so much that he gave up making predictions.

> I confess that, in 1901, I said to my brother Orville that men
> would not fly for 50 years. Two years later, we were making
> flights. This demonstration of my inability as a prophet gave me
> such a shock that I have ever since refrained from all
> prediction.
>
> — Wilbur Wright, Speech at Aero-Club de France (1908)
