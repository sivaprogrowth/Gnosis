# Even your smallest actions carry tremendous power

Instead of feeling powerless, recognize that how you conduct yourself matters and even small actions can create great change. Taking responsibility for that power is an ordinary and urgent duty.

> "Rather than feel impotent and useless, you must come to terms
> with the fact that as a human being you are infinitely powerful,
> and take responsibility for this tremendous power. Even our
> smallest actions have potential for great change, positively or
> negatively, and the way in which we all conduct ourselves within
> the world means something. You are anything but impotent, you
> are, in fact, exquisitely and frighteningly dynamic, as are we
> all, and with all respect you have an obligation to stand up and
> take responsibility for that potential. It is your most ordinary
> and urgent duty."
>
> — Nick Cave, The Red Hand Files, Issue \#216
