# Eventually everyone faces the consequences of their choices

Your actions catch up with you in the end. Everyone eventually has to live with the results of what they did.

> Sooner or later we all sit down to a banquet of consequences.
>
> — Robert Louis Stevenson, Old Mortality (1884)
