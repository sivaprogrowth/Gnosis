# Eventually you must leap rather than look

Commitment requires accepting danger; the path ahead is steeper than it appears, and at some point you have to jump instead of only watching.

> The sense of danger must not disappear:
> The way is certainly both short and steep,
> However gradual it looks from here;
> Look if you like, but you will have to leap.
>
> — W.H. Auden, “Leap Before You Look” (December 1940)
