# Every action is a vote for your identity

Each thing you do casts a small vote toward the identity you want to build. Repeated actions accumulate into evidence of who you are.

> Every action is a vote for the type of person you wish to
> become.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish
> to become.
>
> No single instance will transform your beliefs, but as the votes
> build up, so does the evidence of your identity.
>
> This is why habits are crucial. They cast repeated votes for
> being a certain type of person.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 2

> Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you
> wish to become.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> If you show up at the gym five
> days in a row—even if it’s just for two minutes—you are casting
> votes for your new identity.
>
> — James Clear

> to become. No single instance will transform your beliefs, but as
> the votes build up, so does the evidence of your new identity.

> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> The truth is, small habits can reinforce the identity you want to build. If you show up at the gym five days in a row—even if it’s just for two minutes—you are casting votes for your new identity. As the votes accumulate, so does the evidence of your new identity.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> “Everything you do - with or without an audience - provides
> evidence to you about who you are and what you're capable of.”
>
> — Steven Bartlett
