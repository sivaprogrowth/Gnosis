# Every breakthrough started as heresy

Schools present correct answers as obvious, but most truths were once hidden behind the feeling that something doesn't make sense. Teaching kids to avoid that feeling trains them away from discovery.

> School often teaches that correct answers are obvious. Reality is
> the opposite.
>
> We drill kids on facts that seem obvious once known, never
> mentioning that almost all of them were buried behind a door of
> 'that doesn't make sense.' Gravity baffled us for millennia. We
> didn't think hand washing mattered, even the idea of germs
> causing sickness sounded insane.
>
> Every breakthrough started as heresy. But we teach kids to flee
> from the very feeling that precedes discovery.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
