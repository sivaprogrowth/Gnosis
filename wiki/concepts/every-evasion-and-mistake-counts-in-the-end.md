# Every evasion and mistake counts in the end

Didion recalls discovering that some things are irrevocable and that all the small choices, evasions, and mistakes added up and mattered.

> That was the year, my twenty-eighth, when I was discovering that
> not all of the promises would be kept, that some things are in
> fact irrevocable and that it had counted after all, every evasion
> and every procrastination, every mistake, every word, all of it.
>
> — Joan Didion, Slouching Towards Bethlehem, "Goodbye to All That"
