# Every human wants to belong, be heard, and matter

All people want and need to be part of something larger, to be paid attention to, listened to, respected, loved, and to matter.

> What every human being wants and needs:
>
> 1. To be part of something larger than themselves 2. To be paid
> attention to 3. To be listened to 4. To be respected 5. To be
> loved 6. To matter
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
