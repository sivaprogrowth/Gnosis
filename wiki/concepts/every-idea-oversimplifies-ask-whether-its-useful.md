# Every idea oversimplifies; ask whether it's useful

All ideas simplify a messy reality. The real test is usefulness, and knowing an idea's limits lets you apply it well.

> Everything is an oversimplification. Reality is messy and
> complex.
>
> The question is whether it is a useful simplification.
>
> Know the limitations of an idea and you can apply it to great
> effect—despite the messiness of reality.
>
> — James Clear
