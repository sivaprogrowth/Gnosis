# Every object you own pulls on your attention

Items in your environment act as magnets for your attention. Discarding something releases its tug and gives you some attention back.

> "Look around your environment.
>
> Rather than seeing items as objects, see them as magnets for your
> attention. Each object gently pulls a certain amount of your
> attention toward it.
>
> Whenever you discard something, the tug of that object is
> released. You get some attention back."
>
> — James Clear
