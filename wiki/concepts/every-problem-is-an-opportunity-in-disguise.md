# Every problem is an opportunity in disguise

Problems should be reframed as opportunities to create value, strengthen relationships, and differentiate yourself.

> We need to redefine "problems" into opportunities.
>
> Problems are an opportunity to create value. Problems are an
> opportunity to strengthen relationships. Problems are an
> opportunity to differentiate yourself from others.
>
> Every problem is an opportunity in disguise.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
