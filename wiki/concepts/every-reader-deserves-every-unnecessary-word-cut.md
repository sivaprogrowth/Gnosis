# Every reader deserves every unnecessary word cut

When writing for an important person you cut every unnecessary word, and anyone who reads what you publish is an important person.

> “1. When you write something intended to be read by an important
> person, go through it and cut every unnecessary word. 2. The
> reader of anything you publish is an important person.”
>
> — [[paul-graham|Paul Graham]]
