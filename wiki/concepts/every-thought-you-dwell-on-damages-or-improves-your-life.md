# Every thought you dwell on damages or improves your life

The thoughts you repeatedly dwell on shape your life for better or worse, even if you never voice them.

> Every thought a person dwells upon, whether he expresses it or
> not, either damages or improves his life.
>
> — Lucy Mallory, A Calendar for Wisdom
