# Every yes is a no to something else

Every choice has an opportunity cost: what you give up by choosing. Trade-offs and opportunity costs are two sides of the same coin, and being conscious of them leads to better decisions aligned with what matters most.

> Opportunity cost is what you give up when choosing; trade-offs are the
> balancing acts you perform when deciding between competing
> options. They’re two sides of the same  coin—whenever you make a
> trade-off, you incur an opportunity cost for the options you
> didn’t choose. The key in both cases is to be thoughtful and
> intentional about your choices.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
