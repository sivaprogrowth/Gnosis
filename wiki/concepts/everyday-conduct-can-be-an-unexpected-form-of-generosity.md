# Everyday conduct can be an unexpected form of generosity

Small behaviors are quiet gifts to others. Being early, leaving the last word unsaid, delivering work on time, and not taking things personally all make life easier for other people.

> "Unexpected forms of generosity:
>
> * Being early can be a form of generosity. You wait, so they
> don't have to.
> * Leaving something unsaid can be a form of generosity. You don't
> always need the last word.
> * Delivering your work on time can be a form of generosity. You
> make life easier for everyone downstream.
> * Not taking things personally can be a form of generosity. You
> give people the space to say things imperfectly."
>
> — James Clear
