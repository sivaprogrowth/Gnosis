# Everyday goodness goes unnoticed while wrongs stand out

Cruelty and wrongs are highly visible, but even in the worst times there is a great deal of ordinary kindness and decency that never makes the news.

> It is important to remember that the viciousness and wrongs of
> life stick out very plainly but that even at the worst times
> there is a great deal of goodness, kindness, and day-to-day
> decency that goes unnoticed and makes no headlines.
>
> — Isaac Asimov, The Roman Empire
