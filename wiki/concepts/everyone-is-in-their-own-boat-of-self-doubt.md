# Everyone is in their own boat of self-doubt

We assume others have it better, but eventually realize everyone struggles privately with self-doubt and second-guessing.

> “We go through life thinking everyone else has it better than us
> until we grow up and realize we’re all in our own tiny boats of
> self-doubt and second-guessing.”
>
> — Alexi Pappas
