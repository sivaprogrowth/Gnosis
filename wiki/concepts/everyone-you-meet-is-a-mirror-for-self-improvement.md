# Everyone you meet is a mirror for self-improvement

When you meet someone better, aim to equal them; when you meet someone worse, examine yourself for the same flaws.

> When you meet someone better than yourself, turn your thoughts to becoming his equal. When you meet someone not as good as you are, look within and examine your own self.
>
> — Confucius, The Analects (Book IV)
