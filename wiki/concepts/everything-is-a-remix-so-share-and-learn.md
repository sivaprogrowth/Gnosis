# Everything is a remix, so share and learn

Many things associated with one culture actually originated elsewhere, showing that ideas and creations are combinations borrowed from others. The world benefits when we share what we know and learn from others.

> Italy is known for tomatoes. Thailand for chilies. Germany for
> sauerkraut.
>
> But tomatoes originated in Peru. Thailand imported chilies from
> Central America. Sauerkraut started in China.
>
> Everything is a remix—and the world is better for it. Share what
> you know. Learn from others.
>
> — James Clear
