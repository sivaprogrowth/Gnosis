# Everything you know is only a model

All knowledge is a model of reality, not reality itself. Share your model openly and invite others to challenge and improve your assumptions.

> Remember, always, that everything you know, and everything
> everyone knows, is only a model. Get your model out there where
> it can be viewed. Invite others to challenge your assumptions and
> add their own.
>
> — Donella Meadows, Thinking in Systems
