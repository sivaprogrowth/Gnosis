# Excellence comes from obsessing over the right details

Both people who excel and people who struggle obsess over details; the difference is whether they focus on meaningless minutiae or meaningful polish. Most things don't matter, but when they do, get the details right.

> People who excel tend to obsess over the details.
>
> People who struggle also tend to obsess over the details.
>
> The difference is what details they focus on. Minutiae vs polish.
>
> Most things don’t matter—but when it does, you want to get the
> details right.
>
> — James Clear
