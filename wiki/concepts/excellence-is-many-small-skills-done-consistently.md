# Excellence is many small skills done consistently

Elite performance is not superhuman; it is the combination of dozens of small, ordinary skills drilled into habit and done consistently and correctly together. The little things really do count.

> Excellence is mundane. Superlative performance is really a
> confluence of dozens of small skills or activities, each one
> learned or stumbled upon, which have been carefully drilled into
> habit and then are fitted together in a synthesized whole. There
> is nothing extraordinary or superhuman in any one of those
> actions; only the fact that they are done consistently and
> correctly, and all together, produce excellence.
>
> — Daniel Chambliss, The Mundanity of Excellence
