# Excellence is pain tolerance disguised as genius

Most people treat discomfort as a reason to stop, while top performers see it as a sign of progress. The real edge is learning to appreciate the discomfort others avoid, not raw talent.

> A Taste for Saltwater: Most people mistake discomfort as a signal
> to stop; the great ones see it as evidence they’re on the right
> track. Excellence is just pain tolerance disguised as genius. The
> real advantage isn’t talent but cultivating a perverse
> appreciation for the discomfort others instinctively avoid.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
