# Excellence requires deliberate practice, not talent

The best keep learning through deliberate habits: reading widely, thinking alone, journaling, reviewing, memorizing big ideas, and attacking their own best ideas. Being deliberate matters more than being gifted.

> The best are always learning.
>
> Read like crazy. Think alone. Keep a journal. Write stuff down
> the moment you see it. Review regularly. Memorize the big ideas
> to fluency. Attack your best ideas. And never get high on your
> own supply.
>
> You don't have to be gifted. You do have to be deliberate.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
