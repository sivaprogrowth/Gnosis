# Excelling at current opportunities unlocks better ones

Begin with the best opportunity you have now. Fully using what is in front of you leads to better opportunities over time.

> Start with the best opportunity available to you. If you make
> the most of what you have in front of you right now, better
> opportunities will become available as you go along.
>
> — James Clear

> To get the job you want, master the one you
> have.
>
> — Barry Diller, The Knowledge Project
