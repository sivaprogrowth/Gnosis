# Exceptional art requires curiosity, observation, and joy

According to George Grosz, excelling at painting and drawing takes endless curiosity, careful observation, and deep enjoyment of the work.

> Endless curiosity, observation, and a great
> amount of joy in the thing.
>
> — George Grosz, A letter by Marianne Moore to Mary Reeves (August 28, 1963)
