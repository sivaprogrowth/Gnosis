# Exceptional looks boring until suddenly it isn't

Real success is built through unglamorous, consistent, incremental work that nobody sees. The building is quiet, and only the eventual success makes noise.

> Success is built in rooms no one would photograph.
>
> The people who change things aren't at the parties, they're home
> on Friday night, obsessed with problems everyone else finds
> tedious. Their Instagram looks empty because their life isn't
> shareable. Just the same unsexy routine, day after day. They
> aren't doing anything glamorous. They're just consistently making
> steady incremental progress over a long period of time.
>
> Success makes noise. Building doesn't. That's the paradox:
> Exceptional looks boring until suddenly it isn't.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
