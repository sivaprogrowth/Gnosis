# Exceptional performers are deeply engaged with details

Top performers actively pursue and attend to details rather than avoiding them.

> The best seek out the details.
>
> — Ron Shaich, The Knowledge Project podcast

> It's rare that I meet an exceptional person who is not deeply involved in the details.
>
> In fact, I can't think of one. But I'm sure they exist.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
