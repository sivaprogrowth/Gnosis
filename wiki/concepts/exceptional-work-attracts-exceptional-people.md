# Exceptional work attracts exceptional people

Doing exceptional work is the best way to earn the attention and respect of exceptional people, because like attracts like.

> The best way to get the attention and respect of exceptional
> people is to do exceptional work.
>
> Like attracts like.
>
> — James Clear
