# Exchanging ideas doubles them, unlike exchanging objects

Trading physical things leaves each person with the same amount, but sharing ideas leaves each person with more. Knowledge multiplies when shared.

> If you have an apple and I have an apple and we exchange apples
> then you and I will still each have one apple. But if you have an
> idea and I have an idea and we exchange these ideas, then each of
> us will have two ideas.
>
> — Charles F. Brannan, "The United States in World Affairs." NBC. April 3, 1949.
