# Excitement leaving and returning home reveals your life's truth

How you feel walking out the door to work and walking back in at home tells you whether your life is balanced. Escaping an empty home into work makes you look successful to everyone except the people who matter most.

> Your body tells you the truth about your life twice a day, at the
> door each morning and night, by answering a question you didn't
> ask: Am I excited to go to work? Am I excited to come home?
>
> If you pour yourself into work because home feels empty, you look
> successful to everyone but those who matter most.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
