# Excitement motivates better than discipline

People who seem highly disciplined are often just genuinely curious or interested in their field. Enjoyment sustains effort longer than forcing yourself.

> Excitement is a better motivator than discipline. The people who
> appear to have an exceptional work ethic or remarkable discipline
> are often those with a genuine curiosity or interest in that
> area. The person who smiles is more likely to keep working than
> the person gritting their teeth.
>
> — James Clear
