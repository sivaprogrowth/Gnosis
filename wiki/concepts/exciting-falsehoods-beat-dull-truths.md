# Exciting falsehoods beat dull truths

Being right is not enough; people with good and true messages must make them exciting and new, or their work will be ignored.

> A dull truth will not be looked at. An exciting lie will. That is what good, sincere people must understand. They must make their truth exciting and new, or their good works will be born dead.
>
> — Bill Bernbach, Bill Bernbach Said…

> "A juicy falsehood is more powerful than a boring truth."
>
> — Vlad Tenev, The Knowledge Project
