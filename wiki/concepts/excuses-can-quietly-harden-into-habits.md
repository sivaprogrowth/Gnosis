# Excuses can quietly harden into habits

An excuse used once can repeat until it becomes a habit. One bad day should not be allowed to turn into a lasting way of living.

> What starts as an excuse can easily become a habit.
>
> Don't let a bad day become a lifestyle.
>
> — James Clear
