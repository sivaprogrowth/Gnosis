# Exercise and sleep restore perspective on problems

After hard physical exertion and good rest, problems shrink back to their real importance. Fatigue and stress make issues seem bigger than they are.

> Your problems adjust to their true level of importance after a
> hard workout and a good night of sleep.
>
> — James Clear
