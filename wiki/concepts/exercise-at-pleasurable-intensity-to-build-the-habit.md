# Exercise at pleasurable intensity to build the habit

Most entry points to exercise are too intense for sedentary people to enjoy. Exercising slowly and only at intensities that feel pleasurable makes repeat exercise more likely, and the enjoyment builds on itself.

> Unfortunately, most on-ramps to exercise are at an intensity too
> high for previously-sedentary people to find them pleasurable. If
> people go to a fitness class, or focus on running a particular
> distance at a particular speed, they’ll likely miss the pleasure
> zone entirely.
>
> Refocusing on exercising only for one’s own individual pleasure,
> as slowly as one prefers, and only at intensities that are
> pleasurable, is more likely to motivate repeat and habitual
> exercising. At that point, the enjoyment of exercise pleasure can
> build on itself, motivating longer and longer intervals of
> experiencing the pleasure.
>
> — Sarah Perry, Body Pleasure
