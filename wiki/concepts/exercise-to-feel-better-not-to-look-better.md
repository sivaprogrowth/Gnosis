# Exercise to feel better, not to look better

Exercising for appearance is unsustainable because the daily effect is too small to notice. Exercising to feel better pays off every single day.

> "Exercising to look better isn’t
> sustainable, the daily effect is too small. Exercise to feel
> better. It works every day."
>
> — Unknown Twitter user, Twitter
