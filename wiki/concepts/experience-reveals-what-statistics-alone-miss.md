# Experience reveals what statistics alone miss

Conclusions from experience differ from those drawn only from numbers. A 30 percent scoring rate looks weak on paper, yet matching Messi's level is practically impossible.

> “What I know for certain is that the conclusions I would draw
> from experience are different from the ones based only on
> figures. Because if Lionel Messi scores three times out of every
> ten attempts, he might be criticized by someone who sees only the
> statistics for being just 30 per cent effective. I’d say: just
> copy him and see if you can get up to that level. It’s
> practically impossible.”
>
> — Johan Cruyff
