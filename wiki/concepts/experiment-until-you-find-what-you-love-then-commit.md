# Experiment until you find what you love, then commit

Until you know what you love, keep commitments few and try many things. Once you know, cut back on experiments and commit deeply.

> Before you discover what you love: fewer commitments, more
> experiments.
>
> After you discover what you love: fewer experiments, more
> commitments.
>
> — James Clear
