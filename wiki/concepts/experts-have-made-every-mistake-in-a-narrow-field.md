# Experts have made every mistake in a narrow field

Expertise comes from painfully experiencing all the possible mistakes within a narrow domain.

> An expert is a person who has found out by painful experience
> all the mistakes that one can make in a very narrow field.
>
> — Niels Bohr, "Dr. Edward Teller's Magnificent Obsession" in LIFE magazine, September 6, 1954
