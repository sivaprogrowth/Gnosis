# Extraordinary results require being publicly bad first

What gets you into anything extraordinary is not talent or money but being willing to look bad in public while you learn.

> The price of admission to anything extraordinary isn't talent or capital. It's a willingness to be publicly bad at something for a while.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
