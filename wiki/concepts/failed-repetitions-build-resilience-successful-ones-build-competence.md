# Failed repetitions build resilience, successful ones build competence

Self-trust grows from both successes and failures. Working through past failures makes you fear them less because you know you can bounce back.

> Trust in yourself is not only built through successful
> repetitions, but also through failed ones.
>
> When you have worked through failures in the past, you fear them
> less in the future. You know you can bounce back.
>
> Successful repetitions build competence. Failed repetitions build
> resilience.
>
> — James Clear
