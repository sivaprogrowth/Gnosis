# Failing is forgiven, but refusing to try is not

Do what you can. Falling short of what you wanted is forgivable, but not wanting to try at all is not.

> Do what you can. If you can't do something you wanted to do,
> then you will be forgiven, but if you don't want to try to do
> anything, you will not be forgiven.
>
> — Nikolai Vavilov, The Murder of Nikolai Vavilov
