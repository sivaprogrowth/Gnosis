# Failing to say what you mean causes regret

Embarrassing moments often come from saying yes when you wanted to say no. Missed opportunities often come from saying no when you wanted to say yes, or staying silent either way.

> "Most of the embarrassing moments of my life happened when I
> wanted to say no, but ended up saying yes (or saying nothing at
> all).
>
> Most of the missed opportunities in my life happened when I
> wanted to say yes, but ended up saying no (or saying nothing at
> all)."
>
> — James Clear
