# Failure depends on when performance is measured

A project can look like a failure at many points along the way and a success once it is finished. If you are partway through, you are not failing, just in the middle of succeeding.

> Whether or not something is deemed a "failure" is dependent on
> when performance is measured.
>
> — James Clear
