# Failure is accumulated small daily lapses

Failure rarely comes from one event; it builds from many small daily failures of discipline. Treating a wasted day as harmless is dangerous because days add up to a lifetime.

> Failure is rarely the result of some isolated event. Rather, it
> is a consequence of a long list of accumulated little failures
> which happen as a result of too little discipline.
>
> — Jim Rohn, 7 Strategies for Wealth and Happiness
