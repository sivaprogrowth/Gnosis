# Failure is staying down, so keep getting up

You can start fresh at any moment you choose. Falling is not failure; failure is refusing to get back up.

> You may have a fresh start any moment you choose, for this thing
> that we call "failure" is not the falling down, but the staying
> down.
>
> — Mary Pickford, Why Not Try God

> Everyone gets knocked down; the difference is who stays down.
>
> When you’re on the ground, you’ll hear people saying, ‘That's not
> fair’ or ‘That shouldn't happen.’ The longer you lie there, the
> louder the voices become.
>
> Getting up isn’t easy, but it’s the only way forward.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> I ask myself: are defeats necessary?
>
> Well, necessary or not, they happen. When we first begin fighting for a dream, we have no experience and make mistakes. The secret of life, though, is to fall seven times and to get up eight times.
>
> — Paulo Coelho, The Alchemist
