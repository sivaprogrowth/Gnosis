# Failure teaches most when you give your best effort

If you fail with half-hearted effort, you learn little because better focus might have worked. If your best effort fails, you learn that the approach itself doesn't work.

> Failure is most useful when you give your best effort.
>
> If you fail with a lackluster effort, you haven't learned much.
> Perhaps you could have succeeded with a proper focus.
>
> But if your best effort fails, you have learned something
> valuable: this way doesn't work.
>
> — James Clear
