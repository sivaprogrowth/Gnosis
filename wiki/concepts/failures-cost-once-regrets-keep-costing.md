# Failures cost once, regrets keep costing

A failure usually hurts once and is over, while regret over not acting keeps hurting. The pain of inaction lasts longer than the pain of acting wrongly.

> Most failures are one-time costs. Most regrets are recurring
> costs.
>
> The pain of inaction stings longer than the pain of incorrect
> action.
>
> — James Clear
