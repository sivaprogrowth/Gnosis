# Family is the people who want you in their lives

Family is not defined by blood but by the people who accept you as you are, want you in their lives, and love you unconditionally.

> Family isn't always blood. It's the people in your life who want you in theirs; the ones who accept you for who you are. The ones who would do anything to see you smile and who love you no matter what.
>
> — Maya Angelou, Maya Angelou's Facebook page
