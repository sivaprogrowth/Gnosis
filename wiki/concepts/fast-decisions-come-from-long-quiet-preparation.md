# Fast decisions come from long quiet preparation

Munger and Buffett decide quickly only because they spent enormous time preparing through reading. Speed in the moment rests on prior preparation.

> Neither Warren nor I are smart enough to make decisions with no
> time to think. We make actual decisions very rapidly, but that's
> because we have spent so much time preparing ourselves by quietly
> reading.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]
