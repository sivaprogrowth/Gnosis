# Faster replies require fewer words

Responding quickly saves time because a fast reply can be brief, while a delayed reply needs a full explanation.

> The faster you reply, the less you need to say. If you reply in
> a minute, you can say two words. If you wait a day, it’s a
> paragraph.
>
> — Nicolai Tangen, The Knowledge Project
