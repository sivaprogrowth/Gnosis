# Fear-based thinking is a liar and a thief

Steve Young believed worry and fear of failure were the price of success, but learned that was false. Paying for success with fretting robs you, as fame and fear both do.

> "Fear-based thinking is a liar. I was afraid of failure, so much so that I paid this price so that I could have success. I learned as an older person, that's a fallacy. That's fraudulent. ... I'm not going to pay for success with my worry and fretting. Man, fame is a thief. Fear-based thinking is a thief. There's a lot of thieves in life.”
>
> — Steve Young
