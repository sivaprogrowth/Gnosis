# Fear doesn't fade, so fall and reach early

Waiting won't make risks less scary; it only leaves you less life, so it's better to take chances and fail early.

> It doesn’t get any less scary. All that happens is that you have
> less life left. It helps if you do your falling early, and it
> really helps if you do your reaching early.
>
> — Mary H.K. Choi, ILY Magazine
