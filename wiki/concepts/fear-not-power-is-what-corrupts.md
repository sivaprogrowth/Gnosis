# Fear, not power, is what corrupts

Corruption stems from fear: those in power fear losing it, and those under power fear its punishment, and both fears corrupt.

> It is not power that corrupts but fear. Fear of losing power
> corrupts those who wield it and fear of the scourge of power
> corrupts those who are subject to it.
>
> — Aung San Suu Kyi, Freedom from Fear (speech)
