# Fear of failure is the only real stumbling block

When learning to cook, or learning anything, the main obstacle is fear of failing. A carefree, what-the-hell attitude helps you get past it.

> The only real stumbling block is fear of failure. In cooking you've got to have a what-the-hell attitude.
>
> — Julia Child, Particular Passions
