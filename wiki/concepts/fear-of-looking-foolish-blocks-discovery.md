# Fear of looking foolish blocks discovery

People unwilling to look foolish rarely find anything new. Ideas that later prove brilliant often look idiotic at first.

> Those who fear appearing foolish rarely discover anything new.
> The genius of tomorrow often looks like an idiot today.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
