# Fear of opinion targets an imaginary collective

When worried about what people will think, we are rarely concerned about any specific person. The fear is of an imagined collective opinion that doesn't really exist.

> When I notice myself worrying about “what other people will
> think” I find I’m usually not worried about any single person’s
> opinion.
>
> If I pick a specific person, I‘m rarely concerned about what they
> will think.
>
> What I fear is the collective opinion in my head. It’s
> imaginary.
>
> — James Clear
