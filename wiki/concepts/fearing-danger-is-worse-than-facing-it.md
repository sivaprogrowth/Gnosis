# Fearing danger is worse than facing it

Fishermen accept that the sea is dangerous yet still go out. Reality is often less frightening than our imagination of it, so it is better to face the danger itself.

> “Fishermen know that the sea is dangerous and the storm fearsome,
> but they have never found these dangers sufficient reason for
> remaining ashore. They leave that wisdom to those to whom it
> appeals. When the storm comes—when night falls—what’s worse: the
> danger or the fear of danger? Give me reality, the danger
> itself.”
>
> — Vincent Van Gogh
