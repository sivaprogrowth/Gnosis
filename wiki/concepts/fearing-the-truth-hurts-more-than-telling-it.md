# Fearing the truth hurts more than telling it

Telling the truth can be painful, but not telling it is more painful. The fear of telling the truth is the most painful feeling in a moral life.

> As a child I was taught that to tell the truth was often
> painful. As an adult I have learned that not to tell the truth is
> more painful, and that the fear of telling the truth — whatever
> the truth may be — that fear is the most painful sensation of a
> moral life.
>
> — June Jordan, Some of Us Did Not Die
