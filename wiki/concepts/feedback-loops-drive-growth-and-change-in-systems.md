# Feedback loops drive growth and change in systems

Feedback loops let a system's output influence its own input, making systems dynamic. Noticing feedback and iterating on it shows where to direct changes and how fast to monitor their effects.

> Feedback loops are the engines of growth and change. They’re the
> mechanisms by which the output of a system influences its own
> input.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v3: Systems and Mathematics

> Feedback loops are what make systems dynamic. Without feedback, a
> system does the same thing over and over. Understand them,
> respect them, and use them wisely.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v3: Systems and Mathematics
