# Feel great by proving worthy of better company

Self-worth does not come from finding people below you and feeling superior. It comes from seeking people above you and showing you deserve to be among them.

> The secret to feeling great about yourself is not to be found in
> searching for people who are less than you and then show yourself
> superior to them, but in searching for people who are more than
> you and then show yourself worthy of their company.
>
> — Erik Naggum, Email (January 2, 2003)
