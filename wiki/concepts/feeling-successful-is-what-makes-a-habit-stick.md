# Feeling successful is what makes a habit stick

A habit sticks when you feel successful, even in a small way. That feeling signals to your brain that the habit paid off and was worth the effort.

> The vital thing in getting a habit to stick is to feel
> successful—even if it’s in a small way. The feeling of success is
> a signal that tells your brain that the habit paid off and that
> it was worth the effort.
>
> — James Clear
