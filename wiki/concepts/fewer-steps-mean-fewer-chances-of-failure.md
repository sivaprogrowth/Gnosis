# Fewer steps mean fewer chances of failure

Every extra step in a process is another place it can break, so use the fewest steps possible.

> Simplify. What can be done in three steps should never be done
> in four. Each link in the chain is a potential breaking point.
> Fewer links means fewer chances of failure.
>
> — James Clear
