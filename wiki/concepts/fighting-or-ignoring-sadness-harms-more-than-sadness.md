# Fighting or ignoring sadness harms more than sadness

Sadness and depression themselves won't kill you, but resisting or ignoring them will.

> "Sadness will not kill you. Depression won’t, either. But
> fighting it will. Ignoring it will."
>
> — Brianna Wiest
