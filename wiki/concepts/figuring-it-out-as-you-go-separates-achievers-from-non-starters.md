# Figuring it out as you go separates achievers from non-starters

Those who achieve difficult things are often distinguished simply by being willing to start before they have everything figured out.

> The willingness to figure things out as you go is often the only
> difference between those who achieve difficult things and those
> who never begin.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
