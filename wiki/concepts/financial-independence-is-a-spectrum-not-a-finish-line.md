# Financial independence is a spectrum, not a finish line

Independence is not all or nothing. Even a small amount of savings gives you more freedom than none.

> “Independence is a spectrum. Even \$100 in the bank beats zero.”
>
> — Morgan Housel, The Knowledge Project podcast
