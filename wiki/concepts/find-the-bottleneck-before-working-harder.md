# Find the bottleneck before working harder

Effort is often better spent locating the real constraint than simply working hard. Hard work aimed at the wrong thing produces frustration instead of progress.

> Time spent working hard is often better spent identifying where
> the bottleneck is located.
>
> Working hard on the wrong thing leads to frustration, not
> progress.
>
> — James Clear
