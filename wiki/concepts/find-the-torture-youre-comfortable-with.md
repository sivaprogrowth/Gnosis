# Find the torture you're comfortable with

Every meaningful pursuit involves some pain. The blessing is finding the kind of hard work you can tolerate endlessly, like Seinfeld constantly working on material.

> Your blessing in life is when you find the torture
> you're comfortable with.
>
> — [[jerry-seinfeld|Jerry Seinfeld]], Conversation with Howard Stern
