# Find your work by noticing what energizes your focus

If you don't know what work you like, notice which activities are easy to concentrate on and give you energy versus which make you tune out and feel tired.

> If you
> can't figure out what kind of work you like, pay attention to
> what's easy to concentrate on and gives you energy vs. what makes
> you tune out and feel tired.
>
> — [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]]
