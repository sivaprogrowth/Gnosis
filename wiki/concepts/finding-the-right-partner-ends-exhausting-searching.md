# Finding the right partner ends exhausting searching

Once you find the right partner, you realize how much physical and emotional energy searching consumed. That cost is why starting over in midlife is so hard.

> “One of the more restful achievements of having found the right
> partner in life is that we suddenly realize how much effort has
> previously gone into all the searching. Finding a mate takes
> enormous amounts of physical and emotional energy—dressing,
> comings and goings, and endless drama—which is why it can be so
> difficult to look again in midlife if we lose that happiness and
> must seek anew.”
>
> — David Whyte
