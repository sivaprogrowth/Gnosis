# Finishing one task beats endlessly optimizing tools

Someone who sticks with one task until it's done, even using slow or old methods, outperforms the person who keeps switching tools hoping technology will help them finish.

> The person who focuses on one task and sees it through to
> completion—even if they work in a somewhat slow or outdated
> manner—beats the endless optimizer who jumps from tool to tool
> and always hopes a new piece of technology will help them finish
> what they start.
>
> — James Clear
