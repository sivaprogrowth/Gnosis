# Five minutes of action can change your day

Just starting, like a first set at the gym, dramatically shifts how you feel. You don't need a full session; a few minutes can redirect your whole day.

> The difference between how I feel before my first set in the gym
> and how I feel after my first set is enormous.
>
> You don't even need a full workout (or work session, etc.) to
> feel good again. You are 5 minutes away from putting your day on
> a completely different trajectory.
>
> — James Clear
