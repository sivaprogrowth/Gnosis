# Five traps keep talented people from potential

Talented people fall short when they try to please everyone, imitate others' desires, chase status without asking why, try to do everything alone, or spread their attention across too many projects.

> Things that keep talented people from fulfilling their
> potential:
>
> - Trying to please everyone- Imitating the desires of others-
> Chasing status without questioning why- Playing superhero and
> trying to do it all alone- Dividing your attention between too
> many projects
>
> — James Clear
