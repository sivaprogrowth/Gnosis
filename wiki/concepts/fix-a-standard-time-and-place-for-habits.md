# Fix a standard time and place for habits

Habits last longer when you pick a set time and place for them, so you don't have to decide each day when to fit them in. Once it's decided, you only need to show up.

> A quick and easy tip for building habits that last:
>
> Pick a standard time and place to do it.
>
> It’s easier to wake up knowing "I exercise at 4pm" than to decide
> each time when to fit a habit into your day.
>
> If it’s already decided, all you need to do is show up.
>
> — James Clear
