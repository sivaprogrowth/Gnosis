# Fix small problems before they grow

Many problems are small if handled immediately but become big conflicts when left alone. The default rule should be to fix things right away.

> Many problems are minor when you solve them right away, but grow
> into an enormous conflict when you let them linger.
>
> As a rule of thumb, fix it now.
>
> — James Clear

> Deal with the difficult while it is yet easy;
> Deal with the great while it is yet small.
>
> — Laozi, Tao Te Ching

> When you ignore a problem that is small enough to handle, it often grows into a problem too big to manage.
>
> — James Clear
