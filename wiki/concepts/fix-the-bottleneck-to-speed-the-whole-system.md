# Fix the bottleneck to speed the whole system

The slowest part of a system limits the whole, so effort spent on already-fast parts is wasted. Bottlenecks can also be deliberate and useful, so choose them intentionally.

> The tricky thing about bottlenecks is that they’re not always
> obvious. It’s easy to focus on the parts of the system that are
> moving quickly and assume everything is fine. But the real
> leverage is in finding and fixing the bottlenecks. Speed up the
> slowest part, and you speed up the whole system. This is the
> theory of constraints in a nutshell. Figure out your bottleneck
> and focus all your efforts on alleviating it. Don’t waste time
> optimizing the parts that are already fast. They’re not the
> limiting factor.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v3: Systems and Mathematics
