# Fixing what's broken costs strengthening what works

Energy spent repairing weaknesses is energy not spent making your strengths unstoppable.

> All the energy we spend trying to fix what's broken comes at the
> expense of making what already works unstoppable.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
