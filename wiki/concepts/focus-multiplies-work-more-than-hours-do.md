# Focus multiplies work more than hours do

Choosing the right thing to work on matters more than working many hours. Once priorities are set, pursue them quickly and relentlessly, since slow movers rarely succeed.

> Focus is a force multiplier on work. Almost everyone I’ve ever
> met would be well-served by spending more time thinking about
> what to focus on. It is much more important to work on the right
> thing than it is to work many hours. Most people waste most of
> their time on stuff that doesn’t matter.
>
> "Once you have figured out what to do, be unstoppable about
> getting your small handful of priorities accomplished quickly. I
> have yet to meet a slow-moving person who is very successful.
>
> — [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], How to Be Successful
