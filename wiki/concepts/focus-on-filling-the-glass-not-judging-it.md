# Focus on filling the glass, not judging it

Rather than debating whether a situation is good or bad, focus on figuring out how to improve it.

> I'm not interested in whether the glass is half empty or half
> full. I'm interested in figuring out how to fill the glass.
>
> — Donald Kaberuka, Donald Kaberuka: The cautious optimist
