# Focus on inputs, not outcomes

Better results come from better inputs: better sources produce better thoughts, better questions produce better answers, and better habits produce better results. Focus on inputs rather than outcomes.

> The edge is in the inputs.
>
> The person who consumes from better sources, gets better
> thoughts. The person who asks better questions, gets better
> answers. The person who builds better habits, gets better
> results.
>
> It's not the outcomes. It's the inputs.
>
> — James Clear

> It's never about the outcome. It's always about the day.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> "The great mistake is to anticipate the outcome of the
> engagement; you ought not to be thinking of whether it ends in
> victory or in defeat."
>
> — Bruce Lee

> Focus on the inputs you control and the outputs will follow.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> "One of the things that I love is focusing on the process over
> the prize."
>
> — Rory McIlroy

> “I can get real caught up in the outcome. I just really need to remind myself that the outcome will ultimately happen if you just focus on the process. It takes care of itself.”
>
> — Rory McIlroy
