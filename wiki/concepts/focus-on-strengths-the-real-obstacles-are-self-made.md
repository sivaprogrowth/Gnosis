# Focus on strengths; the real obstacles are self-made

Mariam Paré would advise her younger self to focus on strengths and what she could do, hone them to excellence, and recognize that the only real obstacles are the ones we create.

> If I could talk to myself twenty years ago, I would tell myself
> to focus on my strengths, and not on my weaknesses; on the things
> I could do and not the things I couldn’t do; to strive to excel
> and hone those skills to the point of excellence. That this was
> the best strategy to secure my future. I would say to myself that
> the only real obstacles you have are those you create for
> yourself.
>
> — Mariam Paré, In the Company of Women
