# Focus on the light, keeping shadows behind you

Some people move from problem to problem all day, but you can choose to go from joy to joy. A good life isn't problem-free; it's about turning toward what is good so problems stay behind you.

> Some people get addicted to chain-smoking their problems. They
> spend all day going from sorrow to sorrow. It doesn't have to be
> that way. You can live each day going from joy to joy—like a
> sunflower that turns to face the sun as it moves across the sky.
> It's not about having a problem-free life, but about focusing on
> the light. Sunflowers still have shadows, but they are always
> behind them.
>
> — James Clear
