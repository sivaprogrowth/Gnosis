# Focus on the next corner, not the finish line

Confidence is often a bigger obstacle than competence, and action builds both confidence and momentum. When action feels hard, shrink your focus to the next small step, like a struggling marathoner aiming only for the next corner.

> Competence is often less of a problem than confidence.
>
> An underrated aspect of doing anything hard is believing in
> yourself. Action creates both confidence and momentum.
>
> When action seems hard, narrow the gap between where you are and
> what you focus on.
>
> A marathoner who hits a wall running at mile 5 doesn't focus on
> the end of the race; they focus on getting around the next
> corner. Then, the next corner.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
