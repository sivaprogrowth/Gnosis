# Focus on the two tasks that drive results

List the ten things you spend the most time on, do more of the two that truly drive results, and eliminate, automate, outsource, or pause the rest. Repeat the process.

> How to 80/20 your work:
>
> (1) Make a list of the 10 things you spend the most time on.
>
> (2) Circle the two that truly drive your results. Do more of
> those.
>
> (3) Look at the others. Eliminate ruthlessly. Automate or
> outsource what you can. Press pause on the rest.
>
> (4) Repeat.
>
> — James Clear
