# Focus on what stays the same, not what changes

Long-term growth comes from building around the things that won't change rather than chasing predictions about what will.

> “Rather than focus on what will change, focus on what stays the
> same.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Why Focusing on What Won’t Change is Key to Long-Term Growth
