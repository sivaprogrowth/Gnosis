# Focus on winning the present moment

Focus on doing well in the present moment instead of worrying about everything at once.

> Win the moment in front of you right now.
>
> — James Clear

> Win the day and go on your way.
>
> There are always doubts:
>
> * But what if…
> * Will I be able to…
> * Should I be worried about…
>
> Leave it be. Have the best day you can today. Win the moment in
> front of you right now.
>
> — James Clear
