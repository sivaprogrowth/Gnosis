# Focus on your own progress, not others'

Instead of gossiping about and critiquing other people's bodies, relationships, and businesses, spend that energy improving your own. Everyone has plenty to work on.

> People spend a lot of time talking about other people's bodies,
> gossiping about other people's relationships, critiquing other
> people's business, etc.
>
> Keep your eyes on your own paper. Work on your body. Nurture your
> relationships. Improve your business.
>
> We all have plenty to improve.
>
> — James Clear

> Swim in your lane.
>
> Every second you spend looking at a person in another lane comes
> at the cost of your progress.
>
> Focus on yourself.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
