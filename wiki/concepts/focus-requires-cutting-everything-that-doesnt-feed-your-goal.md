# Focus requires cutting everything that doesn't feed your goal

Most people stay attached to their distractions and never reach their potential. Real courage lies in eliminating whatever doesn't serve your goal, not in adding more.

> Most people are too loyal to their distractions to ever meet
> their destiny.
>
> The courage isn't in taking on more, it's in cutting off
> everything that doesn't feed your goal.
>
> Focus requires subtraction.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
