# Focusing on lack creates hell, gratitude creates heaven

Your experience of life depends on attention: dwelling on what you lack makes you miserable, while rejoicing in what you have brings joy.

> "You can go to hell without moving an inch, just focus on what
> you lack.
>
> You can taste heaven without leaving earth, just rejoice in what
> you have."
>
> — James Clear
