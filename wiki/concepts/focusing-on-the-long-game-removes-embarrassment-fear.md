# Focusing on the long game removes embarrassment fear

Keeping the end result in mind made missed shots and embarrassment an acceptable price for learning a new skill. Trusting your work and holding higher self-expectations frees you from outside pressure.

> I wasn’t scared of missing, looking bad, or being embarrassed.
> That’s because I always kept the end result, the long game, in my
> mind. I always focused on the fact that I had to try something to
> get it, and once I got it, I’d have another tool in my arsenal.
> If the price was a lot of work and a few missed shots, I was OK
> with that.
>
> — Kobe Bryant

> “if I wanted to implement something new into my game, I’d see it
> and try incorporating it immediately. I wasn’t scared of missing,
> looking bad, or being embarrassed. That’s because I always kept
> the end result, the long game, in my mind.”
>
> — Kobe Bryant
