# Follow your heart and intuition; all else is secondary

It takes courage to follow your heart and intuition, because they already know what you truly want to become, and that matters more than anything else.

> “Have the courage to follow your heart and intuition. They
> somehow already know what you truly want to become. Everything
> else is secondary.”
>
> — Steve Jobs
