# Forget finishing and just write one page a day

Steinbeck advises dropping the idea of ever finishing a big project and focusing only on a single day's page. The work gets done almost by surprise.

> “Abandon the idea that you are ever going to finish. Lose track
> of the 400 pages and write just one page for each day, it helps.
> Then when it gets finished, you are always surprised.”
>
> — John Steinbeck
