# Forget past mistakes and act right now

Instead of dwelling on past mistakes or missed starts, put that energy into the next good choice today.

> You can't start last week, but you can start now. Stop dwelling on what you should have done or ruminating on the mistakes you made. Channel that energy into the next good choice. You have today and you can try again.
>
> — James Clear

> Forget mistakes, forget failures, forget everything, except what you’re going to do now and do it. Today is your lucky day.
>
> — Will Durant
