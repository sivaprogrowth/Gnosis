# Forget yourself and take interest in the world

Spend less time ruminating about yourself and more time appreciating the richness of people, things, literature, and music around you.

> Develop interest in life as you see it; in people, things, literature, music — the world is so rich, simply throbbing with rich treasures, beautiful souls and interesting people. Forget yourself.
>
> — Henry Miller, Henry Miller Memorial Library
