# Forgive past, discipline present, stay flexible about future

Treat your past self with forgiveness, hold your present self to high standards, and stay flexible about your future self.

> Be forgiving with your past self.
>
> Be strict with your present self.
>
> Be flexible with your future self.
>
> — James Clear

> Be forgiving with your past self. What's done is done. Take the
> lessons with you and release the guilt.
>
> Be strict with your present self. Win the moment in front of you
> right now.
>
> Be flexible with your future self. There are many paths to
> success. You don't need life to be a certain way to live well.
>
> — James Clear

> Be forgiving with your past self. What's done is done. No sense in beating yourself up about it.
