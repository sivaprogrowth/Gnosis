# Forgiveness lets you take back power over your story

How you depict your life matters; framing it as revenge or tragedy keeps you stuck, while forgiving reclaims your power and lets healing begin.

> The way you depict your own life is important. For some people,
> it's either revenge—they're always looking for someone else to
> blame and to get back at—or it's tragedy because the whole thing
> is such a bloody mess and they'll never get out of it. But the
> moment that you can forgive, you take back the power and the
> healing waters flow.
>
> — Jeanette Winterson, Women at Work Vol II: Interviews from the Paris Review
