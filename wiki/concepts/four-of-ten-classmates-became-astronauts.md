# Four of ten classmates became astronauts

Mike Massimino's small MIT robotics class produced four astronauts out of ten students. A room with a high normal level of performance passes on habits people may not even notice they are absorbing.

> Massimino took a small robotics class at MIT. Of the ten people in the class, four became astronauts. If the goal was to make it into space, then that room was one of the best tribes to be in. The "normal" level of performance in that room was much higher than average.
>
> — James Clear, Mike Massimino, “Finding the Difference between ‘Improbable’ and ‘Impossible,’” The James Altucher Show, January 2017
