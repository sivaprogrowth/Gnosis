# Frame goals as do behaviors, not don't behaviors

Goals phrased as proactive actions are easier to measure and more motivating than goals about abstaining from something.

> You want to frame your goals in terms of “do” behaviors versus
> “don’t” behaviors. It’s easier to measure progress when engaging
> in proactive decisions or proactive behaviors rather than
> abstaining from things.
>
> — Maya Shankar
