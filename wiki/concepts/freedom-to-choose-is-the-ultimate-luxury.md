# Freedom to choose is the ultimate luxury

Quality of life means being able to make choices that are not driven by fear, such as picking projects to learn from or taking time off to travel.

> Quality of life is having the freedom to make choices that are
> not fear based. Whether it’s the ability to choose the kinds of
> projects I want to take on and can learn from, or the ability to
> take a month off to travel. Freedom to choose is the ultimate
> luxury.
>
> — Danielle Colding, In the Company of Women
