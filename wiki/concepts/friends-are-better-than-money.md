# Friends are better than money

Almost anything money can do, friends can do better; having a friend with a boat beats owning a boat.

> Friends are better than money. Almost anything money can do,
> friends can do better. In so many ways a friend with a boat is
> better than owning a boat.
>
> — Kevin Kelly, 68 Bits of Unsolicited Advice
