# Friends who will stay in touch don't need to promise

People who promise to stay in touch often drift apart, while those who genuinely will keep in contact feel no need to make the promise.

> They had drifted apart, as people do when they promise to stay
> in touch; the ones who are going to stay in touch don't need to
> promise.
>
> — Edward St. Aubyn, Lost for Words
