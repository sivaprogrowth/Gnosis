# Friendship happens on the way to something else

Trying directly to make friends feels forced and tends to fail. Friendship comes naturally when you pursue learning or achieving something together with others.

> Friendship happens on the way to something else.
>
> If you "try to meet new people" it feels weird and forced. The
> more you aim for friendship, the more it eludes you.
>
> But if you aim to learn or achieve something with others,
> friendship happens naturally during the shared pursuit.
>
> — James Clear
