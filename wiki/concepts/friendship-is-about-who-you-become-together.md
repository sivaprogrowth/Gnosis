# Friendship is about who you become together

A great friendship or relationship is measured not by what the other person does for you, but by how the two of you are changed in each other's company.

> It’s not about what someone can do for you, it's who and what
> the two of you become in each other's presence.
>
> — Ronald Sharp, Do Your Friends Actually Like You?
