# Full attention is the greatest gift

In any personal or professional relationship, giving someone your complete attention is the single most powerful thing you can do.

> The single most powerful thing you can do in a relationship,
> whether it’s personal or professional, is to give someone 100% of
> your attention.
>
> — Brad Jacobs, The Knowledge Project podcast

> Attention is the rarest and purest form of generosity.
>
> — Simone Weil, Letter to the poet Joë Bousquet (April 13, 1942)
