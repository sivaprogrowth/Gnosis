# Full effort for years beats lukewarm effort for decades

Committing fully to something for a few years usually achieves more than half-hearted effort over a lifetime. Pick one priority for this season of life and give it your best.

> You can usually accomplish more by giving something your full
> effort for a few years rather than giving it a lukewarm effort
> for fifty years.
>
> Pick a priority for this season of your life and do it to the
> best of your ability.
>
> — James Clear
