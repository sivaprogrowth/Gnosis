# Future crises are decided in today's quiet weeks

How you handle your biggest future sorrow or temptation is determined by how you live now, in ordinary uneventful times. The real struggle happens in preparation.

> Someday, in years to come, you will be wrestling with the great
> temptation, or trembling under the great sorrow of your life. But
> the real struggle is here, now, in these quiet weeks. Now it is
> being decided whether, in the day of your supreme sorrow or
> temptation, you shall miserably fail or gloriously conquer.
>
> — Phillips Brooks, The Purpose and Use of Comfort
