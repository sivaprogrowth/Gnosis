# Generations of integrity earn trust even from opponents

The Bragg family's long record of integrity was so strong that even a political opponent backed his loan application.

> Five generations of Bragg family integrity meant a political
> opponent supported his loan application, saying, "If we can't
> lend money to the Bragg family, we can't lend money to anybody."
>
> — John Bragg, The Knowledge Project
