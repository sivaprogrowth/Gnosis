# Generosity means giving without keeping score

Buffett admired Tom Murphy for doing many things for others without tracking whether they returned the favor, and for working calmly without shouting.

> My good friend and hero, Tom Murphy, had an incredible
> generosity of spirit. He would do five things for you without
> thinking about whether you did something for him. After he was
> done with those five things, he'd be thinking about how to do the
> sixth.
>
> — Warren Buffett, Getting There: A Book of Mentors
