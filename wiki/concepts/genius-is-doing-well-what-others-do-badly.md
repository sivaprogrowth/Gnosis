# Genius is doing well what others do badly

Good work takes time and attention to detail, while mediocrity rushes. Genius is simply doing ordinary things carefully and well.

> Everything good needs time. Don’t do work in a hurry. Go into
> details; it pays in every way. Time means power for your work.
> Mediocrity is always in a rush; but whatever is worth doing at
> all is worth doing with consideration. For genius is nothing more
> nor less than doing well what anyone can do badly.
>
> — Amelia Barr, "A Successful Novelist" in How They Succeeded
