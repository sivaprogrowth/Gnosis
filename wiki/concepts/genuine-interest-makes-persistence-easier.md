# Genuine interest makes persistence easier

Persistence comes more easily when you are genuinely interested in what you are doing. Finding the right subject makes you tougher.

> Mental toughness immediately increases when you find the right
> subject. You're more likely to stick with things when you're
> genuinely interested.
>
> — James Clear

> “Often times, the ones that are successful loved what they did so they could persevere when it got really tough.”
>
> — Steve Jobs

> One way to instantly gain a competitive edge is to work on
> things that genuinely interest you. The person who is having fun
> has a better chance of winning because they are more likely to
> stick with it when things get challenging.
>
> If it felt like a hassle from the start, you're likely to quit as
> soon as things get tough. But if you've been having fun the whole
> time, you're better positioned to work through the hard parts.
>
> — James Clear

> There is no path to greatness without hard work. And the only person who feels like working hard for years is someone who is excited about the task.
>
> You only get one life and you’re going to burn the days either way, so the real question is: What excites you and interests you? What makes you feel curious and alive, perhaps even joyful? What is so "worth it" that you're not worried about the tradeoffs, the hard work, the hassle?
>
> Follow your enthusiasm.
>
> — James Clear
