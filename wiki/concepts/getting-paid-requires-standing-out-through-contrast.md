# Getting paid requires standing out through contrast

You get noticed and rewarded when you stand out against your surroundings, so it's better to dominate a small niche than be lost in a huge crowd.

> If you want to get paid, find the contrast.
>
> No one hears you if you sneeze in the middle of New York. But if
> you sneeze in a library, everyone does. The same concept applies
> to work.
>
> It's better to dominate a small pond than drown in an ocean.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
