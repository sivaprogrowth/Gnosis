# Getting people wrong is what living is

No matter how open-minded we try to be, we always misjudge people before, during, and after meeting them. Being wrong about others is part of being alive.

> The fact remains that getting people right
> is not what living is all about anyway. It's getting them wrong
> that is living, getting them wrong and wrong and wrong and then,
> on careful reconsideration, getting them wrong again. That's how
> we know we're alive: we're wrong.
>
> — Philip Roth
