# Give advice as a reminder, not a revelation

When advising someone, frame it as reminding them of something they already knew. Don't present it as pointing out something they were unable to see.

> When you counsel someone, you should appear to be reminding him
> of something he had forgotten, not of the light he was unable to
> see.
>
> — Baltasar Gracian, The Art of Worldly Wisdom
