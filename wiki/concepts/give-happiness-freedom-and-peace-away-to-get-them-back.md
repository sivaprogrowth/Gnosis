# Give happiness, freedom, and peace away to get them back

The things we want most, such as happiness, freedom, and peace of mind, come to us when we give them to others.

> There is a wonderful, almost mystical, law of nature that says
> three of the things we want most—happiness, freedom, and peace of
> mind—are always attained when we give them to others. Give it
> away to get it back.
>
> — John Wooden, Wooden: A Lifetime of Observations and Reflections On and Off the Court
