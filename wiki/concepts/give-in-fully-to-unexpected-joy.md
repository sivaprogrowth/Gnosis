# Give in fully to unexpected joy

When joy arrives suddenly, don't hold back or be afraid of its abundance. Joy is meant to be embraced fully, not rationed.

> If you suddenly and unexpectedly feel joy, don’t hesitate. Give
> in to it.
>
> ...whatever it is, don’t be afraid of its plenty.
>
> Joy is not made to be a crumb.
>
> — Mary Oliver, Swan: Poems and Prose Poems
