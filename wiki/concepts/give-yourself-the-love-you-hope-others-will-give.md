# Give yourself the love you hope others will give

Self-love means offering yourself the acceptance and affirmation you dream of receiving from someone else. Don't expect love from others that you withhold from yourself.

> One of the best guides to how to be self-loving is to give
> ourselves the love we are often dreaming about receiving from
> others.
>
> — bell hooks, All About Love: New Visions
