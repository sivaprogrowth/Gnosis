# Goals matter for who they make you become

The main value of setting goals isn't getting what you want but being compelled to become the person capable of achieving them.

> “The real value in setting goals is not in their achievement. The
> acquisition of the things you want is strictly secondary. The
> major reason for setting goals is to compel you to become the
> person it takes to achieve them.”
>
> — Jim Rohn, 7 Strategies for Wealth and Happiness

> “The ultimate reason for setting goals is to entice you to become
> the person it takes to achieve them.”
>
> — Jim Rohn
