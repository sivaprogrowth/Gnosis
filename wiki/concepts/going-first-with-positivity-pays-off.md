# Going first with positivity pays off

Gabby Reece makes a habit of saying hello or smiling first. Initiating warmth, she says, pays off most of the time.

> I always say that I'll go first...
>
> That means if I'm checking out at the store, I'll say hello
> first.
>
> If I'm coming across somebody and make eye contact, I'll smile
> first.
>
> [I wish] people would experiment with that in their life a little
> bit. Be first, because – not all times, but most times – it comes
> in your favor.
>
> — Gabby Reece, The Tim Ferriss Show

> One of the biggest misperceptions about reciprocity is that
> people should sit around waiting for others to go first rather
> than unlocking the power of reciprocity in their favor by going
> positive and going first without expectation.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
