# Good advice at the wrong time is bad advice

Life has seasons with different requirements, so advice is only useful when it fits your current season. Knowing which season you are in helps you pick which ideas to apply.

> "Good advice at the wrong time is bad advice.
>
> Life is full of seasons and each season has different
> requirements.
>
> Know what season you are in, and you can better identify which
> ideas to utilize."
>
> — James Clear
