# Good apologies heal, bad apologies worsen the wound

A sincere apology repairs harm like medicine, while a poor one makes the hurt worse.

> A good apology is like antibiotic, a bad apology is like rubbing
> salt in the wound.
>
> — Randy Pausch, The Last Lecture
