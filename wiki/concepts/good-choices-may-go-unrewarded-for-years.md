# Good choices may go unrewarded for years

The best choices compound, and in compounding the biggest rewards come late. So keep working and be patient even when results don't show yet.

> A good choice may go unrewarded for a long time.
>
> The best choices tend to provide exponential returns and a
> hallmark of any compounding process is that the greatest rewards
> are delayed. Things don't really take off until years later.
>
> Keep working. Be patient.
>
> — James Clear
