# Good cooks love quality, hard work, and creating

Being a good cook takes three loves: a love of the good, a love of hard work, and a love of creating.

> To be a good cook you have to have a love of the good, a love of hard work, and a love of creating.
>
> — Julia Child, Particular Passions
