# Good fortune creates an obligation to use it well

Robinson makes sense of her unexplained good luck by treating it as a special duty to put that luck to good use.

> I can only make sense of my unaccountable good fortune by
> assuming that it means I am under special obligation to make good
> use of it.
>
> — Marilynne Robinson, The Paris Review Interviews: Volume IV
