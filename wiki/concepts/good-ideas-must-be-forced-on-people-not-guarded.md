# Good ideas must be forced on people, not guarded

You don't need to fear people stealing your ideas. Good ideas meet so much resistance that you have to push hard to get people to accept them.

> “Don't worry about people stealing your ideas. If your ideas are
> any good, you'll have to ram them down people's throats.”
>
> — Howard Aiken
