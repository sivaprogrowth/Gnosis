# Good judgment uses both mind and heart

Few people use reason, fewer use feeling, and those who combine both are truly rare. Good judgment requires warmth and logic together.

> Rare are those people who use the mind; few use the heart; and really unique are those who use both.
>
> — Rita Levi-Montalcini, WIRED Interview (March 2009)
