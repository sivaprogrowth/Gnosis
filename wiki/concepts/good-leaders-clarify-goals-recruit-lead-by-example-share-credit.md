# Good leaders clarify goals, recruit, lead by example, share credit

A short leadership guide: always know what the team is optimizing for, keep recruiting, never ask others to do what you won't do, and give away credit while taking blame.

> A brief guide to leadership:
>
> * Always know the answer to, “What are we optimizing for?”
> * Recruit. Recruit. Recruit.
> * Never ask someone to do something you aren’t willing to do
> yourself.
> * Give away the credit. Take the blame.
>
> — James Clear
