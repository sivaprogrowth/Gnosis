# Good listening requires genuine interest, not just silence

Anyone can listen by being quiet, but good listening requires real curiosity. That curiosity leads to understanding and helps the other person feel understood.

> Anyone can listen. All you have to do is stop talking.
>
> But to be a good listener, you have to stop talking and be
> interested.
>
> Genuine curiosity is the precursor to understanding, and a good
> listener helps the other person feel understood.
>
> — James Clear
