# Good luck is made, bad luck endured

People create their own good fortune by trusting their instincts, taking risks, and embracing possibilities, while bad luck is something you simply bear.

> Rose Kennedy once told me that good luck
> is something you make and bad luck is something you endure, a
> very wise observation indeed. People do make their luck by daring
> to follow their instincts, taking risks, and embracing every
> possibility.
>
> — Rose Kennedy
