# Good opportunities are ruined by dreaming of slightly better ones

Agonizing over unchosen options keeps you from making the most of the one you chose. Commitment to your actual choice matters as much as the choice itself.

> Many good opportunities are ruined for the dream of slightly
> better ones.
>
> — James Clear
