# Good positioning beats being smarter

You can outperform smarter people by being in a better position. Good positions make anyone look brilliant, and bad ones make the smartest look foolish.

> You don’t need to be smarter than others to outperform them if
> you can out-position them. Anyone looks like a genius when
> they’re in a good position, and even the smartest person looks
> like an idiot when they’re in a bad one.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Clear Thinking

> Everyone looks smart when they’re in a good position, and
> even the smartest person looks like a fool in a bad one.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Outliers episode: The Witch of Wall Street

> "A good position allows you to think clearly rather than be
> forced by circumstances into a decision. One reason the best in
> the world make consistently good decisions is they rarely find
