# Good questions matter more than answers

Rabi credits his mother asking whether he asked a good question, rather than what he learned, with making him a scientist. Valuing questions over answers shapes curious minds.

> “My mother made me a scientist without ever intending to.
>
> Every other Jewish mother in Brooklyn would ask her child after
> school: 'So? Did you learn anything today?'
>
> But not my mother.
>
> 'Izzy,' she would say, 'did you ask a good question today?'
>
> That difference — asking good questions — made me become a
> scientist.”
>
> — Isidor Isaac Rabi, "Great Minds Start With Questions" in Parents Magazine (September 1993)

> "It is not the answer that enlightens, but the question."
>
> — Eugene Ionesco, Decouvertes (1970)
