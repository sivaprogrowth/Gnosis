# Good relationships make both sides feel they got the better deal

In a good marriage or friendship, each person privately believes they are the lucky one.

> A good marriage is one in which each spouse secretly thinks he
> or she got the better deal, and this is true also of our
> friendships.
>
> — Anne Lamott, Help, Thanks, Wow
