# Good software often starts quick and embarrassing

Great software often begins as a rough reaction to something that doesn't work, not as a grand vision. Google Maps, for example, started as Bret Taylor's weekend rewrite of a mapping tool that annoyed him.

> Good software
> often starts as something quick and embarrassing—a reaction to
> what doesn't work rather than a grand vision.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project
