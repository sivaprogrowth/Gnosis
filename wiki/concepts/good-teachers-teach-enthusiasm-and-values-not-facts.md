# Good teachers teach enthusiasm and values, not facts

The real job of a good teacher is to pass on enthusiasm, open-mindedness and values rather than just facts.

> A good teacher does not teach facts, he or she teaches
> enthusiasm, open-mindedness and values.
>
> — Gian-Carlo Rota, Indiscrete Thoughts
