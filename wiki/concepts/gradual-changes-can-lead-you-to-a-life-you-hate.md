# Gradual changes can lead you to a life you hate

Tiny, unnoticed shifts over time can pull you away from who you are until you are living a life untrue to yourself.

> “By increments so exquisitely gradual that they might have passed
> unnoticed, I could have ended up being totally untrue to myself
> and living a life I hated.”
>
> — Peter Barton, Not Fade Away by Laurence Shames and Peter Barton
