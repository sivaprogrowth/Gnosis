# Great advances come from dreams and attempts

Every major advance, personal or global, comes from dreaming and then trying. So dream more and try more.

> As far as I can tell, every great advance — whether in an
> individual life or the world at large — has come from dreams and
> attempts.
>
> Devise more dreams. Make more attempts.
>
> — James Clear
