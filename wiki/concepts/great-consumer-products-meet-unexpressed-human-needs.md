# Great consumer products meet unexpressed human needs

The best consumer products connect at a deep level with an instinct or need people have felt but that has gone unspoken or unmet.

> "Great products in the consumer world speak to us on some deep level. They speak to some human instinct or need that we’ve been feeling and it’s been unexpressed or unmet."
>
> — Mark Pincus, The Knowledge Project
