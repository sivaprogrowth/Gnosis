# Great employees anticipate problems and seek opportunities

Employees operate at three levels: doing what is asked, also solving problems before they happen, and also proactively finding and pursuing growth opportunities for the business.

> The 3 Levels of Employees:
>
> Level 1 — You do what you are asked to do.
>
> Level 2 — Level 1 + You think ahead and solve problems before
> they happen.
>
> Level 3 — Level 2 + You proactively look for areas of
> opportunity and growth in the business, and figure out how to tap
> into them.
>
> — James Clear
