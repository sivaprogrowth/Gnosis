# Great events hang by a single thread

Big outcomes often depend on small details; the clever person neglects nothing, while neglecting one thing can cost everything.

> “All great events hang by a single thread. The clever man takes
> advantage of everything, neglects nothing that may give him some
> added opportunity; the less clever man, by neglecting one thing,
> sometimes misses everything.”
>
> — Napoleon
