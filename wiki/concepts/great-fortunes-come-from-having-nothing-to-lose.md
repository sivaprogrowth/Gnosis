# Great fortunes come from having nothing to lose

Most entrepreneurial fortunes were built by people with nothing to lose who either weren't told something was impossible or ignored those who said so.

> “Nearly all the great fortunes acquired by entrepreneurs arose
> because they had nothing to lose. Nobody had bothered to tell
> them that such a thing could not be done or would be likely to
> fail. Or if they had been told, then they weren’t listening. They
> were too busy proving those around them wrong.”
>
> — Felix Dennis
