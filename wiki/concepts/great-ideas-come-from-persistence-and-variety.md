# Great ideas come from persistence and variety

Solving important problems comes from staying with a topic for a long time while trying many different approaches and refining the best solution so far. What looks like genius may just be the result of this combination.

> Persistence and variety. These are the two primary ways to
> develop great ideas or to solve important problems.
>
> Keep leaning your head against a topic for a long time. Certainly
> for weeks, possibly for years. And along the way, try many lines
> of attack. Continue to generate options, explore paths, and
> propose silly ideas. Copy and paste concepts from widely
> different disciplines and see if it gets you anywhere. All the
> while, continue to refine the best solution you’ve found thus
> far.
>
> What looks like genius may simply be the byproduct of persistence
> and variety.
>
> — James Clear
