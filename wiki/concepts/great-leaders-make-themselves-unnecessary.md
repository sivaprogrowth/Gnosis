# Great leaders make themselves unnecessary

A teacher's greatest success is when students work as if the teacher did not exist. The same holds for leadership: give people space and they flourish.

> The greatest sign of success for a teacher is to be able to say, "The children are now working as if I did not exist." But this holds true for leadership in general—give people space and they flourish.
>
> — Maria Montessori, The Montessori Method (1912)
