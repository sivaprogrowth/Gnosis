# Great outcomes come from consistent days, not perfect ones

Results are built from showing up every day, easy or hard, rather than from a few great days. No single day makes or breaks the goal.

> Great outcomes aren't built on great days, but on consistent
> ones.
>
> You can't just count the days when it's easy. Each day moves you
> closer to the goal. No day is a hero; no day is a villain.
>
> Perfect days don't compound. Consistent ones do.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
