# Great presenters think about the audience, not themselves

World-class presenters focus on their audience rather than on how they themselves come across.

> "I truly believe that most world-class presenters are thinking
> about their audience and not about themselves. They’re not trying
> to come across a certain way."
>
> — Blake Eastman, The Knowledge Project podcast
