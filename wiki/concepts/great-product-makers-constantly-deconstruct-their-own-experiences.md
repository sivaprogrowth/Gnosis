# Great product makers constantly deconstruct their own experiences

To become a great product maker, commit to studying your own reactions to every new product and noticing what feels great or doesn't.

> "If you want to be a great product maker, you need to commit to a career of deconstructing and just anytime a new product comes out, be a student of yourself in that experience and what is it that feels great or doesn’t?"
>
> — Mark Pincus, The Knowledge Project
