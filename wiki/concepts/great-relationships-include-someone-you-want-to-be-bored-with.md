# Great relationships include someone you want to be bored with

A great partner is not only someone you have fun with but someone you enjoy ordinary, boring time with. Much of the beauty of long-term relationships lives in those mundane moments.

> A great relationship is not only finding the person you have fun with, but also finding the person you want to be bored with. The beauty of long-term relationships is often hidden in boring, ordinary moments.
>
> — James Clear
