# Great salespeople genuinely like people and tell them

Joe Girard, considered one of the best car salesmen in history, built relationships by genuinely liking people and letting them know it.

> The legendary car salesman Joe Girard was considered one of the
> best in history. His secret? Building relationships by genuinely
> liking people and telling them.
>
> — Joe Girard, Selling: The Crucial Skill Nobody Taught You
