# Great storytelling is strategic, not innate

People who tell amazing stories weren't born with the skill. They are being strategic, and anyone who becomes more strategic can do it too.

> It turns out that’s not actually something that people are born with; it is just strategic. And if you become a little more strategic, you can be one of those people.
>
> — Matthew Dicks, The Knowledge Project podcast
