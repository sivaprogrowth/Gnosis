# Great things come from small things brought together

Great achievements are built from a series of small steps, not impulses. Focus on the first small thing, because small beginnings often lead to extraordinary results.

> “Great things are not done by impulse, but by a series of small
> things brought together. The trick is to focus on the first small
> thing. Starting small is still starting, and small beginnings
> often lead to extraordinary endings.”
>
> — Vincent Van Gogh
