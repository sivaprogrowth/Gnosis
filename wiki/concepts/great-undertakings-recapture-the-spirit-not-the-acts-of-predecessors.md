# Great undertakings recapture the spirit, not the acts, of predecessors

Rather than repeating what great people did, carry forward the spirit behind their work and adapt it to your own time.

> All great undertakings do not consist of doing again what others
> have done before, but in recapturing the spirit that went into
> what they did—and would have done differently in a different
> age.
>
> — Paul Valéry, The Collected Works of Paul Valéry
