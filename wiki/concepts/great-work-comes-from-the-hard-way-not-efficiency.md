# Great work comes from the hard way, not efficiency

Seinfeld argues that efficiency is the wrong approach to making something great; his show succeeded because he obsessively controlled every detail.

> If you’re efficient, you’re
> doing it the wrong way. The right way is the hard way. The show
> was successful because I micromanaged it—every word, every line,
> every take, every edit, every casting. That’s my way of life.
>
> — [[jerry-seinfeld|Jerry Seinfeld]], Life’s Work: An Interview with Jerry Seinfeld
