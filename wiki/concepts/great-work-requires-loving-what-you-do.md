# Great work requires loving what you do

Since work fills much of life, true satisfaction comes from doing great work, which comes from loving it, so don't settle.

> “You've got to find what you love. And that is as true for your
> work as it is for your lovers. Your work is going to fill a large
> part of your life, and the only way to be truly satisfied is to
> do what you believe is great work. And the only way to do great
> work is to love what you do.... Don't settle.”
>
> — Steve Jobs
