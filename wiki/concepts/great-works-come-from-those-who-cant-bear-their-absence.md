# Great works come from those who can't bear their absence

The greatest works were not ordered by others. They were made by people who felt compelled to create something that didn't exist yet.

> The world's greatest works weren't commissioned; they were
> created by people who couldn't bear their absence.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
