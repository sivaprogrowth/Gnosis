# Greater artists feel greater doubt

The closer you get to excellence, the more you notice what is missing. Perfect confidence tends to belong to the less talented.

> The greater the artist, the greater the doubt; perfect
> confidence is granted to the less talented as a consolation
> prize.
>
> — Robert Hughes, Time Magazine (June 10, 1996)
