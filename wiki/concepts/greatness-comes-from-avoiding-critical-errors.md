# Greatness comes from avoiding critical errors

What separates greatness from mediocrity is not spectacular moments but avoiding big mistakes, because losses are mathematically hard to recover from. Excellence is consistent execution, not occasional brilliance.

> The difference between greatness and mediocrity isn't in the
> spectacular moments but rather in avoiding critical errors.
>
> Warren Buffett's first rule of investing? Never lose money. His
> second rule? Never forget rule one. This isn't clever wordplay -
> it's the mathematics of success. A 50% loss demands a 100% gain
> to break even. Or look at elite athletes: while average
> quarterbacks mix brilliant throws with costly interceptions, Tom
> Brady and Patrick Mahomes protect the ball first.
>
> Excellence isn't about occasional brilliance - it's about
> consistent execution.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
