# Greatness comes from consistency, not single acts

Doing something once is common, but doing it repeatedly over time is rare and remarkable. Ordinary days of consistent effort add up to extraordinary results over decades.

> Greatness is consistency.
>
> Meditating once is common. Meditating daily is rare.
>
> Exercising today is simple. Training every week is simply
> remarkable.
>
> Writing one essay rarely matters. Write every day and you're
> practically a hero.
>
> Unheroic days can make for heroic decades.
>
> — James Clear
