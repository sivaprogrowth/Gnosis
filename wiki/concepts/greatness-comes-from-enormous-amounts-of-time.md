# Greatness comes from enormous amounts of time

What looks like magic or mastery is often the result of someone investing far more time in something than anyone would reasonably expect.

> “Sometimes magic is just someone spending more time on something
> than anyone else might reasonably expect.”
>
> — Raymond Joseph Teller, The Honor System

> “No one’s really that great. You know who’s great? The people that just put tremendous amount of hours into it. It’s a game of tonnage.”
>
> — [[jerry-seinfeld|Jerry Seinfeld]]
