# Greatness depends on what you will give up

Greater results usually require greater tradeoffs. The real question is not whether you want to be great, but what you are willing to sacrifice to get there.

> Life is a series of tradeoffs, and greater results usually
> require greater tradeoffs.
>
> The question is not, "Do you want to be great at this?"
>
> The question is, "What are you willing to give up in order to be
> great at this?"
>
> — James Clear
