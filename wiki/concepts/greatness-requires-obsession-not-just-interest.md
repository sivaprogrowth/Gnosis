# Greatness requires obsession, not just interest

Being good at something you aren't interested in is hard, and being great at something you aren't obsessed with is nearly impossible.

> “It's tough to be good at something you're not interested in.
> It's nearly impossible to be great at something you're not
> obsessed with.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
