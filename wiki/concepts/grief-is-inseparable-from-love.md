# Grief is inseparable from love

Grief and love cannot be separated. Grief reminds us how deeply we loved, and like love it cannot be negotiated away.

> "It seems to me, that if we love, we grieve. That's the deal.
> That's the pact. Grief and love are forever intertwined. Grief is
> the terrible reminder of the depths of our love and, like love,
> grief is non-negotiable."
>
> — Nick Cave, Letter to a fan named Cynthia (Letters of Note)

> As long as there is love, there will be grief. The grief of time
> passing, of life moving on half-finished, of empty spaces that
> were once bursting with the laughter and energy of people we
> loved.
>
> As long as there is love there will be grief because grief is
> love's natural continuation. It shows up in the aisles of stores
> we once frequented, in the half-finished bottle of wine we pour
> out, in the whiff of cologne we get two years after they've been
> gone.
>
> Grief is a giant neon sign, protruding through everything,
> pointing everywhere, broadcasting loudly, "Love was here." In the
> finer print, quietly, "Love still is."
>
> — Heidi Priebe, Instagram
