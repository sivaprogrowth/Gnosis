# Growing richer beats simply being rich

The enjoyment comes from steady improvement over yesterday rather than from having arrived at wealth. Progress is more satisfying than a static state.

> “It is so much more fun to be a little richer than you were
> yesterday, than merely to be rich.”
>
> — Alice Wellington Rollins
