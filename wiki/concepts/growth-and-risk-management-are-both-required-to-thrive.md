# Growth and risk management are both required to thrive

Two central questions in life are how to grow and how to manage risk. Surviving and excelling require attention to both.

> Two of the key questions in life: How do I grow? How do I manage
> risk?
>
> You need to do both if you wish to survive and excel.
>
> — James Clear
