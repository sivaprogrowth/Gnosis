# Growth comes from challenges you feel unready for

Challenges that make you grow never arrive at a moment when you feel ready. At some point you must accept uncertainty, because feeling unready is part of what makes it growth.

> There will never be a perfect time to do something that
> stretches you.
>
> That’s true whether you are starting a business, having a child,
> changing careers, or wrestling with any number of challenges.
> That’s not a license to be reckless and never think things
> through, but at some point you have to embrace the uncertainty
> because it is the only path forward.
>
> If you were ready for it, it wouldn't be growth.
>
> — James Clear

> I always did something I was a little not ready to do. I think
> that's how you grow — when there's that moment of, 'Wow, I'm
> really not sure I can do this,' and you push through.
>
> — Marissa Mayer, MAKERS Interview
