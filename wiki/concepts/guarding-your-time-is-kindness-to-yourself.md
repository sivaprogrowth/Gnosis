# Guarding your time is kindness to yourself

Protecting your time is not rude; it is how work gets done. Letting things like meetings run long is being nice to others while being unkind to yourself.

> Guarding your time isn't rude; it's how you get stuff done.
>
> Letting a meeting go on longer than necessary is nice to them, but unkind to yourself.
>
> Nice is what people want from you. Kindness is what you owe yourself.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
