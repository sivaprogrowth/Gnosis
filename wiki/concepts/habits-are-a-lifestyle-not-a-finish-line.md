# Habits are a lifestyle, not a finish line

Habits are not something you complete and move past; they are an ongoing way of living. So it is better to learn principles for a lifestyle than to sprint through a short challenge.

> Habits are not a finish line to be crossed, they’re a lifestyle
> to be lived.
>
> — James Clear
