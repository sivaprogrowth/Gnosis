# Habits are how you embody an identity

Each habit you perform is an act of being a certain kind of person, so your habits shape who you become.

> Your habits are how you embody a particular identity.
>
> When you make your bed, you embody the identity of someone who is
> clean and organized. When you study, you embody the identity of
> someone who is studious.
>
> What identity are you embodying today? Who are your habits
> helping you become?
>
> — James Clear
