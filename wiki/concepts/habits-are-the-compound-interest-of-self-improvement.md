# Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement

Small habits repeated consistently accumulate over time into significant results, much like compound interest.

> Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement.
>
> A small habit—when repeated consistently—grows into something
> significant.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
