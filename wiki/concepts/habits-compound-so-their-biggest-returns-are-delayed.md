# Habits compound, so their biggest returns are delayed

Habits don't produce results linearly with effort; they follow a compound growth curve. The gap between expected and experienced results is the plateau of latent potential, where effort accumulates before rewards show.

> Rather than having some
> linear relationship with achievement, habits tend to have more of
> a compound growth curve. The greatest returns are delayed. This
> gap between what we expect and what we experience is what I refer
> to as the “plateau of latent potential.”
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 1
