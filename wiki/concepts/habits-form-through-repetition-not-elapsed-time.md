# Habits form through repetition, not elapsed time

Time passing does not create a habit; the number and frequency of repetitions does. In 30 days you could do something once or a hundred times.

> The truth is, there is
> nothing about time passing that magically forms a habit. Habits
> are created based on repetition and frequency, not by the clock
> ticking. The next 30 days can pass and you can do something once
> or you can do it 100 times.
>
> — James Clear
