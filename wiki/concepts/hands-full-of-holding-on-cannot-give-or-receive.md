# Hands full of holding on cannot give or receive

When you cling tightly to something, you lose the ability to give to others or receive anything new. Letting go makes room for exchange.

> If my hands are fully occupied in holding on to something, I can
> neither give nor receive.
>
> — Dorothee Sölle, The Strength of the Weak
