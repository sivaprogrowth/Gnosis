# Happiness comes from extracting sweetness from every circumstance

A key to a happy life is the skill of finding comfort and joy in whatever situation you're in.

> One of the best secrets of a happy life is the art of extracting
> comfort and sweetness from every circumstance.
>
> — Thomas Mitchell
