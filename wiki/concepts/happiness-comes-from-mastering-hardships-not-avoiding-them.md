# Happiness comes from mastering hardships, not avoiding them

A happy life is not one free of difficulty but one in which difficulties are overcome and mastered.

> A happy life consists not in the absence, but in the mastery of
> hardships.
>
> — Helen Keller, The Simplest Way to Be Happy
