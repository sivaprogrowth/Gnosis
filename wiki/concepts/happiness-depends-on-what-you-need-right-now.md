# Happiness depends on what you need right now

There is no single answer to what makes you happy, because your needs change over time. Too much of any good thing, like rest, work, or exercise, stops serving you, so ask what you need at this moment.

> You will never find one answer to what makes you happy. There
> are many answers, and they change based on your current state.
>
> People need to relax, but if all you do is sit on the beach, it
> gets old. People find meaning in work, but if all you do is work,
> it gets exhausting. People benefit from exercise, but if all you
> do is exercise, it gets unhealthy.
>
> Happiness will always be fleeting because your needs change over
> time. The question is: what do you need right now?
>
> — James Clear
