# Happiness depends on what you think about

Your circumstances, identity, location, or activity don't determine your happiness; your thoughts do.

> “It isn’t what you have, or who you are, or where you are, or
> what you are doing that makes you happy or unhappy. It is what
> you think about.”
>
> — Dale Carnegie
