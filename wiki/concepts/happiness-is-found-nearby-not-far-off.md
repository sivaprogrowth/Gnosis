# Happiness is found nearby, not far off

Believing happiness lies in a new place, job, or person keeps it out of reach. Find happiness in the people and places close to you.

> People often think that something "far off" will make them
> happy. That moving to a new place or getting a different job or
> meeting someone new will change how they feel. But happiness will
> always be withheld if you believe it is somewhere else. The
> secret is to find happiness in the people and places that are
> close to you. See the beauty in the things that are nearby.
>
> — James Clear
