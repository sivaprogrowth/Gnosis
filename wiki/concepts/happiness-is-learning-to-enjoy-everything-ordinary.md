# Happiness is learning to enjoy everything ordinary

The secret of happiness is knowing how to enjoy the simplest everyday things, like eating, resting, or a ray of sunshine. In short, love everything.

> The secret of being happy consists of knowing how to enjoy yourself—enjoy being at table, in bed, enjoy standing up, sitting down, enjoy the nearest ray of sunshine, the slightest bit of landscape: in other words, love everything.
>
> — Gustave Flaubert, His private notebook, Souvenirs, written from 1838–1841
