# Happiness is the absence of desire to change

Happiness is not the pursuit of pleasure but the lack of desire. It is the state you are in when you no longer want to feel differently.

> Happiness is simply the absence of desire... Happiness is not
> about the achievement of pleasure (which is joy or satisfaction),
> but about the lack of desire. It arrives when you have no urge to
> feel differently. Happiness is the state you enter when you no
> longer want to change your state.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
