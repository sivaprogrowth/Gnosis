# Happiness not found now is rarely found later

People expect happiness in some future circumstance, but if it isn't found where and as we are now, it likely never will be. The art of living is extracting comfort and sweetness from every circumstance, like a bee seeking honey.

> One of the best secrets of a happy life is the art of extracting
> comfort and sweetness from every circumstance...
>
> People are always looking for happiness at some future time and
> in some new thing, or some new set of circumstances, in
> possession of which they some day expect to find themselves. But
> the fact is, if happiness is not found now, where we are, and as
> we are, there is little chance of it ever being found. There is a
> great deal more happiness around us day by day than we have the
> sense or power to seek and find.
>
> If we are to cultivate the art of living, we should cultivate the
> art of extracting sweetness and comfort out of everything, as the
> bee goes from flower to flower in search of honey.
>
> — Thomas Mitchell, Essays on Life
