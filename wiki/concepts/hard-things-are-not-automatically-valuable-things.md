# Hard things are not automatically valuable things

Great effort does not guarantee a great result. Before working hard on something, check that the goal is actually worth it.

> Do not confuse things that are hard with things that are
> valuable.
>
> Many things in life are hard. Just because you are giving a great
> effort does not mean you are working toward a great result.
>
> Make sure that mountain is worth climbing.
>
> — James Clear
