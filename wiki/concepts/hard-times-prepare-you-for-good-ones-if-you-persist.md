# Hard times prepare you for good ones if you persist

Darkness, sadness, and failure can set the stage for light, joy, and success, but only if you keep going and stay open to what comes next.

> Darkness is preparation for light.
>
> Sadness is preparation for joy.
>
> Failure is preparation for success.
>
> But only if you keep going and remain open to what comes next.
>
> — James Clear
