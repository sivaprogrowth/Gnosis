# Hard times provide contrast to appreciate joy

Stars are visible only against dark space. In the same way, difficult periods give the contrast that lets us appreciate joyful moments, so we should not wish them away.

> The brilliance of the stars would be invisible without the vast
> darkness of space behind them.
>
> Do not wish away the difficult portions of life. They provide the
> contrast needed to appreciate the joyful moments.
>
> — James Clear
