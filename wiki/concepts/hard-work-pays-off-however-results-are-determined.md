# Hard work pays off however results are determined

Working hard is worthwhile whether outcomes depend on effort, on effort plus luck, or on luck alone. Even when luck decides everything, you still win the battle with yourself.

> Work hard.
>
> If results depend on effort, then you will carry yourself far.
>
> If results depend on effort and luck, then you will have done
> what you can to influence the outcome.
>
> And if results depend on luck alone, then the outcome is random,
> but you will have won the battle with yourself.
>
> — James Clear
