# Having enough is itself a form of wealth

Cucinelli recalls a rural childhood without wealth but full of mutual support, shared work, and patience. He says the family was happy, and that having enough is a kind of wealth.

> There was no wealth, but we were happy. And it
> makes me think: having enough is itself a form of wealth.
>
> — Brunello Cucinelli, The Dream of Solomeo
