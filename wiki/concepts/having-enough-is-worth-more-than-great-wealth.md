# Having enough is worth more than great wealth

Joseph Heller, told a hedge fund manager made more in a day than Catch-22 ever earned him, replied that he had something the billionaire would never have: enough. Contentment is a kind of wealth money can't buy.

> "Heller responded, 'Yes, but I have something he will never have
> ... Enough.'"
>
> — Joseph Heller, Enough by John Bogle
