# Health and money fundamentals provide a margin of safety

Health and money aren't everything, but lacking them dominates life. You don't need extremes, but learning the basics of fitness and finance gives you a margin of safety.

> Having good health isn’t everything, but not having it is.
>
> Having money isn’t everything, but not having it is.
>
> You don’t need six-pack abs or ten million dollars to be happy,
> but it is worth learning the fundamentals of fitness and finance.
>
> They bring a margin of safety.
>
> — James Clear
