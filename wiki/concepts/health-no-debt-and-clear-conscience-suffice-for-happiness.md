# Health, no debt, and clear conscience suffice for happiness

A person who is healthy, debt-free, and has a clear conscience already has what matters most for happiness; little more can be added.

> What can be added to the happiness of a person who is in health, out of debt, and has a clear conscience?
>
> — Adam Smith, The Theory of Moral Sentiments (1759)
