# Healthy boundaries come from self-love and self-respect

Boundaries grow out of valuing yourself. When you love yourself, you are less willing to tolerate people who don't love you back.

> The source of healthy boundaries is self-love and self-respect.
> When you learn to love yourself, you are less likely to put up
> with people who don't love you back.
>
> — James Clear
