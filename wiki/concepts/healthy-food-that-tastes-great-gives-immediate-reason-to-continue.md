# Healthy food that tastes great gives immediate reason to continue

Eating healthy just to look better gives feedback too slowly to sustain the habit. Choosing healthy food that also tastes great gives you an immediate reason to keep going.

> If you're eating "healthy food" to look better, the feedback is
> too slow. If you are eating healthy food that tastes great, then
> you have an immediate reason to keep going.
>
> — James Clear
