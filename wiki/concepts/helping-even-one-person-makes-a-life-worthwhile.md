# Helping even one person makes a life worthwhile

Small acts of kindness, like easing one person's pain, are enough to give a life meaning.

> If I can stop one heart from breaking,I shall not live in
> vain;If I can ease one life the aching,Or cool one pain,Or help
> one fainting robinUnto his nest again,I shall not live in vain.
>
> — Emily Dickinson, If I Can Stop One Heart From Breaking
