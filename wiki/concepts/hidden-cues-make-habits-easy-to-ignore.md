# Hidden cues make habits easy to ignore

Every habit starts with a cue, and we notice cues that stand out. When the cues for a habit are subtle or hidden, we tend to skip the behavior.

> Every habit is initiated by a cue, and we are more likely to
> notice the cues that stand out. Creating obvious visual cues can
> draw your attention toward your desired habit.
>
> — James Clear
