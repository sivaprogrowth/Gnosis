# Hiding your true self keeps kindred people from finding you

Two people with the same hidden trait spent their lives searching for someone like them, passed each other, and never knew. Concealing who you are can cost you the connection you seek.

> She had blue skin, And so did he.He kept it hid And so did
> she.They searched for blueTheir whole life through, Then passed
> right by—And never knew.
>
> — Shel Silverstein, Everything On It
