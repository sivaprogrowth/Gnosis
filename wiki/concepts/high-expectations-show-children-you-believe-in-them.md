# High expectations show children you believe in them

Setting expectations for kids is a gift because it tells them you have faith they can do great things.

> Expectations are one of the greatest things you can give kids because it’s a way of saying, “I have faith in you. You can do great things."
>
> — Abigail Shrier, The Knowledge Project podcast
