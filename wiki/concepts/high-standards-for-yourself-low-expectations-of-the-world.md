# High standards for yourself, low expectations of the world

Life gets harder when you demand much from the world and little from yourself, and easier when you do the reverse. Hold yourself to high standards while expecting little from others.

> Life is harder when you expect a lot of the world and little of
> yourself.
>
> Life is easier when you expect a lot of yourself and little of
> the world.
>
> High standards, low expectations.
>
> — James Clear
