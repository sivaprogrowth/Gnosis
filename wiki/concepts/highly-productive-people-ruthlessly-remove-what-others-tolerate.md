# Highly productive people ruthlessly remove what others tolerate

People who get unusually large amounts of work done are obsessive about cutting out the things others simply put up with.

> People who get an unusual amount of work done are maniacal about removing things from their lives that others tolerate.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
