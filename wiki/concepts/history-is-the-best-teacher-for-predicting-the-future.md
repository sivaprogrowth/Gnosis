# History is the best teacher for predicting the future

Studying history is the best way to anticipate what comes next. Cheap history books contain hugely valuable answers.

> There is no better teacher than history in determining the
> future. There are answers worth billions of dollars in a \$30
> history book.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]
