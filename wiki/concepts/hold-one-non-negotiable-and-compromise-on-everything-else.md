# Hold one non-negotiable and compromise on everything else

When a negotiation stalls, identify the single thing you truly cannot give up, then be flexible on all the rest.

> If you're stuck in a negotiation, figure out the 1 thing that is
> truly non-negotiable for you and then compromise on everything
> else.
>
> — James Clear
