# Hold people accountable while believing they can transform

Forgiveness and compassion are linked. The challenge is holding people accountable for wrongdoing while still seeing their humanity and their capacity to change.

> For me, forgiveness and compassion are always linked: how do we
> hold people accountable for wrongdoing and yet at the same time
> remain in touch with their humanity enough to believe in their
> capacity to be transformed?
>
> — bell hooks, “There’s No Place to Go But Up” — bell hooks and Maya Angelou in conversation
