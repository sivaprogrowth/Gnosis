# Holding anger burns you more than its target

Clinging to anger in hope of harming someone else mostly harms yourself.

> Holding on to anger is like grasping a hot coal with the intent
> of throwing it at someone else — you are the one who gets
> burned.
>
> — Buddhaghosa, Visuddhimagga IX, 23 (paraphrased Buddhist saying)
