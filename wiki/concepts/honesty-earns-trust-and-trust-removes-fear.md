# Honesty earns trust, and trust removes fear

Being honest and transparent makes people trust you. With that trust, there is no reason for fear, suspicion, or jealousy.

> "If you are honest, truthful, and transparent, people trust you.
> If people trust you, you have no grounds for fear, suspicion, or
> jealousy."
>
> — Dalai Lama
