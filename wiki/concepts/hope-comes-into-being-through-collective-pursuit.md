# Hope comes into being through collective pursuit

Like a country road formed by people walking the same path, hope is created collectively when many people act on it together.

> Hope is like a road in the country; there was never a road, but
> when many people walk on it, the road comes into existence.
>
> — Lu Xun, The Epigrams of Lusin (found in The Wisdom of China and India)
