# How are you contributing to your own struggle?

Your situation may not be your fault, but you bring something, at least your attitude, into every interaction. Consider how you add to your own difficulties.

> How are you collaborating in your defeat?
>
> Don't take it the wrong way. This question doesn't mean your
> current situation is entirely your fault or even that it is
> 50/50. But the truth is you bring something into every
> interaction you will ever experience. (At the very least, you
> bring your attitude.)
>
> How are you contributing to your own struggle?
>
> — James Clear
