# How can I improve the energy of today's situations?

Ask how you can raise the energy of the situations you are in. You may not control the outcome, but you can influence it.

> How can I improve the energy of the situations I am involved in
> today?
>
> You may not control the outcome, but you can influence it.
>
> — James Clear
