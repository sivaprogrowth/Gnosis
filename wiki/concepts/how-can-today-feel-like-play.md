# How can today feel like play?

A prompt to find ways to make the day's work and activities feel enjoyable and playful rather than burdensome.

> How can today feel like play?
>
> — James Clear

> Is taking things so seriously improving my performance? How can
> today feel like play?
>
> — James Clear
