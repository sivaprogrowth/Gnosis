# How can we design a world where doing right is easy?

Individuals, parents, coaches, and leaders should all ask how to shape their surroundings so the right behavior is the easy one.

> “How can we design a world where it’s easy to do
> what’s right? How can we prime our environments so our future
> habits are easier?”
>
> — James Clear
