# How could this be as easy as possible?

Ask how to make whatever you are pursuing as easy as possible, whether meditation, appreciation, or investing. The easy version is often surprisingly effective.

> Take whatever you are trying to accomplish and ask, "How could
> this be as easy as possible?"
>
> — James Clear
