# How do you handle moments that go wrong?

Brady asks whether we face setbacks with class, integrity, courage and resilience, and whether we can share our emotions and lean on others.

> How do you deal
> with them? Do you handle them with class and integrity, with
> courage and resilience? Are you able to share your emotions with
> others?
>
> Do you have people in your life who can help you through those
> challenges?
>
> — Tom Brady
