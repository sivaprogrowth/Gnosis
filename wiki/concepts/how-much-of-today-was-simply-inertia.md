# How much of today was simply inertia?

Ask how much of what you did today happened just out of inertia. Don't get so busy that you stop actively designing your life.

> How much of what you did today was simply due to inertia?
>
> Never get so busy that you forget to actively design your life.
>
> — Steph Smith
