# How much of your media is from the last year?

Compare how much of the media you consume daily is recent with how much of the best and most useful information is recent, to check whether you overvalue new content.

> What percentage of the media you consume on a daily basis was
> created in the last year? What percentage of the best and most
> useful information was created in the last year?
>
> — Nat Eliason
