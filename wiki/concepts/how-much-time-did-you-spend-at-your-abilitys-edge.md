# How much time did you spend at your ability's edge?

Kids may seem to learn faster because they constantly attempt things at the edge of their ability; adults can ask how often they do the same.

> The common narrative is that kids learn faster than adults, but
> if you watch any toddler they spend a large portion of the day
> attempting things that are on the edge of their ability.
>
> How much time have you spent on the edge of your ability today?
>
> — James Clear
