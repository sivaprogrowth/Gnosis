# How people react to honest feedback reveals their intent

People who want to improve welcome honest feedback, while people who want to stay comfortable resist it. You can use that reaction to tell the two apart.

> The people who want to be better welcome honest feedback.
>
> The people who want to stay comfortable fight it.
>
> Let this be a filter.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
