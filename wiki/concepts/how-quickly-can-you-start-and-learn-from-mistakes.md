# How quickly can you start and learn from mistakes?

Improvement centers on the speed of getting started and the speed of learning from mistakes.

> Two questions at the center of improvement:
>
> How quickly can you get started?
>
> How quickly can you learn from your mistakes?
>
> — James Clear
