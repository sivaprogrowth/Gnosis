# How you carry burdens matters most

What breaks people down is not the weight of their burdens but the way they handle them.

> It's not the load that breaks you down, it's the way you carry
> it.
>
> — Lena Horne, Wisdom for the Soul

> You don’t always get to choose the load, but you can choose how
> to carry it.
>
> — James Clear
