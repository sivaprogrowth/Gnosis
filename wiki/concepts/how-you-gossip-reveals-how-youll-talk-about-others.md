# How you gossip reveals how you'll talk about others

When you speak badly about people who are absent, listeners learn you will do the same to them. Your talk about others signals how trustworthy you are.

> How you talk about people when they're not around tells everyone
> what you'll say about them when they're not around.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
