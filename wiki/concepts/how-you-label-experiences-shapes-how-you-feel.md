# How you label experiences shapes how you feel

The story you tell about your experiences determines how you feel about them. Reframing lets you turn shortcomings into something meaningful.

> "How you talk about your experiences will dictate how you feel
> about them. Reframing our goals and rewriting our stories are
> powerful tools. Nobody can tell us how to feel about something.
> We can make our shortcomings into something beautiful if we want
> to. How we label an experience can completely change how we
> perceive it."
>
> — Alexi Pappas, Bravey: Chasing Dreams, Befriending Pain, and Other Big Ideas
