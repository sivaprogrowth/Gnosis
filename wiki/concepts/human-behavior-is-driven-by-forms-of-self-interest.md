# Human behavior is driven by forms of self-interest

Major drivers of behavior include safety, sex, convenience, social norms, and status, all of which come down to how something serves the person.

> Some of the major drivers of human behavior...
>
> * Safety: Does it provide peace of mind or reduce risk?
> * Sex: Does it help them find love or make love?
> * Convenience: Does it save time or energy?
> * Social norms: Does it help them get along with others?
> * Status: Does it improve their standing or help them gain
> approval?
>
> ...which all fit into the larger category of self-interest: How
> does it serve the person?
>
> — James Clear
