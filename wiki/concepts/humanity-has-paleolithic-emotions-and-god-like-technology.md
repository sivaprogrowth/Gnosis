# Humanity has paleolithic emotions and god-like technology

Humanity's core problem is a mismatch: ancient emotions and outdated institutions are managing extremely powerful modern technology.

> The real problem of humanity is the following: we have
> paleolithic emotions; medieval institutions; and god-like
> technology.
>
> — E.O. Wilson, An Intellectual Entente
