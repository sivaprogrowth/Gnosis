# Hustling is a metaphor for human struggle

Hustling captures the basic human struggles to survive, resist, win, and make sense of life.

> “I love metaphors, and for me hustling is the ultimate metaphor
> for the basic human struggles: the struggle to survive and
> resist, the struggle to win and to make sense of it all.”
>
> — Jay Z
