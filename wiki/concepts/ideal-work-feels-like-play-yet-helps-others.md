# Ideal work feels like play yet helps others

The best kind of work is enjoyable to do while also producing something useful and valuable for other people.

> The ideal form of work feels like play, but still accomplishes
> something useful and valuable.
>
> Joyful for you. Helpful to others.
>
> — James Clear
