# Identify bad habit triggers to reduce exposure

Finding the root causes of bad behavior helps because once you know what triggers an unwanted habit, you can reduce your exposure to that trigger.

> taking the time to spot the root causes
> of bad behavior can be helpful because once you know what
> triggers an unwanted habit you can reduce exposure to that
> trigger.
>
> — James Clear
