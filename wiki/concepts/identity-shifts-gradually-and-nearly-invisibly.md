# Identity shifts gradually and nearly invisibly

Habits reshape identity so slowly you rarely notice day to day, but each repetition shifts your internal story.

> Your habits reshape your identity in a gradual way. It's slow and
> nearly impossible to see. You can rarely tell a difference
> between who you were yesterday and who you are today. But with
> each rep, with each vote cast, your internal story begins to
> shift.
>
> — James Clear
