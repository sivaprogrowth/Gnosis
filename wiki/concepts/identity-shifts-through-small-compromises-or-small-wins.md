# Identity shifts through small compromises or small wins

Who you become is shaped incrementally. Small compromises can erode your sense of self, while small wins can gradually transform it.

> You can lose yourself one small compromise at a time.
>
> You can transform yourself one small win at a time.
>
> — James Clear
