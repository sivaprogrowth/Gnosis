# If an idea isn't clear in ten minutes, nothing's there

If you can't understand a founder's idea within ten minutes, the founder likely doesn't understand it either and there is nothing substantive to understand.

> “If in 10 minutes you cannot understand what’s going on, it means
> the person on the other end doesn’t understand what’s going on
> and there isn’t anything to understand.”
>
> — Garry Tan, The Knowledge Project
