# If learning feels tedious, it isn't your passion

Finding it tedious to learn about your field signals it isn't a true passion and you may be in the wrong lane.

> "If it’s tedious to learn, it isn’t a passion. I don’t think you’re in the right lane."
>
> — Bill Gurley, The Knowledge Project
