# If life is all work, make work play

Phil Knight embraced an unbalanced life devoted to work. His answer was to make the work itself feel like play.

> "My life was out of balance, sure, but I didn't care. In fact, I
> wanted even more imbalance. ... If my life was to be all work and
> no play, I wanted my work to be play."
>
> — Phil Knight
