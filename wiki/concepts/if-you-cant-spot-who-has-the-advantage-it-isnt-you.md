# If you can't spot who has the advantage, it isn't you

In any situation someone holds an edge. If you cannot identify who that is, you are probably the one at a disadvantage.

> Someone always has an advantage. If you don’t know who, it isn’t
> you.

> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
