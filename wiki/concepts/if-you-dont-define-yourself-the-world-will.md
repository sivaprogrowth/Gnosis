# If you don't define yourself, the world will

Knowing who you are matters, because if you don't, other people and society will define your identity for you.

> The world will ask you who you are, and if you don't know, the
> world will tell you.
>
> — Carl Jung, Memories, Dreams, Reflections (paraphrased)
