# If you'll regret not doing it, do it

If you expect to regret not doing something, you should probably do it. People regret inaction far more than action.

> “If you think you’re going to regret not doing something, you
> should probably do it. Regret is the worst, and most people
> regret far more things they didn’t do than things they did do.”
>
> — [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]]
