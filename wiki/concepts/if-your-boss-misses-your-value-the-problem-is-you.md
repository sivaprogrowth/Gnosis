# If your boss misses your value, the problem is you

When your manager doesn't see what you contribute, the responsibility lies with you to make that value clear or create more of it.

> If your boss doesn't understand the value you bring, then you've got a problem and it's not your boss.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
