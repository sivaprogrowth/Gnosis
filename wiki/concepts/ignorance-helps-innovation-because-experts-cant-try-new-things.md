# Ignorance helps innovation because experts can't try new things

Brenner argues innovation comes from the young because their ignorance frees them to try new things, while knowing too much holds you back. He deliberately works in fields he knows nothing about.

> I strongly believe that the only way to encourage innovation is
> to give it to the young. The young have a great advantage in that
> they are ignorant. Because I think ignorance in science is very
> important. If you’re like me and you know too much you can’t try
> new things. I always work in fields of which I’m totally
> ignorant.
>
> — Sydney Brenner, A Conversation with Sydney Brenner
