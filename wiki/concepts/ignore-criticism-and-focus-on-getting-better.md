# Ignore criticism and focus on getting better

Worrying about what others say about you wastes time and energy. Put all your attention on improving at your work.

> All this hand wringing worry and concern over how are people
> viewing me — someone said something bad about me, and you get so
> upset about it — is wasted time and energy. Your only focus
> should be on getting better at what you're doing. Focus on what
> you are doing. Get better at what are you doing. Everything else
> is a waste of time.
>
> — [[jerry-seinfeld|Jerry Seinfeld]]
