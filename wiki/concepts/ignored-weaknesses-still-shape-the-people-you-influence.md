# Ignored weaknesses still shape the people you influence

You can choose not to address your weaknesses, but they will still affect you and those who depend on you. A leader's flaws get passed on to the people they lead, teach, or raise.

> You can ignore your weaknesses, but you can't expect them to not
> impact you. The weakness of the coach shapes the players. The
> weakness of the teacher influences the students. The weakness of
> the parent molds the child.
>
> — James Clear
