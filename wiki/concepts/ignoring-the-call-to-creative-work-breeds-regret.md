# Ignoring the call to creative work breeds regret

The most regretful people are those who felt a pull toward creative work but gave it neither energy nor time.

> The most regretful people on earth are those who felt the call
> to creative work, who felt their own creative power restive and
> uprising, and gave to it neither power nor time.
>
> — Mary Oliver
