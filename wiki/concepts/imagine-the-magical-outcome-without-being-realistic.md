# Imagine the magical outcome without being realistic

When envisioning a goal, picture the ideal outcome in detail and don't limit yourself with realism yet. Reality will intervene later, so don't be your own bottleneck at the vision stage.

> Imagine the outcome you want to create.
>
> Envision where you are headed in great detail. Don't talk
> yourself out of it. Don't encourage yourself to be realistic. You
> will have to wrestle with reality soon enough. Don't be your own
> bottleneck at this stage.
>
> What would the magical outcome be?
>
> — James Clear
