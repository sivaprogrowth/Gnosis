# Imagine your project failed: why did it fail?

Picture your most important goal six months from now as a failure, then ask what caused it. This premortem exposes risks before they happen.

> Imagine the most important goal or project you are working on
> right now. Fast forward six months. Imagine the project has
> failed.
>
> Why did you fail?
>
> — James Clear
