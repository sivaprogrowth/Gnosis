# Imitate the trees: let go in order to renew

Like trees shedding leaves, we can let go without grief and turn inward for renewal. Nothing stays the same for long, not even pain, so sit it out and let it pass.

> I think of the trees and how simply they let go, let fall the
> riches of a season, how without grief (it seems) they can let go
> and go deep into their roots for renewal and sleep...
>
> Imitate the trees. Learn to lose in order to recover, and
> remember that nothing stays the same for long, not even pain,
> psychic pain. Sit it out. Let it all pass. Let it go.
>
> — May Sarton, The Journals of May Sarton Volume One: Journal of a Solitude, Plant Dreaming Deep, and Recovering
