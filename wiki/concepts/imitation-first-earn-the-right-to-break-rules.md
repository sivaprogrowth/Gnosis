# Imitation first; earn the right to break rules

Mastery begins with imitation, and you only earn the right to break the rules after you've fully internalized them.

> Imitation is the first stage of mastery. You earn the right to
> break the rules only after you've internalized them.
>
> — Vlad Tenev, The Knowledge Project
