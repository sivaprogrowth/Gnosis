# Immediate, clear feedback makes people learn quickly

People learn fast when feedback arrives quickly and is concrete. When feedback is delayed, abstract, or hidden, they rarely learn at all.

> When feedback is immediate, clear, and concrete, people learn
> quickly.
>
> When feedback is delayed, abstract, and opaque, people rarely
> learn.
>
> — James Clear
