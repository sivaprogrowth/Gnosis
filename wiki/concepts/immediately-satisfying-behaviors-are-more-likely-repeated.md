# Immediately satisfying behaviors are more likely repeated

The Stockholm piano stairs got 66 percent more people to take the stairs because each step gave instant fun. The more immediately satisfying a habit is, the more likely it is to be repeated.

> This type of immediate feedback is a powerful factor in getting
> habits to stick. The more immediately satisfying a habit is, the
> more likely it will be repeated in the future.
>
> — James Clear

> The more instantaneous
> the reward, the more likely you are to learn that a behavior is
> worth repeating in the future.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
