# Impossible often means pain you won't endure

People who achieve extraordinary things are willing to endure failure and ridicule others won't, as SpaceX did. What looks impossible is often just a cost you are unwilling to pay.

> When you think something's impossible, consider this: people who
> achieve extraordinary things are willing to endure what others
> won't.
>
> Take SpaceX. In 2002, most experts said private companies
> couldn't build orbital rockets. Musk accepted years of failure
> and ridicule that others wouldn't.
>
> What you call impossible is often just pain you're unwilling to
> endure.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
