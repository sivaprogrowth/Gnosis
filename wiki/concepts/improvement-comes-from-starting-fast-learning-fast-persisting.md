# Improvement comes from starting fast, learning fast, persisting

Getting better depends on three things: beginning quickly, learning from mistakes quickly, and staying in the game long enough to keep trying.

> Three keys to improvement:
>
> * Do you start quickly?
> * Do you learn from your mistakes quickly?
> * Do you stay in the game and keep trying?
>
> — James Clear
