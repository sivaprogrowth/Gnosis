# Improvement often comes from eliminating mistakes

Getting better is often less about adding more right actions and more about removing wrong ones. Look for what to eliminate rather than what to add.

> In many cases, improvement is not about doing more things right,
> but about doing less things wrong.
>
> Don't look for things to add. Look for things to eliminate.
>
> — James Clear
