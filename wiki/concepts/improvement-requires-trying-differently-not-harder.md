# Improvement requires trying differently, not harder

Trying harder often fails to produce the outcome you want. If your current choices can't carry you to your desired future, you need to change trajectory.

> In many cases, the outcome you want will continue to elude
> you—even if you try harder.
>
> But it may be possible if you try differently.
>
> Can your current choices carry you to your desired future? If
> not, something has to change. You can’t get there from here. You
> have to get on a different trajectory.
>
> — James Clear

> If you want to find a better path, you have to be willing to
> explore a different path.
>
> That sounds simple, but how often do you try something
> different?
>
> — James Clear
