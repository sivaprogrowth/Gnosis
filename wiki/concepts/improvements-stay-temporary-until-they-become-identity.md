# Improvements stay temporary until they become identity

Changes stay temporary until they become part of who you are. Aim to become a reader, runner, or musician rather than just completing a single task.

> Improvements are only temporary until they become part of who
> you are.
>
> -The goal is not to read a book, the goal is to become a reader.
>
> -The goal is not to run a marathon, the goal is to become a
> runner.
>
> -The goal is not to learn an instrument, the goal is to become a
> musician.
>
> This year, focus on the identity you want to build.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> True behavior change is identity change. Anyone can convince
> themselves to visit the gym or eat healthy once or twice, but if
> you don’t shift the belief behind the behavior, then it is hard
> to stick with long-term changes. Improvements are only temporary
> until they become part of who you are. Every action you take is a
> vote for the type of person you wish to become.
>
> — James Clear
