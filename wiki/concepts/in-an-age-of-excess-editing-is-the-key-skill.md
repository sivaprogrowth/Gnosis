# In an age of excess, editing is the key skill

Because modern life offers more information, products, and options than ever, the ability to curate and filter matters more than ever. The best editors find the signal in the noise.

> Modern society is defined by an excess of opportunity. We have
> more information, more products, and more options than ever
> before.
>
> As a result, curating, filtering, and refining are more important
> skills than ever before. Those who edit best will find the signal
> in the noise.
>
> — James Clear
