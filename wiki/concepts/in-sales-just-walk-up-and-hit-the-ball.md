# In sales, just walk up and hit the ball

Sales can be overcomplicated to the point of impossibility; the better approach is to keep it simple and just do it.

> Sales is a lot like golf. You can make it so complicated as
> to be impossible or you can simply walk up and hit the ball. I've
> been leading and building sales orgs for almost 20 years and my
> advice is to walk up and hit the ball.
>
> — Colin Dowling, Hacker News
