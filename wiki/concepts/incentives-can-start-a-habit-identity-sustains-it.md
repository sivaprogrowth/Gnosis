# Incentives can start a habit; identity sustains it

External incentives help get a habit going, but over time intrinsic rewards kick in and the identity itself becomes the reinforcer. You keep doing it because it is who you are.

> Incentives can start a habit. Identity sustains a habit.
>
> — James Clear
