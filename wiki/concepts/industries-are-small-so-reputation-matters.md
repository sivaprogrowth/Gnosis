# Industries are small, so reputation matters

Even though the world is big, people in an industry eventually learn the quality of your work. Your reputation follows you.

> The world is big, but industries are small.
>
> After a few years, everyone comes to know the quality of your
> work.
>
> Reputation matters.
>
> — James Clear
