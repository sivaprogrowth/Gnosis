# Innovation requires increasing variation; efficiency reduces it

Manufacturing seeks to reduce variation for efficiency, while creativity and innovation depend on increasing variation. The two paradigms pull in opposite directions.

> A way to think about it is, innovation requires variation. And
> in manufacturing—six sigma, all that stuff—you're trying to
> reduce variation.
>
> The fundamental manufacturing paradigm is to reduce variation,
> and the fundamental creative, innovation paradigm is to increase
> it.
>
> — Reed Hastings, 3 secrets to Netflix's success
