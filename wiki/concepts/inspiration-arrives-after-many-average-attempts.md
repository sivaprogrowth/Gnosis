# Inspiration arrives after many average attempts

Excellent work rarely comes from waiting for a brilliant idea. Make many versions, around twenty-five, and one will be great once the average ideas are out of the way.

> Inspiration comes on the twenty-fifth attempt, not the first. If
> you want to make something excellent, don't wait for a brilliant
> idea to strike. Create twenty-five of what you need and one will
> be great. Inspiration reveals itself after you get the average
> ideas out of the way, not before you take the first step.
>
> — James Clear
