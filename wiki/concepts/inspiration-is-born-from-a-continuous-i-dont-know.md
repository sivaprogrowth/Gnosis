# Inspiration is born from a continuous I don't know

Inspiration is not just for artists; it visits anyone who chose their calling and works with love and imagination. It comes from ongoing curiosity, where every solved problem raises new questions.

> Inspiration is not the exclusive privilege of poets or artists.
> There is, there has been, there will always be a certain group of
> people whom inspiration visits. It's made up of all those who've
> consciously chosen their calling and do their job with love and
> imagination. It may include doctors, teachers, gardeners — I
> could list a hundred more professions. Their work becomes one
> continuous adventure as long as they manage to keep discovering
> new challenges in it. Difficulties and setbacks never quell their
> curiosity. A swarm of new questions emerges from every problem
> that they solve. Whatever inspiration is, it's born from a
> continuous I don't know.
>
> — Wislawa Szymborska, The Poet and the World
