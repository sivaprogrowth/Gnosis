# Instincts are usually right while specific ideas are wrong

Your underlying sense of what matters tends to be correct, but the particular ideas you come up with to act on it usually miss.

> "Our instincts are almost always right and our ideas are usually wrong."
>
> — Mark Pincus, The Knowledge Project
