# Intelligence includes not being your own bottleneck

Being smart is not only about knowledge but about not holding yourself back. Many capable people talk themselves out of trying instead of learning skills, building connections, or figuring things out as they go.

> Intelligence isn't just about what you know. It is also the
> ability to avoid being your own bottleneck.
>
> * If you lack the skills, be willing to look foolish while you
> learn them.
> * If you lack the connections, be courageous enough to reach out
> and build them.
> * If you feel uncertain, be bold enough to figure it out along
> the way.
>
> Many people have the ability, but they talk themselves out of
> trying.
>
> — James Clear
