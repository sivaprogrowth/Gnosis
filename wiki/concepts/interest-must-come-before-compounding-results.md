# Interest must come before compounding results

If you don't enjoy something you won't stick with it, and without sticking with it there is no compounding. Being interested is what makes results possible.

> A brief guide to compounding:
>
> If you don’t enjoy something, you won’t stick with it.
>
> If you don’t stick with it, it won’t compound.
>
> Being interested precedes the results.
>
> — James Clear
