# Invest in a future you will never see

Act for the long-term good, like planting sequoias, and value the work whose results others planted before you and that you will not live to harvest.

> Invest in the millennium. Plant sequoias. Say that your main crop is the forest that you did not plant, that you will not live to harvest.
>
> — Wendell Berry, Manifesto: The Mad Farmer Liberation Front
