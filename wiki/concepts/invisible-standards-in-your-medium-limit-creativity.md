# Invisible standards in your medium limit creativity

Approach work with as few accepted rules as possible, because the standards of a medium are so common they go unquestioned and trap thinking in the standard paradigm.

> "It’s a healthy practice to approach our work with as few
> accepted rules, starting points, and limitations as possible.
> Often the standards in our chosen medium are so ubiquitous, we
> take them for granted. They are invisible and unquestioned. This
> makes it nearly impossible to think outside the standard
> paradigm."
>
> — Rick Rubin, The Creative Act
