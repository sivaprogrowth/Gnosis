# Irrational dedication, not talent, separates people

What distinguishes people is an almost irrational commitment to pushing through pain that would break most others. Every convenience and service around us exists because someone refused to accept the world as it was.

> What truly separates people isn’t some magical talent, but an
> almost irrational commitment to pushing through pain that would
> break most people.
>
> ... Everything around you—every convenience you enjoy, every
> space you inhabit, every service you use—was one person’s refusal
> to accept the world as it was.
>
> The world progresses from a collection of irrational dedication.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Irrational Dedication
