# Is it hard, or does it require courage?

When unhappy about something, ask whether it is truly difficult to change or whether you simply lack the courage to change it.

> This thing that I am unhappy about... is it actually hard to
> change or is it simply hard to have the courage to change it?
>
> — James Clear

> Is the situation actually complicated or is it really quite
> straightforward, but you're making it complicated because it
> requires a lot of courage to make the straightforward decision?
>
> — James Clear
