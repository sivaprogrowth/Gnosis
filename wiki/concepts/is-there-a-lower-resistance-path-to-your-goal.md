# Is there a lower-resistance path to your goal?

Consider your ultimate desired outcome and ask whether there is an easier route to reach it with less friction.

> Think of the ultimate outcome you are hoping to achieve. Is there
> a path to accomplishing this where you would encounter less
> resistance?
>
> — James Clear
