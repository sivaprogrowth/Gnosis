# Is this pulling me in or am I pushing it?

Ask whether a project or relationship naturally draws you in or whether you are forcing it forward. The best outcomes often come from doubling down on what naturally pulls you in.

> Is this project pulling me in or am I pushing it forward? Is this
> relationship pulling me in or am I pushing it forward?
>
> Some of my best outcomes arise when I double down on the things
> that are naturally pulling me in.
>
> — James Clear
