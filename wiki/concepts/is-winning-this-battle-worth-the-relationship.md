# Is winning this battle worth the relationship?

The benefits of good relationships build over long time spans, so it is worth asking whether winning a particular conflict matters more than keeping the relationship.

> The benefits that arise from a good relationship—whether it be in
> marriage, friendship, business, or otherwise—are often produced
> over a long time span.
>
> Is it more important to win this battle or to maintain this
> relationship?
>
> — James Clear
