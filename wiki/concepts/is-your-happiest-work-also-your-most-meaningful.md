# Is your happiest work also your most meaningful?

Asks the reader to identify which work brings the most happiness and which brings the most meaning, and whether they are the same.

> What type of work brings you the most happiness?
>
> What type of work brings you the most meaning?
>
> Are they the same or different?
>
> — James Clear
