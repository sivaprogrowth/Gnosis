# Is your mind focused on past, present, or future?

Notice which timeframe your thoughts have been dwelling on lately, and consider how shifting to a different timeframe would change your thinking.

> Where has your mind been focused recently? Are you primarily
> thinking about your past, your present, or your future? How does
> focusing on a different timeframe change your thoughts?
>
> — James Clear
