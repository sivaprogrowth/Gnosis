# It is a great mistake not to seize opportunity

When offered his first job, Carnegie said he could start immediately, because delay might let the chance slip to someone else. Grabbing that opportunity gave him his real start in life.

> It is a great mistake not to
> seize the opportunity. The position was offered to me; something
> might occur, some other boy might be sent for. Having got myself
> in, I proposed to stay there if I could...
>
> — Andrew Carnegie, The Autobiography of Andrew Carnegie
