# Join a group where your desired behavior is normal

Habits last when you belong to a group in which the behavior you want is the normal thing everyone does. Social norms make the habit easier to sustain.

> The key—if you want to build habits that last—is to join a group
> where the desired behavior is the normal behavior.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> Your culture sets your expectation for what is “normal.” Surround yourself with people who have the habits you want to have yourself. You’ll rise together.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 9

> The best option is to join a culture where (1) your desired
> behavior is the normal behavior and (2) you already have
> something in common with the group.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
