# Judge ideas by truth, not by their source

Judge an idea by whether it is true, not by who said it.

> Accept the truth from whatever source it comes.
>
> — Moses Maimonides, Shemonah Peraqim

> Sinners often speak the truth. And saints have led people
> astray. Examine what is said, not the one who says it.
>
> — Anthony de Mello, Awakening
