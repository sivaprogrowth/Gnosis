# Judge opportunities by how you want to spend your days

When weighing a new project, first ask how you want to spend your days and avoid choices that conflict with that answer. Many exciting opportunities actually give you less of the life you want.

> When considering a new project or opportunity, one of the first
> questions to ask is, “How do I want to spend my days?”
>
> Make as few choices as possible that violate your answer.
>
> Many opportunities seem exciting but actually give you less of
> the life you want.
>
> — James Clear
