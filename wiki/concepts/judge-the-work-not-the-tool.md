# Judge the work, not the tool

Refusing a useful tool like AI doesn't improve your work; it only makes it slower and harder. What matters is the quality of the result.

> "I don't use Artificial Intelligence."
>
> A carpenter who refuses a hammer doesn't make the house any better. He has only made his work slower and harder.
>
> Judge the work, not the tool.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
