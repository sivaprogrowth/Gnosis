# Judgment is knowing what room to be in

Being in the right environment matters more than being the smartest person in it. Good judgment with average intelligence usually beats great intelligence with average judgment.

> It’s usually more important to be in the right room than to be
> the smartest person in the room.
>
> A person with great judgement and average intelligence will
> usually beat someone with great intelligence and average
> judgment.
>
> Judgment is knowing what room to be in.
>
> — James Clear
