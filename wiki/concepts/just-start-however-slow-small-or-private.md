# Just start, however slow, small, or private

Starting matters more than starting well. It is fine to begin slowly, at a small scale, or privately, as long as you begin.

> Just start.
>
> Start slow if you have to. Start small if you have to. Start
> privately if you have to.
>
> Just start.
>
> — James Clear
