# Keep daily actions small but ambitions big

Aim for small daily improvements of about 1% while keeping a big mindset about the larger game you can play. Start small but never dream small.

> Build small habits. Make big plans.
>
> 1) Keep your daily actions small. Strive to get 1% better every
> day.
>
> 2) Keep your daily mindset big. Think about how you can play a
> bigger game.
>
> Start small, but never dream small.
>
> — James Clear
