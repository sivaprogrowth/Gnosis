# Keep only what is useful or beautiful

A golden rule for life is to have nothing in your home that you don't know to be useful or believe to be beautiful.

> If you want a golden rule that will fit everything, this is it:
> Have nothing in your houses that you do not know to be useful or
> believe to be beautiful.
>
> — William Morris, Hopes and Fears for Art
