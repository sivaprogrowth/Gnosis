# Keep pursuing what you love and you can't go wrong

Don't give up on what you really want to do; when love and inspiration drive you, you are unlikely to go wrong.

> “Just don’t give up trying to do what you really want to do.
> Where there is love and inspiration, I don’t think you can go
> wrong.”
>
> — Ella Fitzgerald
