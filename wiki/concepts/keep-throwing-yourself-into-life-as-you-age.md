# Keep throwing yourself into life as you age

Rather than becoming protected and fearful with age, fully engage with parenting, relationships, and work, accepting both pain and joy as proof of being alive.

> “It’s the strangest thing: A lot of people as they get older get
> more protected and terrified. My desire is to keep throwing
> myself into things. My parenting, my relationship, my work. I’ll
> take the pain. I’ll take the joy. Because the feeling makes me
> go, I’m in life. It’s an enormous gift, this life.”
>
> — Nicole Kidman
