# Keep your plans quiet to succeed

Don't broadcast your investments or plans; keep them to yourself.

> Move in silence. Keep your positions private.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Outliers episode: The Witch of Wall Street

> “A Tiger wearing a bell will starve.”
>
> — Mongolian proverb
