# Keeping a no list makes saying no feel rewarding

Write down every distraction you ignore or opportunity you decline. Watching the list grow gives you a sense of accomplishment for staying focused on what matters.

> If you'd like to get better at saying no, try this:
>
> Keep a list of things you say no to. Whenever you ignore a
> distraction or turn down an opportunity, add it to the list. As
> the list grows, you begin to feel a sense of accomplishment for
> your ability to say no and remain focused on what matters.
>
> — James Clear
