# Keeping options open costs energy; commit to one yes

Every option you keep open takes energy to maintain, so many maybes can weigh more than a single commitment. Drop some options.

> The problem with keeping your options open is that every option
> requires energy to hold. And a shelf full of maybes is often
> heavier than a hand holding one yes. Put something down.
>
> — James Clear
