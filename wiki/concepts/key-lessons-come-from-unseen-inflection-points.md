# Key lessons come from unseen inflection points

People only see the final success or failure, not the turning points that could have gone either way. Those inflection points are where the most important lessons in business and life are learned.

> “Every entrepreneur knows the feeling: that moment of despair
> when the only thing you are aware of is the giant gap between
> where you find yourself and the life and business you imagine.
> Once you succeed, people see only the success. If you fail, they
> see only the failure. Rarely do they see the turning points that
> could have taken you in a completely different direction. But
> it’s at these inflection points that the most important lessons
> in business and life are learned.”
>
> — Stephen Schwarzman
