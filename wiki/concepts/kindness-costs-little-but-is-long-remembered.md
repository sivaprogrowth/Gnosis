# Kindness costs little but is long remembered

Being kind—praising good work, forgiving mistakes, listening to problems—costs almost nothing, yet the other person may remember it forever.

> The longer I live, the more I notice how valuable it is to do one
> simple thing: be kind.
>
> - When someone does a good job, tell them.
> - When someone makes a mistake, forgive them.
> - When someone tells you their problems, listen.
>
> Being kind barely costs a thing. You'll hardly remember you did
> it, but the other person may never forget that you did.
>
> — James Clear

> Compliment others more.
>
> You’ll barely remember you did it, but the other person may never
> forget that you did.
>
> Kindness has unlimited upside.
>
> — James Clear
