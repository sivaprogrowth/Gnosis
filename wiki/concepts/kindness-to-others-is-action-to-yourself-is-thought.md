# Kindness to others is action, to yourself is thought

Being kind to other people shows up mainly in what you do, while being kind to yourself shows up mainly in how you think about yourself.

> Being kind to others is mostly about your actions.
>
> Being kind to yourself is mostly about your thoughts.
>
> — James Clear
