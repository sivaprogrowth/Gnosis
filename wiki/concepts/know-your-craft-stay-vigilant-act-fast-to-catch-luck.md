# Know your craft, stay vigilant, act fast to catch luck

You can improve your luck by understanding your craft well enough to recognize good opportunities, staying alert so you notice them, and acting quickly to seize them.

> 3 things that help luck:
>
> 1. Deconstructing your craft, so you know what good opportunities
> look like. 2. Remaining vigilant, so you notice when lucky breaks
> come your way. 3. Acting quickly, so you are more likely to seize
> luck when it arrives.
>
> — James Clear
