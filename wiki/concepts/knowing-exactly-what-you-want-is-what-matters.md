# Knowing exactly what you want is what matters

Many people don't know what they want. Martin believed that knowing exactly what you want is the only thing you really need to know.

> There are so many people who don’t know what they want. And I
> think that, in this world, that’s the only thing you have to know
> — exactly what you want.
>
> — Agnes Martin, Interview with Agnes Martin
