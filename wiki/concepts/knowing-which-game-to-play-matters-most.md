# Knowing which game to play matters most

In sports, advantage comes from choosing how to play. In life, a major advantage comes from choosing which game to play at all.

> In sports, one of the primary sources of advantage is choosing
> how to play the game.
>
> In life, one of the primary sources of advantage is choosing
> which game to play.
>
> — James Clear

> “In life
> the challenge is not so much to figure out how best to play the
> game; the challenge is to figure out what game you’re playing.”
>
> — Kwame Anthony Appiah
