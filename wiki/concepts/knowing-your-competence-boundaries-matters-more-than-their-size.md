# Knowing your competence boundaries matters more than their size

You win more by playing where you have an advantage, which requires knowing the limits of your expertise. Operating inside your circle brings effectiveness, while venturing outside without awareness invites trouble, though learning new domains is still valuable.

> The size of your circle isn’t as important as knowing the
> boundaries. The wise person is the one who knows the limits of
> their knowledge, who can say with confidence, “This falls within
> my circle,” or “This is outside my area of expertise.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v1: General Thinking Tools
