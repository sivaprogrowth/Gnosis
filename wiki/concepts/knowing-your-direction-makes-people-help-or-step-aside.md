# Knowing your direction makes people help or step aside

When you are clear about where you want to go, others tend either to assist you or to stay out of your way. Both responses benefit you.

> If you know where you want to go in life, people tend to help or
> get out of the way.
>
> Both of those are useful.
>
> — James Clear
