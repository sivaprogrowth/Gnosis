# Knowing yourself is true wisdom

Observing the world outside you builds intelligence. Examining yourself is what builds wisdom.

> “One who looks around him is intelligent; one who looks within
> him is wise.”
>
> — Matshona Dhliwayo

> “Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true
> wisdom.”
>
> — Lao Tzu
