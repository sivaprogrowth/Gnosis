# Knowledge from tinkering beats knowledge from reasoning

Knowledge earned through trial and error, experience, and time is far superior to knowledge derived from reasoning alone.

> “The knowledge we get by tinkering, via trial and error,
> experience, and the workings of time, in other words, contact
> with the earth, is vastly superior to that obtained through
> reasoning, something self-serving institutions have been very
> busy hiding from us.”
>
> — Nassim Taleb
