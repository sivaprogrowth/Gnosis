# Knowledge is about the past, decisions about the future

Everything you know comes from the past, but every decision you make is about the future. No sophistication removes that uncertainty, so you have to act anyway.

> No amount of sophistication is going to allay the fact that all
> your knowledge is about the past and all your decisions are about
> the future.
>
> — Ian Wilson, From Scenario Thinking to Strategic Action (as read in The Illusion of Knowledge)
