# Knowledge that raises no new questions dies

Knowledge stays alive only when it keeps generating new questions. Without them it loses its vitality and fades away.

> "Any knowledge that doesn't lead to new questions quickly dies
> out: it fails to maintain the temperature required for sustaining
> life."
>
> — Wisława Szymborska, The Poet and the World
