# Labor passes but good endures; pleasure passes but shame endures

The effort behind good work fades quickly while its benefits last, whereas shameful pleasure fades quickly while its shame lasts.

> If you accomplish something good with hard work, the labor
> passes quickly, but the good endures; if you do something
> shameful in pursuit of pleasure, the pleasure passes quickly, but
> the shame endures.
>
> — Musonius Rufus, Lectures and Fragments, Fragment 51
