# Lack of confidence kills more dreams than lack of ability

People usually talk themselves out of full effort long before talent limits them. You are capable of more than you think, so don't be your own bottleneck.

> Lack of confidence kills more dreams than lack of ability.
>
> Talent matters—especially at elite levels—but people talk
> themselves out of giving their best effort long before talent
> becomes the limiting factor.
>
> You're capable of more than you know. Don't be your own
> bottleneck.
>
> — James Clear
