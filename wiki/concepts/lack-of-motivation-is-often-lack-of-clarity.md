# Lack of motivation is often lack of clarity

People often believe they lack motivation when what they really lack is a clear plan or understanding of what to do.

> Most people think they lack motivation when they really lack clarity.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> Many people think they lack motivation when what they really lack
> is clarity. It is not always obvious when and where to take
> action.
>
> — James Clear
