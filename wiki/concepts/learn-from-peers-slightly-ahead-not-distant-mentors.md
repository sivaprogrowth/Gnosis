# Learn from peers slightly ahead, not distant mentors

People a couple of years ahead of you usually offer more useful lessons than mentors decades ahead. Life changes and most insights go out of date.

> It's almost always better to learn from peers who are 2 years
> ahead of you than mentors who are 20 years ahead of you.
>
> Life evolves and most insights get outdated.
>
> — James Clear
