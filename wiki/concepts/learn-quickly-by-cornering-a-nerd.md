# Learn quickly by cornering a nerd

A fast way to learn a topic is to find someone who is obsessed with it and ask them every question you can think of.

> One of the best strategies for learning something quickly:
> corner a nerd.
>
> Find someone who totally geeks out about that topic and ask them
> every question you can think of.
>
> — James Clear
