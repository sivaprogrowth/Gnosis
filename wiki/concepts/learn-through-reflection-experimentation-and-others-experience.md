# Learn through reflection, experimentation, and others' experience

You can learn something new by reflecting on your own past attempts, trying something new, or reading about what others have tried.

> Three ways to learn something new:
>
> * Reflect on what you have already tried.
> * Attempt something you have not tried.
> * Read about what someone else has tried.
>
> — James Clear
