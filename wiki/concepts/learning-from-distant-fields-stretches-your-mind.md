# Learning from distant fields stretches your mind

Fields very different from your own have ways of thinking that can prove useful unexpectedly. Learning to think like an anthropologist, psychologist, and philosopher stretches the mind.

> "Learn from fields very different from your own. They each have
> ways of thinking that can be useful at surprising times. Just
> learning to think like an anthropologist, a psychologist, and a
> philosopher will beneficially stretch your mind."
>
> — David Chapman, How to Think Real Good
