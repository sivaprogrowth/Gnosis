# Learning machines rise further than the smartest people

People who keep getting a little wiser every day often outpace those who are smarter or more diligent, especially over a long time.

> “I constantly see people rise in life who are not the smartest,
> sometimes not even the most diligent, but they are learning
> machines. They go to bed every night a little wiser than they
> were when they got up and boy does that help, particularly when
> you have a long run ahead of you.”
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]
