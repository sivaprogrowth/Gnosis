# Learning matters most after you think you know it all

Learning is not only for the young; keep learning through middle age and beyond, from both failures and successes. When trouble comes, ask what it is trying to teach you.

> "There's a myth that learning is for young people. But as the
> proverb says, 'It's what you learn after you know it all that
> counts.' The middle years are great, great learning years. Even
> the years past the middle years. I took on a new job after my
> 77th birthday – and I'm still learning. Learn all your life.
> Learn from your failures. Learn from your successes, When you hit
> a spell of trouble, ask 'What is it trying to teach me?' The
> lessons aren't always happy ones, but they keep coming.”
>
> — John Gardner, Personal Renewal
