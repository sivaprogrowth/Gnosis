# Learning to ask good questions means learning how to learn

Once you can ask relevant, appropriate, substantial questions, you know how to learn. Then nobody can stop you from learning whatever you want or need.

> Once you have learned to ask questions – relevant and
> appropriate and substantial questions – you have learned how to
> learn and no one can keep you from learning whatever you want or
> need to know.
>
> — Neil Postman, Teaching as a Subversive Activity
