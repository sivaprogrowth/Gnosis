# Learning to think means choosing your attention

Learning how to think really means gaining control over what you pay attention to and how you construct meaning from experience; without that choice, adult life goes badly.

> Learning how to think really means learning how to exercise
> some control over how and what you think. It means being
> conscious and aware enough to choose what you pay attention to
> and to choose how you construct meaning from experience. Because
> if you cannot exercise this kind of choice in adult life, you
> will be totally hosed.
>
> — David Foster Wallace, This is Water
