# Less friction makes a habit more likely

The easier and more convenient a habit is to perform, the higher the odds you follow through on it.

> The less friction
> associated with a habit, the more likely it is to occur. In other
> words, as convenience increases, so do the odds that you follow
> through on your habit.
>
> — James Clear

> The 3rd Law of Behavior Change is to make it easy. One way to make a habit easy is to reduce the amount of friction associated with the behavior. The habits with the least amount of friction are generally the ones that are most likely to occur.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
