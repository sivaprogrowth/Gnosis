# Lessons, behaviors, and love stick only through repetition

Whether it is a lesson, a habit, or love, it is unlikely to last unless it is repeated. Practice is what makes things solid.

> Lessons are unlikely to stick unless they are repeated.
>
> Behaviors are unlikely to stick unless they are repeated.
>
> Love is unlikely to stick unless it is repeated.
>
> The practice solidifies.
>
> — James Clear
