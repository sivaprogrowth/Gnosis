# Let go of people who leave you almost loved

You deserve full love, not a diminished version of it. Have the courage to let go of relationships that confuse you and to stop waiting for others to return the kindness and respect you give.

> You deserve to be loved and chosen —
> not almost loved, or almost chosen. ... Have the courage to let
> go of the people who leave your heart confused.
>
> — Bianca Sparacino, A Gentle Reminder
