# Let the high score settle family disagreements

When kids can't agree on takeout, Parrish lets whoever has the high score choose, turning a dispute into a simple, fair rule.

> When the kids can't agree on what takeout we should have for
> dinner, I tell them the high score gets to pick.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
