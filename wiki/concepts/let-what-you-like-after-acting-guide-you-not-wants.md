# Let what you like after acting guide you, not wants

Wanting is the desire before doing something, while liking is the satisfaction after. You may not want to exercise, write, or wake early, but you like how it feels afterward, so let those likes guide your behavior.

> Focus on your likes, not your wants.
>
> * You may not want to exercise, but you like how it makes you
> feel.
> * You may not want to write, but you like the feeling of
> accomplishment.
> * You may not want to wake up early, but you like the calm beauty
> of morning.
>
> Wanting is the desire you feel before doing something. Liking is
> the satisfaction you feel after doing something. Let your likes
> guide you.
>
> — James Clear
