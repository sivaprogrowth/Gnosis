# Leverage comes from value, alternatives, and likability

In negotiation you gain leverage by creating value, being able to walk away, being irreplaceable, having a second option, knowing the numbers, and being liked. Because negotiations repeat, being enjoyable to work with sets you up for the next one.

> Negotiation Tips:
>
> -You want leverage.
> -The person who creates more value has leverage.
> -The person who can walk away has leverage.
> -The person who is irreplaceable has leverage.
> -The person who has a second option has leverage.
> -The person who knows the numbers has leverage.
> -The person who everyone likes and wants to work with has
> leverage.
> -The person who asks for more, gets more.
>
> And finally, most negotiations are not one-time affairs. The
> person others continue to like at the end of this negotiation is
> in a better position for the next one. Be enjoyable to work with.
>
> — James Clear
