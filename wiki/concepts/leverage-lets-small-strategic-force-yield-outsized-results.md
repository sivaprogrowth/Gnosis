# Leverage lets small strategic force yield outsized results

Leverage is amplification: a small force applied strategically produces large outputs, and it sits behind many nonlinear outcomes like bestselling books or big investment decisions.

> Leverage is the force multiplier of the world, the principle that
> allows the small to move the large and the few to influence the
> many. It’s the idea that a little force, strategically applied,
> can yield outsize outputs.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
