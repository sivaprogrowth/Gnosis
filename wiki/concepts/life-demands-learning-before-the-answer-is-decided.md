# Life demands learning before the answer is decided

In school you study problems whose answers are already known. In life you must learn and act while the outcome is still unfolding.

> "School requires you to learn about things after the answer has
> already been decided.
>
> Life requires you to learn about things while the answer is in
> the process of being decided."
>
> — James Clear
