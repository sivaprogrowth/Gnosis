# Life improves when you stop blaming others

Your life gets better right away when you stop blaming other people and put your attention on the things within your control.

> Life instantly improves when you don’t blame other people and focus on what you can control.
>
> — James Clear

> “The best years of your life are the ones in which you decide your problems are your own. You do not blame them on your mother, the ecology, or the President.”
>
> — Albert Ellis
