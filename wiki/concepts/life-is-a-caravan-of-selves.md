# Life is a caravan of selves

Living things change beyond recognition as they grow, so people are constant reinventors. A life contains many successive selves.

> "Living things tend to change unrecognizably as they grow. Who
> would deduce the dragonfly from the larva, the iris from the bud,
> the lawyer from the infant?
>
> Flora or fauna, we are all shapeshifters and magical reinventors.
> Life is really a plural noun, a caravan of selves."
>
> — Diane Ackerman, Cultivating Delight
