# Life is a daily miracle if you notice it

Countless small miracles happen around us every moment, like a flower blooming or a raindrop falling. If you learn to live attentively, everyday life becomes magical.

> "Every moment there are a million miracles happening around you:
> a flower blossoming, a bird tweeting, a bee humming, a raindrop
> falling, a snowflake wafting along the clear evening air. There
> is magic everywhere. If you learn how to live it, life is nothing
> short of a daily miracle."
>
> — Sadhguru, Inner Engineering: A Yogi's Guide to Joy
