# Life is not a dress rehearsal, make it count

Time is limited and passes quickly, so do what makes you happy and fulfilled. Avoid doing things just because others want you to and cut out negative relationships.

> “Life is not a dress rehearsal—this is probably it. Make it
> count.
>
> "Time is extremely limited and goes by fast. Do what makes you
> happy and fulfilled—few people get remembered hundreds of years
> after they die anyway. Don’t do stuff that doesn’t make you happy
> (this happens most often when other people want you to do
> something). Don’t spend time trying to maintain relationships
> with people you don’t like, and cut negative people out of your
> life…
>
> "The days are long but the decades are short."
>
> — [[sam-altman|Sam Altman]], The Days are Long but the Decades are Short
