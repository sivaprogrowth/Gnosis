# Life is not a race; you are here

There is no ahead or behind in life. Focus on where you are, enjoy it, and make the most of it.

> It's not a race.
>
> You are not ahead. You are not behind. You are here.
>
> Enjoy it and make the most of it.
>
> — James Clear
