# Life is often playing a poor hand well

You won't always get good circumstances. Much of life is about doing the best with a bad situation.

> Life is not always a matter of holding good cards, but
> sometimes, playing a poor hand well.
>
> — Jack London, Sacred Journey of the Peaceful Warrior by Dan Millman
