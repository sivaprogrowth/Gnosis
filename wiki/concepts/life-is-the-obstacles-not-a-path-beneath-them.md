# Life is the obstacles, not a path beneath them

Resenting obstacles assumes there is a smooth path underneath them. In fact the obstacles are life itself.

> I used to resent obstacles along the path, thinking, 'If only
> that hadn't happened life would be so good.' Then I suddenly
> realized, life is the obstacles. There is no underlying path.
>
> — Janna Levin, Tribe of Mentors
