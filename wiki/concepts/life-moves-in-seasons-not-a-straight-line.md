# Life moves in seasons, not a straight line

Life is not a linear rise and decline but a meandering path with seasons of flourishing and seasons of loss, and what falls away can grow again.

> We are in the habit of imagining our lives to be linear, a long
> march from birth to death in which we mass our powers, only to
> surrender them again, all the while slowly losing our youthful
> beauty. This is a brutal untruth. Life meanders like a path
> through the woods. We have seasons when we flourish and seasons
> when the leaves fall from us, revealing our bare bones. Given
> time, they grow again.
>
> — Katherine May, Wintering
