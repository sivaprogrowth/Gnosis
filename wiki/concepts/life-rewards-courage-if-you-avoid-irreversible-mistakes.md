# Life rewards courage if you avoid irreversible mistakes

People brave enough to bet on something or someone put themselves in a position to win. This holds as long as they avoid mistakes they can't recover from, like running out of cash or going to jail.

> Life rewards courage.
>
> The person who is brave enough to bet on something or someone—as
> long as they avoid irreversible mistakes like running out of cash
> or going to jail—is in a position to win.
>
> — James Clear
