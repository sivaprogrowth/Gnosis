# Life's purpose is to be useful, not happy

Rosten argues that the point of life is not happiness but being useful, honorable, and compassionate, so that your having lived makes a difference.

> “The purpose of life is not to be happy at all. It is to be useful, to be honorable. It is to be compassionate. It is to matter, to have it make some difference that you lived."
>
> — Leo Rosten
