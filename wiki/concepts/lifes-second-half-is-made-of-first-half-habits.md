# Life's second half is made of first-half habits

The habits you accumulate early in life largely make up how you live later on.

> It seems, in fact, as though the second half of a person's life
> is made up of nothing but the habits they accumulated during the
> first half.
>
> — Fyodor Dostoyevsky, Demons
