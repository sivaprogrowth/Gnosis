# Linger with loved ones because time together is short

The hours we get with the people we love are few, so we should stretch our moments together and find reasons to come back.

> The number of hours we have together is actually not so large. Please linger near the door uncomfortably instead of just leaving. Please forget your scarf in my life and come back later for it.
>
> — Mikko Harvey, For M
