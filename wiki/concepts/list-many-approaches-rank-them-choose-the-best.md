# List many approaches, rank them, choose the best

There is always more than one way to reach an outcome. Write down as many options as you can, rank them by time, cost, and expected effectiveness, then pick the best one.

> There is always more than one way to do something. Write down as
> many ways as you can think of to achieve your desired outcome.
> Then, rank them based on how much time they take, how much money
> they cost, and how effective you expect them to be. Choose the
> best.
>
> — James Clear
