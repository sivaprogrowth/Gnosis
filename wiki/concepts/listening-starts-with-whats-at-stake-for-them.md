# Listening starts with what's at stake for them

The most critical part of listening is understanding what matters to the other person, not what you think matters.

> The most critical part of listening is asking what is at stake
> for the other person. I try to understand what matters to them,
> not what I think matters.
>
> — Valarie Kaur, See No Stranger
