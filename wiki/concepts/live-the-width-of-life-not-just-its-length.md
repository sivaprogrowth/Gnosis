# Live the width of life, not just its length

A full life is measured by its depth and richness, not only by how many years it lasts.

> I don't want to get to the end of my life and find that I just
> lived the length of it. I want to live the width of it as well.
>
> — Diane Ackerman, Meditations for Women Who Do Too Much
