# Living is cheap but showing off is expensive

The basic costs of life are modest; spending to display status is what makes life costly.

> “Living doesn’t cost much, but showing off does.”
>
> — Jeffrey D. Sachs, The Price of Civilization: Reawakening American Virtue and Prosperity
