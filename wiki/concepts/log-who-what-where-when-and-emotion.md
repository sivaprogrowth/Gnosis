# Log who, what, where, when, and emotion

To uncover what triggers a bad habit, write down five things each time you do it: who you're with, what you're doing, where you are, when it is, and what emotions are driving you. Within a week, patterns emerge.

> * Who am I with?
> * What am I doing right now?
> * Where am I?
> * When is it?
> * What emotions are driving my actions?
>
> — James Clear
