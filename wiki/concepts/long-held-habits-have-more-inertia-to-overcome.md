# Long-held habits have more inertia to overcome

Like mass in physics, the longer we've held habits and beliefs, the more force it takes to change them. Starting with tiny steps reduces the force needed to overcome the status quo.

> But inertia isn’t just a physical phenomenon. It’s an illuminating lens to see habits, beliefs, and our resistance to change. The longer we’ve held them, the larger the mass and the more force required to change them. The path of least resistance is always the status quo.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry and Biology
