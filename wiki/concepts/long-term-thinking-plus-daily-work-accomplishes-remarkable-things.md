# Long-term thinking plus daily work accomplishes remarkable things

Don't shrink your vision. Thinking longer term than most people while working hard each day can achieve a remarkable amount.

> No sense in thinking small. Don't water down your vision. A remarkable amount can be accomplished if you are willing to think longer term than most and work hard each day.
>
> — James Clear
