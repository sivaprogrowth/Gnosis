# Long-term thinking turns spending time into investing it

Focusing only on what you want today leads you to consume your time, while focusing on what you want years from now leads you to use time in ways that pay off later.

> Think about what you want today and you'll spend your time.
>
> Think about what you want in 5 years and you'll invest your
> time.
>
> — James Clear
