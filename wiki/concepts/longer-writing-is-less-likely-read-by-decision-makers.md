# Longer writing is less likely read by decision-makers

The longer your writing, the less likely the people with power to act will actually read it, so keep it short.

> “The longer your memos, the less likely they are to be read by
> men who have the power to act on them.”
>
> — David Ogilvy

> The longer your email, the less likely it is to be read by someone who can act on it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
