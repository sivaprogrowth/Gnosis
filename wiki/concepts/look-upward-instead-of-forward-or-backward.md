# Look upward instead of forward or backward

Worry less about the past or the future and focus on what inspires and elevates you.

> I avoid looking forward or backward, and try to keep looking
> upward.
>
> — Charlotte Bronte, Letter to her friend Ellen Nussey (January 15, 1849)
