# Looking foolish short term enables looking genius long term

Being willing to appear foolish in the short run is required to achieve results that look brilliant in the long run.

> If you're not willing to look like an idiot in the short term,
> you will never look like a genius in the long term.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
