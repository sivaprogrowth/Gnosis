# Looking for things to appreciate creates joy

If you made a habit of searching for things to appreciate, your life would feel wonderful. Gratitude shapes how good life feels.

> “If all you did was just looked for things to appreciate, you
> would live a joyously spectacular life.”
>
> — Esther Hicks, Abraham-Hicks workshop in San Antonio, TX, on January 26, 2002
