# Losing a crutch frightens more than affliction relief pleases

We want to be freed from our problems, but giving up the coping habits that support us is terrifying, which makes change hard.

> Nothing is more desirable than to be released from an
> affliction, but nothing is more frightening than to be divested
> of a crutch.
>
> — James Baldwin, Nobody Knows My Name
