# Love, energy, and cheerfulness make success nearly inevitable

Bringing enthusiasm and good spirit to your work is a real asset that strongly drives success.

> She brought so much love, energy, and cheerfulness to the work that she could not but succeed.
>
> — Louisa May Alcott, Little Women
