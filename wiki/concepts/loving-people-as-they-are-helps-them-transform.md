# Loving people as they are helps them transform

Accepting someone exactly as they are lets them feel seen and appreciated, which empowers them to become their truest self.

> Go and love someone exactly as they are. And then watch how
> quickly they transform into the greatest, truest version of
> themselves. When one feels seen and appreciated in their own
> essence, one is instantly empowered.
>
> — Wes Angelozzi, Instagram
