# Loving someone means helping them be more themselves

Real love supports a person in becoming who they truly are, which may differ from who you want them to be.

> Loving someone means helping them to be more themselves, which
> can be different from being what you'd like them to be, although
> often they turn out the same.
>
> — Merle Shain, Some Men are More Perfect Than Others
