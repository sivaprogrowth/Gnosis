# Loving the process brings happiness now

If you fall in love with the process instead of the outcome, you don't need to wait for results to feel happy. You can feel satisfied whenever your system is running.

> When you fall in love with the process rather than the product,
> you don’t have to wait to give yourself permission to be happy.
> You can be satisfied anytime your system is running.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> When you fall in love with the process rather than the outcome,
> you don't have to wait to be happy.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
