# Low aim, not failure, is the real crime

Failing is nothing to fear; aiming too low is the true mistake. Failing while attempting something great is itself honorable.

> Don't fear failure. Not failure, but low aim, is the crime. In
> great attempts it is glorious even to fail.
>
> — Bruce Lee, Striking Thoughts
