# Low leverage keeps options open in hard times

Gayner avoids situations where limited options force decisions, which in practice means keeping leverage low so he always has hole cards to play.

> So what that
> means is low leverage, and what that means is not being at a
> point where you’re faced with a very difficult decision and you
> really don’t have many options or hole cards to go to.
>
> — Thomas Gayner, The Knowledge Project Podcast
