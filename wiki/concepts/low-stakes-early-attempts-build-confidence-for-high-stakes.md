# Low-stakes early attempts build confidence for high stakes

First attempts often seem unimportant or foolish and are easy to dismiss. But doing low-stakes work prepares you for high-stakes moments and builds confidence.

> Early attempts are easy to dismiss because they don't seem to
> amount to much. But you have to do the low stakes stuff to
> prepare for the high stakes stuff. They are the building blocks
> of confidence, and that's an enormous thing.
>
> — James Clear
