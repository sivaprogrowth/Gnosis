# Loyal friendship delights the mind more than anything

True friends are a blessing because you can safely share secrets with them and they soothe distress, advise you, and lift your sorrow.

> "...nothing delights the mind so much as fond and loyal
> friendship. What a blessing it is to have hearts that are ready
> and willing to receive all your secrets in safety, with whom you
> are less afraid to share knowledge of something than keep it to
> yourself, whose conversation soothes your distress, whose advice
> helps you make up your mind, whose cheerfulness dissolves your
> sorrow, whose very appearance cheers you up!"
>
> — Seneca, On the Shortness of Life
