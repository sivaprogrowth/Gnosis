# Luck is the delayed byproduct of good positioning

What looks like luck often isn't random. It is the delayed result of repeatedly putting yourself in good positions.

> What we call luck is often not purely random, but rather the delayed byproduct of consistently putting yourself in good positions.
>
> — James Clear
