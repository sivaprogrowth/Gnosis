# Luck is where preparation meets opportunity

Opportunities pass everyone constantly. Seeing and seizing them requires attention, risk-taking, exposure to failure, and enough belief to act anyway.

> “Luck is the intersection of preparation and opportunity.
> Opportunities parade past all of us all the time. The key is that
> you must be paying attention to see them, you must be willing to
> take risks, you must expose yourself to the possibility of
> massive failure and you must believe in what you are doing so
> much that you do it anyway."
>
> — Richard Garriott, Explore/Create
