# Major life changes often get harder before improving

Big transitions like moving, a new job, or having kids often make life harder for the first 100 days. Early struggle doesn't mean the choice was bad.

> Major life changes—moving to a new city, starting a new job,
> ending a relationship, getting married, having kids, etc.—will
> often make life harder for the first 100 days before improving.
>
> This is not always true, but it’s a nice reminder that
> experiencing early struggle doesn’t mean it was a bad choice.
>
> — James Clear
