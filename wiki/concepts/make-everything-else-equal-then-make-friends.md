# Make everything else equal, then make friends

When offerings are equal, people buy from friends, so match competitors on everything else and then build many relationships.

> All things being equal, people
> buy from their friends. So make everything else equal, then go
> make a lot of friends.
>
> — Colin Dowling, Hacker News
