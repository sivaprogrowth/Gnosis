# Make good habits the path of least resistance

Design your environment so that doing the right thing is the easiest option available.

> The central idea is to create an environment where doing the
> right thing is as easy as possible. You want to make your good
> habits the path of least resistance.
>
> — James Clear

> The less friction associated with a habit, the more likely it
> is to occur. Create an environment where doing the right thing is
> as easy as possible. You want to make your good habits the path
> of least resistance.
>
> — James Clear

> Spend less time trying to find motivation and more time making good habits the path of least resistance.
>
> — James Clear
