# Make habits easy enough to do unmotivated

Motivation fluctuates, so consistent behavior shouldn't depend on it. Set the difficulty low enough that you'll do it even on unmotivated days.

> "Motivation comes and goes. If you want to do something
> consistently, then don't pick a level of difficulty that requires
> great motivation.
>
> Make it easy enough and simple enough that you'll do it even when
> you don't feel very motivated."
>
> — James Clear
