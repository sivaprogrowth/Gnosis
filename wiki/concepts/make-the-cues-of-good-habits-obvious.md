# Make the cues of good habits obvious

The 1st Law of Behavior Change is to make it obvious. Making the cues of good habits obvious increases the chance you'll remember to do them.

> The 1st Law of Behavior Change is to make it obvious. In the
> book, I cover many ways to make the cues of your good habits
> obvious, which increases the likelihood that you'll remember to
> do them.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
