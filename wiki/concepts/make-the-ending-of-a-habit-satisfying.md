# Make the ending of a habit satisfying

We remember the ending of an experience more than other phases, so a habit should end with something satisfying, like a favorite show after a hard run.

> The ending of any
> experience is vital because we tend to remember it more than
> other phases. You want the ending of your habit to be satisfying
>
> — James Clear
