# Make the habit cue prominent in your environment

For a habit to become a big part of your life, its cue needs to be prominent in your surroundings. Arrange things so the best choice is the most obvious one.

> If you want to
> make a habit a big part of your life, you need to make the cue a
> big part of your environment. Make sure the best choice is the
> most obvious one.
>
> — James Clear

> If you want to make a habit a big part of your life, you need
> to make the cue a big part of your environment.
>
> — James Clear

> If you want to make a habit a big part of your life, make the cue
> a big part of your environment. By sprinkling triggers throughout
> your surroundings, you increase the odds that you’ll think about
> your habit throughout the day.
>
> — James Clear
