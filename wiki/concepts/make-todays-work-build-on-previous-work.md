# Make today's work build on previous work

Look for ways your daily work can accumulate and stack on earlier work so your efforts compound over time.

> How can the work you're doing today accumulate and layer on top
> of what you did yesterday? Find ways to compound your efforts.
>
> — James Clear

> How does this action feed the next? How does this project make
> the next project easier?
>
> — James Clear
