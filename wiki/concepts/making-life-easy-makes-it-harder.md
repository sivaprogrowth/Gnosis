# Making life easy makes it harder

Avoiding hard things like exercise, difficult conversations, or skill-building creates bigger difficulties later. Choosing the easy path has a cost.

> Strangely, life gets harder when you try to make it easy.
>
> Exercising might be hard, but never moving makes life harder.
> Uncomfortable conversations are hard, but avoiding every conflict
> is harder. Mastering your craft is hard, but having no skills is
> harder.
>
> Easy has a cost.
>
> — James Clear
