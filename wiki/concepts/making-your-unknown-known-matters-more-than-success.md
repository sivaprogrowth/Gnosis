# Making your unknown known matters more than success

Whether you succeed or fail is beside the point. What counts is bringing what is inside you out into the world.

> Whether you succeed or not is irrelevant — there is no such thing. Making your unknown known is the important thing.
>
> — Georgia O'Keeffe, Georgia O'Keeffe: Art and Letters
