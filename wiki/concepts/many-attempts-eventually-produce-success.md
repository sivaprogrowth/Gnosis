# Many attempts eventually produce success

Breakthroughs like book deals and media coverage came only after hundreds of attempts. Not everything works, so keep producing and trying.

> "It took me...200+ articles before I got a book deal.250+
> articles before I got major media coverage.100+ interviews before
> my book hit the bestseller list.You need a lot of shots on goal.
> Not everything will work, but some of it will. Keep shooting."
>
> — James Clear

> For who is there who, shooting all day, will not sometimes hit
> the mark?
>
> — Marcus Tullius Cicero, De Oratore
