# Many beliefs are residue of others' thinking

Much of what we believe was absorbed from other people rather than chosen. Pausing to question whether things must be this way, and whether you actually want them, helps you think for yourself.

> So much of what we believe is the residue of someone else's
> thinking.
>
> Pause and question things for a moment. Is this really how it has
> to be? Is this really what you want?
>
> — James Clear
