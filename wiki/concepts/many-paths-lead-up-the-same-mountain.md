# Many paths lead up the same mountain

There are many valid ways to reach the same goal. The only person wasting time is the one who circles around telling others their path is wrong.

> There are hundreds of paths up the mountain, all leading in the
> same direction, so it doesn't matter which path you take. The
> only one wasting time is the one who runs around and around the
> mountain, telling everyone that his or her path is wrong.
>
> — Hindu proverb
