# Many problems are really unmet basic needs

What feels like a big life problem is often just a need for a glass of water, a short walk outside, or a good night of sleep. These simple basics ease much daily stress and anxiety.

> It’s crazy how often I feel like I need to resolve some big
> problem in my life when all I really need is one of the
> following:
>
> 1. A glass of water
> 2. A 10-minute walk outside
> 3. A good night of sleep
>
> Three simple things that resolve many of the stresses and
> anxieties of daily life.
>
> — James Clear

> Are things truly not going well ... or do I just need some food,
> water, and a short break?
>
> — James Clear
