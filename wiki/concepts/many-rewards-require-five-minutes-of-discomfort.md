# Many rewards require five minutes of discomfort

Activities like exercising, conversing, and writing get easier once started. Many of life's rewards go to those willing to tolerate a little discomfort at the beginning.

> Are you willing to be uncomfortable for 5 minutes?
>
> Exercising is easier once you've started the workout.
>
> Conversation is easier once you're already talking.
>
> Writing is easier once you're in the middle of it.
>
> But many rewards in life will elude you if you're not willing to
> be a little uncomfortable at first.
>
> — James Clear
