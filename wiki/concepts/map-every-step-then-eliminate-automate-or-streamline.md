# Map every step, then eliminate, automate, or streamline

To reduce friction, write down every small action in a process. Then find steps you can remove, automate, or simplify, as ride-sharing apps did by making payment automatic.

> You can reduce the friction associated with any task by mapping out all of the behaviors connected to a given process and then identifying areas where you can automate or streamline the process.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
