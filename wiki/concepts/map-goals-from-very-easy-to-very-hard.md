# Map goals from very easy to very hard

Find gateway habits by ranking the steps toward a goal from very easy to very hard, then starting with the very easy end rather than the big ambition.

> You can usually figure out the gateway habits that will lead to
> your desired outcome by mapping out your goals on a scale from
> “very easy” to “very hard.” Most people start with ambitions that
> are big and very hard, but need to transition to habits that are
> small and very easy.
>
> — James Clear
