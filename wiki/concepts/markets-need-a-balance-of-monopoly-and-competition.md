# Markets need a balance of monopoly and competition

Competition is the default market state that keeps firms efficient and honest, while monopoly rewards innovation and funds big bets but eventually erodes. The ideal is enough of each to check the other.

> Monopoly and competition are the yin and yang of the business
> world.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
