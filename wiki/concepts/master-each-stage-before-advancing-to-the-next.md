# Master each stage before advancing to the next

Habit shaping means mastering the two-minute version first, then an intermediate step, and repeating. Eventually you reach the habit you hoped to build despite starting small.

> Start by mastering the first two minutes of the smallest version
> of the behavior, then advance to an intermediate step and repeat
> the process. Mastering each stage before moving on to the next
> level. Eventually, you’ll end up with the habit you had
> originally hoped to build despite starting so small.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 13
