# Mastering strategy and habits tilts luck in your favor

Results come from luck, strategy, and actions. Only strategy and actions are controllable, but mastering them improves the odds that luck works for you.

> There are 3 primary drivers of results in life:
>
> 1) Your luck (randomness). 2) Your strategy (choices). 3) Your
> actions (habits).
>
> Only 2 of the 3 are under your control. But if you master those
> 2, you can improve the odds that luck will work for you rather
> than against you.
>
> — James Clear
