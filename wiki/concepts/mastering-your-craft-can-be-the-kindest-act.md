# Mastering your craft can be the kindest act

Buhrman credits his son's survival to medical professionals who spent decades mastering their craft. He suggests that radically pursuing something in service to others may be the kindest thing we can do, because we can't know whose life it will change.

> But maybe in some sense, the kindest thing that all of us can do
> is to pursue something radically that in some way is in service
> to others, because you just don't know how it's going to change
> the trajectory of human life.
>
> — Rick Buhrman, Invest Like the Best
