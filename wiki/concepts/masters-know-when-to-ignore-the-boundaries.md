# Masters know when to ignore the boundaries

Beginners stay within the rules, competent people test their limits, and masters know when the rules don't apply.

> The beginner plays within the boundaries.
>
> The competent explore the boundaries.
>
> The master knows when to ignore them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
