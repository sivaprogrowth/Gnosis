# Mastery means knowing what to leave out

The highest form of mastery is simplicity. Most information and effort don't matter, and experts stand out because they know what to leave out.

> The highest level of mastery is simplicity.
>
> Most information is irrelevant and most effort is wasted, but
> only the expert knows what to ignore.
>
> — James Clear

> The amateur does not know what to do.
>
> The master knows what not to do.
>
> — James Clear
