# Mastery moves from ignorant to profound simplicity

Beginners are simple because they don't know much, intermediates build working complexity, and experts return to a deeper kind of simplicity.

> Beginner = ignorant simplicity
>
> Intermediate = functional complexity
>
> Advanced = profound simplicity
>
> — James Clear
