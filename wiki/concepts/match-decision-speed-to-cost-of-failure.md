# Match decision speed to cost of failure

When mistakes are costly, take time to plan carefully. When mistakes are cheap, move fast and act.

> When failure is expensive, plan carefully.
>
> When failure is cheap, act quickly.
>
> — James Clear

> When I give talks all around the world on decision-making, I
> tell people that when the cost of failure is low, move quickly.
> When it's high, move slowly. There are two nuances here. First,
> the word cost is important and it's always changing. A low cost
> for one team won't necessarily be a low cost for another. Second,
> and perhaps more important, most high-cost decisions can be
> broken down into lower-cost decisions.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
