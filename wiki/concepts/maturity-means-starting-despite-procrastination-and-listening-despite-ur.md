# Maturity means starting despite procrastination and listening despite urge to talk

Being mature involves acting when you'd rather delay and listening when you'd rather speak. It is about overriding immediate impulses.

> Maturity is learning how to start when you feel like
> procrastinating and learning how to listen when you feel like
> talking.
>
> — James Clear
