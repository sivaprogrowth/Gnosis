# Measure people by how they turn adversity to advantage

Every executive will face crises and setbacks. What matters is not just coping with them but turning them to your advantage.

> “Crises, setbacks, obstacles—these will certainly be met by any
> executive in the course of his career. The measure of a person in
> such circumstances is not only how they cope with adversity, but
> also how they turns it to their advantage. Business is always a
> battle—for sales, improvements, efficiency —and an executive must
> lead very much as a general would to win.”
>
> — J. Paul Getty
