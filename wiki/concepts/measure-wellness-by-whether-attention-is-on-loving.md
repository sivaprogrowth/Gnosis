# Measure wellness by whether attention is on loving

You can gauge your wellbeing at any moment by asking whether your attention is on giving love or on those who aren't loving you.

> In any moment on any given day I can measure my wellness by this
> question:
>
> Is my attention on loving or is my attention on who isn't loving
> me?
>
> — Andrea Gibson, X
