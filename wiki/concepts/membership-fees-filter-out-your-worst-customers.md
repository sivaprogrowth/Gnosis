# Membership fees filter out your worst customers

Costco's membership requirement keeps out a cohort that includes all the shoplifters and thieves and most small-ticket buyers. Who you keep out can matter as much as who you let in.

> Think about who you’re keeping out. Think
> about the cohort that won’t give you their license and their ID
> and get their picture taken. Or they aren’t organized enough to
> do it, or they can’t do the math to realize how they’re…” He said
> that cohort will have a hundred percent of your shoplifters and a
> hundred percent of your thieves. Now it’ll also have most of your
> small tickets.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]], Conversation with Chris Davis, The Knowledge Project podcast
