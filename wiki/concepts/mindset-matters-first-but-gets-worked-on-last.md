# Mindset matters first but gets worked on last

Mindset is the most important factor, yet it is the last thing people try to improve. Seeing problems as opportunities gives an advantage that those who see obstacles can't grasp, and eventually people realize everything comes down to mindset.

> Mindset is the first thing that matters and the last thing anyone
> works on.
>
> The person who approaches a problem like an opportunity has an
> advantage that the person who sees an obstacle will never
> understand.
>
> Sooner or later people realize that everything comes down to
> mindset.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
