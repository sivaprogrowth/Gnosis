# Minimalism seeks the optimal, not the least, amount

The aim of minimalism is having the right amount of things for your goals, and that amount is usually less than you expect.

> The goal is not to have the least amount of things, but the
> optimal amount of things.
>
> Two important footnotes:
>
> (1) The optimal amount depends on your goals.
> (2) The optimal amount is almost always less than you think.
>
> — James Clear
