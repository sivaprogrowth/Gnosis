# Misery and happiness take the same amount of work

What we choose to emphasize determines whether we make ourselves miserable or happy. Either way, the effort required is the same.

> The trick is in what one emphasizes. We either make ourselves
> miserable, or we make ourselves happy. The amount of work is the
> same.
>
> — Carlos Castaneda, Journey To Ixtlan: The Lessons of Don Juan
