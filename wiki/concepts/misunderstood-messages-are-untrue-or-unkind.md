# Misunderstood messages are untrue or unkind

If someone does not understand your message, either what you said was not true or you delivered it without kindness.

> You should know that when a message you convey to another person
> is not understood by him, at least one of the following things is
> true: what you have said is not true, or you have conveyed it
> without kindness.
>
> — Leo Tolstoy, A Calendar of Wisdom
