# Modest appearance can conceal great wealth

Hetty Green dressed so plainly she looked poor, using her clothes as a costume to hide her enormous fortune. Appearances can be deliberately misleading about wealth.

> "A passerby might say she looked as
> poor as a church mouse, but her clothes were merely a costume to
> conceal her incredible wealth."
>
> — Charles Slack, The Richest Woman in America: Hetty Green in the Gilded Age
