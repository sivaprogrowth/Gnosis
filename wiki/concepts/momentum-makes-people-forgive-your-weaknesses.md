# Momentum makes people forgive your weaknesses

When things are going well, people focus on your strengths and overlook flaws. Once momentum stops, everything gets scrutinized.

> "When momentum is on your side, people focus on your strengths
> and forgive your weaknesses. When the momentum stops, they
> scrutinize the whole thing."
>
> — Packy McCormick, Momentum, Consolidation, and Breakout
