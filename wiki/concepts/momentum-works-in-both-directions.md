# Momentum works in both directions

Inaction breeds more inaction and action breeds more action. Moving, talking, or creating makes it easier to keep doing so, while stopping makes you feel sluggish, timid, or stuck.

> Momentum goes both ways.
>
> Don’t move, feel sluggish. Start moving, feel like moving a
> little more.
>
> Don't talk, feel timid. Start chatting, conversation gets a
> little easier.
>
> Don't ship, feel stuck. Start creating, ideas begin to flow.
>
> — James Clear
