# Money cannot be the only filter for time

Money matters, but it shouldn't be the sole criterion for spending time. The most meaningful moments with family never pay you anything.

> Money plays an important role in life, but it can't be the only
> filter for how you decide to spend your time. Nobody will ever
> pay you to go on a date with your spouse or take your kids to the
> park or grab coffee with your parents.
>
> — James Clear
