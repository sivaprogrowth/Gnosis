# Monopolies sow the seeds of their own destruction

A monopoly can dominate a market so fully it becomes the market, but it inevitably declines; the only question is how long it lasts.

> But monopolies inevitably sow the seeds of their own
> destruction. The question is how long they will last.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
