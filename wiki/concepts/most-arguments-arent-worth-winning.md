# Most arguments aren't worth winning

Office debates and family squabbles are usually only loosely connected to your real goals. Focus on the outcomes and life you actually want instead.

> Odds are, the latest office debate or family squabble isn't
> worth winning. Most arguments are only tangentially related to
> your end goal.
>
> What outcomes are you actually trying to create? What type of
> life are you actually trying to cultivate? Stay focused.
>
> — James Clear
