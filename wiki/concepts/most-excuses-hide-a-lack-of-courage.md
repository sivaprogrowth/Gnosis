# Most excuses hide a lack of courage

Excuses like not enough time, money, knowledge, or connections often mask the real bottleneck, which is not enough courage. There is usually a small step available if you have the guts to take it.

> Most of the excuses I made...
>
> - not enough time- not enough money- not enough knowledge- not
> the right connections
>
> ...were just ways to avoid the real bottleneck: Not enough
> courage.
>
> There was always a small step I could have taken—if I had the
> guts to take it.
>
> — James Clear
