# Most people quit at temporary defeat

Everyone meets temporary defeat before success, and the easy response most people choose is to quit.

> “Before success comes in any person's life, he is sure to meet
> with much temporary defeat, and, perhaps, some failure. When
> defeat overtakes a person, the easiest and most logical thing to
> do is to quit. That is exactly what the majority of people do.”
>
> — Napoleon Hill
