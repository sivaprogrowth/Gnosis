# Most people self-limit their ability to learn

Formal study isn't required to master a field; reading books and talking to people lets you pick it up along the way.

> “Most people self-limit their ability to learn. Just read books
> and talk to people. I didn’t study rocket engineering, I picked
> it up along the way.”
>
> — [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], The Book of Elon
