# Most problems are solved by simply listening

Many problems don't need a solution offered; letting the other person talk while you listen resolves most of them.

> “Many of the problems I hear don’t require me to offer solutions. I solve most of them by just listening and letting the involved party do the talking.”
>
> — Mary Kay Ash
