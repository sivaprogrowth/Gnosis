# Most reading value comes from understanding and mastery

Reading has four levels: to entertain, to inform, to understand, and to master. Most people stop at reading to inform, but most of the value lies in the last two levels.

> “Think of these levels as reading to entertain, reading to
> inform, reading to understand, and reading to master. When you
> learned to read in elementary school, you were taught to read for
> entertainment. If you made it to high school and college, you
> learned to read to inform. This is where most people stop. But
> most of the value comes at the last two levels.”
>
> — Mortimer Adler, How to Read a Book
