# Most results come from boring unglamorous work

Nearly all of success comes from consistently doing unglamorous things no one sees. Only the small visible, exciting part gets talked about.

> 98% of success is consistently doing boring things that no one
> sees. 2% is visible and exciting, so that's all everyone talks
> about.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> The 98/2 Rule: people spend 98% of their time talking about
> flashy things that contribute only 2% to the results, while
> overlooking the fact that 98% of the results come from
> consistently doing the boring basics that few notice.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Flashy gets attention. Boring gets results.
>
> While most chase the views, the greats obsess over the basics.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
