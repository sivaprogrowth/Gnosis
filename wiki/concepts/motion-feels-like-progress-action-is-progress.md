# Motion feels like progress, action is progress

Preparing, researching, and exploring are motion that feels productive, while small concrete acts like doing 10 squats or writing one sentence are actual progress.

> The difference between motion and action.
>
> Motion:
>
> -talk to a personal trainer
>
> -research your book idea
>
> -explore different types of meditation
>
> Action:
>
> -do 10 squats
>
> -write 1 sentence
>
> -meditate for 1 minute
>
> Motion feels like progress. Action is progress.
>
> — James Clear
