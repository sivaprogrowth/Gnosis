# Motivation fluctuates like waves throughout the day

Motivation is not constant; it rises and falls during the day, so habits are easier to stick with when motivation is high.

> The challenge, however, is that motivation is a fluctuating
> resource. Sometimes you feel motivated, and sometimes you don't.
> It comes and goes throughout the day like a series of waves.
>
> — James Clear
