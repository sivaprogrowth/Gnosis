# Move toward the next thing, not away

Heading somewhere new because you want it feels different from fleeing what came before, even if the direction is the same. The motivation changes the energy you bring.

> "Move toward the next thing, not away from the last thing.
>
> Same direction. Completely different energy."
>
> — James Clear
