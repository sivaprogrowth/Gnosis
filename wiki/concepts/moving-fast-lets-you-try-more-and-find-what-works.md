# Moving fast lets you try more and find what works

A bias toward action helps most people. Moving quickly means more attempts, which raises the odds of finding something that works.

> As far as life philosophies go, "The right time is right now"
> isn't a bad one. Most of us would benefit from a greater bias
> toward action.
>
> If you move fast, you can try more things. And if you try more
> things, you're likely to find something that works for you.
>
> — James Clear
