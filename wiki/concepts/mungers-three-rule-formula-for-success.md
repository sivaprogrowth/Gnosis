# Munger's three-rule formula for success

Only sell what you'd buy yourself, only work for people you'd want to become, and build relationships where everyone wins.

> I learned this formula for success from Charlie Munger:
>
> 1. Don't sell stuff you don't want to buy. 2. Don't work for
> people you don't want to become. 3. Create win-win relationships
> for all.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]]
