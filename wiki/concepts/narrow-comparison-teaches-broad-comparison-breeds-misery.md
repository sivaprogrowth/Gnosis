# Narrow comparison teaches, broad comparison breeds misery

Comparing narrow tactics like technique or strategy helps you learn skills, while comparing broad life outcomes like career or net worth makes you unhappy.

> To improve, compare little things.
>
> -marketing strategies
> -exercise technique
> -writing tactics
>
> To be miserable, compare big things.
>
> -career path
> -marriage
> -net worth
>
> Comparison is the thief of joy when applied broadly, but the
> teacher of skills when applied narrowly.
>
> — James Clear
