# Narrowing focus creates more opportunities later

Making the most of a single opportunity tends to create more opportunities. Committing boldly to one direction opens up more paths than spreading yourself thin.

> The Paradox of Focus:
>
> Make the most of one opportunity and more opportunities will come
> your way.
>
> Moving boldly in one direction causes more paths to unfold before
> you.
>
> To get more, focus on less.
>
> — James Clear

> "Limiting your options now will expand your opportunities in the
> long run because you can remain focused enough to master
> something.
>
> Keeping your options open now will reduce your opportunities in
> the long run because you divide your attention and end up doing
> an average job on seven different things.
>
> Are you falling into the pattern of always mastering one thing or
> always chasing the next thing?"
>
> — James Clear
