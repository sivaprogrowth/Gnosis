# Narrowing focus expands your freedom

Freedom in any area comes from sustained focus on one discipline: saving brings financial freedom, training brings physical freedom, learning brings intellectual freedom.

> The Paradox of Freedom:
>
> The way to expand your freedom is to narrow your focus.
>
> Stay focused on saving to achieve financial freedom.
>
> Stay focused on training to achieve physical freedom.
>
> Stay focused on learning to achieve intellectual freedom.
>
> — James Clear

> The Paradox of Freedom: The way to expand your freedom is to
> narrow your focus.
>
> * Stay focused on saving to achieve financial freedom.
> * Stay focused on training to achieve physical freedom.
> * Stay focused on learning to achieve intellectual freedom.
>
> The disciplined become the free.
>
> — James Clear
