# Nature offers peace from despair about the future

When worry about the world and the future grows, going into nature among wild things that don't burden themselves with anticipated grief brings rest and a sense of freedom.

> When despair for the world grows in me and I wake in the night
> at the least sound in fear of what my life and my children's
> lives may be, I go and lie down where the wood drake rests in his
> beauty on the water, and the great heron feeds I come into the
> peace of wild things who do not tax their lives with forethought
> of grief. I come into the presence of still water. And I feel
> above me the day-blind stars waiting with their light. For a time
> I rest in the grace of the world, and am free.
>
> — Wendell Berry, The Peace of Wild Things
