# Nature's repeated cycles offer lasting healing strength

Contemplating the beauty of the earth gives people enduring reserves of strength. The reliable rhythms of nature, like dawn after night and spring after winter, are deeply healing.

> Those who contemplate the beauty of the earth find reserves of strength that will endure as long as life lasts. There is something infinitely healing in the repeated refrains of nature — the assurance that dawn comes after night, and spring after winter.
>
> — Rachel Carson, The Sense of Wonder
