# Nearly everything becomes unfavorable past a certain size

Even good things like clients, work, fame, or free time become harmful in excess. Notice when what you pursue stops being useful.

> Nearly everything in life is unfavorable once it grows to a
> certain size.
>
> It is entirely possible to have too many clients, too much work,
> too much fame, too much free time, and so on.
>
> Pay attention to when the thing you're chasing exceeds its
> usefulness.
>
> — James Clear
