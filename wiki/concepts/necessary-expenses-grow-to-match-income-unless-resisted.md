# Necessary expenses grow to match income unless resisted

What we consider necessary spending naturally rises to consume whatever we earn. Only deliberate resistance stops lifestyle creep.

> “That what each of us calls our 'necessary expenses' will always
> grow to equal our incomes unless we protest to the contrary.”
>
> — George Clason
