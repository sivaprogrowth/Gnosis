# Needing a contract signals a relationship to avoid

If you feel you need a contract to protect yourself from someone, that's a sign you'd rather not deal with them at all.

> I like to avoid dealing with people where I feel like I need a
> contract.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> If I need a forty-page contract before I can work with you, the cost is already too high.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
