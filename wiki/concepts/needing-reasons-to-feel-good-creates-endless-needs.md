# Needing reasons to feel good creates endless needs

If your good feelings depend on having reasons, you will always find new things you need. Contentment that depends on conditions never runs out of conditions.

> Those who need reasons to feel good never run out of needs.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
