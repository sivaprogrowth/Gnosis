# Needing things a certain way makes you their hostage

If you insist things must be a particular way, you become controlled by them. Staying flexible keeps you free.

> If I need things to be a certain way, I'm held hostage by them
>
> — Jonny Wilkinson, Series Two: Jonny Wilkinson
