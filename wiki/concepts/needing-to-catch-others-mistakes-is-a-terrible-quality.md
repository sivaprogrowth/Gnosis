# Needing to catch others' mistakes is a terrible quality

Showing how smart you are by pointing out others' errors may help in some areas. In everyday life it does great damage.

> One of the worst qualities a person can have is the need to show how smart they are by catching others' mistakes. In some areas, this may be useful, but in general life, it's a disaster.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
