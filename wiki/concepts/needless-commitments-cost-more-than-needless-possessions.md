# Needless commitments cost more than needless possessions

You can ignore an unneeded possession, but an unneeded commitment keeps charging you time and attention again and again, like a recurring debt.

> Needless commitments are more wasteful than needless
> possessions.
> Possessions can be ignored, but commitments are a recurring debt
> that must be paid for with your time and attention.
>
> — James Clear
