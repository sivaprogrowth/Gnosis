# Negativity only sinks you once it gets inside

Like water that can only sink a ship once it gets inside, negativity can only harm a person once it enters their mind. Protecting your mind is what matters.

> All the water in the world cannot sink a ship unless it gets inside the ship. All the negativity in the world cannot sink a person unless it gets inside the mind.
>
> — Mary Pickford, Why Not Try God (1934)
