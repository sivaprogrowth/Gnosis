# Never be greedy over a single transaction

Squeezing extra from one deal is not worth it, even if you think no one will find out, because your reputation follows you everywhere.

> It is not worth it to be greedy over a single transaction. Even
> if you're not going to work with the person again. Even if you
> think word won't get out. Even if you think this is your one shot
> to make it. Reputation follows you everywhere.
>
> — James Clear
