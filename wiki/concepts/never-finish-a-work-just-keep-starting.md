# Never finish a work, just keep starting

Gorky rejects the idea of finishing because finished means dead. He treats painting as endless, constantly restarting and changing his mind.

> I don't like that word 'finish'. When something is finished,
> that means it's dead, doesn't it? I believe in everlastingness. I
> never finish a painting – I just stop working on it for a while.
>
> I like painting because it's something I never come to the end
> of. Sometimes I paint a picture, then I paint it all out.
> Sometimes I'm working on fifteen or twenty pictures at the same
> time. I do that because I want to – because I like to change my
> mind so often.
>
> The thing to do is always to keep starting to paint, never
> finishing painting.
>
> — Arshile Gorky, Movements in Art Since 1945
