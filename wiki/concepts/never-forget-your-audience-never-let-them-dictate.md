# Never forget your audience, never let them dictate

Creators should design with the audience in mind and value its genuine, unfakeable reactions, while avoiding pandering.

> Great artists design their work for these silent judges,
> balancing authenticity with expectation without succumbing to
> pandering. The audience is their silent collaborator and their
> ultimate judge.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
