# Never offer what you'd resent being accepted

Only make offers you are genuinely willing to follow through on, so you don't end up resenting someone for taking you up on them.

> Never offer what you'd hate someone for accepting.
>
> — Tara Ploughman (reportedly an anagram of Paul Graham), Quotes
