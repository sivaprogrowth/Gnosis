# Never questioning limits you to others' imaginations

If you accept things without questioning, your life gets shaped by what other people imagine for you. It is better to be limited by your own dreams than by your place in someone else's.

> If you never question things, your life ends up being limited by
> other people's imaginations.
>
> Take the time to think and dream, to question and reconsider. It
> is better to be limited by what you can dream for yourself than
> by where you fit in someone else's dream.
>
> — James Clear
