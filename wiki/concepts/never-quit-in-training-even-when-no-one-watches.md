# Never quit in training, even when no one watches

Refusing to quit in small private struggles builds the belief that you will find a way when things get truly hard; letting quitting in lets it take over.

> because if the treadmill can make me quit, what happens when I
> get into the ring with a guy who's hit me, and I'm hurt? He's
> going to make me quit too. It's hugely important because it
> teaches you to believe that no matter how hard things get, you
> are the type of person that will find a way.
>
> — Chris Eubank Jr.
