# New habits should take under two minutes

When starting a new habit, scale it down so it takes less than two minutes to do, making it as easy as possible to start. This counters our tendency to start too big.

> The Two-Minute Rule states, “When you start a
> new habit, it should take less than two minutes to do.”
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
