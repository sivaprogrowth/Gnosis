# New lifestyles, not new goals, deliver new results

Setting new goals doesn't change outcomes; changing how you live does. Since a lifestyle is a process, energy should go into building better habits rather than chasing results.

> New goals don't deliver new results. New lifestyles do.
>
> And a lifestyle is not an outcome, it is a process. For this
> reason, all of your energy should go into building better habits,
> not chasing better results.
>
> — James Clear
