# New paths appear once you start walking

Like a hike, your view of a situation changes after you begin. You don't need every answer before starting; taking the first step uncovers new options.

> Many situations in life are similar to going on a hike: the view
> changes once you start walking.
>
> You don't need all the answers right now. New paths will reveal
> themselves if you have the courage to get started.
>
> — James Clear
