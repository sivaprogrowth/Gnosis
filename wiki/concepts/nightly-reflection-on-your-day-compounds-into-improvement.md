# Nightly reflection on your day compounds into improvement

Reviewing each evening what went well and badly that day leads you to improve naturally the next day, and over time this adds up to a lot.

> “How noble and good everyone could be if, every evening before
> falling asleep, they were to recall to mind the events of the
> whole day and consider exactly what has been good and bad. Then,
> without realizing it, you try to improve yourself at the start of
> each new day; of course, you achieve quite a lot in the course of
> time.
>
> — Anne Frank, The Diary of Anne Frank: The Revised Critical Edition
