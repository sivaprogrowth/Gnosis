# No act is too small because impact ripples outward

Making a difference in one person's life also affects everyone that person influences for the rest of their life. Small acts add up one by one into large change.

> When you create a difference in someone's life, you not only
> impact their life, you impact everyone influenced by them
> throughout their entire lifetime.
>
> No act is ever too small. One by one, this is how to make an
> ocean rise.
>
> — Danielle Doby, Ripples poem, found in I Am Her Tribe
