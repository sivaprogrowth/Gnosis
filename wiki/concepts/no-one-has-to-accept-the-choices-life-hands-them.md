# No one has to accept the choices life hands them

You are not obligated to live a life you don't want; people who stay stuck often convince themselves they had no choice.

> It is not necessary to accept the choices handed down to you by
> life as you know it. There is more to it than that — no one HAS
> to do something he doesn't want to do for the rest of his life.
> But then again, if that's what you wind up doing, by all means
> convince yourself that you HAD to do it. You'll have lots of
> company.
>
> — Hunter S. Thompson, Letter to Hume Logan (April 22, 1958), Letters of Note
