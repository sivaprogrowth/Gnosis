# No one is self-made; interdependence is both strength and risk

Everyone relies on others, enabling synergy and value no one creates alone, but depending on others for critical things exposes you if they fail, so choose carefully whom you depend on in a crisis.

> Interdependence is the reality that
> underlies the illusion of  self-sufficiency. No one is entirely
> self-made.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
