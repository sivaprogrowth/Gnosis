# Nobody is rooting for you to fail

Most people don't care whether you succeed or fail. That indifference is freeing, because you can pursue your dreams without worrying about others' opinions.

> Nobody is rooting for you to fail.
>
> You may succeed. You may fail. But, for the most part, nobody
> cares one way or the other.
>
> This is good. The world is big and you are small, which means you
> can chase your dreams with little worry for what people think.
>
> — James Clear
