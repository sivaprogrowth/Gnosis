# Not all writing is worth reading

Being able to put words on a page does not make writing valuable. Much of what is written adds nothing and is not worth your time.

> Not all writing is worth reading. Just because someone can put
> words on a page doesn’t mean they are worth reading or add
> value.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Schopenhauer’s Strategy Against Clickbait
