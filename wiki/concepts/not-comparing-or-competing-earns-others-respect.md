# Not comparing or competing earns others' respect

Being content with who you are, without measuring yourself against others, makes people respect you.

> When you are content to be simply yourself and don't compare or
> compete, everybody will respect you.
>
> — Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching (paraphrased)
