# Not enough time usually means low energy or interest

Saying you lack time often really means you lack energy or genuine interest. Caring for yourself and finding your true interests matters more than managing time, because rested and excited people find time easily.

> "I have learned that whenever I think "I don't have enough time
> to do that" what I usually mean is "I don't have enough energy"
> or "I am not actually interested in doing this."
>
> What I need to do a better job of is not managing my time, but
> rather caring for myself and identifying my true interests. When
> I am well rested and working on something I am genuinely excited
> about, finding time is rarely a problem."
>
> — James Clear
