# Not getting what you want reveals bargaining over the price

When you fail to get what you want, either you didn't truly want it or you tried to pay less than it cost.

> "If you don't get what you want, it's a sign either that you did
> not seriously want it, or that you tried to bargain over the
> price."
>
> — Rudyard Kipling, An Unqualified Pilot
