# Not stopping lets you build remarkable things

Sustained, uninterrupted effort in business, fitness, or learning produces surprisingly large results over time.

> It’s remarkable what you can build if you just don’t stop.
>
> It’s remarkable the business you can build if you don’t stop
> working.
>
> It’s remarkable the body you can build if you don’t stop
> training.
>
> It’s remarkable the knowledge you can build if you don’t stop
> learning.
>
> — James Clear
