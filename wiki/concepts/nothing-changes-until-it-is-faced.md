# Nothing changes until it is faced

Facing a problem does not guarantee it can be changed, but change is impossible without first confronting it.

> Not everything that is faced can be changed, but nothing can be
> changed until it is faced.
>
> — James Baldwin, Unfinished book manuscript, shared in the documentary I Am Not Your Negro
