# Nothing extraordinary happens unless you cause it

Waiting for something remarkable to happen wastes years; extraordinary things only come when you make them happen.

> “I was waiting for something extraordinary to happen, but as the
> years wasted on, nothing ever did unless I caused it.”
>
> — Charles Bukowski
