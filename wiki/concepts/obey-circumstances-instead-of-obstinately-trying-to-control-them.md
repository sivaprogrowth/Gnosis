# Obey circumstances instead of obstinately trying to control them

Napoleon said he kept few fixed ideas because he followed circumstances, which constantly changed his mind, so he held projects rather than definite plans.

> I had few really definite ideas, and the reason for this was that, instead of obstinately seeking to control circumstances, I obeyed them, and they forced me to change my mind all the time. Thus it happened that most of the time, to tell the truth, I had no definite plans but only projects.
>
> — Napoleon Bonaparte, A conversation in 1816 as quoted in The Mind of Napoleon
