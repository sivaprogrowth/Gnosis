# Observation is a trainable skill, so train it toward good

What you notice is a skill you can hone. Many people are skilled at spotting negatives, but you can train yourself to notice opportunities, good luck, and small moments of joy.

> "Observation is a skill, and like any skill, it can be trained
> and honed.
>
> Even if you're not a negative person, you may be skilled at
> noticing negative things. Sometimes people are good at noticing
> the reason things won't work out or have a tendency to fixate on
> the latest distressing story.
>
> But you can train your eye toward the opportunities each day
> quietly presents. You can become competent at noticing your good
> luck: the little moments of joy, the stranger who helped, the
> small things that went right, the opportunity in front of you
> right now.
>
> — James Clear
