# Observing without evaluating is the highest intelligence

Pausing to simply observe, without immediately judging, is the highest form of intelligence.

> The ability to observe without evaluating is the highest form of
> intelligence.
>
> — Jiddu Krishnamurti, The First and Last Freedom
