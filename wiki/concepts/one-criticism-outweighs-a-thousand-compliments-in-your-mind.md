# One criticism outweighs a thousand compliments in your mind

People tend to dwell on a single criticism even when many others have praised them. Negative feedback sticks far harder than positive feedback.

> It’s crazy how 1,000 people can compliment you and you’ll spend all day thinking about the one person who criticized you.
>
> — James Clear
