# One dominant worldview distorts how you explain problems

If a single worldview dominates your thinking, you will force every problem into it. Reading widely shows you that many answers exist.

> When one worldview dominates your thinking, you'll try to explain every problem you face through that worldview. Read widely and realize there are many answers.
>
> — James Clear
