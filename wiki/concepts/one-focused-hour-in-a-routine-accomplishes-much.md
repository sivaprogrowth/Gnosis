# One focused hour in a routine accomplishes much

A single hour of focused work can achieve a great deal, especially when it is part of a consistent daily routine.

> “So much can be accomplished in one focused hour, especially when
> that hour is part of a routine, a sacred rhythm that becomes part
> of your daily life.”
>
> — Dani Shapiro
