# Only a few big decisions really matter

Identify the one action or choice with outsized impact that makes many others irrelevant. Master the big moves and move quickly through the rest, since most things aren't worth optimizing.

> What's the one action that moves the needle more than 100 other
> actions? What's the one choice that renders 1000 other choices
> irrelevant?
>
> Most things are not worth optimizing. Master the big moves and
> move quickly and peacefully through the other stuff.
>
> — James Clear

> “The most important thing is to stay focused on what matters.
> Most little things ultimately have no effect on an enterprise.
> It's the big deals - and the big decisions that do. Don't spend
> too much time on little things. The important choices and
> opportunities are the ones that move the dial.”
>
> — Larry Tisch
