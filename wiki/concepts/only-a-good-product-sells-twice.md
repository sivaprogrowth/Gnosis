# Only a good product sells twice

Marketing can win a first sale, but repeat business depends on real quality. Over time, results return to the actual value you provide.

> "Good marketing can sell once, but only a good product can sell
> twice.
>
> In the long run, your performance reverts to the value you
> provide."
>
> — James Clear
