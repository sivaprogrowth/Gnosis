# Only a living thing can go against the stream

Drifting along with life takes no effort, but living with energy, will, and conviction means being willing to resist the current.

> "A dead thing can go with the stream, but only a living thing can
> go against it."
>
> — G. K. Chesterton, The Everlasting Man
