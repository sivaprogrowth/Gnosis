# Only attempting more reduces fear, not learning more

Gaining knowledge doesn't remove fear of doing something. Repeatedly trying it is what makes the fear shrink.

> Learning more will increase knowledge, but only attempting more
> will reduce fear. The more you try it, the less you will fear
> it.
>
> — James Clear
