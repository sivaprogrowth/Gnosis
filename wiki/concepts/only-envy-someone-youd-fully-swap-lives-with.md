# Only envy someone you'd fully swap lives with

Jealousy only makes you unhappier and changes nothing for the other person. You can't take just the parts of someone's life you want, so unless you'd swap your whole life for theirs, jealousy is pointless.

> When I was young, I had a lot of jealousy in me... I learned to
> get rid of it. It still crops up every now and then. It's such a
> poisonous emotion because, at the end of the day, you're no
> better off, you're unhappier, and the person you're jealous of is
> still successful or good-looking, or whatever they are.I
> realized that all these people that I was jealous of, I couldn't
> just cherry-pick and choose little aspects of their life. I
> couldn't say I want his body; I want her money; I want his
> personality. You have to be that person. Do you want to actually
> be that person with all of their reactions, their desires, their
> family, their happiness level, their outlook on life, their
> self-image? If you're not willing to do a wholesale, 24/7, 100%
> swap with who that person is, then there is no point in being
> jealous.
>
> — Naval Ravikant, The Knowledge Project, Episode 18
