# Only movement relieves the anxiety that precedes change

Change is often preceded by anxious discomfort, like birds before migration. The only thing that eases it is to act and move.

> Birds feel something akin to pain (and fear) just before migration. Nothing alleviates this feeling except flight.
>
> — Lorine Niedecker, Letter to Cid Corman (January 30, 1968), paraphrasing W.H. Hudson
