# Opportunities come from other people doing dumb things

Investment opportunities arise when others act foolishly, and Buffett observes such behavior has increased over Berkshire's history.

> “What gives you opportunities is other people doing dumb things. And I’d say that in the fifty-eight years that we’ve been running Berkshire, there’s been a great increase in the number of people doing dumb things.”
>
> — Warren Buffett
