# Opportunities come through people

Opportunities don't exist in the abstract; they come through people. Looking for an opportunity really means looking for the right person.

> Every opportunity is attached to a person. Opportunities do not
> float like clouds in the sky. They’re attached to people.
>
> If you’re looking for an opportunity — including one that has a
> financial payoff — you’re really looking for a person.
>
> — Ben Casnocha, Why Entrepreneurial Thinking Is For Everyone Now

> Luck flows through people and travels by conversation. The people you talk to determine the opportunities you find.
>
> Keep talking to the same people, keep finding the same opportunities. Start talking to new people, start finding new opportunities.
>
> If you want different luck, start walking into different rooms.
>
> — James Clear
