# Optimism is a legal, free performance enhancer

Optimism boosts performance at no cost. Optimists like Sam Walton and FedEx acted despite critics and made history, while pessimists only wrote reports.

> Optimism is a performance-enhancing drug that's both legal and
> free.
>
> While experts predicted failure, Sam Walton opened discount
> stores in small towns. When critics said 'too risky,' FedEx
> launched overnight delivery.
>
> While pessimists write reports, optimists write history.
>
