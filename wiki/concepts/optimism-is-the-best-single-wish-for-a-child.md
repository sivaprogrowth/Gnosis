# Optimism is the best single wish for a child

Kahneman argues optimists are happier, more resilient, healthier, and disproportionately shape the world, though they are often luckier and more overconfident than they realize.

> If you are allowed one wish for your child, seriously consider
> wishing him or her optimism. Optimists are normally cheerful and
> happy, and therefore popular; they are resilient in adapting to
> failures and hardships, their chances of clinical depression are
> reduced, their immune system is stronger, they take better care
> of their health, they feel healthier than others and are in fact
> likely to live longer.Optimistic individuals play a
> disproportionate role in shaping our lives. Their decisions make
> a difference; they are the inventors, the entrepreneurs, the
> political and military leaders – not average people. They got to
> where they are by seeking challenges and taking risks. They are
> talented and they have been lucky, almost certainly luckier than
> they acknowledge... the people who have the greatest influence on
> the lives of others are likely to be optimistic and
> overconfident, and to take more risks than they realize.
>
> — Daniel Kahneman, Thinking, Fast and Slow
