# Optimize for being slightly better tomorrow

Focus your actions on improving just one day from now: save, exercise, and read to be a little better tomorrow. Small daily gains add up.

> Optimize for tomorrow—as in, literally, one day from now.
>
> Save to be a little richer tomorrow. Exercise to be a little
> fitter tomorrow. Read to be a little smarter tomorrow.
>
> 1% better every day.
>
> — James Clear
