# Optimize for time and leisure, not just money

Money is not the only way to improve life. You can also widen your leisure, optimize for time, and prioritize recovery over efficiency.

> Money is not the only way to improve life. Instead of widening
> your margin of profit, widen your margin of leisure. Instead of
> optimizing for money, optimize for time. Instead of seeking
> efficiency, seek recovery.
>
> — James Clear
