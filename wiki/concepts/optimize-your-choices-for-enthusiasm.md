# Optimize your choices for enthusiasm

A reliable life rule is to make choices that leave you energetic and interested. Notice when you feel the urge to pursue something and do more of it.

> A simple rule for life that rarely fails:
>
> Optimize for enthusiasm. Make as many choices as you can that
> leave you feeling energetic and interested. Pay attention to when
> you have the urge to pursue or participate in something and do
> more of it.
>
> — James Clear
