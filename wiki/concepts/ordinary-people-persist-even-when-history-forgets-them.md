# Ordinary people persist even when history forgets them

History is mostly recorded by and about elites, so ordinary people are left out of it. Yet ordinary people keep persisting anyway, and that defiance gives strength to those not expected to succeed.

> The phrase 'but no matter' is a statement of defiance. It doesn’t
> matter that history has failed us because ordinary people have
> persisted anyway. This idea gives me an enormous amount of
> strength and hope as a writer because I am an ordinary person.
>
> — Min Jin Lee, Min Jin Lee: 'History has failed almost everybody who is ordinary'
