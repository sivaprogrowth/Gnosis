# Ordinary people succeed by being consistent and patient

Success does not require being special. Ordinary people achieve extraordinary things by being what most won't be: consistent, hardworking, patient, and determined.

> You don't have to be special to be successful. Ordinary people
> can do extraordinary things by choosing to be what most people
> are unwilling to be: consistent, hardworking, patient, and
> determined.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
