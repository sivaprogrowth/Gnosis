# Organizations drift to bureaucracy, people to distraction

Without deliberate effort, priorities multiply, meetings grow, and decisions move to committees. The most productive people and organizations actively resist what feels good in favor of what gets results.

> Left unchecked organizations default to bureaucracy. People
> default to distraction.
>
> It doesn't take long for 3 priorities to become 10. It doesn't
> take long for 2 people in a meeting to become 8. It doesn't take
> long to move from people making decisions to committees.
>
> The most productive organizations and people spend a lot of
> energy fighting what feels good in pursuit of what gets results.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
