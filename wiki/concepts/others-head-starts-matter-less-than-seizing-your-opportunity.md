# Others' head starts matter less than seizing your opportunity

There will always be people with more money, better schools, or connections. What matters is making the most of your opportunity when it arrives.

> Someone will start with more money than you. Someone will get
> into a better school. Someone will inherit connections you need
> to build from scratch. Someone will get picked before you. In
> other words, you’ll have no problem finding people with a head
> start.
>
> That’s not the point.
>
> The point is: when you get your opportunity, you make the most of
> it.
>
> — James Clear
