# Others' judgments should follow deeds, not drive them

Caring too much about praise prevents serious accomplishment. Let others' opinions be a result of your work rather than its goal.

> If you care too much about being praised, in the end you will
> not accomplish anything serious.
>
> ...
>
> Let the judgments of others be the consequence of your deeds, not
> their purpose.
>
> — Leo Tolstoy, A Calendar of Wisdom
