# Others know you only by your actions

You judge yourself by your inner thoughts, but others can only judge you by what you do or say. If you feel misunderstood, you must express yourself through action or words.

> You know yourself mostly by your thoughts.
>
> Everyone else in the world knows you only by your actions.
>
> Remember this when you feel misunderstood. You have to do or say
> something for others to know how you feel.
>
> — James Clear
