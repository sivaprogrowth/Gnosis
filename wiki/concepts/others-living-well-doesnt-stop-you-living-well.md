# Others living well doesn't stop you living well

Success and a good life are not zero-sum; someone else's good life takes nothing away from yours because there are many ways to win.

> Someone else living a good life does not prevent you from living
> a good life.
>
> There are many ways to win and plenty of space.
>
> — James Clear
