# Others' perceptions of you are their concern

How others perceive your actions comes from their experiences, preferences, and expectations, none of which you control. If your choices don't match their expectations, that is their problem, not yours.

> The way someone else perceives what you do is a result of their
> own experiences (which you can’t control), their own preferences
> (which you can’t predict), and their own expectations (which you
> don’t set).
>
> If your choices don’t match their expectations that is their
> concern, not yours.
>
> — James Clear
