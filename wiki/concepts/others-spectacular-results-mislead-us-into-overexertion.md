# Others' spectacular results mislead us into overexertion

Hearing only about others' spectacular results makes us think we must push to the limit; instead, simplify by focusing on the first movement.

> When all we hear about are other people’s spectacular results,
> it’s natural to think that we need to push ourselves to the limit
> to achieve anything worthwhile.
>
> — James Clear
