# Others won't believe in you unless you do

If you are unwilling to back yourself, you can't expect others to back you.

> If you’re not willing to bet on yourself, don’t expect other
> people to bet on you.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> "If you don't believe in yourself, why should I believe in you?"
>
> — Greg Harden
