# Our doubts are traitors that stop us attempting

Fear of trying makes us lose good things we could have won. We can't know our full power unless we find the courage to begin.

> Our doubts are traitors, and make us lose the good we oft might
> win, by fearing to attempt.
>
> — William Shakespeare, Measure for Measure (1603)
