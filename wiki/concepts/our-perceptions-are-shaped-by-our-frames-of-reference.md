# Our perceptions are shaped by our frames of reference

Perceptions and judgments are not absolute; they depend on our vantage point. Relativity is not relativism, though. It calls us to examine assumptions, ask others what they see that we can't, and update our beliefs.

> Relativity is the idea that our perceptions and judgments are not
> absolute, but are shaped by our unique vantage points and frames
> of reference. It’s the understanding that our experiences are
> subjective.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
