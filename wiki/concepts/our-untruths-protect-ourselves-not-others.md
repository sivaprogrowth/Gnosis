# Our untruths protect ourselves, not others

We claim we avoid the truth to spare others' feelings, but usually we're avoiding the consequences of our own choices, like being tasked with fixing the problem we flag.

> The stories we tell aren't protecting others. They're protecting ourselves.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
