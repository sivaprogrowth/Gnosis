# Outcomes come from many choices, not one decision

No good result comes from one decision. Profitable businesses, fit bodies, and strong relationships are all built through many good choices over time.

> Successful outcomes are never the result of a single choice.
> They are built up through good choices over time.
>
> A profitable business is never a choice, it is a series of
> choices.
>
> A fit body is never a choice, it is a series of choices.
>
> A strong relationship is never a choice, it is a series of
> choices.
>
> — James Clear

> Every choice is a brushstroke. No single stroke creates a
> masterpiece, but eventually the portrait emerges.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
