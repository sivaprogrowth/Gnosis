# Over-communicate because silence breeds false assumptions

Delaying a reply until you have an answer or good news leads others to guess at what the silence means. Communicating early and often prevents frustration and confusion.

> It's generally better to over-communicate.
>
> If you wait to reply because you don't have an answer yet (or
> because you don't want to share bad news), the other party often
> ends up making assumptions about what the delayed reply might
> mean.
>
> Silence frustrates and confuses people. Better to communicate
> early and often.
>
> — James Clear
