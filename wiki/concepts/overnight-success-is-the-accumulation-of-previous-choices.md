# Overnight success is the accumulation of previous choices

What looks like sudden success is really an opportunity releasing the stored energy of many earlier decisions. It is never one decision but everything that came before.

> Whenever you see an overnight success, your eyes deceive you.
>
> What you are witnessing is the hour of opportunity unleashing the
> potential energy of previous choices. It was not one decision,
> but the accumulated power of all that came before.
>
> The fuse was lit on a loaded cannon.
>
> — James Clear
