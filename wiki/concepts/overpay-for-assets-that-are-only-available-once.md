# Overpay for assets that are only available once

Bragg deliberately overpaid for acquisitions because unique opportunities don't come again, and his fair reputation brought him more deals than haggling would have.

> Bragg intentionally overpaid for acquisitions, sometimes double
> market value, because "it's only available once," and his
> reputation brought him more deals than fighting over pennies.
>
> — John Bragg, The Knowledge Project
