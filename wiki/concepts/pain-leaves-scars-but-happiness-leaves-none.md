# Pain leaves scars but happiness leaves none

We remember pain easily because it marks us, while happy and peaceful times leave no trace and teach us little.

> It's so hard to forget pain, but it's even harder to remember
> sweetness. We have no scar to show for happiness. We learn so
> little from peace.
>
> — Chuck Palahniuk, Diary
