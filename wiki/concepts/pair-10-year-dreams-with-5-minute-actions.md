# Pair 10-year dreams with 5-minute actions

Connect long-term aspirations to immediate small steps by asking where you want to be in a decade and what you can do in the next five minutes toward it.

> 10-year dreams. 5-minute actions.
>
> Where do I want to be in 10 years?
>
> What can I do in the next 5 minutes to contribute to that
> outcome?
>
> — James Clear
