# Parenthood means your heart walks outside your body

Becoming a parent means permanently accepting that what you love most lives outside of you and beyond your control.

> "It is to decide forever to have your heart go walking around
> outside your body."
>
> — Elizabeth Stone, A Boy I Once Knew
