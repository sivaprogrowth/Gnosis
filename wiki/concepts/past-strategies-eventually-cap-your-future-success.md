# Past strategies eventually cap your future success

What worked before will eventually hit its limit. Being willing to try new ideas keeps you from letting old choices set your ceiling.

> The strategies that made you successful in the past will, at
> some point, reach their limit.
>
> Don't let your previous choices set your future ceiling. The
> willingness to try new ideas allows you to keep advancing.
>
> — James Clear
