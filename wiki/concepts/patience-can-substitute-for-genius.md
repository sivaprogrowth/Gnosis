# Patience can substitute for genius

Instead of relying on frequent brilliant ideas, you can think about one topic for an unusually long time and revise repeatedly until only the best remains. It is slow but effective.

> I rarely have good ideas. To overcome this limitation, I think
> about one topic (like habits) for an unreasonable amount of time.
> Then, I revise, revise, revise until only the best stuff remains.
> It’s slow, but it works.You can either be a genius or you can be
> patient.
>
> — James Clear
