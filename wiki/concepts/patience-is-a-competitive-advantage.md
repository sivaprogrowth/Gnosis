# Patience is a competitive advantage

In many fields, success comes simply from being willing to keep doing the reasonable thing for longer than most people do.

> Patience is a competitive advantage.
>
> In a surprising number of fields, you can find success if you are
> simply willing to do the reasonable thing longer than most
> people.
>
> — James Clear
