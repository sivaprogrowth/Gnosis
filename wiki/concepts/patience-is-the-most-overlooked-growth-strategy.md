# Patience is the most overlooked growth strategy

The most underappreciated way to grow is patience, meaning consistently producing great work over a long time horizon.

> The most overlooked and underappreciated growth strategy is
> patience.
>
> (More specifically, consistently producing great work over a long
> time horizon.)
>
> — James Clear
