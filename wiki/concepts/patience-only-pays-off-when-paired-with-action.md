# Patience only pays off when paired with action

Patience produces results only when you are actually doing the work. Planning to work while being patient is just waiting.

> Patience only works if you do.
>
> Doing the work + patience = results.
>
> Planning to work + patience = you're just waiting.
>
> — James Clear

> My personal rule is that it's a good idea to be patient as long
> as I'm in the mix.
>
> If I'm taking action, putting in my reps, and trying things out,
> then I should remain patient and see what opportunities arise.
> But if I'm not taking action consistently, then I'm not
> practicing patience. I'm just waiting.
>
> — James Clear
