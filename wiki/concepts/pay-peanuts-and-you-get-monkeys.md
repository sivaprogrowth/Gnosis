# Pay peanuts and you get monkeys

Clients who haggle over agency fees miss where the leverage is. They should focus on getting results from the much larger amount they spend on media, because underpaying talent never made anyone rich.

> "Clients who haggle over their agency's compensation are looking
> through the wrong end of the telescope. Instead of trying to
> shave a few measly cents off the agency's fifteen per cent, they
> should concentrate on getting more sales results from the
> eighty-five per cent they spend on time and space. That is where
> the leverage is. No manufacturer ever got rich by underpaying his
> agency. Pay peanuts and you get monkeys."
>
> — David Ogilvy
