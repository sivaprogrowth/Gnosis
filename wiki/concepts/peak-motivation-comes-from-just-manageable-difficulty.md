# Peak motivation comes from just manageable difficulty

The Goldilocks Rule says people are most motivated on tasks that are neither too hard nor too easy. That zone keeps habits exciting.

> The Goldilocks Rule states that humans experience peak levels of
> motivation when working on tasks of just manageable difficulty.
> Not too hard, not too easy, just right.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 19
