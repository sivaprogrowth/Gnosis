# People are not rational, they are rationalizing

People decide first and justify afterward. Seeing this makes strange human behavior much easier to understand.

> “People are not rational. They are rationalizing. Once you
> understand this simple fact, all the oddest human behavior will
> suddenly make way more sense.”
>
> — Billy Markus
