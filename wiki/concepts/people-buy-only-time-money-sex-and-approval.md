# People buy only time, money, sex, and approval

Every purchase serves one of four desires: time, money, sex, or approval/peace of mind. Selling anything else fails.

> People buy 4 things and 4 things only. Ever. Those 4 things
> are time, money, sex, and approval/peace of mind. If you try
> selling something other than those 4 things you will fail.
>
> — Colin Dowling, Hacker News
