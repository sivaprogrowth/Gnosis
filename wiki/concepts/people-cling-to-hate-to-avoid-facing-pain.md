# People cling to hate to avoid facing pain

Hate can act as a defense mechanism. People hold onto it because letting it go would force them to confront the underlying pain.

> I imagine one of the reasons people cling to their hates so
> stubbornly is because they sense, once hate is gone, they will be
> forced to deal with pain.
>
> — James Baldwin, Me and My House
