# People delay releasing to protect the dream of success

Creators often hold back from shipping because releasing means exchanging an imagined success for real, possibly painful feedback. This reluctance explains much inaction.

> By far the dominant reason for not releasing sooner was a
> reluctance to trade the dream of success for the reality of
> feedback.
>
> — Kent Beck, Approaching a Minimum Viable Product (August 2009)
