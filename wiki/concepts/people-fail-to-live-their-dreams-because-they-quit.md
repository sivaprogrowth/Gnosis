# People fail to live their dreams because they quit

Sontag believed most people with youthful ideals fall short simply because they give up. Her answer was to decide never to quit.

> It never occurred to me that I couldn’t live the life I wanted
> to lead. It never occurred to me that I could be stopped...
>
> I had this very simple view: that the reason people who start out
> with ideals or aspirations don’t do what they dream of doing when
> they’re young is because they quit.
>
> I thought, well, I won’t quit.
>
> — Susan Sontag, Susan Sontag Lightens Up
