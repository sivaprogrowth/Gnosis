# People help underdogs but root against those who forget

When people feel you deserve more than you have, they go out of their way to help you. Once you forget you were the underdog, they start rooting against you.

> When people feel like you deserve more than you have, they'll go
> out of their way to help you. But the minute you forget you were
> once the underdog, they'll root against you.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
