# People on the edge see big things first

Staying near the edge without going over lets you see possibilities invisible from the center. Growth and originality come from the margins.

> I want to stay as close to the edge as I can without going over.
> Out on the edge you see all kinds of things you can't see from
> the center. Big, undreamed‑of things—the people on the edge see
> them first.
>
> — Kurt Vonnegut, Player Piano
