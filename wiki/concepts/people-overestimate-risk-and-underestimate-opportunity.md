# People overestimate risk and underestimate opportunity

Human nature leads us to see risks as bigger and opportunities as smaller than they really are. Correcting for this bias opens up more possibilities.

> “I think it's generally human nature to over-estimate risk and
> under-estimate opportunity. ... The risks are probably not as big
> as you perceive and the opportunities may be bigger than you
> percieve.”
>
> — Jeff Bezos
