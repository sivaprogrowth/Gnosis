# People overvalue their ability to judge talent in interviews

Interviewers are overconfident because interviewees are so skilled at interviewing, so interviews are a weak selection tool.

> Interviewees are so
> good at interviewing. ... I think everyone, including me,
> overvalues their ability to select talent in the interview
> process. I see that as overconfidence.
>
> — Brian Halligan, Scaling Culture From Startup to IPO (podcast)
