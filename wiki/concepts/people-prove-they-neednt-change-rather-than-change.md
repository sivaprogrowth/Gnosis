# People prove they needn't change rather than change

When faced with evidence that might change their mind, most people instead work to prove they don't need to change it. Open-mindedness is rare.

> Faced with the choice between changing one's mind and proving
> that there is no need to do so, almost everyone gets busy on the
> proof.
>
> — John Kenneth Galbraith, Economics, Peace and Laughter
