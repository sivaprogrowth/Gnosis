# People seek validating information, not accurate information

Most people prefer information that confirms what they already believe. Growing requires being willing to unlearn ideas that once served you.

> Most people don’t want accurate information, they want
> validating information.
>
> Growth requires you to be open to unlearning ideas that
> previously served you.
>
> — James Clear
