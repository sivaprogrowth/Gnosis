# Perfection is when nothing is left to remove

Completion comes through thoughtful elimination: something is finished when there is nothing left you can take away.

> “Your garden is not complete until there is nothing else you can
> take out of it.”
>
> — Japanese saying

> "A designer knows he has achieved perfection not when there is
> nothing left to add, but when there is nothing left to take
> away."
>
> — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
