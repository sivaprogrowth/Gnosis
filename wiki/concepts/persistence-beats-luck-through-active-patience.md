# Persistence beats luck through active patience

You don't need every break to go your way if you keep trying, since every winner has many losses and each attempt creates a chance to win. Be patient without being passive.

> You don’t need to catch every break if you’re willing to keep
> trying.
>
> Every winner has an archive of losses, but each attempt creates
> the chance for a victory.
>
> You need to be patient, but not passive. Active patience.
>
> — James Clear
