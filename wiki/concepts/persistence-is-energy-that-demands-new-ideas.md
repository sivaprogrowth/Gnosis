# Persistence is energy that demands new ideas

True persistence is not repeating the same approach harder but having restless energy that pushes you to imagine new solutions when one fails. Energy without imagination is mere force.

> Persistence isn't just pushing harder—it's having energy that
> demands new ideas.
>
> Think of a founder solving a problem. Someone with just
> determination keeps trying the same approach. But a truly
> persistent founder has a restless energy that demands new
> solutions. When one approach fails, their energy compels them to
> imagine new ones. This cycle—energy demanding imagination,
> imagination feeding energy—is rare.
>
> Energy without imagination is force. Energy with imagination is
> persistence.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
