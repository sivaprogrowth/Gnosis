# Persistence matters more than talent

Talent, genius, and education are common and often go unrewarded; persistence and determination are what actually produce results.

> Nothing in this world can take the place of persistence. Talent
> will not; nothing is more common than unsuccessful men with
> talent. Genius will not; unrewarded genius is almost a proverb.
> Education will not; the world is full of educated derelicts.
> Persistence and determination alone are omnipotent.
>
> — Calvin Coolidge

> It's persistence. It's that certain little spirit that compels
> you to stick it out just when you're at your most tired. It's
> that quality that forces you to persevere, find the route around
> the stone wall. It's the immovable stubbornness that will not
> allow you to cave in when everyone says give up
>
> — Estée Lauder

> What separates good work from great isn't talent but persistence.
>
> The most successful people aren't those who feel motivated all
> the time; they're the ones who work even when they don't feel
> like it. Too often, waiting to feel ready means never starting.
>
> Outliers act despite their feelings, not because of them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
