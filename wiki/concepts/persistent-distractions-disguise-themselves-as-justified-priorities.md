# Persistent distractions disguise themselves as justified priorities

The distractions that keep pulling you away are hard to escape because you convince yourself they are reasonable or necessary.

> A difficult lesson to learn: Your most persistent distractions will seem justified to you.
>
> — James Clear

> Which distractions in your life have become disguised as priorities?
>
> — James Clear
