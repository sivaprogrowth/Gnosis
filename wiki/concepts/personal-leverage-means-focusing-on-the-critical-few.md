# Personal leverage means focusing on the critical few

In personal development, leverage means finding the habits, skills, and relationships with the most impact and focusing energy there, where small changes cascade into big results.

> In personal development, leverage is about identifying the key
> habits, skills, and relationships that will impact your life and
> work most. It’s about focusing your energy on the critical few
> rather than the trivial many, about finding the points of maximum
> leverage where small changes can cascade into massive results.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
