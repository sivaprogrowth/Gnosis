# Placing problems in larger context shrinks them

Separating context from content helps you find solutions. Seeing a problem within the larger universe puts it in perspective and makes it feel smaller.

> “I think it may be easier to see solutions if you can distinguish
> between context and content. If you can place a problem within
> the framework of the larger universe its dimensions are put into
> perspective and automatically diminished.”
>
> — Peter Cundill
