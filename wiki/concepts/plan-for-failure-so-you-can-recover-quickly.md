# Plan for failure so you can recover quickly

Don't expect to fail, but have a plan ready for when you do. Having a plan for getting off track is the fastest way back on track.

> Never expect to fail, but always plan for it.
>
> The fastest way to get back on track is to have a plan for when
> you're off it.
>
> — James Clear
