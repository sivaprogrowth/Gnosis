# Plan like a lazy person, work like a dumb one

Before starting, assume others will work harder so you must find a better strategy. After starting, assume others are smarter so you must outwork them.

> Before you begin, think as if you are a lazy person. Imagine the
> competition will work harder. Your only chance is a better
> strategy.
>
> After you begin, work as if you are a dumb person. Imagine the
> competition is smarter and more talented. Your only chance is to
> outwork them.
>
> — James Clear
