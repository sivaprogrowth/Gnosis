# Plan when and where to act with implementation intentions

An implementation intention is a plan made in advance stating the behavior, time, and location. Research shows it makes people more likely to follow through on habits.

> The simple way to apply this strategy to your habits is to fill
> out this sentence:
>
> I will [BEHAVIOR] at [TIME] in [LOCATION].
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 5
