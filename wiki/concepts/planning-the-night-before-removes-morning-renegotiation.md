# Planning the night before removes morning renegotiation

Planning the night before matters most because it takes away your chance to re-plan, and so to waver, the next morning.

> The real reason to plan the night before is to deny you the
> option of planning again tomorrow morning.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
