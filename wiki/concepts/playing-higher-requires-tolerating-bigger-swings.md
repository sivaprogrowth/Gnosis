# Playing higher requires tolerating bigger swings

Big wins come with bigger decisions, more uncertainty, and greater risk. To play at a higher level you must accept larger mental, emotional, and financial highs and lows; avoiding the swings may keep you at a lower level.

> Big wins tend to be accompanied by some combination of bigger
> decisions, more uncertainty, and greater risk.
>
> If you want to play at a higher level, you need to be comfortable
> with greater swings of highs and lows: mentally, emotionally,
> financially.
>
> You can avoid the swings, but you might be forced to play at a
> lower level.
>
> — James Clear
