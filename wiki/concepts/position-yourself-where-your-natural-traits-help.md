# Position yourself where your natural traits help

Fighting your core traits is exhausting and futile. Instead, change your environment so that your instincts, personality, and preferences become advantages rather than limitations.

> Stop fighting your nature. Start winning with it.
>
> You're born with certain core traits. Fighting them is like being
> a sprinter forced to run marathons – exhausting and futile. But
> these "limitations" can become your biggest advantage.
>
> Your instincts, personality, and preferences aren't flaws -
> they're features.
>
> When something seems to be holding you back that you can't
> change, the key is to change your environment. What's a headwind
> in one situation is a tailwind in another.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Stop asking: "How do I fix myself?" Start asking: "How do I
> position myself where my natural traits are assets?"
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
