# Positivity and enthusiasm leave fear nowhere to go

If you're somewhere you don't want to be, it's your job to improve it by reframing positively and approaching it with enthusiasm. Applying this mindset everywhere helps create the outcome you want and corners fear.

> "If you’re in a place in life you don’t want to be, it’s up to
> you to make it better. To reframe it by focusing on the positive,
> and approaching it with enthusiasm. In the end, your positivity
> and enthusiasm will contribute to creating the outcome you’re
> after. Once you apply this mindset to all aspects of your life,
> you corner fear. Fear basically has nowhere to go."
>
> — Tom Brady
