# Possessions are good only if they increase giving

Possessions have no value in themselves. They are good when they help us give, and bad when they turn us inward or become ways to judge others.

> We must always remember that possessions have no inherent value.
> They become what we make them. If they increase our capacity to
> give, they become something good. If they increase our focus on
> ourselves and become standards by which we measure other people,
> they become something bad.
>
> — Kent Nerburn
