# Power is a responsibility to pass on, not a prize

Arnault sees power as stewardship: his job is to hand the houses on stronger than he found them, preserving craftsmanship rather than family privilege.

> “You describe power as though it were a prize to be won. I experience it as a responsibility to be passed on. The houses entrusted to me are older than I am. Most will outlive me. My role is to hand them on stronger than I found them. That is why I reject the idea of a dynasty. Dynasties exist to preserve privileges. We work to preserve craftsmanship.”
>
> — Bernard Arnault
