# Power is fleeting and never lasts

Power is temporary. Anyone who doubts it only needs to look at what happened to those who once held it.

> “Power is fleeting and it doesn’t last. And if you don’t
> believe that, take a look at anyone that’s had it.”
>
> — Michael Ovitz, The Knowledge Project podcast
