# Practice until you can't get it wrong

Practicing only until you succeed once leaves you dependent on perfect conditions. Practicing until failure becomes nearly impossible makes your performance hold up under any conditions.

> Practice until you get it right, and you'll need perfect
> conditions.
>
> Practice until you can't get it wrong, and conditions no longer
> matter.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
