# Praise others but expect no praise back

Giving praise to others brings them peace of mind, while not expecting praise from others brings you peace of mind.

> Praise others. It will bring them peace of mind.
>
> Do not expect others to praise you. It will bring you peace of
> mind.
>
> — James Clear
