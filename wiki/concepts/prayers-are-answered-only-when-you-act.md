# Prayers are answered only when you act

Hoping and waiting brought no results; only taking action did. Change comes from moving, not just wishing.

> I prayed for twenty years but received no answer until I prayed
> with my legs.
>
> — Frederick Douglass, Frederick Douglass: Autobiographies
