# Precisely defined problems are easier to solve

A vague problem has countless possible causes, while a specific one points straight to its solution. Defining the problem precisely makes the answer easier to find.

> The more precisely you define the problem, the more easily you
> can find a solution.
>
> "I feel bad" can have a million causes.
>
> "I didn't sleep much last night and I haven't exercised in a
> week" has a very straightforward answer.
>
> — James Clear
