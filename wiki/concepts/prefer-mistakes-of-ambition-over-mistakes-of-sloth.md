# Prefer mistakes of ambition over mistakes of sloth

Failing at a big goal or making something nobody wants is better than never trying or only consuming. Ambitious mistakes teach you something, while lazy mistakes just keep you comfortable.

> “Make mistakes of ambition and not mistakes of sloth.”
> —Machiavelli
>
> Mistakes of ambition:
> -failing on a big goal
> -creating something nobody wants
>
> Mistakes of sloth:
> -not attempting a big goal
> -consuming instead of creating
>
> Mistakes of ambition teach. Mistakes of sloth comfort.
>
> — James Clear
