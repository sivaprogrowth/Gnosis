# Preparation becomes procrastination when replacing action

Preparation helps only while it supports action. Once it replaces action, it has become a way to procrastinate.

> Planning and preparation are useful until they become a form of
> procrastination.
>
> Is this task enhancing my actions or substituting for them?
>
> — James Clear

> Is your reading and research supplementing your actions or
> substituting for them? Research is useful until it becomes a form
> of procrastination.
>
> — James Clear
