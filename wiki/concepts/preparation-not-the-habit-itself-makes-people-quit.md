# Preparation, not the habit itself, makes people quit

The core action of a habit is often the easiest part. The preparatory steps around it are what cause people to give up.

> Eating a healthy meal is
> actually the easiest part. It’s often the preparation that causes
> you to quit.
>
> — James Clear
