# Preparation positions you to benefit from lucky breaks

Many people aren't ready when luck arrives. Building skills, savings, and a network in advance lets you capitalize on unexpected opportunities.

> Many people are not ready for their lucky break when it comes to
> them.
>
> Develop your skills. Study your craft. Save some money. Build a
> network before you need it. Lay the ground work.
>
> The prepared person is positioned to benefit from unexpected
> opportunities.
>
> — James Clear
