# Preparation wins but only action earns credit

Two truths pull against each other: being more prepared usually leads to winning, yet people are recognized for what they do, not for how they prepared.

> Two competing truths:
>
> - The more prepared person usually wins.
>
> - You get credit for action, not preparation.
>
> — James Clear
