# Prepare for the known, position for the unknown

When you know what is coming, prepare specifically for it. When the future is uncertain, put yourself in a strong position, such as spending less than you earn, exercising, and being a reliable friend.

> When you know what's coming, prepare. When you don't know what's
> coming, position.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
