# Pressure is a privilege, not a threat

You feel pressure because your decisions matter and people depend on you. That discomfort is a sign of relevance, since no pressure means no one relies on you.

> Pressure feels like a threat, but it's not.
>
> You feel pressure when your decisions matter, and people depend
> on you. It can feel uncomfortable at times, but it's also a
> privilege. When no one relies on you — when no one expects
> something from you — you're irrelevant.
>
> Pressure is a privilege.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
