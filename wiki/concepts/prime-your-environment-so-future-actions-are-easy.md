# Prime your environment so future actions are easy

Set up a space in advance so it is ready for the habit when you walk in. Organizing a space for its intended purpose makes your future actions easy, as with laying out paints beforehand or moving the phone charger out of the bedroom.

> Whenever you organize a space for its intended purpose, you are
> priming it to make your future actions easy. Now your environment
> is ready for immediate use the next time around.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 12

> Prime your environment to make the next action easy.
>
> — James Clear
