# Prioritize 10-year goals worked on in 1-hour sessions

The two time frames worth prioritizing are 10 years and 1 hour. Pursue long-term, meaningful projects through focused sessions that finish something complete each time.

> 10 years or 1 hour. Those are the two time frames worth
> prioritizing.
>
> — James Clear
