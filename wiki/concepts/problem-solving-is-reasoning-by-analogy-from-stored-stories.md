# Problem solving is reasoning by analogy from stored stories

People solve problems by drawing on a vast store of commonsense anecdotes and somehow retrieving the few most relevant to the current situation.

> The way people solve problems is first by having an enormous
> amount of commonsense knowledge, like maybe 50 million little
> anecdotes or entries, and then having some unknown system for
> finding among those 50 million old stories the 5 or 10 that seem
> most relevant to the situation. This is reasoning by analogy.
>
> — Marvin Minsky
