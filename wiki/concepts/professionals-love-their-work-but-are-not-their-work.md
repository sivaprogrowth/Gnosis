# Professionals love their work but are not their work

A professional commits wholeheartedly to the work while keeping a boundary between the work and their identity.

> The professional loves her work. She is invested in it
> wholeheartedly. But she does not forget that the work is not
> her.
>
> — Steven Pressfield, The War of Art
