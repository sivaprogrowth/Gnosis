# Professionals produce many theories before hitting the jackpot

Amateurs cling to one favorite idea, while professionals keep generating idea after idea, knowing most will fail before one succeeds.

> It is amateurs who have one big bright beautiful idea that they
> can never abandon. Professionals know that they have to produce
> theory after theory before they are likely to hit the jackpot.
>
> — Francis Crick, What Mad Pursuit
