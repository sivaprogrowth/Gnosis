# Progress can be slow if you never stop

Progress can be slow, as long as you keep moving forward and don't regress.

> "Walk slowly, but never backward."
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits

> “To get through the hardest journey, we need take only one step
> at a time, but we must keep on stepping.”
>
> — Ancient Chinese proverb
