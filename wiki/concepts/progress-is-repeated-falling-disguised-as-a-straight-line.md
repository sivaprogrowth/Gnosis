# Progress is repeated falling disguised as a straight line

Progress looks smooth from the outside, but it is really a series of failures and recoveries repeated many times.

> Progress is just falling and getting back up over and over again,
> disguised as a straight line.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
