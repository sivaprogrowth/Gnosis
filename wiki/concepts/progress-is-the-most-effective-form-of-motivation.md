# Progress is the most effective form of motivation

Getting a signal that you are moving forward motivates you to keep going. Seeing progress is the strongest motivator.

> The most effective form of motivation is progress. When we get a
> signal that we are moving forward, we become motivated to
> continue down that path.
>
> — Greg McKeown, Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less
