# Progress needs confidence, humility, and skill-building together

Making progress means being confident enough to pursue what you want and humble enough to accept where you are now. You also need the willingness to build the skills that close the gap between the two.

> "The challenge for anyone interested in making progress is to
> simultaneously have (1) the confidence to go after what you want
> and (2) the humility to accept who you are right now and (3) the
> willingness to build skills that bridge the gap between 1 and 2."
>
> — James Clear
