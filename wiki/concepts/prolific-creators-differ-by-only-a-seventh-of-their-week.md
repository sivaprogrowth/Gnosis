# Prolific creators differ by only a seventh of their week

A prolific writer working 15 hours a week shares most of their life with someone who writes nothing. The difference is not constant work but consistency, which adds up to a book in about 40 weeks.

> The difference between the prolific writer and the self-loathing person who doesn't write anything is one writes 15 hours a week out of their 112 waking hours a week and the other does zero out of 112 waking hours. So 1/7th versus 0/7ths.
>
> 6/7ths of those two people's lives are the same! People who can't put something together have this daunting assumption that the prolific writer is fundamentally different, they're working constantly all the time. It doesn't have to be that way. It's consistency.
>
> — Tim Urban, The Tim Ferriss Show
