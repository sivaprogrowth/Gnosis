# Protégés absorb their mentor's thinking, cadence, and mannerisms

Parrish noticed that protégés like Mario Harik and Connor Teskey think, speak, and move like their mentors Brad Jacobs and Bruce Flatt. Close apprenticeship transmits far more than skills.

> Something I'd never noticed until recently: when I was talking
> to Mario Harik, Brad Jacobs' protégé, I kept feeling like I was talking to Brad. I went
> back and watched Brad's video to check — same way of thinking,
> same cadence, same mannerisms. So I wondered about Bruce Flatt and Connor Teskey. Sure enough, same pattern.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
