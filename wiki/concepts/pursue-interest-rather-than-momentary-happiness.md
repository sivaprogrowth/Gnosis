# Pursue interest rather than momentary happiness

Georgia O'Keeffe found happiness too fleeting to aim for. Being busy and interested in something held more meaning for her.

> I do not like the idea of happiness — it is too momentary. I
> would say that I was always busy and interested in something —
> interest has more meaning to me than the idea of happiness.
>
> — Georgia O'Keeffe, The Complete Correspondence of Georgia O’Keeffe & Anita Pollitzer
