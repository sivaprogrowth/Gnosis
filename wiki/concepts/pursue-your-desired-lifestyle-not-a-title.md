# Pursue your desired lifestyle, not a title

Fixating on the status of a specific title or path blinds you to other ways of getting the joy, purpose, or freedom you actually want. Focus on the substance of what you want, not the label.

> Don't let a title become a trap.
>
> * "I have to be a doctor."
> * "I need to get into that school."
> * "I can't quit this job—it's Google."
>
> There are many ways to live an amazing life. Stop thinking about
> the status of this particular title and focus on the substance of
> what you want. You’ll often find there are many ways to get
> similar amounts of joy, purpose, or freedom.
>
> If you attach your happiness to a single path, you'll become
> blind to other wonderful opportunities around you. And many of
> them might be a better fit for you or deliver a similar result
> with less struggle.
>
> — James Clear

> Chase your desired lifestyle, not your desired title. People are blinded by status and labels. Once you release the need for a specific title, there is almost always an easier path to living your preferred lifestyle.
>
> — James Clear

> Optimize for your desired lifestyle, not your desired title.
>
> — James Clear
