# Pursuing excellence beats pursuing bigness for satisfaction

Choosing to stay small and excellent may earn less money than chasing scale, but it can be more satisfying.

> “I have no ambition to preside over a vast bureaucracy. That is
> why we have only nineteen clients. The pursuit of excellence is
> less profitable than the pursuit of bigness, but it can be more
> satisfying.”
>
> — David Ogilvy
