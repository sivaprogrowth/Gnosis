# Push your ideals but always listen to customers

A business has to balance its ideals against what the market wants, because customers vote with their money. Mackey compares it to a dance: if you don't meet your partner where they are, they dance away.

> I always like to say we’re always pushing
> our ideals, but always listening to our customers. It’s like a
> dialogue you’re having or a dance. And if you don’t dance with
> your partner, your partner’s going to dance away from you, so you
> have to meet them where you find them.
>
> — John Mackey, The Knowledge Project podcast
