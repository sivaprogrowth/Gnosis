# Put the most important point in the first line

Smart people often bury their most valuable point at the end. Lead with it, cut everything unnecessary, and keep writing clear, conversational, and short.

> The first line is the most important.
>
> Too many otherwise smart people bury the most valuable part of
> the message at the end. Delete everything but what's necessary.
>
> Good writing is clear, conversational, and as short as possible.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
