# Putting things off risks it becoming too late

Telling ourselves we'll do something later risks waking up to find it will never happen, not from failure but because it became too late to try.

> When we tell ourselves we'll do something later we run the risk
> of waking up one day and finally realizing it's just not going to
> happen, not because we failed, but rather because it just became
> too late to try.
>
> — Strauss Zelnick, Success: A Concise Guide to Having The Life You Want
