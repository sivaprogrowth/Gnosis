# Quick riches depend on luck, eventual riches on consistency

Getting rich fast is mostly a matter of luck. Getting rich over time is mostly a matter of being consistent.

> If you want to get rich quickly, the biggest factor is luck.
>
> If you want to get rich eventually, the biggest factor is
> consistency.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
