# Raise ambitions while lowering expectations

High ambitions push you to take bold actions, while low expectations make you more satisfied with results. Combining both lets you achieve more and stay happy.

> Raise your ambitions. Lower your expectations.
>
> The higher your ambitions, the bolder your actions.
>
> The lower your expectations, the greater your satisfaction.
>
> Achieve more and be happy along the way.
>
> — James Clear
