# Rapid shifts are usually preceded by gradual habits

Changes that look sudden are often the result of small daily habits building up over time, leading results to either explode or vanish. Ask which radical change you are slowly moving toward.

> What appears to be a rapid shift is often preceded by a gradual
> process. Our results gradually explode or vanish thanks to the
> small habits we repeat each day.
>
> What radical change are you slowly marching toward? An
> incremental explosion or an incremental vanishing?
>
> — James Clear
