# Reacting without first principles yields poor strategy

If you only respond to the facts in front of you instead of reasoning from first principles, you are almost certain to get strategic decisions wrong.

> “If you're responding to the facts in front of you and not
> thinking from first principles… the likelihood that you'll make
> the right strategic decision is almost zero.”
>
> — [[bret-taylor|Bret Taylor]], The Knowledge Project
