# Read the best books and write the best ideas

It is not enough to simply read or write. Aim for quality by choosing the best book available and writing the best idea you can come up with.

> Read, but don't just read. Read the best book you can find.
>
> Write, but don't just write. Write the best idea you can
> conceive.
>
> — James Clear
