# Reading aloud to parents educated Gilded Age outliers

Andrew Mellon and Hetty Green both learned a great deal by reading to their fathers, possibly more than from formal education; Hetty knew more finance than many financiers by fifteen.

> More than one outlier from the Gilded Age learned from reading to
> their parents.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Mellon by David Cannadine; Hetty by Charles Slack
