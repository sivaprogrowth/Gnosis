# Reading lets you borrow original ideas

Rather than struggling to generate original ideas alone, you can shortcut the process by finding them in books.

> You could try to pound your head against the wall and think of
> original ideas or you can cheat by reading them in books.
>
> — [[patrick-collison|Patrick Collison]], Patrick Collison on Hiring at Stripe and the Role of a Product-Focused CEO
