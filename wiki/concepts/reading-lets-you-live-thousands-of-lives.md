# Reading lets you live thousands of lives

Books are the greatest bargain because they let a reader experience many lives, times, and places, and great books hold a lifetime of wisdom. Enjoying even nonsense teaches us to laugh at ourselves, which is necessary for being truly educated and happy.

> I consider reading the greatest bargain in the world. A shelf of
> books is a shelf of many lives and ideas and imaginations which
> the reader can enjoy whenever he wishes and as often as he
> wishes. Instead of experiencing just one life, the book-lover can
> experience hundreds or even thousands of lives.
>
> — Gene Roddenberry, Letters to Star Trek
