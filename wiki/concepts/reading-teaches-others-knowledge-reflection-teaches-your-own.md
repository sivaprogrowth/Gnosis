# Reading teaches others' knowledge, reflection teaches your own

Reading gives you the best of what other people already know. Reflection gives you the best of what only you can know.

> Reading can teach you the best of what others already know.
>
> Reflection can teach you the best of what only you can know.
>
> — James Clear
