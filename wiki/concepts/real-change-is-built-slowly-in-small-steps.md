# Real change is built slowly in small steps

Real change is not sudden but built slowly every day through effort, humility, and setbacks, and watching it take shape can be thrilling.

> "Change is not a bolt of lightning that arrives with a zap. It is
> a bridge built brick by brick, every day, with sweat and humility
> and slips. It is hard work, and slow work, but it can be
> thrilling to watch it take shape."
>
> — Sarah Hepola, My relapse years

> Large, sweeping changes often sound good in theory, but the faster you try to change your life, the more likely you are to backslide. Real change is inch by inch. Go out and win the next five minutes.
>
> — James Clear
