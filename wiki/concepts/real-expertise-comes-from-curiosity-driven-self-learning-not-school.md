# Real expertise comes from curiosity-driven self-learning, not school

People who are truly good at what they do rarely credit school; they learned on their own because they were curious, not to pass a test.

> Ask anyone how they got really good at what they do, and there is one answer they won't tell you: ... School.
>
> They learned it on their own. Not because there was a test to pass, but because they were curious.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
