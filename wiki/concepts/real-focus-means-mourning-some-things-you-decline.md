# Real focus means mourning some things you decline

A test for time management: if saying no doesn't cost you anything you'd genuinely miss, you aren't focused enough.

> A simple filter for managing your time: You're not focused
> enough unless you're mourning some of the things you're saying no
> to.
>
> — James Clear
