# Real growth comes from releasing comforts, not just vices

Removing bad habits brings some growth. The biggest leaps come when you stop holding on to what is comfortable.

> Plenty of growth can be found in removing your vices, but the real leaps are unlocked when you stop clinging to your comforts.
>
> — James Clear
