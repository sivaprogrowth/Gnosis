# Real wealth is freedom, not money

True wealth means freedom from meetings, jerks, status games, obligatory yeses, and others claiming your time. Money can help, but many people with lots of money aren't free.

> Real wealth is not about money.
>
> Real wealth is:
> -not having to go to meetings
> -not having to spend time with jerks
> -not being locked into status games
> -not feeling like you have to say “yes”
> -not worrying about others claiming your time and energy
>
> Real wealth is about freedom. Money can help achieve these
> things, but there are plenty of people who make lots of money yet
> aren't free.
>
> — James Clear
