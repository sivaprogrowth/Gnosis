# Reality eventually overrules a compelling narrative

A good story can rally people for a while, but reality decides in the end. A company either generates cash or it doesn't, and its financial statements show this no matter how charismatic its managers are.

> Truth trumps narrative. You can temporarily rally people around
> what sounds good, but reality eventually imposes its verdict.
>
> A company either generates cash or it doesn't, regardless of how
> compelling the story. Financial statements reveal truths that
> even the most charismatic management teams cannot talk their way
> around.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
