# Realizing nothing is lacking makes the world yours

Contentment with what you have and acceptance of how things are brings a sense of abundance in which nothing is missing.

> Be content with what you have; rejoice in the way things are.
> When you realize there is nothing lacking, the whole world
> belongs to you.
>
> — Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching
