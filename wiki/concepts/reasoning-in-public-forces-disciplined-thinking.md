# Reasoning in public forces disciplined thinking

Showing your reasoning openly is like doing math on a chalkboard: every step must be clear or others will catch the error. If you are right you have nothing to fear, and if you are wrong you get to learn.

> Reasoning in public forces discipline of thought. It's like doing math on the chalkboard in front of the class. Every step must be clear and correct, or someone will point out the mistake. There's no room for fuzzy thinking or skipped steps. If your reasoning is correct, you have nothing to fear. If it's wrong, you have everything to learn.
