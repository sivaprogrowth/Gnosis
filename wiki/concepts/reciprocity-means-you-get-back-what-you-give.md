# Reciprocity means you get back what you give

People tend to return the treatment they receive. Cynicism brings cynicism, and opportunity and the benefit of the doubt bring the same back.

> Reciprocation teaches us that if you give people cynicism and
> curtness or nothing at all, you are likely to receive the same.
> But if you give people an opportunity and the benefit of the
> doubt, you will often be on the receiving end of the same
> behavior.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
