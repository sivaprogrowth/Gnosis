# Recognize life-changing moments and seize them immediately

Every life has a pivotal moment that changes it permanently; to make the change a good one, you must spot it and act without delay.

> In every life there is a moment—an event or a
> realization-that changes that life irrevocably. If the change is
> to be a happy one, one must be able to recognize the moment and
> seize it without delay.
>
> — Estée Lauder
