# Redirect worry energy into one useful controllable action

The energy spent worrying about the past, the future, others' opinions, or possible failure is better channeled into a single useful action you can control.

> "Take all the energy you spend on...
>
> * worrying about the past
> * worrying about the future
> * worrying about what others think
> * worrying about if you might fail
>
> ... and channel that energy into one useful action within your
> control."
>
> — James Clear
