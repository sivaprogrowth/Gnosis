# Reducing friction boosts productivity as much as effort

Net productivity is the balance of productive and unproductive forces in your life. Besides increasing effort, you can gain a lot by removing the friction that slows you down.

> Your net productivity is the balance of the productive and
> unproductive forces in your life.
>
> A great deal of time and energy is spent thinking about how to
> increase effort, but there is a lot to be gained by reducing
> friction.
>
> A car will travel faster not only if you press the accelerator,
> but also if you remove the speed bumps.
>
> — James Clear
