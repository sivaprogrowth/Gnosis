# Reducing needs beats chasing higher returns

Lowering what you need to be happy has the same effect as raising income, but it is more certain and within your control, so it has higher expected value. People pour huge effort into small investment gains while ignoring lifestyle bloat, which is true at every level of wealth.

> The idea that reducing your needs has the same impact as increasing your income – but the former is more certain and in your control than the latter, so it has a higher expected value – is as true for someone spending \$15,000 a year as it is someone spending \$15 million per year.
>
> — Morgan Housel, The Biggest Returns
