# Reframe setbacks as healing, learning, and redirection

Simple mindset shifts can reframe negative events: being hurt as healing, losing as learning, rejection as redirection. Negative events happen, but negative mindsets make them harder.

> Simple mindset shifts:
>
> - I'm not hurt, I'm healing.
>
> - I'm not losing, I'm learning.
>
> - I was not rejected, I was redirected.
>
> Negative things happen. Negative mindsets make them harder.
>
> — James Clear
