# Refuse to let failure define the outcome

The goal is not to avoid failure altogether but to keep failure from determining how things ultimately turn out.

> The lesson isn't avoiding failure. It's refusing to let failure
> define the outcome.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
