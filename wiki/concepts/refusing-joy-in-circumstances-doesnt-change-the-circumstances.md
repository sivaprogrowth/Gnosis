# Refusing joy in circumstances doesn't change the circumstances

If you refuse to enjoy what you can't change, you simply lose joy while the situation stays the same.

> If you choose not to find joy in the snow, you will have less joy in your life but still the same amount of snow.
>
> — Unknown
