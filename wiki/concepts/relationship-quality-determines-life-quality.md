# Relationship quality determines life quality

You can't always tell in advance which moments matter, but the quality of your relationships shapes the quality of your life, so invest even in connections that seem minor.

> Life will present you with unexpected opportunities, and you
> won't always know in advance which are the important moments.
> Above all, it's the quality of your relationships that will
> determine the quality of your life. Invest in your connections,
> even those that seem inconsequential.
>
> — Esther Perel, Tribe of Mentors

> “The quality of your relationships determines the quality of your
> life.”
>
> — Esther Perel
