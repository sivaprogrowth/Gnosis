# Reliability in normal moments beats chasing peak performance

Results often improve more from showing up consistently and doing the fundamentals well than from pursuing peak performance. Simple improvements are usually available before anything complicated is needed.

> Forget about peak performance. Would your results improve if you
> simply focused on being reliable in the normal moments?
>
> Show up when it's easy to skip. Do the fundamentals and do them
> well. And so on.
>
> Before you make it complicated, remember there are always simple
> improvements waiting to be made.
>
> — James Clear
