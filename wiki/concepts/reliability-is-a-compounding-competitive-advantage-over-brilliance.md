# Reliability is a compounding competitive advantage over brilliance

Brilliance is worthless if people can't count on you. Being reliable can let you outperform smarter people, and its benefits compound over time.

> Being brilliant won't save you if no one can count on you.
>
> It can be hard to appreciate just how much reliability impacts
> opportunity. It's fun to outperform people who are way smarter
> than you by being more reliable than they are.
>
> Reliability isn't just a virtue - it's a competitive advantage
> that compounds over time.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
