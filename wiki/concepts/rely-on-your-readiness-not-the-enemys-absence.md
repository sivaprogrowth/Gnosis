# Rely on your readiness, not the enemy's absence

Don't count on threats not arriving; prepare so thoroughly that your position cannot be attacked successfully.

> The art of war teaches us to rely not on the likelihood of the
> enemy's not coming, but on our own readiness to receive him; not
> on the chance of his not attacking, but rather on the fact that
> we have made our position unassailable.
>
> — Sun Tzu, The Art of War
