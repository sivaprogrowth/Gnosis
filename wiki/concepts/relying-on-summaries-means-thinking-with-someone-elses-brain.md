# Relying on summaries means thinking with someone else's brain

A summary reflects someone else's priorities and filters about what matters. Operating on summaries replaces your own judgment with theirs.

> Everyone wants the summary. But the summary is what's left after
> someone else decided what matters. Their priorities aren't yours.
> Their filters aren't yours. When you operate on summaries, you're
> thinking with someone else's brain.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
