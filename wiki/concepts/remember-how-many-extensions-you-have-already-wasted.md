# Remember how many extensions you have already wasted

Reflect on how long you have procrastinated and how many chances you were given but didn't use, as a spur to act now.

> Remember how long you’ve been putting this off, how many
> extensions the gods gave you, and you didn’t use them.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations
