# Remove the bend in the hose instead of forcing water

Pumping up motivation to overpower friction is exhausting; reshaping your environment to remove the friction lets behavior flow naturally.

> Trying to pump up your motivation to stick with a hard habit
> and overpower the friction in your environment is like trying to
> force water through a bent hose. You can do it, but it requires a
> lot of effort and increases the tension in your life.
>
> — James Clear, Adapted from Josh Waitzkin on The Tim Ferriss Show, Episode 2
