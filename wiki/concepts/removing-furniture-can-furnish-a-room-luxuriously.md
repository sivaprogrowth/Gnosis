# Removing furniture can furnish a room luxuriously

Luxury and quality can come from subtraction rather than addition; taking things away can improve a space.

> One can furnish a room very luxuriously by taking out furniture
> rather than putting it in.
>
> — Francis Jourdain, The Simple Guide to a Minimalist Life
