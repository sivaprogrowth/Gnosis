# Repeatedly asking what you want sharpens the answer

Writing the same question each morning, "What do I actually want?", made the answer more precise over time. Once the answer was clear, it could be turned into action steps.

> For a few weeks, I started each morning by writing “What do I
> actually want?” at the top of a blank page.It’s surprising how
> useful it is to keep asking the same question. Each time, my
> answer became more precise.Once I knew what I wanted, I turned
> it into action steps.
>
> — James Clear

> Ask yourself this question three times and try to refine and
> improve your answer each time: What do I really want?
>
> — James Clear
