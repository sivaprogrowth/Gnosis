# Repetition, stable context, and positive emotion make habits stick

Habits form through frequent repetition rather than elapsed time. They also need a reliable environment and good feelings that make you want to repeat them.

> 3 things that help habits stick:
>
> 1) Repetition. Habits form based on frequency, not time.
>
> 2) Stable context. If the context is always changing, so is the
> behavior. You need a reliable environment.
>
> 3) Positive emotions. If it feels good, you’ll want to repeat
> it.
>
> — James Clear
