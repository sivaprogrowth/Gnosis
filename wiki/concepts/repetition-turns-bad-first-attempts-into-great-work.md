# Repetition turns bad first attempts into great work

Your first attempts at anything will be weak, but after a thousand repetitions you will be good. Improvement comes from putting in the reps.

> Your 1st blog post will be bad, but your 1000th will be great.
>
> Your 1st workout will be weak, but your 1000th will be strong.
>
> Your 1st meditation will be scattered, but your 1000th will be
> focused.
>
> Put in your reps.
>
> — James Clear
