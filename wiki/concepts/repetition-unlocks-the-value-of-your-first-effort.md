# Repetition unlocks the value of your first effort

The value of a first workout, article, or conversation grows the more you keep repeating the activity. Your starting point keeps compounding through continued practice.

> Repetition unlocks value.
>
> The value of your first workout increases the more you exercise.
>
> The value of your first article increases the more you write.
>
> The value of your first conversation increases the longer you
> stay in the relationship.
>
> Day One continues to compound.
>
> — James Clear
