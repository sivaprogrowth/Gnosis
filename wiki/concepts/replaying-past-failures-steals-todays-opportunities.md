# Replaying past failures steals today's opportunities

Replaying past failures and mistakes steals time from the future. Treat the past as a teacher rather than a judge, because your next move matters more than your last mistake.

> Don't let your worst moments become your story.
>
> You blow the presentation and spend the drive home scripting
> perfect comebacks to an audience that's already gone. Your
> relationship ends and you spend months replaying conversations
> that can't be changed. All the time spent perfecting the past is
> stolen from the future that's still waiting.
>
> The past is a teacher, not a judge. Your next move matters more
> than your last mistake.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> If you spend too much time thinking about the past, you miss out on what you have right now.
>
> Reflecting, learning, and moving on is a fruitful cycle. But replaying old mistakes and revisiting past injustices is a fast way to lose the opportunity of today.
>
> Don't let one bad event beat you twice.
>
> — James Clear
