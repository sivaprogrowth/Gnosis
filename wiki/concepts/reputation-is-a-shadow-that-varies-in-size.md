# Reputation is a shadow that varies in size

Your reputation doesn't match you exactly; sometimes it exceeds who you are and sometimes it falls short.

> Reputation is like a shadow. Sometimes it's bigger than you, and
> sometimes it's smaller.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
