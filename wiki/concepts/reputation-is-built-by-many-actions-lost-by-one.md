# Reputation is built by many actions, lost by one

A good reputation takes many actions to earn but can be destroyed by a single one. It is unfair to judge someone by one act, but the world often does, so guard against weaknesses that risk it.

> "A good reputation is acquired by many actions; and can be lost
> by one. Be upon your guard, therefore, against those weaknesses
> which may risk it. Nothing can be more unjust than to judge a
> person by one single action; but the world is seldom just."
>
> — Philip Dormer Stanhope, 4th Earl of Chesterfield, Letters to His Son
