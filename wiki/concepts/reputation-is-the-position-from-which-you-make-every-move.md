# Reputation is the position from which you make every move

Like a billiards professional who plays for the next shot, you should treat reputation as a strategic asset: present actions set up or trap your future options. Pass on flashy wins that leave you poorly positioned.

> Your reputation isn't just what people say about you—it's the
> position from which you make every move.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
