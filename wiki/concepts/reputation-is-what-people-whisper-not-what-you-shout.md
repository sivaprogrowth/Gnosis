# Reputation is what people whisper, not what you shout

Your reputation is not built by what you say about yourself loudly. It is what other people say about you quietly when you are not around.

> Your reputation isn’t what you shout, it’s what people whisper.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
