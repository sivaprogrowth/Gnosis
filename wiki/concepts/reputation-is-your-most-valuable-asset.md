# Reputation is your most valuable asset

Your reputation is your most valuable asset because people know it before they meet you and remember it long after the work ends.

> Your reputation is your most important asset. It precedes you
> before you walk into the room and lingers long after the work is
> done.
>
> — James Clear

> Your name is your biggest asset.
>
> — Ed Stack, The Knowledge Project podcast episode on Ed Stack
