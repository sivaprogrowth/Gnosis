# Requalify for your role every year

Act as if you could lose your job each year; if you're not requalifying annually, you're already falling behind.

> The president believes that if
> you're not requalifying for your role annually, you're already
> falling behind.
>
> — Harley Finkelstein, The Knowledge Project
