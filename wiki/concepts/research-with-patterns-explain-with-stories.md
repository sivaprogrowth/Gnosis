# Research with patterns, explain with stories

When evaluating strategies, rely on patterns across many cases rather than single success stories. When communicating strategies, use stories because people remember them better than data.

> When researching strategies, emphasize patterns over stories.
> One person succeeding means nothing. 100 people succeeding is a
> signal.
>
> When explaining strategies, emphasize stories over patterns.
> People forget numbers and charts. Everyone remembers a great
> story.
>
> — James Clear
