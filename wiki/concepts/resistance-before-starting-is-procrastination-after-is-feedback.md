# Resistance before starting is procrastination; after is feedback

Resistance felt before beginning usually signals procrastination, so you should start. Resistance felt after beginning usually signals feedback, so you should adjust.

> If you feel resistance before you begin, it's usually
> procrastination and you need to get started.
>
> If you feel resistance after you begin, it's usually feedback and
> you need to make adjustments.
>
> — James Clear
