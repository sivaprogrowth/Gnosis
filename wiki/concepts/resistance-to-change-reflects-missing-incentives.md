# Resistance to change reflects missing incentives

When people say they don't want to change, they usually lack an incentive to change. Change the incentives and behavior will follow.

> When people say they don’t want to change what they really mean
> is they don’t have an incentive to change.
>
> Change the incentives and the behavior follows suit.
>
> — James Clear
