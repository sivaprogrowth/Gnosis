# Resourcefulness finds an affordable way into business

Unable to afford buses, K.C. Irving built his own by cutting Ford bodies in two and mounting them on imported chassis, simply finding an affordable way into the business.

> The solution: he decided to cut Ford bodies into
> two, add space to put in seats, and then sit them on top of
> imported British Leyland chassis.
>
> — Donald Savoie, Thanks for the Business: K.C. Irving, Arthur Irving, and the Story of Irving Oil
