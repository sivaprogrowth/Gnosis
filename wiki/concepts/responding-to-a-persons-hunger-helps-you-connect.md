# Responding to a person's hunger helps you connect

Understanding what someone deeply wants and responding to it is a powerful way to reach and connect with anyone in business or personal life.

> Understanding a person's hunger and responding to it is one of
> the most potent tools you'll ever discover for getting through to
> anyone you meet in business or your personal life.
>
> — Mark Goulston, Just Listen
