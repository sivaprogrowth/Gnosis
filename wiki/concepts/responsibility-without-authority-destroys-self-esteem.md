# Responsibility without authority destroys self-esteem

Giving people responsibility makes them feel important, but if they are not also given the authority to act, it can damage their self-esteem.

> “People also feel important when they’re given responsibility. But responsibility without authority can destroy a person’s self-esteem.”
>
> — Mary Kay Ash
