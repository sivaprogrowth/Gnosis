# Restlessness plus high standards finds the right opportunities

High standards keep you from chasing every new thing and make you patient, while restlessness keeps you searching. Together they help you notice and act quickly on opportunities that truly meet your bar.

> It can be a great advantage to (1) be somewhat restless and (2)
> have very high standards.
>
> Holding yourself to a high standard prevents you from jumping
> into whatever new thing comes your way. It encourages you to be
> thoughtful and ensures you remain patient for the right
> opportunity.
>
> However—because you are somewhat restless—you are always digging
> around and watching for opportunities. When most people stop
> searching, you continue to explore. And when an opportunity comes
> your way that meets your standard, you'll be more likely to
> notice it and more likely to act upon it immediately.
>
> — James Clear
