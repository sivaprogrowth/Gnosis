# Restlessness signals it's time to leave

Restlessness shows you've outgrown where you are; our love of stability holds us back, so it's best to leave just before you feel ready, since leaving too late misses the opportunity.

> Restlessness is a sign you've overstayed your welcome with where
> you are and need to leave by moving toward the person you can be.
> Our love of stability keeps us where we are. The key is to leave
> just before you’re ready. If you leave too late, you miss the
> opportunity.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
