# Results equal hard work times time, raised to strategy

Working on the right thing matters more than working hard, because a great strategy multiplies results exponentially. But strategy is worthless without effort, since zero to any power is still zero.

> The math of success...
>
> Results = (Hard Work*Time)^Strategy
>
> Working hard is important, but working on the right thing is more
> important. A great strategy can deliver exponential results.
>
> Of course, the best strategy is worth nothing if you never get to
> work. Zero to the millionth power is still zero.
>
> — James Clear
