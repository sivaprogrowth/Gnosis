# Results go to those who enjoy the preceding lifestyle

People who get results are usually the ones who enjoy the daily routines that lead to those results. Enjoying the process is what makes the outcome likely.

> Results tend to accumulate to the person who enjoys the
> lifestyle that precedes the result.
>
> — James Clear
