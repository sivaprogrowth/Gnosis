# Returning to one project daily lets work compound

Jumping between projects divides your effort, which makes quality work hard. Coming back to the same project every day lets even a relaxed workday be productive, and the results build on each other.

> People who jump from project to project are always dividing
> their effort, and producing high quality work becomes difficult
> without intense effort.
>
> Meanwhile, your average work day can be leisurely, yet also
> productive, if you return to the same project each day.
>
> Do one thing well and watch it compound.
>
> — James Clear
