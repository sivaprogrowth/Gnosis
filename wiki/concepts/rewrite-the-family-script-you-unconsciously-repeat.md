# Rewrite the family script you unconsciously repeat

Many habits and beliefs come from the people who raised us. Noticing which family patterns we repeat lets us change them for ourselves and the next generation.

> Many of our habits and beliefs are learned from those who raised us. Which family story are you unconsciously repeating and reenacting? How can you rewrite the script to liberate yourself and the next generation?
>
> — James Clear
