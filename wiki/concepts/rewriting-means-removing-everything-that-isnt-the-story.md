# Rewriting means removing everything that isn't the story

The first draft is how a writer discovers the story; revision is mostly cutting whatever doesn't belong to it.

> “When you write a story, you’re telling yourself the story. When
> you rewrite, your main job is taking out all the things that are
> not the story.”
>
> — Stephen King, On Writing: A Memoir of the Craft
