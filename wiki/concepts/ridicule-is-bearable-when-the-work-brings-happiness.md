# Ridicule is bearable when the work brings happiness

Being mocked as an outsider, even within an outcast activity, was crushing, but the happiness the work gave made the disappointment worth enduring. Facing that early built resilience against later criticism.

> But skateboarding gave me much more happiness, so I was prepared to deal with that disappointment. So, yeah, the years of becoming the king sellout, according to the hard-core skaters — I didn’t care. I learned to be resilient early in life.
>
> — Unknown
