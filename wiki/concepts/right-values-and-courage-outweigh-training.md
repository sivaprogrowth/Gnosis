# Right values and courage outweigh training

Kay Graham, with no training or experience, grew into running the Washington Post. Her story shows you can grow into a seemingly impossible job, and that the right values and the courage to act on them matter more than training.

> Graham proved you can grow into a job that seems impossible and
> that no amount of training can substitute for having the right
> values and the courage to act on them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project [Outliers]
