# Rigid demands on life make you needlessly miserable

Dogmatically insisting that you must succeed, be approved, be treated fairly, and live easily creates needless misery. These rigid beliefs can also undermine your most important goals.

> "When you rigidly hold certain irrational beliefs—when you
> dogmatically command that you must do well, have to be approved
> by others, have got to have people treat you fairly, and always
> ought to live with easy and enjoyable conditions—when you stoutly
> hold these irrational beliefs, you will tend to make yourself
> needlessly miserable and will probably defeat some of your most
> cherished goals."
>
> — Albert Ellis
