# Rigidly held beliefs, not beliefs themselves, cause harm

Having beliefs is not the problem; the trouble comes when we hold them rigidly without examination, treat our views as central, and look down on others.

> It’s not the existence of beliefs that is the problem, but what
> happens to us when we hold them rigidly, without examining them,
> when we presume the absolutely centrality of our views and become
> disdainful of others.
>
> — Sharon Salzberg, Faith: Trusting Your Own Deepest Experience
