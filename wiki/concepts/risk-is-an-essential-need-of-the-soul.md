# Risk is an essential need of the soul

People need risk. Without it, boredom sets in and paralyzes almost as much as fear does.

> Risk is an essential need of the soul. The absence of risk
> produces a kind of boredom which paralyses in a different way
> from fear, but almost as much.
>
> — Simone Weil, The Need for Roots
