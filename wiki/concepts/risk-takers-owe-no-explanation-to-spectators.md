# Risk-takers owe no explanation to spectators

People who take bold leaps don't need to justify themselves to those who only watch from the sidelines.

> “He who jumps into the void owes no explanation to those who
> stand and watch.”
>
> — Jean-Luc Godard, Godard On Godard
