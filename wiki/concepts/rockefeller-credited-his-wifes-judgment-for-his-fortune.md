# Rockefeller credited his wife's judgment for his fortune

Rockefeller said his wife Cettie's judgment was better than his own and that her advice was essential to his success.

> “Her judgment was always better than mine. Without her keen advice, I would be a poor man.”
>
> — John D. Rockefeller, Rockefeller Archives
