# Routines turn desired behavior into default behavior

Lacking a routine causes more problems than making poor choices, because routines make the behavior you want automatic.

> "A lack of routine causes more problems than poor choices.
> Routines turn desired behavior into default behavior."
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
