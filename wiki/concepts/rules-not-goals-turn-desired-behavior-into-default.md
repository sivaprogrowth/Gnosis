# Rules, not goals, turn desired behavior into default

Kahneman stopped saying yes to unwanted things by creating a firm rule rather than a goal or intention. A rule reprograms the unconscious so the desired behavior becomes automatic.

> Your Rules Become Your Default: Danny was human just like us;
> he often said yes to things he didn’t want to do. So he created a
> rule. Not a goal, not an intention, a rule. It reprogrammed his
> unconscious mind, turning his desired behavior into his default
> behavior.
>
> — Daniel Kahneman, The Knowledge Project
