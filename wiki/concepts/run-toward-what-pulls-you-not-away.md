# Run toward what pulls you, not away

Moving toward something you want usually feels better than fleeing something you fear. Put your focus on what attracts you rather than on what you want to avoid.

> It generally feels better to run toward something than to run away from something. Focus on what is pulling you in, not what you're trying to avoid.
>
> — James Clear
