# Running out of money is the surefire way to lose

There are many paths to success in business, but running out of money guarantees failure. Entrepreneurship is mainly about surviving.

> There are many ways to win in business, but there is one
> surefire way to lose: run out of money. Entrepreneurship is a
> game of survival.
>
> — James Clear
