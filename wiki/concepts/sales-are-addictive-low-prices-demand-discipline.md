# Sales are addictive; low prices demand discipline

Chasing sales is easy and habit-forming, while keeping prices low requires ongoing discipline.

> Sales are an
> addiction; low prices are a discipline.
>
> — Bernie Marcus, The Knowledge Project [Outliers]
