# Satisfying days are full days, not idle ones

The days that leave you most satisfied are those where you had a lot to do and got it done, not those spent lounging.

> Look at a day when you are supremely satisfied at the end. It's
> not a day when you lounge around doing nothing; it's a day you've
> had everything to do and you've done it.
>
> — Margaret Thatcher, As I Said to Denis: The Margaret Thatcher Book of Quotations
