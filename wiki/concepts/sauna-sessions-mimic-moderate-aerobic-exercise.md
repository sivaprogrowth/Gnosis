# Sauna sessions mimic moderate aerobic exercise

Deliberate heat exposure raises core temperature, heart rate, and plasma flow much like moderate exercise. Studies found 20 minutes in a sauna gave blood pressure and resting heart rate improvements comparable to 20 minutes of stationary cycling.

> And so there’s somewhat of a mimicking effect of
> moderate-intensity aerobic exercise. And there have actually been
> studies that have compared that head to head. They’ve looked at
> being on a stationary cycle doing, not anything crazy, but like
> 120 watts, and then comparing that to sitting in a sauna for 20
> minutes, so doing each of those for 20 minutes.
>
> — Rhonda Patrick, The Knowledge Project Podcast
