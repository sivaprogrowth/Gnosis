# Save more money than you think you need

Because life is unpredictable and your tastes will likely grow more expensive, saving extra is wise. The peace of not worrying about money later is worth more than anything you could buy now.

> "Save more money than you think you need. Life is unexpected and
> your future tastes will likely be more expensive. Not worrying
> about money tomorrow is worth more than whatever you could buy
> today."
>
> — James Clear
