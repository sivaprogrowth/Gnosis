# Say "you're probably right" to skip arguments

When someone disagrees on a small matter, conceding with "you're probably right" and moving on saves time and energy. Winning trivial arguments isn't worth it.

> 'You're probably right' has become of my favorite phrases.
> Whenever someone disagrees with you on a small matter (read: most
> things), you can shrug, say 'you’re probably right' and move on.
>
> Not caring about winning trivial arguments saves so much time and
> energy.
>
> — James Clear

> Most arguments are ego competitions disguised as truth-seeking.
>
> Learning to say "you're probably right" will save you a lot of
> time and energy.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
