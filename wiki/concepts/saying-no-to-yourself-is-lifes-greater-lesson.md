# Saying no to yourself is life's greater lesson

Knowing when to leave things alone and refuse, including saying no to yourself and important people, matters greatly. Spending interest on irrelevant things wastes precious time and is worse than doing nothing.

> Know how to leave things alone, for if knowing how to refuse is
> one of life's great lessons, an even greater one is knowing how
> to say no to yourself, to important people, and in business.
> There are non-essential activities, moths of precious time, and
> it's worse to take an interest in irrelevant things than to do
> nothing at all.
>
> — Baltasar Gracian, The Pocket Oracle and Art of Prudence
