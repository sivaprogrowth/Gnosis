# Saying nothing often says everything

Quiet, thoughtful silence often earns a reputation for wisdom, while talkers talk themselves into corners. The wise learn to use well-placed pauses.

> The quietest person at a dinner party often leaves with a
> reputation for brilliance, while others talk themselves into
> corners.
>
> You see it in boardrooms too - the executive who tilts her head
> thoughtfully during presentations, somehow radiating wisdom
> without speaking a word. Even therapists build their practices
> more on artful silences than clever words.
>
> The wisest among us have learned that saying nothing often says
> everything.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
