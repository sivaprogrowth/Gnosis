# Saying yes rules out every alternative

Declining rules out only one option, but accepting something commits you and excludes all alternatives. Be careful what and who you say yes to because it shapes your whole life.

> When you say no, you are only saying no to one option.
>
> When you say yes, you are saying no to every other option.
>
> No is a decision.
>
> Yes is a responsibility.
>
> Be careful what (and who) you say yes to. It will shape your day,
> your career, your family, your life.
>
> — James Clear
