# Scarcity makes things valuable and forces choices

When something is scarce we value and want it more. Because wants are infinite and resources limited, scarcity applies to time, opportunities, and ideas and guides what we choose.

> Scarcity shapes our choices and drives our actions. When
> something is scarce, it suddenly becomes valuable. We want it
> more because there is less.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
