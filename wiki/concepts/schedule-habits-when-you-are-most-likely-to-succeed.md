# Schedule habits when you are most likely to succeed

Choose a time and place for a new habit where you won't be busy or disrupted; a chaotic moment is the wrong slot for a habit.

> Consider when you are most likely to be
> successful. Don’t ask yourself to do a habit when you’re likely
> to be occupied with something else.
>
> — James Clear
