# Second-order thinking asks: and then what?

Second-order thinking looks beyond immediate effects to the ripple effects of choices over time, choosing the ultimate goal over the immediate one.

> Second-order thinking demands we ask: And then what?
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v1: General Thinking Tools
