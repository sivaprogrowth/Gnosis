# Secure attachment gives a visceral map of good relationships

People who experienced safe connection in childhood have an internal template for what healthy adult bonds feel like.

> Securely attached people have a "visceral map" of what good
> relationships feel like because they experienced safe connection
> in childhood, giving them a template for healthy adult bonds.
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
