# Seeing the glass half full describes reality better

Optimists who see the glass as half full are more grounded in reality because they describe what is actually there, while cynics focus on what is absent.

> People who describe the glass as half full are not delusional
> optimists. In fact, they are more based in reality because they
> are describing a substance that is actually in the glass. They
> are describing reality as it is. The cynic who describes the
> glass as half empty is focusing their energy on something that is
> not actually there.
>
> — Rosamund Zander and Benjamin Zander, The Art of Possibility
