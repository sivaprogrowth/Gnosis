# Seek a daily surprise to live a richer life

Try to be surprised by something each day by paying close attention to ordinary things instead of assuming you already know them. The more widely and deeply you engage with experience, the richer your life becomes.

> Try to be surprised by something every day. It could be
> something you see, hear, or read about. Stop to look at the
> unusual car parked at the curb, taste the new item on the
> cafeteria menu, actually listen to your colleague at the office.
> How is this different from other similar cars, dishes or
> conversations? What is its essence? Don't assume that you already
> know what these things are all about, or that even if you knew
> them, they wouldn't matter anyway. Experience this one thing for
> what it is, not what you think it is. Be open to what the world
> is telling you. Life is nothing more than a stream of experiences
> — the more widely and deeply you swim in it, the richer your life
> will be.
>
> — Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi, Creativity
