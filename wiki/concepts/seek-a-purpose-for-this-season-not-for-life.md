# Seek a purpose for this season, not for life

A lifelong purpose is rare, so look for what to dedicate the current season of life to. Notice which old purposes you have outgrown, and feel free to choose a new direction as life changes.

> "It's rare to find your purpose for life, so instead look for
> your purpose for this season.
>
> What lights you up right now? What's a good thing to dedicate
> this season of your life to? Perhaps more importantly, what
> purpose served you well in your previous season, but you have
> outgrown?
>
> Life is always changing. It's okay to pick a new North Star."
>
> — James Clear
