# Selective ignorance makes you more broadly knowledgeable

Deliberately ignore topics, people, and projects that drain your attention, energy, and time. Not trying to keep up with everything frees you to know more broadly.

> Be "selectively ignorant."
>
> Ignore topics that drain your attention.
>
> Unfollow people that drain your energy.
>
> Abandon projects that drain your time.
>
> Do not keep up with it all. The more selectively ignorant you
> become, the more broadly knowledgable you can be.
>
> — James Clear
