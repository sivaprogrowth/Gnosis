# Self-doubt sets your ceiling

Believing you deserve something won't make it happen, but believing you don't deserve it stops you from trying. Confidence doesn't guarantee results, yet self-doubt limits what you can reach.

> Merely believing you deserve something doesn’t make it a
> reality... but believing you don’t deserve something will prevent
> you from trying.
>
> Most people are capable of more than they believe. Confidence
> won’t automatically get you results, but self-doubt sets your
> ceiling.
>
> — James Clear
