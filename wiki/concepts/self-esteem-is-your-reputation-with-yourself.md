# Self-esteem is your reputation with yourself

Self-esteem reflects how you judge your own conduct, and you can't fool yourself because you always know what you did.

> Self-esteem is just the reputation that you have with yourself.
> You'll always know.
>
> — Naval Ravikant, Tribe of Mentors
