# Self-mastery is the truest power

The greatest dominion anyone has is over themselves; success depends on self-mastery and failure on self-abandonment. Those who can't govern themselves can't govern others.

> “One can have no smaller or greater mastery than mastery of
> oneself; you will never have a greater or lesser dominion than
> that over yourself; the height of your success is gauged by your
> self-mastery, the depth of your failure by your self-abandonment.
> Those who cannot establish dominion over themselves will have no
> dominion over others.”
>
> — Leonardo da Vinci

> "Mastering others is strength; mastering yourself is true power."
>
> — Lao Tzu
