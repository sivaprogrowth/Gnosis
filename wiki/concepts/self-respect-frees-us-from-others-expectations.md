# Self-respect frees us from others' expectations

The unique power of self-respect is that it releases us from living up to others' expectations and returns us to ourselves.

> “To free us from the expectations of others, to give us back to
> ourselves—there lies the great, the singular power of
> self-respect.”
>
> — Joan Didion
