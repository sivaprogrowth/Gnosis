# Self-trust is learned and frees you from intimidation

Trusting yourself doesn't come naturally but can be learned, especially young, and it makes you immune to intimidation by title, skill, or fame.

> “I began to value myself so much more, trust my instincts, trust
> my uniqueness. Trusting oneself does not always come naturally.
> If learned when young, the practice sticks. Today, there is no
> one who can intimidate me because of title or skill or fame. I do
> what's right for me.”
>
> — Estee Lauder
