# Sell aspirin, not vitamins

People reliably pay to relieve pain but only occasionally and unpredictably pay for nice-to-haves, so sell painkillers.

> People buy aspirin always. They buy vitamins only occasionally
> and at unpredictable times. Sell aspirin.
>
> — Colin Dowling, Hacker News
