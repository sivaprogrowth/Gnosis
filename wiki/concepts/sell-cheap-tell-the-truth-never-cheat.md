# Sell cheap, tell the truth, never cheat

Rose Blumkin built Nebraska Furniture Mart on a simple formula of low prices, honesty, and fair dealing, which made her store so formidable that Buffett acquired it.

> Sell cheap, tell the truth, never cheat.
>
> — Rose Blumkin, The Knowledge Project [Outliers]
