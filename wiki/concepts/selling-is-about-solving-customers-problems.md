# Selling is about solving customers' problems

Selling is not about the product or technology itself but about people and solving their problems.

> Sales is about people and it's about problem solving. It is
> not about solutions or technology or chemicals or lines of code
> or artichokes. It's about people and it's about solving problems.
>
> — Colin Dowling, Hacker News

> No one cares about your quota, your payroll, your opex, your
> burn rate, etc. No one. They care about the problem you are
> solving for them.
>
> — Colin Dowling, Hacker News
