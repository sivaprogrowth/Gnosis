# Separate an idea's substance from its delivery

Good ideas often get dismissed because of a hostile or cocky tone, or because of who says them. You can agree with the idea while disagreeing with the tone.

> A phrase I heard recently and found useful: I agree with the
> idea, but I disagree with the tone.
>
> Many ideas get dismissed because they are delivered in a cocky or
> hostile or dismissive tone—or because of who delivers them.
>
> Separate substance from style.
>
> — James Clear
