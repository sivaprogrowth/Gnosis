# Service turns out to be the source of joy

One might dream life is about joy and then find it is about service, but acting on service reveals that service itself is joy.

> I slept, and dreamed that life was joy;
>
> I woke, and found that life was service.
>
> I acted and behold, service was joy.
>
> — Ellen Sturgis Hooper, The Dial (July 1840)
