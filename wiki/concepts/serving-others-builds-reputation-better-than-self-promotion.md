# Serving others builds reputation better than self-promotion

Self-description is forgotten, but what you do for others compounds; people you serve well will spread your reputation further than you can.

> What you say about yourself is forgotten. What you do for others
> compounds.
>
> Serve people well, and their word about you will travel further
> than yours ever could.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
