# Set aside the final goal and cover today's distance

On a long journey, focus on the day's achievable distance rather than the distant end goal.

> “A man on a thousand-mile walk has to forget his ultimate goal
> and say to himself every morning, ‘Today I’m going to cover
> twenty-five miles and then rest up and sleep.’”
>
> — Leo Tolstoy

> ... for a man walking a thousand miles it is absolutely necessary to set aside his final goal and to say to himself each morning: 'Today I will walk twenty-five miles to a resting place and sleep,' and during each day’s journey reaching that resting place eclipses his ultimate goal and becomes his sole focus.
>
> — Leo Tolstoy, War and Peace
