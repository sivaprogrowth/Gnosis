# Set goals in the state you'll execute them

An empathy gap separates your present self from your future self, so goals set on the couch overestimate what you'll do. Commit to ambitious behaviors when you're actually in that state, like at the gym at 4 AM.

> That’s another example of an empathy gap
> between our present selves and our future selves, which can be
> very problematic. If you are up at 4:00 AM and at the gym working
> out, that’s a reasonable moment to say, “I’m going to try to keep
> doing this for however many days I can manage.”
>
> — Maya Shankar
