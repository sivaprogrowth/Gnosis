# Setbacks are feedback, not verdicts

Being rejected doesn't decide your worth or outcome; it gives you information you can use to improve.

> Rejection isn’t a verdict; it’s data. Use it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> 4. There's no
> failure, only feedback.
>
> — Jim Murphy, The Knowledge Project
