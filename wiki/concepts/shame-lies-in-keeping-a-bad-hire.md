# Shame lies in keeping a bad hire

Hiring the wrong person is forgivable; failing to correct the mistake by keeping them is not.

> "There is no shame in hiring the wrong person. There is,
> however, shame in keeping him."
>
> — Harrison McCain, The Knowledge Project episode on Harrison McCain
