# Shape feelings indirectly by controlling actions

We control our actions more than our feelings, but taking action can shift how we feel. Taking one small physical step can change your mental state.

> People generally have more control over their actions than their
> feelings.
>
> But we can influence our feelings by taking action.
>
> Take one small step. Move the body first and the mind will
> follow.
>
> — James Clear

> Action seems to follow feeling, but really action and feeling go
> together; and by regulating the action, which is under the more
> direct control of the will, we can indirectly regulate the
> feeling, which is not.
>
> — William James
