# Shape the future rather than guess about it

Prediction relies on things you can't control, while creation relies on things you can. So rather than guessing what will happen, act to shape the outcome.

> Prediction depends on events outside your control.
>
> Creation depends on events within your control.
>
> Don’t guess about the future. Shape it.
>
> — James Clear
