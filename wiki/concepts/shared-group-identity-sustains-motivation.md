# Shared group identity sustains motivation

Belonging to a group changes your identity from "I am a reader" to "we are readers." That shared identity reinforces your personal identity and sustains motivation.

> Nothing sustains motivation better than belonging to the tribe. It transforms a personal quest into a shared one.
>
> — James Clear
