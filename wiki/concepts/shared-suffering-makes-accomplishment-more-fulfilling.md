# Shared suffering makes accomplishment more fulfilling

Suffering is worth embracing for what lies on the other side of it, and enduring it together makes achievement richer.

> "Suffering isn’t something I avoid. It’s something I love—I love
> what it brings, what’s on the other side of it. When we’re
> climbing Mt. Everest, we’re all suffering together, facing the
> same battle, chasing the same dream. Suffering makes the
> accomplishment that much more fulfilling."
>
> — Elizabeth Rose
