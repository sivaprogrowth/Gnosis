# Sharing half the profits can make your half bigger

Les Schwab split profits 50/50 with store managers because motivated partners made stores so successful that his half exceeded his former whole. You get rich by making others rich.

> Les discovered that splitting
> profits 50/50 with store managers didn’t cut his wealth in half,
> it multiplied it. His reasoning was pure math: “If I share half
> the profits, I still have half. And if Frank makes more money,
> he’ll work harder to make the store successful. If the store is
> more successful, my half is worth more than my whole used to be.”
> You get rich by making others rich.
>
> — Les Schwab, The Knowledge Project [Outliers]
