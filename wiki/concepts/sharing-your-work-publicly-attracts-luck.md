# Sharing your work publicly attracts luck

Putting your work out in public creates opportunities for good fortune to find you.

> You can attract luck simply by sharing your work publicly.
>
> — James Clear

> You can attract luck simply by telling people what you are
> working on.
>
> — James Clear
