# Short focused effort beats long scattered effort

Concentrating all your effort on one thing for a short time gets better results than spreading energy across many things for years. A competitor with a single goal is the most dangerous.

> Going all in on something for a month gets better results than
> dabbling in it for a decade.
>
> The dabbler spreads their energy over many things. The focused
> person concentrates their effort on one.
>
> The most dangerous competitor is the one with a single goal.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> A focused hour outweighs an unfocused day.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
