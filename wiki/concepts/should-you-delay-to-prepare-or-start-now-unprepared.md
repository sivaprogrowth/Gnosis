# Should you delay to prepare or start now unprepared?

Asks whether it is better to wait weeks or months to be fully prepared, or to begin immediately with little preparation.

> Is it better to delay getting started by weeks or months so I can
> be fully prepared? Or is it better to start right now with little
> to no preparation?
>
> — James Clear
