# Show only work you like to get hired for it

The work you put out signals what you want to be hired for. If you show work you dislike, you will get more of that kind of work.

> Only show work you like, or you'll end up being hired to do
> things you don't like.
>
> — Victoria Pater, The Secret Handshake
