# Show up and allow yourself to quit tomorrow

To stay consistent with exercise, just show up today and give yourself permission to quit tomorrow, then repeat. This lowers the commitment barrier each day.

> Two rules to help you be consistent with exercise this year:
>
> 1. Show up.2. You can quit tomorrow.
>
> Repeat.
