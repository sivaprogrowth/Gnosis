# Shower ideas come from time away from screens

People get good ideas in the shower because it is the only screen-free time long enough to think clearly. The fix is to make more time to think, not to take more showers.

> "The reason people get good ideas in the shower is because it's
> the only time during the day when most people are away from
> screens long enough to think clearly. The lesson is not to take
> more showers, but rather to make more time to think."
>
> — James Clear
