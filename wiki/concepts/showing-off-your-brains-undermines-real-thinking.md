# Showing off your brains undermines real thinking

If you are focused on displaying intelligence, you are likely not listening well, thinking hard, or reaching the best conclusions.

> “If you're busy showing off your brains, you're probably not
> listening closely enough, thinking hard enough or reaching the
> smartest conclusions.”
>
> — Strauss Zelnick
