# Shrink a habit until it becomes automatic

If a new habit is hard to stick with, do a smaller version of it. Consistency matters more than intensity at the start.

> If you're having trouble sticking to a new habit, try a smaller
> version until it becomes automatic. Do less than you're capable
> of, but do it more consistently than you have before.
>
> — James Clear
