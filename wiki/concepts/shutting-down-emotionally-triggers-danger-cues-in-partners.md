# Shutting down emotionally triggers danger cues in partners

Withdrawing to protect yourself signals danger to your partner's nervous system, because humans need connection like oxygen.

> When you shut down emotionally to protect yourself, you trigger
> danger cues in your partner's nervous system because humans are
> wired as "bonding mammals" who need connection like oxygen.
>
> — Sue Johnson, The Knowledge Project
