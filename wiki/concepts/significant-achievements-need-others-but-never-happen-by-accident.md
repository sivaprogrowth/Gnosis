# Significant achievements need others but never happen by accident

You need other people to accomplish anything big, but you can't wait for them to make it happen. Act like a force of nature pushing toward your goal while staying pleasant and open to help.

> Nobody accomplishes anything significant alone.
>
> But nobody accomplishes anything significant by accident either.
> Just because you need others doesn't mean you can wait for others
> to make it happen.
>
> You have to act as if you are a force of nature and try to bend
> the universe in your desired direction—while remaining pleasant
> and open to help along the way.
>
> — James Clear
