# Significant results require changing strategy, not working harder

If you want a big change in results, you likely need a different strategy rather than more effort on the current one. Make sure you are working on the right thing before increasing effort.

> If you want a significant change in your results, then you
> probably need a significant change to your strategy. Working
> harder on your current strategy is unlikely to move the needle.
>
> Before you work harder, work on the right thing.
>
> Play better games. Win better prizes.
>
> — James Clear
