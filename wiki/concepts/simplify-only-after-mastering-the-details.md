# Simplify only after mastering the details

Simplification only has value once you have mastered the details. Simplifying without that understanding just reflects not knowing what you are leaving out.

> To simplify before you understand the details is ignorance.
>
> To simplify after you understand the details is genius.
>
> — James Clear
