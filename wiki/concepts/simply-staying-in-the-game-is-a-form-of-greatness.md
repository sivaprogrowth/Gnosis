# Simply staying in the game is a form of greatness

Over long periods, avoiding injury, debt, or going out of business is impressive on its own. Longevity itself is a kind of greatness.

> Over long time frames, simply staying in the game is impressive.
>
> - Health and fitness: Can you stay injury free and not miss
> workouts?
>
> - Wealth and finance: Can you avoid debt and continue to save?
>
> - Business: Can you maintain cash flow and stay in business?
>
> Longevity is its own form of greatness.
>
> — James Clear
