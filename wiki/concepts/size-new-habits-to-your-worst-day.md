# Size new habits to your worst day

Instead of designing a habit around your best days, pick something you can keep doing even on your worst days. Start small, master showing up, and scale up later.

> When choosing a new habit many people seem to ask themselves,
> “What can I do on my best days?”The trick is to ask, “What can I
> stick to even on my worst days?”Start small. Master the art of
> showing up. Scale up when you have the time, energy, and
> interest.
>
> — James Clear

> When determining the size or complexity of a new habit ask
> yourself, "What can I stick to—even on my worst day?"
>
> Start there. Master the art of showing up. Then advance.
>
> — James Clear
