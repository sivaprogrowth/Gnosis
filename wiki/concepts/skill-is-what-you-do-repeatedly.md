# Skill is what you do repeatedly

Real skill shows up only in consistent, repeated performance. Anything that happens once is just a story, not evidence of skill.

> Skill is whatever happens repeatedly. Everything else is a story.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
