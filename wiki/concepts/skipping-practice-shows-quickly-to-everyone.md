# Skipping practice shows quickly to everyone

Missing practice erodes skill fast: first you notice, then experts notice, then everyone does. Consistency is what maintains mastery.

> If I don't practice one day, I know it; two days, the critics
> know it; three days, the public knows it.
>
> — Jascha Heifetz, Heifetz official website
