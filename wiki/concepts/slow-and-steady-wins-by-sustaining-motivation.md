# Slow and steady wins by sustaining motivation

Manageable challenges produce frequent signs of progress, which keep you motivated. Taking on too much stalls progress, and stalled progress makes you want to quit.

> Slow and steady often wins because it keeps you motivated.
>
> Take on manageable challenges and you'll get frequent signals of
> progress. Bite off more than you can chew and progress stalls.
>
> When you make progress, you want to keep going. When you break
> progress, you want to stop.
>
> — James Clear
