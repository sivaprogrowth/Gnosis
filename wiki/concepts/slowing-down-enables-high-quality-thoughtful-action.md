# Slowing down enables high-quality, thoughtful action

When you slow down you can act with more kindness and polish. Rushing makes it hard to be thoughtful.

> Slowing down enables you to act in a high quality way.
>
> Kind rather than curt. Polished rather than sloppy.
>
> It's hard to be thoughtful when you're in a rush.
>
> — James Clear
