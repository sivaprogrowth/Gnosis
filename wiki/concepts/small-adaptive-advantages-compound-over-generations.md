# Small adaptive advantages compound over generations

Species that respond faster to change gain small relative advantages that seem insignificant in the short run but compound over generations into better odds of survival.

> "Species that are more responsive to change can gain a relative
> advantage over the ones they compete with and increase the odds
> of survival. In the short run, these small gains don’t make much
> of a difference, but as generations pass the advantages
> compound."
>
> — Matt Ridley, The Red Queen
