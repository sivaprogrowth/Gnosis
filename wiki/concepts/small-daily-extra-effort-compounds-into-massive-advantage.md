# Small daily extra effort compounds into massive advantage

Small extras like a few more calls or an extra hour a day add up to large gaps over months and years. Effort compounds both ways, so doing slightly less each day compounds into underperformance.

> When you consistently do more, the returns compound dramatically.
> Like superlinear growth curves, each extra bit of effort builds
> on previous overperformance. The key is that extra effort
> compounds - just as doing a bit less daily leads to compounding
> underperformance.
>
> What looks like a small delta day-to-day becomes massive over
> months and years.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
