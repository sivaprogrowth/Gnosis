# Small early deviations from truth grow into huge errors

A small mistake at the beginning leads progressively further astray. Over time, it can become an error a thousand times larger.

> What is at first small is often extremely large in the end. And
> so it happens that whoever deviates only a little from truth in
> the beginning is led farther and farther afield in the sequel,
> and to errors which are a thousand times as large.
>
> — Franz Brentano, On the Several Senses of Being in Aristotle
