# Small habits ground you during uncertain times

When the world feels chaotic and uncontrollable, sticking to small routines gives stability. Return to your habits and focus on what you can control.

> In times of uncertainty, your habits can ground you.
>
> — James Clear

> In times of uncertainty, your habits can ground you.
>
> When you feel overwhelmed, practice 1 minute of mindfulness.
>
> When you feel restless, do a 1-minute workout.
>
> When the world seems uncontrollable, focus on what you can
> control.
>
> — James Clear
