# Small private acts of decency are within everyone's reach

Refraining from gossip, mockery, and hostility while offering more love, patience, and faithful work are modest things anyone can do to improve the world.

> To hold our tongues when everyone is gossiping, to smile without
> hostility at people and institutions, to compensate for the
> shortage of love in the world with more love in small, private
> matters; to be more faithful in our work, to show greater
> patience, to forgo the cheap revenge obtainable from mockery and
> criticism: all these are things we can do.
>
> — Hermann Hesse, If the War Goes On...
