# Small things accumulate into something great

Small additions made frequently accumulate over time into something large. Consistent small improvements compound.

> If you add only a little to a little and do this often, soon
> that little will become great.
>
> — Hesiod, Works and Days

> Small things matter when they accumulate. This is one of the main lessons of my work—and one of the principles I try to follow in my life. The details, when finely polished and carefully combined, add up to something remarkable.
>
> — James Clear
