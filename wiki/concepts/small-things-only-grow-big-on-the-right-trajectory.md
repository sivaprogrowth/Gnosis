# Small things only grow big on the right trajectory

Big things begin small, but not every small start can grow large. Choosing the right playing field makes success come more easily.

> Big things start small, but not every small thing can become
> big.
>
> You need to be on the right trajectory. Choose the right playing
> field and success comes more easily.
>
> — James Clear
