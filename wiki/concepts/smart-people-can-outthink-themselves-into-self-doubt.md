# Smart people can outthink themselves into self-doubt

Intelligence can breed self-doubt that holds people back, while less clever people act confidently and often succeed more.

> "Someone with half your IQ is making 10x as you because they
> aren't smart enough to doubt themselves."
>
> — Ed Latimore, Twitter
