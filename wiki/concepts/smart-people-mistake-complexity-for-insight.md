# Smart people mistake complexity for insight

Intelligent people are especially prone to treating complexity as wisdom; real mastery is finding the elegant simplicity that cuts through complexity.

> Smart people are uniquely vulnerable to mistaking complexity for
> insight.
>
> An executive writes a ten-page memo that could be one. An
> engineer builds an intricate system when a simple one would
> perform better at half the cost. The consultant mesmerizes
> clients with frameworks that conceal rather than reveal. We
> attach prestige to what mystifies us. Complexity intoxicates both
> the creator and audience, drugging us with the illusion of
> wisdom.
>
> Mastery isn't making the simple complex—it's finding the elegant
> simplicity that cuts through the complexity.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
