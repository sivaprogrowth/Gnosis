# Smart people prefer hard meaningful work over easy trivial work

Smart people are more easily motivated to tackle hard problems than to do easy tasks that don't matter.

> "It's easier to get a smart person to do something hard than to
> get them to do something easy that doesn't matter."
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
