# Smart people who overrate themselves do worse

Many smart people overestimate their intelligence and, because of that overconfidence, end up doing worse than less intelligent people.

> A lot of smart people think they’re way smarter than they are,
> and therefore they do worse than dumb people. And it’s very
> common to be utterly brilliant and think you’re way the hell
> smarter than you are.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]], Caltech Interview (December 2020)
