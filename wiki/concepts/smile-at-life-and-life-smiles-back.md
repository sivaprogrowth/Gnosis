# Smile at life and life smiles back

Meeting the day with positive energy positions you to make the most of it. The present belongs to optimists.

> Smile to life this morning and be assured, ma'am, that this life will make you smile. The present belongs to the optimists.
>
> — Doris Veillette, Forbidden to Men
