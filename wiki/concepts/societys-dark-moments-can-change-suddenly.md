# Society's dark moments can change suddenly

Like weather that bluffs, human society can look bleak and then shift for the better quickly. Darkness is not necessarily permanent.

> "Sailors have an expression about the weather: they say, the
> weather is a great bluffer. I guess the same is true of our human
> society — things can look dark, then a break shows in the clouds,
> and all is changed, sometimes rather suddenly."
>
> — E.B. White, E.B. White’s Timeless Letter to Someone Who Lost Faith
