# Soft feedback is selfish

People soften feedback because they care more about how the conversation feels than about solving the problem, which puts their own comfort first.

> Most people give soft feedback because they care more about how the conversation feels than about whether the problem gets solved. This is selfish.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
