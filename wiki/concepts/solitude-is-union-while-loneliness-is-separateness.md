# Solitude is union while loneliness is separateness

Loneliness is feeling apart from the space around you, while solitude is feeling at one with it. They are opposite conditions.

> “Solitude is a condition of peace that stands in direct
> opposition to loneliness. Loneliness is like sitting in an empty
> room and being aware of the space around you. It is a condition
> of separateness. Solitude is becoming one with the space around
> you. It is a condition of union.”
>
> — Kent Nerburn
