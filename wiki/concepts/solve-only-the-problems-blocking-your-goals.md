# Solve only the problems blocking your goals

You don't need to solve every problem; focus on the ones that actually stand between you and where you want to be.

> Not every problem needs to be overcome, just the ones stopping
> you from getting where you want to be.
>
> — Ann Hill, Twitter

> You don't need to solve every problem right now, only those that stand in your way.
>
> — James Clear
