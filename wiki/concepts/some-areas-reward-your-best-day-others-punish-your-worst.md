# Some areas reward your best day, others punish your worst

In fields like creative work and entrepreneurship, reputation is defined by wins and failures fade. In fields like law and ethics, one loss defines you, so consistency matters; knowing which you are in tells you when to take risks and when to play it safe.

> Some areas of life reward your best day. Others punish your worst
> day. Know which situation you're in, and you can better decide
> when to be risky and when to play it safe.
>
> — James Clear
