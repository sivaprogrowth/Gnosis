# Some problems are solved by ignoring them

Attention fuels conflict, so fighting a problem can keep it alive. Withholding attention often makes a problem fade away.

> Some things are better off ignored than attacked.
>
> Attention is the oxygen of conflict. When you fight a problem,
> you breathe life into it. When you starve a problem of your
> attention, you suffocate it.
>
> In a surprising number of cases, the way to solve a problem is to
> ignore it.
>
> — James Clear
