# Some superpowers are choices anyone can make

Several powerful traits are available by choice. They include changing your mind, not taking things personally, not needing to be right, choosing relationships carefully, staying calm, being comfortable alone and with discomfort, and thinking for yourself.

> Superpowers you can choose:
>
> - Ability to change yourself & your mind
> - Not taking things personally
> - Not needing to prove you're right
> - Careful selection of all relationships
> - Staying calm
> - Being alone without being lonely
> - Being ok with being uncomfortable
> - Thinking for oneself
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
