# Sometimes guts pays off more than brains

Following conventional advice or formal training can prevent breakthrough ideas; Schwab's instinct-driven profit sharing would not have existed otherwise.

> “I’ve often said that sometimes guts pays off more than brains,
> because, if I had followed advice, and, if I had had a formal
> business education behind me, I never would have started the
> profit sharing contract in the form I did.”
>
> — Les Schwab, Pride in Performance: Keep It Going
