# Sort activities by whether they energize or drain

A prompt to sort the projects and people in your life by whether they energize you or deplete you.

> Which projects give me energy? Which projects takes it away?
>
> Which people give me energy? Which people take it away?
>
> — James Clear

> Does this activity fill me with energy or drain me of energy?
>
> — James Clear
