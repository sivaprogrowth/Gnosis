# Speak your truth respectfully, not responsible for their feelings

In conflict you can't take responsibility for how others feel. Say what you believe respectfully, then walk away knowing you stood up for yourself.

> "If you’re in a situation of conflict ... you can’t hold yourself
> responsible for their feelings. Regardless of how they handle the
> situation, you need to speak your truth. You need to do it in a
> respectful way, and then walk away knowing that you stood up for
> yourself."
>
> — Michelle McMahon
