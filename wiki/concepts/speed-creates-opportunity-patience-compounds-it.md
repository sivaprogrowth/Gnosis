# Speed creates opportunity, patience compounds it

Moving fast generates more chances, but jumping from thing to thing stalls growth. Find what works quickly, then stick with it for decades.

> Speed creates opportunity. Patience compounds it.
>
> Moving fast will generate more chances. But if you quickly jump
> from thing to thing, growth will stall.
>
> Get moving and find what works, then do it for decades.
>
> — James Clear
