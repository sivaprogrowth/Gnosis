# Speed is rewarded: be decisive, proactive, bold

Acting quickly pays off. Decide fast so you can correct sooner, solve problems while they are small, and pursue your goals now. Don't rush, but don't wait.

> Speed is rewarded in life.
>
> * Decisive. Make decisions quickly and you can correct them faster.
> * Proactive. Solve problems now, while they are still small.
> * Bold. Go for it today rather than delaying your dreams.
>
> Don't rush, but don't wait.
>
> — James Clear
