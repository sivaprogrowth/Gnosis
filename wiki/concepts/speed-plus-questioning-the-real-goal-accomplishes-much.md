# Speed plus questioning the real goal accomplishes much

Moving fast while repeatedly asking what the real goal is and whether there is a better way to reach it lets you get a great deal done in one life.

> If you...
>
> 1) develop a bias for moving fast
>
> 2) consistently ask, “What’s the real goal here and is there a
> better way to accomplish it?”
>
> ... you can accomplish a lot in one life.
>
> — James Clear
