# Spend each day on future payoff and present appreciation

Every day, invest some time in work that compounds over years and some time appreciating what is happening now. This balances ambition with gratitude.

> Each day, spend some time on two things:
>
> * working toward something that will pay off years from now
> * appreciating something that is happening right now
>
> — James Clear
