# Spend five minutes on activities expressing yourself

Identify an activity you are naturally drawn to and consider doing it for just five minutes today.

> What is one activity you are naturally drawn to? Can you spend 5 minutes doing it today?
>
> — James Clear

> Which activities are the best expression of you? Can you spend
> five minutes doing one of them today?
>
> — James Clear
