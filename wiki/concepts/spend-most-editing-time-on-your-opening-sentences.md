# Spend most editing time on your opening sentences

You have about six seconds and thirteen words to hook a reader, then another twenty to thirty seconds to deliver what matters. For longer emails, spend 40% of editing on the first sentence, 40% on the next few, and 20% on the rest.

> You have just six seconds to capture someone’s attention — but if
> you interest them, you earn another twenty to thirty seconds.
>
> Practically, this means you have about thirteen words to hook
> them and another thirty to forty to deliver your most compelling
> information.
>
> If your email exceeds 100 words, allocate your editing time
> wisely: dedicate 40% to perfecting the first sentence, 40% to
> refining the next 2-3 sentences, and just 20% to polishing the
> remainder.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
