# Spending a little energy often gives you more

When you feel sluggish, moving your body a little, like a short walk or stretching, usually leaves you more energized than resting. Getting the body going helps get the mind going.

> One of life's counterintuitive lessons is that you will often
> gain energy by spending a little bit of energy.
>
> When you feel lethargic and like you want to lay around all day,
> it is usually the case that getting up and moving will make you
> feel better than simply sitting around. Getting outside for 10
> minutes or doing the first set of a workout or simply stretching
> on the floor for a moment — anything to get your body moving —
> will often leave you feeling more energized.
>
> If you want to get your day going, then get your body going. It's
> harder for the mind to be sluggish when the body is moving.
>
> — James Clear
