# Split the day into quarters to fail small

Instead of writing off a whole day after a slip, treat the day as four quarters. If you blow one quarter, get back on track in the next.

> Instead of feeling that you’ve blown the day and thinking, "I'll
> get back on track tomorrow," try thinking of each day as a set of
> four quarters: morning, midday, afternoon, evening. If you blow
> one quarter, you get back on track for the next quarter.
>
> Fail small, not big.
>
> — Gretchen Rubin, Better Than Before
