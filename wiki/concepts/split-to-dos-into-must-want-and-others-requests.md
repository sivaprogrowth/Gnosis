# Split to-dos into must, want, and others' requests

Dividing a to-do list into things you have to do, want to do, and others want you to do clarifies priorities. Often not getting to the third category is what having boundaries means.

> "I started dividing my to-do list into 1) things I have to do, 2)
> things I want to do, and 3) things other people want me to do.
> Life changing! I often don’t get to \#3 and I finally realized...
> this is what it means to have boundaries."
>
> — Jenée Desmond-Harris, Twitter
