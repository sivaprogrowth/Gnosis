# Spoken anger ends while hidden anger grows

Telling someone directly that you're angry lets the anger resolve, while keeping it hidden lets it fester. Being clear and direct is a kindness.

> I was angry with my friend; I told my wrath, my wrath did end. I
> was angry with my foe; I told it not, my wrath did grow.
>
> — Robert Blake
