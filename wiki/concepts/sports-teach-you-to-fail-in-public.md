# Sports teach you to fail in public

Fear of looking stupid, of judgment, and of rejection keeps people from acting. Sports give practice at failing publicly and facing that fear.

> "One of the great gift of sports is learning how to fail in
> public.
>
> People never go to the gym because they're scared of looking
> stupid, never share their writing because they're scared of
> judgment, never open their heart because they're scared of
> rejection.
>
> Sports train you to face your fear."
>
> — James Clear
