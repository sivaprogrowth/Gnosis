# Sportsmanship means not being afraid to try

Eileen Gu frames sportsmanship and the Olympic spirit as showing the world you are unafraid to try, and sees that as representing young women well.

> “I showed the world I wasn’t afraid to try. That’s sportsmanship.
> That’s the Olympic spirit. I think it's representing young women
> in a way that I would hope to be.That's my contribution.”
>
> — Eileen Gu
