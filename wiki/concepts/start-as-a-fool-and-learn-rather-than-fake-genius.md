# Start as a fool and learn rather than fake genius

It is better to begin openly inexperienced and learn from your mistakes than to pretend to be brilliant and ignore your errors.

> It is better to start as a fool and learn from your mistakes than to fake being a genius and ignore your errors.
>
> — James Clear
