# Start from a blank slate and add back

To focus on what matters, imagine all your tasks and responsibilities are wiped away, then add back only what you miss.

> A simple way to ensure you are focused on what matters:
>
> Imagine everything gets wiped. You inherit no tasks or
> responsibilities from your past or present. Then, add back only
> what you miss.
>
> Choose what to add to a blank slate, not what to keep from a full
> plate.
>
> — James Clear

> Imagine all your responsibilities and obligations vanish
> overnight.
>
> What would you miss doing? What would you choose to add back to
> your life?
>
> — James Clear
