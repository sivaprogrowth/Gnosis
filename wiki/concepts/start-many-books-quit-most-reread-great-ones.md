# Start many books, quit most, reread great ones

To get more from reading, sample widely, abandon books that aren't worth it, and read the best ones twice.

> A recipe for getting more out of what you read:
>
> Start more books. Quit most of them. Read the great ones twice.
>
> — James Clear
