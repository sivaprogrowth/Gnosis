# Starting from a good position aids judgment most

Good positions, like a company with cash and low debt, turn bad times into great options, while bad positions leave only bad options that get worse.

> The greatest aid to judgment is starting from a good position.
> The company with cash on the balance sheet and low debt has good
> options. When bad times come, and they always do, their options
> go from good to great. On the other hand, a company with no cash
> and high debt has nothing but bad options to choose from. Things
> quickly go from bad to worse.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Clear Thinking
