# Starting from worst-case thinking means you've already lost

If you begin by asking what happens if everything goes wrong, you are playing defense and lose before you start.

> "If we’re starting with what if everything goes wrong, you’re playing defense and you’ve lost before you’re even out of the gates."
>
> — Mark Pincus, The Knowledge Project
