# Starting from zero lets you be bold

When you have little to begin with, you have little to lose. Without something to protect, you are free to take bold action.

> Starting from zero can be a gift.
>
> If you don't have much to begin with, you don't have much to
> lose.
>
> You can be bold when you aren't trying to protect something.
>
> — James Clear
