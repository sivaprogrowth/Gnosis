# Starting is the hardest part of work

The hardest part of work is usually beginning, since continuing is less painful once you start. Early on, building the habit of getting started matters more than whether you're doing enough.

> It's rarely doing the work that is hard, it's starting the work.
> Once you begin, it’s often less painful to continue working. This
> is why—in the beginning—it is often more important to build the
> habit of getting started than it is to worry about whether or not
> you are doing enough.
>
> — James Clear

> Once you've started, you're halfway there.
>
> — Horace, Epistles

> Well begun is half done.
>
> — Horace, Epistles I (20 BC)
