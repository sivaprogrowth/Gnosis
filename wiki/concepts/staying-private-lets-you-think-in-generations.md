# Staying private lets you think in generations

By keeping all money in the company and having no shareholders, Bragg could make decisions on a generational timescale rather than quarterly.

> He left all the money in the company and had no shareholders,
> so he could think in generations rather than quarters.
>
> — John Bragg, The Knowledge Project
