# Steal the hours for meaningful work from comfort

Envy of someone else's great work can either make you give up or push you to cut distractions and prioritize your own. The hours needed never appear on their own; you must take them from comfort.

> When you experience someone else’s genius work, a little part of
> you feels, 'That’s what I could have, would have, and should have
> done!'
>
> Someone else did it. You didn’t. They fought the resistance. You
> gave in to distractions. They made it top priority. You said
> you’d get to it some day. They took the time. You meant to.
>
> When this happens, you can take it two ways:
>
> You could let that part of you give up. 'Oh well. Now I don’t
> need to make that anymore.' Or you could do something about that
> jealous pain. Shut off your phone, kill the distractions, make it
> top priority, and spend the time.
>
> It takes many hours to make what you want to make. The hours
> don’t suddenly appear. You have to steal them from comfort.
>
> — Derek Sivers, Where to find the hours to make it happen
