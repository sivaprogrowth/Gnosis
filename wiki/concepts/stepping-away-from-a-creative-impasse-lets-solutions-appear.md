# Stepping away from a creative impasse lets solutions appear

When stuck in creative work, stepping back from the project creates space for a solution to emerge on its own.

> “When we reach an impasse at any point in the creative process,
> it can be helpful to step away from the project to create space
> and allow a solution to appear.”
>
> — Rick Rubin
