# Stick to the basics of reliable work

A short list of fundamentals: be reliable, do your job, speak for yourself, put outcomes above ego, focus on details, treat challenges as opportunities, and keep learning daily.

> Be reliable. Do your job. Speak for yourself. Outcome over ego.
> Focus on the details. See challenges as opportunities. Go to bed
> smarter than when you woke up.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
