# Stolen moments and sentences add up to a novel

Saying you lack time is a convenient excuse; writing happens one sentence at a time, and sentences fit into brief moments that accumulate into a finished work.

> "The "if I had time" lie is a convenient way to ignore the fact
> that novels require being written and that writing happens a
> sentence at a time. Sentences can happen in a moment. Enough
> stolen moments, enough stolen sentences, and a novel is born —
> without the luxury of time."
>
> — Julia Cameron, The Right to Write
