# Stop expecting others to share your ambition

Ambitious people often feel betrayed when others don't work as hard as they would. They are the unusual ones, and once they stop expecting others to be like them, the frustration goes away.

> The more ambitious you are, the easier it is to fall into this
> trap. You hand someone a project and think, "I would have stayed
> late every night for this." When they leave at 5, you feel
> betrayed.
>
> But here's the thing: you are unusual. Most people aren't like
> you. That's not a flaw in them. It's what makes you different.
>
> Once you stop expecting others to be you, the frustration
> disappears.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
