# Stop fighting reality and redirect energy to action

Getting angry at things you can't change wastes energy that could go toward improving your situation. Fighting what is blinds you to what could be.

> Frustration is bargaining with reality, hoping it will change.
>
> Yelling at traffic doesn't make it move faster. Rehearsing the
> same complaints about your spouse doesn't change their behavior.
> Getting angry at the delayed flight doesn't make it take off
> sooner.
>
> While you're busy fighting what is, you're blind to what could
> be.
>
> All the energy you put into arguing with reality comes at the
> expense of improving your situation. The mountain doesn't care
> how much you yell at it, but you'll find a path around it if you
> stop and look.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Acceptance is not surrender. It is the moment you stop wasting energy on what is already true and start using that energy to decide what to do next.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
