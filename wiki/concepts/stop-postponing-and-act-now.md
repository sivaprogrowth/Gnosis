# Stop postponing and act now

There is never a perfect moment to start something, but the present is usually better than waiting.

> It's never the right time, but right now is usually the best time.
>
> — James Clear

> Don't just imagine doing things someday. Do them now.
>
> Get out of your head and take action.
>
> — James Clear

> If not now, when?
>
> — James Clear
