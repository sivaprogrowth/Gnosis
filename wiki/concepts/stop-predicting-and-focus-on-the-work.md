# Stop predicting and focus on the work

You don't need to foresee how everything will unfold. Focus on doing the next step well and keep heading in the right direction.

> You don't need to predict how everything will play out.
>
> Just master the next step and continue moving in the right
> direction.
>
> — James Clear

> Worrying about the future is like watching a leaf fall and
> trying to predict where it will land. Stop trying to guess where
> the wind will blow and get to work.
>
> — James Clear
