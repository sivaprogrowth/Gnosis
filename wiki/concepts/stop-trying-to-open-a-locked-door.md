# Stop trying to open a locked door

Some approaches simply won't work, and there is usually another route to your goal. Rather than persisting at something blocked, look for a different way.

> Some things work and some things don't. There is almost always
> another way to get where you want to go. Don't keep trying to
> open a locked door.
>
> — James Clear
