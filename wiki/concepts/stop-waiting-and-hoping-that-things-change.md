# Stop waiting and hoping that things change

Waiting for people to finally treat you as you treat them keeps you stuck. Letting go of the waiting itself is freeing.

> Let go of waiting for the people you have always treated
> kindly, to treat you kindly. Let go of waiting for the people you
> have always treated with respect, to treat you with respect. Let
> go of waiting for the people you have always chosen, to finally
> choose you. Let go of waiting. Let go of holding your breath,
> just hoping that things change.
>
> — Bianca Sparacino, A Gentle Reminder
