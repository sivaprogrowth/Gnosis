# Strategy and hard work each need the other

A great strategy without hard work never becomes real. Hard work without a good strategy turns into painful, wasted effort.

> Without hard work, a great strategy remains a dream.
>
> Without a great strategy, hard work becomes a nightmare.
>
> — James Clear
