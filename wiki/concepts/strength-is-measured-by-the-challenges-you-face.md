# Strength is measured by the challenges you face

Strength shows in how hard the challenges are that you're willing to take on, not in how many wins you collect.

> Strength isn't measured by victories, but by the caliber of
> challenges you're willing to face.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
