# Succeeding at unimportant things is dangerous wasted time

The hardest kind of wasted time to notice is doing excellent work on something that doesn't matter. Because the work is done well, it feels productive.

> The most invisible form of wasted time is doing a good job on an unimportant task.
>
> — James Clear

> "I used to be afraid of failing at the things that really
> mattered to me, but now I'm more afraid of succeeding at things
> that don't matter."
>
> — Bob Goff
