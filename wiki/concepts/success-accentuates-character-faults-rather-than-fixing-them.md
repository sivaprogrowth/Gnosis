# Success accentuates character faults rather than fixing them

People's basic character does not change much over time, and success tends to magnify their faults. Life teaches lessons but doesn't necessarily make people grow.

> people’s basic
> character does not change over the years … Far from improving
> them, success usually accentuates their faults or short-comings.
>
> — Henry Miller
