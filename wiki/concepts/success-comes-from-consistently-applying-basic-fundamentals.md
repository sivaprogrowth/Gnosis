# Success comes from consistently applying basic fundamentals

Great success and bitter failure are often separated by whether you keep applying the basics consistently.

> “The difference between great success and bitter failure is often
> found in consistently applying the fundamentals.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> "Success is often found by practicing the fundamentals everyone
> knows they should be doing, but find too boring or basic to
> practice routinely."
>
> — James Clear

> “Success is neither magical nor mysterious. Success if the natural consequence of consistently applying basic fundamentals.”
>
> — Jim Rohn
