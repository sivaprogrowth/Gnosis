# Success comes from helping others succeed

True success is measured by how much you help others succeed.

> “Real success comes from making others successful.”
>
> — Andrew Mellon, The Knowledge Project: Outliers

> Success tends to move away from those who try to capture it and toward those who try to help others achieve it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
