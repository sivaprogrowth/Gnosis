# Success is a private matter of fewer regrets

Success is not about public recognition. It is a private feeling of having fewer and fewer regrets.

> For me, success is not a public thing. It's a private thing.
> It's when you have fewer and fewer regrets.
>
> — Toni Morrison, Interview with The Guardian
