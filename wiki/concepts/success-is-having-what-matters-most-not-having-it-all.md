# Success is having what matters most, not having it all

Wisdom is anticipating the consequences of your choices, and success comes from making good trade-offs toward what you value most rather than trying to have everything.

> Wisdom is anticipating the consequences of your choices. In life
> and business, success is about making good trade-offs. It’s not
> about having it all. It’s about having what matters most.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
