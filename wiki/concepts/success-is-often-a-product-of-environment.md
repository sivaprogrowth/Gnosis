# Success is often a product of environment

People, like animals, thrive in some environments and fail in others. This is why hiring a star away from a competitor often fails if you ignore how much their performance depended on their workplace.

> Success is often a product of environment.
>
> While a polar bear might thrive in the Arctic, it would not
> thrive in a desert. People are no different. The person who
> excels in one environment fails in another.
>
> Understanding how environments impact performance changes how you
> hire. Employees who thrive in one environment easily fail in
> another if their performance was contingent on the workplace
> conditions. This is why hiring a superstar away from a
> competitor, without understanding the role of environment on
> performance, often fails.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
