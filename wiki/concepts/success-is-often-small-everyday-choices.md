# Success is often small everyday choices

Success isn't only big achievements; it can be keeping a promise to yourself, being kind, forgiving yourself, acting with integrity, or doing what you should when you least feel like it.

> Sometimes success is going to the gym because you told yourself
> you would. Sometimes success is being kind even when someone
> doesn’t deserve it. Sometimes success is forgiving yourself for
> your mistakes. Sometimes success is avoiding regret. Sometimes
> success is a smile. Sometimes success is going to bed on time.
> Sometimes success is choosing integrity. Sometimes success is
> doing what you know you should do when you feel like doing it the
> least.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
