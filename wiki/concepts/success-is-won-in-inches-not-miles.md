# Success is won in inches, not miles

Success comes from small, incremental gains: win a little, hold your position, then win a little more later.

> “Victory is won not in miles but in inches. Win a little now, hold your ground, and later, win a little more.”
>
> — Louis L'Amour

> You climb as hard as you can ... just advancing one inch at a time. That's the secret of life.
>
> — [[charlie-munger|Charlie Munger]], Daily Journal Corporation annual shareholder meeting (Feb 15, 2023)
