# Success means finding the price and paying it

Success has a cost. Figure out what that cost is and then commit to paying it.

> “If you want to be successful, find out what the price is and
> then pay it.”
>
> — Scott Adams, How to Fail at Almost Everything and Still Win Big

> The rules:
>
> (1) know precisely what you want,
>
> (2) determine the cost of what it will take to get it,
>
> (3) don't bargain over the price.
>
> — James Clear
