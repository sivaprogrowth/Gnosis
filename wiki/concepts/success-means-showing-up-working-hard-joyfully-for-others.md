# Success means showing up, working hard, joyfully, for others

Success comes from showing up daily, working hard, doing it with joy, and caring more about others than yourself.

> Show up every day. Work hard. Do it with joy. Care more about others than we do ourselves.
>
> — Mike Deegan, The Denisonian (April 22, 2026)
