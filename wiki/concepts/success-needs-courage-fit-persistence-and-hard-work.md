# Success needs courage, fit, persistence, and hard work

A recipe for success combines enough courage to start, focusing on something you're naturally suited for, persisting long enough to catch lucky breaks, and a lot of hard work.

> Enough courage to get started + enough sense to focus on
> something you’re naturally suited for + enough persistence to
> stay in the game long enough to catch a few lucky breaks + a lot
> of hard work. There’s your recipe.
>
> — James Clear
