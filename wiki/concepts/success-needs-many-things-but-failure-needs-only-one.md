# Success needs many things but failure needs only one

No single factor creates success, but a single missing factor can cause failure. Many things are necessary but not sufficient, like sleep, hard work, and good strategy.

> Success is never due to one thing, but failure can be.
>
> Sleeping well won’t make you successful, but not sleeping enough
> will hold you back. Hard work is rarely enough without good
> strategy, but even the best strategy is useless without hard
> work.
>
> Many things are necessary, but not sufficient for success.
>
> — James Clear
