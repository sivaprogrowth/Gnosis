# Success not on your own terms isn't success

Nothing great comes from imitation; the hard work is setting aside others' expectations and becoming yourself. Success that looks good to the world but doesn't feel good in your heart is not success.

> if your success is not on
> your own terms, if it looks good to the world but does not feel
> good in your heart, it is not success at all.
>
> — Anna Quindlen, 1999 Mount Holyoke Commencement Speech
