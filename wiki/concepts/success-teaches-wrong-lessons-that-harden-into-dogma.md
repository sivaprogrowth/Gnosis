# Success teaches wrong lessons that harden into dogma

Past success leads people to treat what worked before as unquestionable truth, which makes them unable to adapt when circumstances change.

> The problem with success is that it teaches you the wrong
> lessons. What worked yesterday becomes religion, and religions
> don't adapt.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
