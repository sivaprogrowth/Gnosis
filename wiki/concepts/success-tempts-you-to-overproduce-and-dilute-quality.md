# Success tempts you to overproduce and dilute quality

After success, people and companies often expand output too fast, sacrificing quality for quantity. Keep your standards and pursue better rather than more.

> One of the common trappings of success is overproducing.
>
> Companies make money and rapidly expand their product line.
> Authors become popular and churn out books at a faster clip.
>
> Scale can empower, but it can also dilute. Something is lost when
> quantity is valued over quality.
>
> You have to maintain your standards even when all the forces
> around you seem to be calling for growth. Push back against more,
> more, more and remain committed to better, better, better.
>
> — James Clear
