# Successful people listen; those who don't, don't survive

Even dominant performers stay open to other people's creative ideas. People who stop listening do not last long.

> “I’m not so dominant that I can’t listen to creative ideas from
> other people. Successful people listen. Those who don’t listen,
> don’t survive long.”
>
> — Michael Jordan
