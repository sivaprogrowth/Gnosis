# Successful people make wrong decisions work anyway

Wrong decisions are unavoidable. What sets successful people apart is the ability to make those decisions work out regardless.

> “Wrong decisions are part of life. Being able to make them work
> anyway is one of the abilities of those who are successful.”
>
> — Warren Buffett
