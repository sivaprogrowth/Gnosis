# Successful rebels adopt the methods they condemned

History shows that rebels who win tend to use the same methods they criticized in the powers they overthrew.

> Nothing is clearer in history than the adoption by successful
> rebels of the methods they were accustomed to condemn in the
> forces they deposed.
>
> — Will and Ariel Durant
