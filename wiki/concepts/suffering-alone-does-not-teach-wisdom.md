# Suffering alone does not teach wisdom

Suffering only teaches when combined with mourning, understanding, patience, love, openness, and vulnerability. If suffering alone taught, everyone would be wise.

> I do not believe that sheer suffering teaches. If suffering
> alone taught, all the world would be wise, since everyone
> suffers. To suffering must be added mourning, understanding,
> patience, love, openness and the willingness to remain
> vulnerable. All these and other factors combined, if the
> circumstances are right, can teach and can lead to rebirth.
>
> — Anne Morrow Lindbergh, Hour of Gold, Hour of Lead
