# Supply and demand determine price and allocation

Supply and demand set prices and decide who gets what, steering investment toward shortages and away from gluts. Cycles are also driven by human nature, with profits breeding overconfidence and scarcity breeding fear and efficiency, and each of us shapes the market through our choices.

> Supply and demand are the push and pull determining availability
> and price. Their dance is never-ending. A sudden shortage can
> send prices soaring; a discovery can send them crashing.
>
> But supply and demand aren’t just about price; they’re also
> about allocation. They determine who gets what and how much of
> it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v4: Economics and Art
