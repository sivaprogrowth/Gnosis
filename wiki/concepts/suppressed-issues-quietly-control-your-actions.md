# Suppressed issues quietly control your actions

Things you push down don't go away; they grow and secretly influence your thoughts and behavior. It is worth asking what needs to be dealt with that you are avoiding.

> What you push down doesn’t vanish. It festers and wields quiet
> power over your thoughts and actions.
>
> What needs to be addressed that isn’t currently being addressed?
>
> — James Clear
