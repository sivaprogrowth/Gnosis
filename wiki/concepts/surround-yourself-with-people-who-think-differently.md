# Surround yourself with people who think differently

Spend time with people who are thoughtful in ways you are not, because they notice things you miss.

> Surround yourself with people who are thoughtful in ways you are
> not, because they see what you can't.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
