# Survive long enough for your advantages to compound

Identify and mitigate your top risks while leveraging your unique strengths. The goal is to stay in the game long enough for the rewards of your advantage to accumulate.

> What are my top risks? How can I mitigate them?
>
> What are my unique strengths? How can I leverage them?
>
> The objective is to survive long enough to see the rewards of
> your advantage accumulate.
>
> — James Clear
