# Take bets with small downside, big upside

Only commit capital to asymmetric bets where you can lose little and gain a lot.

> Only invest when downside risk is low and upside is
> high.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Outliers episode: The Witch of Wall Street

> The best type of risks to take are ones where (1) the worst
> outcome is manageable and (2) the best outcome is life-changing.
>
> Think: Asking someone on a date. Or, investing an amount of money
> you can afford to lose into a business with high upside.
>
> Look for opportunities where it won't kill you if it goes poorly,
> but you'd be blown away if it goes well.
>
> — James Clear
