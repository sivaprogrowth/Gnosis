# Take care of the minutes and hours follow

Managing small units of time well naturally adds up to using larger stretches well. Small productive habits compound.

> Take care of the minutes; the hours will take care of
> themselves.
>
> — Philip Dormer Stanhope, 4th Earl of Chesterfield, Letter to his son (November 6, 1747)
