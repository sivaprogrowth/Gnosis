# Take more risk when you can influence the outcome

Ask how much you can shape the result after making a choice. If effort can change the outcome, risk matters less; if it can't, be more risk averse.

> One filter I use for making decisions: How much can I influence
> the outcome after the initial choice is made?
>
> When I can do a lot to influence the outcome, I'm less worried
> about risk. Even if the choice appears risky on the surface, I
> can likely create a good outcome with effort.
>
> When I can't do much to influence the outcome, I'm more risk
> averse. Even my best effort won't move the needle. Your ability
> to influence the outcome after a decision is made is a crucial
> thing to consider.
>
> — James Clear
