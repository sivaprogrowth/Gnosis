# Taking action means choosing which pain to accept

People delay action hoping to find a path with no tradeoffs, but some suffering is always inevitable. Acting is simply deciding which kind of pain you will take on.

> Many people delay taking action because they hope to avoid
> suffering. They keep searching for a path that won’t involve
> tradeoffs.
>
> But some form of suffering is always inevitable. The process of
> taking action is the process of choosing your pain.
>
> — James Clear
