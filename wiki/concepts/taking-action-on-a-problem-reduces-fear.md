# Taking action on a problem reduces fear

Making progress on a problem, even slow and imperfect progress, makes it hard to keep fearing it. Taking action relieves anxiety.

> Working on a problem reduces the fear of it.
>
> It’s hard to fear a problem when you are making progress on
> it—even if progress is imperfect and slow.
>
> Action relieves anxiety.
>
> — James Clear

> Fear of failure is higher when you're not working on the
> problem.
>
> If you are taking action, you are less worried about failure
> because you realize you can influence the outcome.
>
> — James Clear

> The most common response when we're scared is avoidance.
>
> The longer we wait, the greater the fear.
>
> Action reduces fear.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Stress and worry tend to be higher before you act.
>
> Without action, all you can do is worry.
>
> Once you begin, fear shrinks as you start to influence the
> outcome.
>
> — James Clear
