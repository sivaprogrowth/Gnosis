# Taking many shots on goal produces lucky breaks

Artists who rose from the bottom performed far more shows than average. In many fields, success comes from showing up repeatedly and producing lots of work until a lucky break arrives.

> One study looked at artists who started at the bottom and worked
> their way to successful careers. The researchers identified one
> factor that was consistent across the group: they performed far
> more shows than the average artist.
>
> The same principle holds true in many fields. If you want to
> rise, you need a lot of shots on goal. Keep showing up. Keep
> making new material. Eventually, you catch a lucky break.
>
> — James Clear
