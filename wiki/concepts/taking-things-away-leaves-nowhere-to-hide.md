# Taking things away leaves nowhere to hide

Fewer elements on a plate means each must be excellent, because complexity can hide weaknesses while simplicity exposes them.

> "Jean-Georges Vongerichten showed me the value in taking away,
> taking things off of a plate. He always talked about two, three,
> four elements on a plate. That's it. The more you put on the
> plate, the easier it is to hide. The more you take away, there's
> nowhere to hide—it has to be good."
>
> — Wylie Dufresne, A Conversation with Wylie Dufresne
