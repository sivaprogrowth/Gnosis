# Talent gaps are often focus gaps

Top performers are often just average or above-average people who spend more time on what matters and less on distractions. Staying focused is the real talent.

> "What looks like a talent gap is often a focus gap.
>
> The 'all star' is often an average to above average performer who
> spends more time working on what is important and less time on
> distractions.
>
> The talent is staying focused."
>
> — James Clear
