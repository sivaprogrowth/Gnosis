# Talent grows with use and withers without it

Talent develops only through practice. Many dream of the finished result while skipping the years of hard work it requires, and closing that gap between expectation and reality is painful but necessary.

> A talent grows by being used, and withers if it is not used.
> Closing the gap between expectation and reality can be painful,
> but it has to be done sooner or later. The fact is that millions
> of young people would like to write, but what they dream of is
> the published book, often skipping over the months and years of
> very hard work necessary to achieve that end...
>
> — May Sarton, The House by the Sea
