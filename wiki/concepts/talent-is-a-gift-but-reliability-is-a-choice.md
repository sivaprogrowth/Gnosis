# Talent is a gift but reliability is a choice

You don't choose your talent, but you can choose to be reliable. Families, teams, and organizations need reliability as much as they need talent.

> Talent is a gift. Reliability is a choice.
>
> But every family, team, and organization needs reliability just as much as they need talent.
>
> — James Clear
