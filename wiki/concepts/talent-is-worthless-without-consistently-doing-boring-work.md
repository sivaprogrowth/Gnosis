# Talent is worthless without consistently doing boring work

Talent and potential are worthless unless you can reliably do the work even when you don't feel like it.

> Talent and potential mean nothing if you can't consistently do
> things when you don't feel like doing them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
