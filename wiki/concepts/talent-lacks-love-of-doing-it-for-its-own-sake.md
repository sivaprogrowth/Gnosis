# Talent lacks love of doing it for its own sake

Many talented people are missing the intrinsic love of the activity itself, which is what sets the exceptional apart.

> “I see a lot of people with talent but the one thing they don’t
> have is that just love of doing it for the sake of it.”
>
> — Rodney Mullen
