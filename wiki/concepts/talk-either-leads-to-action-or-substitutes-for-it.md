# Talk either leads to action or substitutes for it

Talking about goals can either launch real action or become a replacement for doing anything. Build instead of babbling.

> “Talk either leads to action or substitutes for action.
>
> Two friends discuss their dreams. One describes starting a
> business, solving a problem mid-conversation, and immediately
> making a call. The other talks about a novel they've been
> "writing" for years but never seem to make progress on.
>
> Build, don't babble.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
