# Talk to yourself as friend, student, or child

Match your self-talk to what you need. For confidence, speak to yourself as you would a friend. For persistence, speak as you would to a student. For patience, speak as you would to a child.

> Self-talk strategies:
>
> If you need confidence, talk to yourself the way you would talk
> to a friend.
>
> If you need persistence, talk to yourself the way you would talk
> to a student.
>
> If you need patience, talk to yourself the way you would talk to
> a child.
>
> — James Clear
