# Teach children they are unique marvels

Schools teach facts but should also teach each child that they are a unique marvel with limitless capacity. Recognizing that everyone is a marvel should lead us to cherish one another and make the world worthy of its children.

> Each second we live in a new and unique moment of the universe,
> a moment that never was before and will never be again. And what
> do we teach our children in school? We teach them that two and
> two make four, and that Paris is the capital of France. When will
> we also teach them what they are? We should say to each of them:
> Do you know what you are? You are a marvel. You are unique. In
> all of the world there is no other child exactly like you. In the
> millions of years that have passed there has never been another
> child like you... You may become a Shakespeare, a Michelangelo, a
> Beethoven. You have the capacity for anything. Yes, you are a
> marvel. And when you grow up, can you then harm another who is
> like you, a marvel? You must cherish one another. You must
> work—we must all work—to make this world worthy of its children.
>
> — Pablo Casals, Joys and Sorrows: Reflections by Pablo Casals, page 295
