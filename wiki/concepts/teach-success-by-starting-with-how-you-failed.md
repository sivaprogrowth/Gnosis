# Teach success by starting with how you failed

Failure stories resonate more than success stories because everyone has failed, while few reach the top. When teaching success or sharing your own, begin with your failures.

> "Stories of failure resonate more than stories of success. Few
> people reach the top, but everyone has failed—including those who
> eventually succeed. If you're teaching people how to succeed in a
> given field (or talking about your own success), start with how
> you failed."
>
> — James Clear
