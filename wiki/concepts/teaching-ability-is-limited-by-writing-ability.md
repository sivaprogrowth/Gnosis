# Teaching ability is limited by writing ability

How well you can teach something depends on how well you can write it down. If you can't put it in writing, you will struggle to teach it.

> Your teaching ability is constrained by your writing ability.
>
> If you can’t write it down, it will be nearly impossible to teach
> it well.
>
> — James Clear
