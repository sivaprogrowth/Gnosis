# Teaching and making teach you more than reading

The teacher learns the most in a classroom, so to learn a topic deeply, teach it or build something with it. Making things forces deeper learning than reading.

> The person who learns the most in any classroom is the teacher.
>
> If you really want to learn a topic, then "teach" it.
>
> Write a book.
> Teach a class.
> Build a product.
> Start a company.
>
> The act of making something will force you to learn more deeply
> than reading ever will.
>
> — James Clear
