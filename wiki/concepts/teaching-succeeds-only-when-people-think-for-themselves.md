# Teaching succeeds only when people think for themselves

The value of teaching lies not in what the teacher thinks but in the independent thinking it sparks in the student.

> Teaching is successful only as it causes people to think for
> themselves. What the teacher thinks matters little; what he makes
> the child think matters much.
>
> — Alice Moore Hubbard, Life Lessons: Truths Concerning People Who Have Lived
