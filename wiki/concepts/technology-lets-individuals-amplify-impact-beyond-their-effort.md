# Technology lets individuals amplify impact beyond their effort

Someone who uses tools like AI can multiply their impact far beyond their experience or effort, since work once requiring labor can now be done with silicon chips.

> An example of personal leverage is an employee who learns to use
> AI to amplify their impact on the organization far beyond their
> experience or effort. While labor is still a form of leverage, it
> can often be done with silicon chips. In this sense, the person
> who can leverage technology can compete in a way never
> imaginable.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
