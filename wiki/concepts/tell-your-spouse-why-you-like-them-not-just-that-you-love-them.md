# Tell your spouse why you like them, not just that you love them

Beyond saying "I love you," point out the specific qualities and moments that make your partner enjoyable to be around. Expressing liking matters as much as expressing love.

> "Don't just tell your spouse that you love them--tell them why
> you like them. Point out the qualities that make them fun to be
> around, the parts of their personality that shine, the little
> moments you genuinely enjoy together. 'I love you' matters, but
> so does 'I like spending time with you.'"
>
> — James Clear
