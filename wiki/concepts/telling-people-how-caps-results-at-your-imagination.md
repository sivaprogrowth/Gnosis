# Telling people how caps results at your imagination

Tell people what to do, not how to do it; prescribing the method limits the outcome to what you could imagine and prevents them from surprising you.

> Let people surprise you. Tell people what to do, not how. When
> you tell them how, you cap the upside at your own imagination.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Shoe Dog
