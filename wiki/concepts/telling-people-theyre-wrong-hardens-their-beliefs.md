# Telling people they're wrong hardens their beliefs

Beliefs are loosely held until they are challenged, and a direct challenge makes them harden. Instead, assume the other person knows something you don't and try to discover it.

> Most beliefs are soft until challenged. When you tell someone
> they're wrong, the belief hardens.
>
> Instead of trying to convince someone they're wrong, assume they
> know something you don't and figure out what it is.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
