# Temptation bundling pairs a want-to-do with a need-to-do

Link an action you enjoy with one you need to do, so the needed behavior becomes more attractive. Use the formula: I will only do the thing I want when I do the thing I need.

> Temptation bundling works by linking an action you want to do
> with an action you need do. You’re more likely to find a behavior
> attractive if you get to do one of your favorite things at the
> same time.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits, Chapter 8
