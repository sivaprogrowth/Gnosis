# The best essays start from curiosity, not certainty

Great essays are explorations rather than statements of what the writer already knows. Both writer and reader discover something along the way.

> The best essays begin with curiosity, not certainty; they're a
> journey of discovery for both writer and reader.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
