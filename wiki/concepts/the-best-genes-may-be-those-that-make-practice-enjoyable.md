# The best genes may be those that make practice enjoyable

Good genes are usually linked to athleticism or intelligence. The bigger genetic advantage may come from genes that make you want to compete, help you stay focused, and let you enjoy practice.

> The usual story is that people with "good genes" are those with
> incredible athleticism or remarkable intelligence, but sometimes
> I wonder if the greatest genetic edge goes to the people with
> whatever genes encourage the desire to compete, the ability to
> stay focused, and the enjoyment of practice.
>
> — James Clear
