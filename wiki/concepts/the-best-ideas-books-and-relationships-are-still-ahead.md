# The best ideas, books, and relationships are still ahead

It is never too late because the best possibilities in business, writing, and relationships have not yet been realized. The space of opportunity remains largely unexplored.

> The best business ideas have yet to be tried. The best books
> have yet to be written. The best relationships have yet to be
> formed.
>
> It's not too late. The space of possibilities is endless. The
> most interesting terrain remains unexplored.
>
> — James Clear
