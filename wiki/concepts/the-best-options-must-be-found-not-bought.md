# The best options must be found, not bought

The best properties, employees, and clients are rarely on the market. That doesn't mean you can't have them; it means you have to seek them out and sell yourself.

> The best properties are rarely for sale.
>
> The best employees are rarely job hunting.
>
> The best clients are rarely shopping.
>
> The best option is usually off the market. Most people think this
> means you can’t have it. What it really means is you have to go
> find it and sell yourself.
>
> — James Clear
