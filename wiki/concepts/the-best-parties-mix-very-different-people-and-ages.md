# The best parties mix very different people and ages

Good parties bring together a wild mix of people from different professions, places, and generations. Mixing age groups especially makes for great conversation.

> The best parties are a wild mixture. Take some corporation
> presidents, add a bearded painter, a professional jockey, your
> visiting friends from Brussels, a politician, a hairdresser, and
> a professor of physics, toss them all together, and try to get
> them to stop talking long enough to eat!
>
> It's especially important to have all age groups.
>
> — Joan Crawford, My Way of Life
