# The best practice what no one wants to

Most people practice the enjoyable parts of a skill. Top performers deliberately work on the unpleasant parts that others avoid.

> A lot of people practice what's fun, but the very best practice
> what no one wants to.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
