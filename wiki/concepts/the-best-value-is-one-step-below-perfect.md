# The best value is one step below perfect

The last bit of quality costs a disproportionate amount of time and money. The best purchases deliver most of the value for much less than the cost of perfection.

> Perfection is expensive. The last 5 percent of quality almost
> always costs a disproportionate amount of time and money.
>
> My favorite type of purchase is where you spend 80 percent of the
> cost, but get 95 percent of the value. The best combination of
> cost and quality is often one step down from perfect.
>
> — James Clear
