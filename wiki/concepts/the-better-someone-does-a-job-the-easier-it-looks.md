# The better someone does a job, the easier it looks

Skilled work tends to look effortless from the outside. You usually only appreciate how difficult it is when you attempt it yourself.

> Usually, the better someone does their job, the easier it looks.
> You rarely realize how hard it is until you try it yourself.
>
> — James Clear
