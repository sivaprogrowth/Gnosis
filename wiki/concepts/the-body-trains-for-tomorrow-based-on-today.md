# The body trains for tomorrow based on today

Modern life keeps us sitting, standing, or lying down, and physical abilities fade from disuse. Break the mold by moving in other ways, because what you ask of your body today shapes what it can do tomorrow.

> Our physical abilities rarely vanish overnight. They usually fade away from disuse. The body trains for tomorrow based on what it is asked to do today.
>
> — James Clear
