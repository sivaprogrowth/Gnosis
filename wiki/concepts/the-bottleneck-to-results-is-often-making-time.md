# The bottleneck to results is often making time

People are usually capable of doing the work that gets results. What holds them back is not making the time to actually do it.

> "In many cases, the bottleneck to achieving results is simply
> making the time to do the work.
>
> You're capable of exercising, but are you making the time?
>
> You're capable of writing, but are you making the time?
>
> You're capable of reading, but are you making the time?"
>
> — James Clear
