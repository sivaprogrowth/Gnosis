# The cost of convenience is vitality

Having everything done for you saves time, but the feeling of being alive comes from full engagement in the right task, not freedom from all tasks.

> The cost of convenience is vitality. You can have a lot of things
> done for you in the modern world — and many of them are great
> time savers! — but the feeling of being alive comes from being
> fully engaged in the right task, not free from all tasks.
>
> — James Clear
