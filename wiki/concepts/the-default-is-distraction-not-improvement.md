# The default is distraction, not improvement

Unless you deliberately choose a better path, social and cultural inertia will push you toward a worse one.

> If you do not actively choose a better way, then society,
> culture, and the general inertia of life will push you into a
> worse way. The default is distraction, not improvement.
>
> — James Clear
