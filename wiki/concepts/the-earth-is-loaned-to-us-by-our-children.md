# The Earth is loaned to us by our children

We should care for the Earth because it belongs to future generations rather than being an inheritance we own.

> "Treat the Earth well. It was not given to you by your parents,
> it was loaned to you by your children.”
>
> — Native American proverb, A Seat at the Table: Huston Smith in Conversation with Native Americans on Religious Freedom
