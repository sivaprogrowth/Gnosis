# The focused lose to the obsessed

Each level of commitment beats the one below it: the average beat the lazy, the focused beat the average, and the obsessed beat the focused.

> The lazy lose to the average. The average lose to the focused.The
> focused lose to the obsessed.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
