# The goal is to live your life, not beat theirs

Someone will always seem to be doing better, but comparison wastes energy. Focus on your own path and keep moving forward even when progress is slow.

> But comparison is a poor use of energy. You were not meant to
> inhabit someone else's story. You have your own work to do. The
> goal is not to beat their life, the goal is to live your life.
> Keep your eyes on your own paper. Stay on the path and continue
> forward, even when progress feels slow.
>
> — James Clear
