# The greatest pleasure is secret good deeds discovered by accident

Doing good quietly, without seeking credit, and having it come to light by chance brings the deepest satisfaction.

> The greatest pleasure I know, is to do a good action by stealth,
> and to have it found out by accident.
>
> — Charles Lamb, Table Talk by the late Elia
