# The hard part is doing it daily regardless of feeling

Knowing what to do is easy; the challenge is doing it every day whether you feel like it or not. Waiting for inspiration means you've already lost.

> The hard part isn't knowing what to do; it's doing it daily,
> whether you feel like it or not.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
