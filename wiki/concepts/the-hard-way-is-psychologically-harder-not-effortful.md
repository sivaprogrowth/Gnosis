# The hard way is psychologically harder, not effortful

Taking the hard path usually doesn't demand more daily effort; what makes it hard is the mental work of thinking long-term, dropping limiting beliefs, resisting status and approval, and trusting small things to add up.

> The hard way is rarely harder in terms of daily effort, but it
> is often harder psychologically.
>
> It requires:
>
> - thinking long-term- removing limiting beliefs- avoiding the
> lure of status and approval- trusting that small things will lead
> to big things
>
> It’s a mental battle.
>
> — James Clear
