# The ideal reader cannot sleep holding the right book

A great book matched to the right reader is so gripping that the reader cannot put it down to sleep.

> "The ideal reader cannot sleep when holding the writer he was
> meant to be with."
>
> — Zadie Smith, The Best American Nonrequired Reading
