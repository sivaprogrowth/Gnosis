# The internet lets you be niche at scale

The best way to make things that take off is to become a small expert in a narrow area. Like Tokyo's huge market supporting odd specialty shops, the internet gives even tiny niches a big enough audience.

> But in a weird way, the best way to
> produce things that take off is to produce small things. To
> become a small expert.
>
> — Derek Thompson, Masters in Business
