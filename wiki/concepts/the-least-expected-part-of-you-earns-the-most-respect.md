# The least expected part of you earns the most respect

Having at least one surprising hobby or passion makes you more interesting, and the unexpected sides of a person tend to be the most respected.

> Broaden your interests. It's nice to have at least one
> surprising hobby or passion. People find it interesting. In many
> ways, the part of you that is least expected is more respected.
>
> — James Clear
