# The loudest signals come from the emptiest sources

People who loudly announce what they have often lack it. Those who truly possess something rarely feel the need to broadcast it.

> The loudest signals come from the emptiest sources. Those who
> truly possess something rarely need to announce it.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
