# The meaning you give work determines its difficulty

How hard work feels depends more on your relationship to it than on the work itself. Meaningful work energizes while work seen as meaningless exhausts quickly.

> The meaning you give work determines its difficulty.
>
> A coder working on a passion project works 12 hours straight and
> calls it energizing. That same programmer, doing maintenance on
> legacy code they consider meaningless, feels exhausted after 2
> hours.
>
> Your relationship with the work shapes its weight more than the
> work itself.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
