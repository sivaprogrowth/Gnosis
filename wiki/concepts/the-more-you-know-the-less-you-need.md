# The more you know, the less you need

Mastery comes from working toward simplicity and replacing complex technology with knowledge. Living more simply need not be impoverished but can be richer in what matters.

> I believe the way toward mastery of any endeavor is to work
> toward simplicity; replace complex technology with knowledge. The
> more you know, the less you need. From my feeble attempts at
> simplifying my own life I’ve learned enough to know that should
> we have to, or choose to, live more simply, it won’t be an
> impoverished life but one richer in all the ways that really
> matter.
>
> — Yvon Chouinard
