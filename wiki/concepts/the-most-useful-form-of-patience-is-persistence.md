# The most useful form of patience is persistence

Patience means waiting for things to get better on their own. Persistence means keeping on working when results take longer than expected, and that is the more useful kind.

> The most useful form of patience is persistence.
>
> Patience implies waiting for things to improve on their own.
>
> Persistence implies keeping your head down and continuing to work
> when things take longer than you expect.
>
> — James Clear
