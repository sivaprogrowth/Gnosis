# The opposite of sensitive is insensitive, not brave

Refusing to notice, feel, or imagine is not courage. Being insensitive is nothing to be proud of.

> The opposite of sensitive is
> not brave. It’s not brave to refuse to pay attention, to refuse
> to notice, to refuse to feel and know and imagine. The opposite
> of sensitive is insensitive, and that’s no badge of honor.
>
> — Glenan Doyle
