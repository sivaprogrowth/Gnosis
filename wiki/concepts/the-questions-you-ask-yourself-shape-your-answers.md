# The questions you ask yourself shape your answers

How you frame questions to yourself determines the answers you get. Asking why you fail invites self-criticism, while asking how you can succeed invites motivation, so choose your questions deliberately.

> The questions you ask yourself will largely determine the answers you get.
>
> * "Why am I not successful?" You'll get answers that berate you.
> * "How can I succeed here?" You'll get answers that push you.
>
> Be deliberate in the questions you ask yourself.
>
> — Julie Gurner, Twitter
