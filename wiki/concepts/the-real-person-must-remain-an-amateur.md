# The real person must remain an amateur

Professionalism is only an outer shell. Underneath, one should stay an amateur who loves the work.

> It is good for a professional to be reminded that his
> professionalism is only a husk, that the real person must remain
> an amateur, a lover of the work.
>
> — May Sarton, Plant Dreaming Deep
