# The real question is when, not which

Many choices are not either-or but a matter of timing. Opposite answers like rest or train can both be right, so the question is which fits right now.

> Many times in life the question is not whether to do thing or
> another, but when to emphasize one aspect or another.
>
> Rest or train? Research or write? Diversify or concentrate?
>
> Opposite answers can both be right. The question is which one is
> the right answer for right now.
>
> — James Clear
