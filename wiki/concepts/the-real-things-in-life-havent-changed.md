# The real things in life haven't changed

Timeless virtues remain the same: honesty, making the most of what you have, enjoying simple pleasures, and courage in hard times.

> The real things haven’t changed. It is still best to be honest
> and truthful; to make the most of what we have; to be happy with
> simple pleasures; and have courage when things go wrong.
>
> — Laura Ingalls Wilder, Laura's Letter to Children
