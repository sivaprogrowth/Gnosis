# The rule-maker might say yes if you ask

Rules aren't always final; the person who made the rule may grant an exception if you show up in person and ask.

> The person who wrote the rule might say yes if you actually
> show up and ask.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project
