# The second question separates earned simplicity from shallowness

Simple and shallow answers sound alike at first. Someone who earned their simplicity can go many levels deeper when questioned, while someone who skipped the work collapses quickly.

> Simple and shallow sound the same until you ask the second
> question. The person who earned their simplicity can go ten
> levels deep when challenged. The person who skipped the work
> falls apart at level two.
