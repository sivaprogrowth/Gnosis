# The shape of genius is simplicity

Great work resists compression: great explanations are short, great software needs fewer clicks, and great processes waste less. Excellence tends toward simplicity.

> Great work resists compression.
>
> Great explanations are short, but potent.
>
> Great software delivers the same outcome in fewer clicks.
>
> Great manufacturing processes create products in less time and
> with less waste.
>
> The shape of genius is simplicity.
>
> — James Clear
