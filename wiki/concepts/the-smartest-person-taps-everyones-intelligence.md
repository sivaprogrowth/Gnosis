# The smartest person taps everyone's intelligence

The smartest person in a room is usually not the one who knows most, but the one who draws on the intelligence of everyone present.

> “The smartest person in the room, I’ve learned, is usually the
> person who knows how to tap into the intelligence of every person
> in the room.”
>
> — Scott Kelly
