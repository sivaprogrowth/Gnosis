# The stories you read or tell quietly reshape your world

Stories work on us beneath conscious awareness, gradually altering how we see and live in the world, so choose them carefully.

> Beware the stories you read or tell; subtly, at night, beneath
> the waters of consciousness, they are altering your world.
>
> — Ben Okri, A Way of Being Free
