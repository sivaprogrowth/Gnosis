# The story you emphasize is the one you notice

Whether you look for a hostile world or a helpful one, you will find evidence for it. The material for a sweet or bitter life is always present, and your chosen focus determines what you see.

> If you look for evidence that people are conspiring to hold you
> back and the world is working against you, then it will take you
> no time at all to find precisely that.
>
> If you expect to encounter helpful people and experience a world
> that is working with you, then you find plenty of evidence to
> support that view as well.
>
> The raw material for a sweet life or a bitter life is always
> there. The story you emphasize is the one you notice.
>
> — James Clear
