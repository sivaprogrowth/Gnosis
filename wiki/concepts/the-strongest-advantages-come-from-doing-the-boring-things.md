# The strongest advantages come from doing the boring things

Many advantages come from what you do, not from what you are given. Most people know what to do. The edge comes from doing it when it's boring, the payoff is far away, or no one is watching.

> We tend to think the people who get ahead have more talent, better connections, greater opportunities, or better luck. Sometimes they do. But some of the strongest advantages aren't things you're given. They're things you do. Most people already know what they should do. The hard part is doing it when it’s boring, the payoff is far away, or no one is watching. That’s where your advantage comes from.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
