# The theory that excuses laziness is probably wrong

When choosing between two explanations, be suspicious of the one that lets you off the hook. The more demanding one is likely correct.

> If you're trying to choose between two theories and one gives
> you an excuse for being lazy, the other one is probably right.
>
> — [[paul-graham|Paul Graham]], What You'll Wish You'd Known
