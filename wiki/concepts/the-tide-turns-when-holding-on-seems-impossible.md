# The tide turns when holding on seems impossible

Don't give up at your lowest point, because that is often exactly when things start to improve.

> When you get into a tight place, and everything goes against you
> till it seems as if you couldn't hold on a minute longer, never
> give up then, for that's just the place and time that the tide'll
> turn.
>
> — Harriet Beecher Stowe, Oldtown Folks (1869)
