# The world is a museum of passion projects

Everything around us exists because people made it happen, and every one of those things required enormous tenacity.

> “As you become an adult, you realize that things around you
> weren't just always there; people made them happen. But only
> recently have I started to internalize how much tenacity
> *everything* requires. That hotel, that park, that railway. The
> world is a museum of passion projects.”
>
> — John Collison
