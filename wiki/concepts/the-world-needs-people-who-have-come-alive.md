# The world needs people who have come alive

Rather than chasing what the world seems to need, pursue what energizes you, because people who are fully alive are what the world needs most.

> “Don’t ask what the world needs. Ask what makes you come alive,
> and go do it. Because what the world needs is people who have
> come alive.”
>
> — Howard Thurman, The Living Wisdom of Howard Thurman: A Visionary for Our Time

> “Don’t ask what the world needs. Ask what makes you come alive. Because what the world needs most is more people who have come alive.”
>
> — Howard Thurman
