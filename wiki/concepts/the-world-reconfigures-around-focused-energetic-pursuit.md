# The world reconfigures around focused, energetic pursuit

The world is more malleable than it seems. If you know what you want and pursue it with full energy and passion, things often rearrange in your favor faster than expected.

> The world is a very malleable place. If you know what you want,
> and you go for it with maximum energy and drive and passion, the
> world will often reconfigure itself around you much more quickly
> and easily than you would think.
>
> — [[marc-andreessen|Marc Andreessen]], Pmarca Guide to Career Planning: Opportunity
