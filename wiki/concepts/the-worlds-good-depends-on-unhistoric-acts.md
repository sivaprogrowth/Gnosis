# The world's good depends on unhistoric acts

Much of the good in the world comes from ordinary people who lived faithful, unnoticed lives. Making the world better matters more than being remembered by history.

> ... for the growing good of the world is partly dependent on unhistoric acts; and that things are not so terrible for you and me as they might have been, is half owing to the number who lived faithfully a hidden life and rest in unvisited tombs.
>
> — George Eliot, Middlemarch
