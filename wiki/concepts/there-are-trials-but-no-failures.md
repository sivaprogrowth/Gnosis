# There are trials but no failures

Setbacks are just attempts along the way, not final failures, and moving fast matters more than minimizing cost.

> Learning that speed matters more than cost and that "there are trials, but there are no failures."
>
> — Chung Ju-yung, Chung Ju-yung's autobiography
