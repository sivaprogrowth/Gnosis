# There is no shortcut for the work

No magic pill replaces doing the work. Results come from habits and doing things even when you don't want to.

> There is no shortcut for the work. Period. There’s no magic
> pill; there’s no magic elixir. It’s about habits. You have to do
> things even when you don’t want to do them.
>
> — David Segal, The Knowledge Project Podcast
