# There is no substitute for courage

Education, books, and supportive people can help, but none of them can act, decide, or live for you. Eventually you must make the choice yourself.

> You can graduate with the finest degrees. You can read the most
> useful books. You can enjoy the loving support of family and
> friends.
>
> But your degrees can't take action for you. Your books can't make
> the decision for you. Your family can't live your life for you.
>
> There is no substitute for courage. At some point, you have to
> make the choice.
>
> — James Clear
