# Things lose their charm once possessed

What we chase often feels less appealing once we have it. Clear's takeaway is to choose pursuits where you enjoy the journey, since the chase is often more fun than the reward.

> An object in possession seldom retains the same charm that it
> had in pursuit.
>
> — Pliny the Younger, Epistulae (Book II, Letter 15)
