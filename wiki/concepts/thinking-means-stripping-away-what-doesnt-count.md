# Thinking means stripping away what doesn't count

Good thinking is the ability to look at complex situations, filter out the irrelevant, and find the core.

> Thinking is all about the ability to look at complex situations
> and strip away things that don't count—the ability to filter out
> situations and find what's at their core.
>
> — Douglas Hofstadter
