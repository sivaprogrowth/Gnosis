# Those who show up are those you helped selflessly

The people who support you in hard times are not the ones you tried to impress. They are the ones you helped when you had nothing to gain.

> The people who show up for you when you need them are never the
> ones you tried to impress.
>
> They're the people you helped when there was nothing in it for
> you.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
