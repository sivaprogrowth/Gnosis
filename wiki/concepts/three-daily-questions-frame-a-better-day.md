# Three daily questions frame a better day

Start the day asking what you are optimizing for, ask during the day what the best small thing to do right now is, and end by asking whether you did your best.

> At the beginning of the day:
>
> "What am I optimizing for?"
>
>
>
> During the day:
>
> "What's the best small thing I can do right now?"
>
>
>
> At the end of the day:
>
> "Did I do my best?"
>
> — James Clear
