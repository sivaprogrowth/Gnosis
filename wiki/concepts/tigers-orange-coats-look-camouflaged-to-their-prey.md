# Tigers' orange coats look camouflaged to their prey

Most prey mammals are dichromats who can't tell red/orange from green, so a tiger's orange coat blends into green forest for them even though it stands out to humans.

> To these prey animals, a tiger’s bright orange coat does not stand out against green forest vegetation the way it does to us. Instead, it appears as muted yellowish, brownish, or greenish tones that blend with the background.
>
