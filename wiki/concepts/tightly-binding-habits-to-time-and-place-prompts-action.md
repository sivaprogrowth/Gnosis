# Tightly binding habits to time and place prompts action

The more specifically a habit is tied to a particular time and location, the more likely you are to notice the moment to act.

> The more tightly bound your new habit is to a specific time and
> location, the better the odds are that you will notice when the
> time comes to act.
>
> — James Clear
