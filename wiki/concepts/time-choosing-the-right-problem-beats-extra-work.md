# Time choosing the right problem beats extra work

Effort alone isn't enough; it must go toward the right problem. Time spent deciding what to focus on pays off more than extra hours of misdirected work.

> “It's not enough to simply apply effort; you must work on the
> right thing.
>
> One hour solving the right problem beats ten hours on the wrong
> one.
>
> Spend time thinking about what to focus on.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> INTERVIEWER: "What is your best tip for saving time?"
>
> ILIC: "Think 8 hours, work 2 hours."
>
> — Mirko Ilić, Office Tales of Mirko Ilić (2016)
