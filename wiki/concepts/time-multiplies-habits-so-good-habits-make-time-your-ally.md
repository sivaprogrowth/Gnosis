# Time multiplies habits, so good habits make time your ally

Over time, small differences between good and bad habits grow into large gaps between success and failure. Good habits make time work for you, bad habits make it work against you.

> Time magnifies the margin between success and failure. It will
> multiply whatever you feed it. Good habits make time your ally.
> Bad habits make time your enemy.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
