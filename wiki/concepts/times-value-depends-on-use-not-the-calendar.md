# Time's value depends on use, not the calendar

The value of time depends on how you use it, not the calendar. One person gets a week's worth from a year while another gets a year's worth from a week.

> Don't be fooled by the calendar. There are only as many days in
> the year as you make use of. One person gets only a week's value
> out of a year while another gets a full year's value out of a
> week.
>
> — Charles Richards
