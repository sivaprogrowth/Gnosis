# To get what something has, get close to it

Qualities like joy and peace are not gained from a distance; you must participate in and draw near to the thing that has them.

> If you want to get warm you must stand near the fire: if you
> want to be wet you must get into the water. If you want joy,
> power, peace, eternal life, you must get close to, or even into,
> the thing that has them.
>
> — C. S. Lewis, Nearer (hat tip to Dylan O'Sullivan)
