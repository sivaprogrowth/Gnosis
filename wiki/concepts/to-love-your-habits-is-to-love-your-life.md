# To love your habits is to love your life

Daily routines are not chores to get through but life itself. Giving them care and attention turns them into fulfilling rituals, and enjoying your days adds up to enjoying your life.

> Find the beauty and joy in your daily rituals and you will find
> beauty and joy in your daily life. To love your habits is to love
> your days, and to love your days is to love your life.
>
> — James Clear
