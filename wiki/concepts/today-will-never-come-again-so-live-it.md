# Today will never come again, so live it

Each moment is unrepeatable, so the time to act is now. There are no second chances at a single moment.

> It's hard to remember that this day will never come again. That
> the time is now and the place is here and that there are no
> second chances at a single moment.
>
> — Jeanette Winterson, The Passion

> This day will never come again and anyone who fails to eat and drink and taste and smell it will never have it offered to them again in all eternity. The sun will never shine as it does today.
>
> — Hermann Hesse, Klingsor's Last Summer
