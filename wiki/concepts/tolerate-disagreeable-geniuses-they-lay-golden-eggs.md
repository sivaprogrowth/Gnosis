# Tolerate disagreeable geniuses; they lay golden eggs

Mediocre people resent and try to destroy genius, but rare geniuses, though usually disagreeable, are extremely valuable and should be protected.

> “Tolerate genius.
>
> My observation has been that mediocre men recognize genius,
> resent it, and feel compelled to destroy it.
>
> There are very few men of genius.
>
> But we need all we can find. Almost without exception, they are
> disagreeable. Don't destroy them. They lay golden eggs.”
>
> — David Ogilvy
