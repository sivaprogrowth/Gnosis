# Tolerating more pain lets you learn more

Pain is a signal that you still need to learn. The more pain you can bear, the more you can learn.

> “Pain is the universe’s way of demanding that you continue to
> learn. The more pain you can tolerate, the more you can learn.”
>
> — Phil Stutz and Barry Michels
