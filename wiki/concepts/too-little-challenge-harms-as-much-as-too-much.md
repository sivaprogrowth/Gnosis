# Too little challenge harms as much as too much

Like muscles that need stimulus but break under excessive load, life goes badly with either too much or too little challenge.

> In the gym, if you experience no stimulus your muscles won't
> grow. If you step under 10,000 pounds your body will break.
>
> Life is similar.
>
> Too much challenge makes life hard, but so does too little.
>
> — James Clear
