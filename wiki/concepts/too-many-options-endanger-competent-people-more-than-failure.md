# Too many options endanger competent people more than failure

Competence brings more opportunities, and more opportunities make it harder to focus on one thing, which can be more harmful than failing.

> Options can be more dangerous to a competent person than failure.
>
> The more competence you have, the more opportunity you have. And the more opportunity you have, the harder it is to focus on one thing.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
