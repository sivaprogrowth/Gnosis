# Too much bad news for complacency, too much good for despair

The world has enough problems that we shouldn't be complacent, and enough progress that we shouldn't give up hope. Be proactive yet positive.

> There is too much bad news to justify complacency. There is too much good news to justify despair.
>
> — Donella Meadows, Thinking in Systems
