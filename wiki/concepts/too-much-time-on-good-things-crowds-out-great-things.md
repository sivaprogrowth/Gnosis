# Too much time on good things crowds out great things

Opportunity cost means that spending time on good work leaves less time for great work. Understanding it requires eliminating good uses of time, which is why it is hard.

> A practical definition of opportunity cost:
>
> If you spend too much time working on good things, then you don’t
> have much time left to work on great things.
>
> Understanding opportunity cost means eliminating good uses of
> time. And that's what makes it hard.
>
> — James Clear
