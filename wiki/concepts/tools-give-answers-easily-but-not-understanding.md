# Tools give answers easily but not understanding

Tools can produce answers for you, but they do not give you the understanding behind those answers.

> A calculator can give you the answer, but it can’t teach you
> math.
>
> — Anthony Scilipoti, The Knowledge Project

> This is fascinating to think about through the lens of AI. You can get the answer without much effort, but you can't get the understanding.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
