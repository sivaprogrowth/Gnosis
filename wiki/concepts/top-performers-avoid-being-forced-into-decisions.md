# Top performers avoid being forced into decisions

People who get consistently better results stay in positions where almost every option is good, rather than letting circumstances force bad choices on them.

> One of the differences between people who
> consistently get better results than other people is that they’re
> rarely in a position where circumstances are thinking for them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project Podcast

> “One reason the best in the world make consistently good
> decisions is they rarely find themselves forced into a decision
> by circumstances.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Clear Thinking
