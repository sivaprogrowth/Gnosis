# Top performers cannot follow the norms of most people

Reaching the top 1% requires not imitating the social norms of the other 99%. Because humans imitate by nature, the higher you aim, the more deliberately you must choose the people around you.

> If you want to be in the top 1% of a particular domain, then you
> can’t take your cues from and follow the social norms of 99% of
> people.
>
> This is harder than it sounds. We are wired to imitate. The
> further you want to climb, the more carefully you need to
> construct your tribe.
>
> — James Clear
