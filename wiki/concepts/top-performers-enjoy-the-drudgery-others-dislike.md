# Top performers enjoy the drudgery others dislike

Elite swimmers enjoy the very hard, boring parts of training that weaker swimmers dislike. They do not experience their work as sacrifice; they like it.

> It is incorrect to believe
> that top athletes suffer great sacrifices to achieve their goals.
> Often, they don’t see what they do as sacrificial at all. They
> like it.
>
> — Daniel Chambliss, The Mundanity of Excellence

> "I read once that the true mark of a pro — at anything — is that
> he understands, loves, and is good at even the drudgery of his
> profession."
>
> — Paul Halmos, I Want to be a Mathematician
