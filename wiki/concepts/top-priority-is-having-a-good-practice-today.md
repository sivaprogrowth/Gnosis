# Top priority is having a good practice today

Despite six Super Bowl wins, Belichick says what he most wants to accomplish is a good practice today. The focus stays on the present day's work, not past achievements or distant goals.

> Belichick: "I'd like to go out and have a good practice today.
> That would be at the top of the list right now."
>
> — Bill Belichick, SB Nation
