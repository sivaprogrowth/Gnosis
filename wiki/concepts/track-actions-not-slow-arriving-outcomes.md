# Track actions, not slow-arriving outcomes

Waiting for outcomes like weight loss or savings creates a feedback loop too long to sustain motivation. Tracking actions gives immediate proof you are showing up.

> If you have to wait for the number on the
> scale to change, or for your bank account to increase, or for a
> sense of calm to wash over your life, then the feedback loop is
> often too long for you to maintain motivation.
>
> — James Clear
