# Track only your most important habits, simply

You don't need to track every habit, since tiny behaviors built with the Four Laws often stick anyway. For the most important ones, a simple calendar where you mark an X each day you do the habit is the easiest way to start.

> First, it’s not necessary to track every habit. If you
> employ the Four Laws of Behavior Change, then you’ll find that
> you can often stick to a tiny behavior whether you track it or
> not.
>
> However, I do believe tracking can be beneficial for the most
> important tasks.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
