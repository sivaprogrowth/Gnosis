# Trading a good daily life for more money is bad

If your life is already comfortable, earning more at the cost of a worse daily life is a poor trade. People often overfocus on wealth and neglect lifestyle.

> If you already live a comfortable life, then choosing to make
> more money but live a worse daily life is a bad trade.
>
> And yet, we talk ourselves into it all the time. We take
> promotions that pay more, but swallow our free time. We already
> have a successful business, but we break ourselves trying to make
> it even more successful.
>
> Too much focus on wealth, not enough focus on lifestyle.
>
> — James Clear
