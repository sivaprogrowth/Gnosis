# Trading time for money, then money for time

True wealth is measured by control over your time rather than by money alone.

> Rich people have money. Wealthy people have time.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> We spend time chasing money, then spend money chasing time.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> We spend time chasing money, then spend money chasing time.
>
> Most of what you want when you finally have money is cheap: a slow morning, a walk with no destination, a day without meetings.
>
> Real wealth is time.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
