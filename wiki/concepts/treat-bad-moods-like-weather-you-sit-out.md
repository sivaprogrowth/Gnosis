# Treat bad moods like weather you sit out

Moods are like weather: real, not changeable by wishing, and sometimes lasting a while, but they pass. Accept a bad day, find a mental umbrella, and take advantage when the sun returns.

> I've found that it's of some help to think of one's moods and
> feelings about the world as being similar to weather.
>
> Here are some obvious things about the weather: It’s real. You
> can't change it by wishing it away. If it's dark and rainy it
> really is dark and rainy and you can't alter it. It might be dark
> and rainy for two weeks in a row.
>
> But. It will be sunny one day.
>
> In the same way that one has to accept the weather, so one has to
> accept how one feels about life sometimes. "Today's a crap day,"
> is a perfectly realistic approach. It's all about finding a kind
> of mental umbrella. "Hey-ho, it's raining inside: it isn’t my
> fault and there's nothing I can do about it, but sit it out. But
> the sun may well come out tomorrow and when it does, I shall take
> full advantage."
>
> — Stephen Fry, It Will Be Sunny One Day
