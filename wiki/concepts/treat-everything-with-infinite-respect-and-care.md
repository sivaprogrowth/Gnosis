# Treat everything with infinite respect and care

Estée Lauder described an uncompromising attention to detail that ran through every part of her life, from meals and parties to business, friends, and family.

> “I have an uncompromising sense of detail which permeates
> everything I do. The meals I serve, the parties I have, the
> business I created, the places I go, my friends, my family, my
> business associates everything must be treated with infinite
> respect and care.”
>
> — Estée Lauder
