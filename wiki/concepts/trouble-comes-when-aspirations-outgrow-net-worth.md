# Trouble comes when aspirations outgrow net worth

Financial trouble arises when expectations grow faster than wealth; keep aspirations growing more slowly than net worth.

> If your aspirations are outgrowing your net worth, that’s when
> people get into trouble. If your net worth grows 10%, but your
> expectations grow 12%, that’s when you get into trouble.
>
> — Morgan Housel, The Knowledge Project Podcast
