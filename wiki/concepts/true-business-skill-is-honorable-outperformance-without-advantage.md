# True business skill is honorable outperformance without advantage

A businessman is proven not by luck or boom-year profits but by beating competitors in a tough field without initial advantages, honorably and while keeping his own and his community's respect.

> The test of a business man is not whether he can make money in
> one or two boom years, or can make money through the luck of
> getting into the field first, but whether in a highly competitive
> field, without having any initial advantage over his competitors,
> he can outdistance them in a perfect honourable way and keep the
> respect of himself and of his community.
>
> — Harvey Firestone
