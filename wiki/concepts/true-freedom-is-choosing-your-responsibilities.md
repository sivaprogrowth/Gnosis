# True freedom is choosing your responsibilities

The most satisfying freedom is not having no responsibilities. It is being free to pick which responsibilities you take on.

> The most satisfying form of freedom is not a life without
> responsibilities, but a life where you are free to choose your
> responsibilities.
>
> — James Clear
