# True pictures can still tell different stories

A camera records what is real, but the choice of what to frame produces many different pictures of the same scene. Accurate facts can still be selected to create blind spots.

> “The camera never lies… but you can take a thousand different
> pictures of the same scene.”
>
> — Hector Macdonald
