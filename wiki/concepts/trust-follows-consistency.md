# Trust follows consistency

Trust is earned through reliable, repeated behavior, whether a business delivering quality every time or a person keeping promises. The consistent pattern itself is the proof of trustworthiness.

> Trust follows consistency.
>
> The business that delivers a quality product every time earns the
> customer's trust.
>
> The person in the relationship who shows up reliably — who keeps
> promises, who responds with steadiness — earns the trust of the
> other.
>
> The pattern is the proof.
>
> — James Clear
