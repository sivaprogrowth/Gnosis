# Trust is built in small moments of restraint

Trust comes not from grand gestures but from small choices, like not taking the last slice when you could.

> Trust isn’t built in grand gestures. It’s built when you could
> take the last slice and don’t.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], Grinding it Out
