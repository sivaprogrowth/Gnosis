# Trust patterns across many successes, not single stories

Advice-givers are biased by their personal history, and there is no single path to success. Look for patterns repeated across many successful people rather than relying on one story.

> When reading books or listening to podcasts or taking advice,
> remember that everyone is biased to their personal history.
>
> The world is complex and there is no single path to a success.
> Look for patterns that are repeated across many successful
> people, not single stories.
>
> — James Clear
