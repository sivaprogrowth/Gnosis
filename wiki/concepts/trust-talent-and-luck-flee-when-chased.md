# Trust, talent, and luck flee when chased

The best things in life, such as trust, talent, and luck, run away the harder you chase them. A good reputation is what draws them to you.

> The best things in life—trust, talent, luck—have one thing in
> common: the harder you chase them, the faster they run.
>
> Your reputation is the only magnet strong enough to make them
> come to you.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
