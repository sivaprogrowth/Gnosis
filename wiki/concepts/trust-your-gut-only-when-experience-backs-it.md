# Trust your gut only when experience backs it

Gut reactions tend to be either very wrong or very right. They are wrong when driven purely by emotion in unfamiliar domains, and right when rooted in deep understanding and well-developed taste.

> Gut reactions are usually very wrong or very right.
>
> They tend to be wrong when they are based purely on emotion and
> in domains where you lack experience. They tend to be right when
> they are rooted in deep understanding and well-developed taste.
>
> Trust your gut when you have the experience to back it up.
>
> — James Clear
