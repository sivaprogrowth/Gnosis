# Trust your obsessions; they become usable later

Follow what you find yourself obsessed with, even if it seems uncommercial. Obsessions compost in the back of your mind and later combine into something useful.

> Trust your obsessions.
>
> — Neil Gaiman, 1997 PRO/con speech
