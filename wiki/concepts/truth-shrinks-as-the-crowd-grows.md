# Truth shrinks as the crowd grows

In large meetings people hold back honest opinions, so conversations avoid offense rather than reach truth. Smaller groups find truth more readily.

> “The truth shrinks as the crowd grows.
>
> In a large meeting at work, people hold back their honest
> opinions. The resulting conversation offends the fewest people
> but is often not the truth.
>
> Smaller groups are more likely to find truth than larger ones.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
