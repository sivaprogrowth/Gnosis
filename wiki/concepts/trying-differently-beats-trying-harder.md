# Trying differently beats trying harder

Extra effort gives small gains, but a new approach can give far bigger ones. Stay focused on the core problem while changing your strategy.

> You're more likely to unlock a big leap in performance by trying
> differently than by trying harder. You might be able to work 10%
> harder, but a different approach might work 10x better. Remain
> focused on the core problem, but explore a new line of attack.
> Persistence is not just about effort, but also strategy. Don't
> merely try harder, try differently.
>
> — James Clear
