# Turnaround stories sound clean because winners tell them later

Most turnaround stories are told years afterward by the winner, once the ending is known, which makes them sound neater than they really were.

> Most turnaround stories are told years later, by the winner, after the ending is already known. That’s why they always sound clean and polished.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project, conversation with Kaz Nejatian
