# Unchanged life structures keep you on the same path

Like water following an unchanged riverbed, you will keep heading in the same direction unless you change the underlying structures of your life.

> “If a riverbed remains unchanged, the water will continue to flow
> along the path it always has, since that is the most natural
> route for it to take. If the underlying structures of your life
> remain unchanged, the greatest tendency is for you to follow the
> same direction your life has always taken.”
>
> — Robert Fritz
