# Uncommon ideas arrive after pushing past boredom

Common ideas come before boredom sets in and uncommon ones come after it. Staying with a project past the point of boredom is where the most useful insights appear.

> Boredom is a filter. Common ideas come before it. Uncommon ideas
> come after it. Sit with a project long enough to get bored with
> it, then sit a little more. The most useful insights bubble up
> after you get bored.
>
> — James Clear
