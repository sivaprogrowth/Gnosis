# Understanding more lets us fear less

Nothing should be feared, only understood. Gaining understanding is the way to reduce fear.

> Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood. Now is the time to understand more, so that we may fear less.
>
> — Marie Curie, Madame Curie: A Biography by Eve Curie
