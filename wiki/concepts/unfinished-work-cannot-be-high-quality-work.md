# Unfinished work cannot be high quality work

Finishing is part of delivering quality. If perfectionism stops you from finishing, the work isn't high quality.

> Finishing projects is part of what it means to deliver high
> quality work. It's not high quality if your perfectionism
> prevents you from finishing.
>
> — James Clear
