# Universal appeal means universal indifference

Trying to please everyone leaves you meaningful to no one. Being distinct to some people matters more than being acceptable to all.

> “Too many people want to be something to everyone and end up
> being nothing to anyone.
>
> Universal appeal means universal indifference.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
