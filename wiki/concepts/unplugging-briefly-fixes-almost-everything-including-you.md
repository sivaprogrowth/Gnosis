# Unplugging briefly fixes almost everything, including you

Just as devices often work again after a short reset, people recover through brief rest.

> Almost everything will work again if you unplug it for a few
> minutes, including you.
>
> — Anne Lamott, 12 Truths I Learned from Life and Writing
