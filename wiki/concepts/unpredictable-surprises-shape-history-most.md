# Unpredictable surprises shape history most

Black swans are rare, unexpected events that defy our expectations and have a profound impact. No one sees them coming, and they change what seems possible.

> Black swans are the unexpected outliers, the rare and
> unpredictable events that defy our usual expectations and
> profoundly impact our world. They are the surprises that no one
> sees coming, the game-changers that reshape the landscape of the
> possible.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> We convince ourselves that the future is knowable, yet the pivotal moments of history are surprises.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
