# Unreflected knowledge is worth less than considered knowledge

A small amount of knowledge you have reflected on is more valuable than a vast amount you have not. Only by comparing truths against each other do you truly own what you know.

> “You may accumulate a vast amount of knowledge but it will be of
> far less value to you than a much smaller amount if you have not
> thought it over for yourself; because only through ordering what
> you know by comparing every truth with every other truth can you
> take complete possession of your knowledge and get it into your
> power."
>
> — Arthur Schopenhauer, Essays and Aphorisms
