# Unspoken expectations breed resentment and frustration

When you expect something from someone without saying so, you are setting yourself up to resent them when they fail to meet it.

> Unspoken expectations are premeditated resentments.
>
> — Neil Strauss

> Many frustrations are the result of unspoken expectations.
> Before you get too annoyed, make sure you are clearly expressing
> your thoughts.
>
> — James Clear
