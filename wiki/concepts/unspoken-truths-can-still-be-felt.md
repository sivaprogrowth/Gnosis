# Unspoken truths can still be felt

Even when something important goes unsaid, people sense it. Identify what needs discussing and clear the air.

> A truth unsaid can still be felt.
>
> What needs to be discussed, but hasn't been said yet?
>
> Clear the air.
>
> — James Clear
