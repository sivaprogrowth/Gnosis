# Until death, all defeat is psychological

As long as you are alive, being defeated is a matter of mindset rather than a final fact. The wording is attributed to Marcus Aurelius, though likely not his exact phrase.

> Until death, all defeat is psychological.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Attributed; similar themes in Meditations, but no source with this exact wording
