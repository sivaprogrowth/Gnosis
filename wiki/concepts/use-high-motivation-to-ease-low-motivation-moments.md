# Use high motivation to ease low-motivation moments

When motivation is high, prepare your environment so the next instance of the habit is easier, such as laying out gym clothes, buying thank-you cards, or picking a recipe in advance.

> Use the moments of high motivation to make it easier when you
> have low motivation.
>
> * When you feel like you should exercise, set out your gym
> clothes and water bottle so it's easier to do the next workout.
> * When you feel grateful, buy some Thank You cards so it's easier
> to write the next Thank You note.
> * When you feel the urge to eat healthy, pick a recipe now so you
> don't have to decide what to make for the next meal.
>
> Use the occasional burst of motivation to make the next habit
> easier.
>
> — James Clear
