# Use leverage wisely by considering second-order effects

Leverage is a tool requiring skill and judgment; use it by understanding the system you influence and thinking through second- and third-order effects.

> The key is to use leverage wisely and judiciously by
> understanding the systems you want to influence and considering
> the second- and third- order effects of your actions.
>
> Leverage is a tool, not a toy, and like any tool, it requires
> skill, judgment, and respect.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Great Mental Models v2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
