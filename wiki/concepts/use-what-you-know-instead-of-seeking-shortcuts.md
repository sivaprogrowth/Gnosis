# Use what you know instead of seeking shortcuts

People waste opportunities hunting for shortcuts or easier paths. Often results come simply from doing what is already in front of you with what you already know.

> Use what you already know. People are so busy searching for a
> shortcut — or hoping an easier path will reveal itself — they let
> numerous moments slip by when they could get results simply by
> doing what is right in front of them.
>
> — James Clear
