# Vanderbilt ruined cheaters instead of suing them

Vanderbilt's ethos was to bypass slow legal remedies and retaliate directly against those who wronged him.

> “Gentlemen: You have undertaken to cheat me. I won’t sue, for the
> law is too slow. I’ll ruin you.”
>
> — Cornelius Vanderbilt
