# Visible costs can hide larger invisible benefits

Things that look like obvious costs or waste may be delivering benefits that aren't easy to see.

> Visible costs often hide
> invisible benefits.
>
> — Rory Sutherland, The Knowledge Project

> Invisible benefits often outweigh visible costs.
>
> — Bernie Marcus, The Knowledge Project [Outliers]
