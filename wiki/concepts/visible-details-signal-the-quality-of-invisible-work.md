# Visible details signal the quality of invisible work

People judge the hidden parts of your work by the parts they can see. Sloppy visible work suggests the unseen work is just as bad or worse.

> People can’t see every choice you make, so they use the details they can see to infer the quality they cannot see.
>
> You can see it in the clean joints, the clear sentences, and the product's simplicity.
>
> If the work you can see is visibly sloppy, the work you can't see is likely to be the same (or worse).
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
