# Visible floss next to toothbrush built the habit

Clear only flossed occasionally while floss sat in a drawer, even with a good plan. Putting it in a bowl next to his toothbrush made the cue obvious, and the habit stuck for years.

> I never saw the floss, so I never used it. The cue
> wasn’t obvious.
>
> — James Clear
