# Visible success rests on unseen work

To shine in front of large audiences, you must put in more unseen work than everyone else when no one is watching.

> If you want to look good in front of thousands, you have to
> outwork thousands in front of nobody.
>
> — Damian Lillard, Twitter

> The price of success is paid in private. Visible triumphs are
> built on invisible work.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Behind every great achievement lies a long, unseen journey.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
