# Vision and action together can change the world

Vision alone is just a dream and action alone just passes time; combining the two is what creates change.

> “Vision without action is merely a dream. Action without vision
> just passes the time. Vision with action can change the world.”
>
> — Joel Arthur Barker Ratcliffe
