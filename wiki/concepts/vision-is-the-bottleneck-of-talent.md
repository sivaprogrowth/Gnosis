# Vision is the bottleneck of talent

Most talent is wasted because people lack clear direction about what they want, not because they lack effort. Few capable people focus on what matters.

> Vision is the bottleneck of talent.
>
> Most talent is wasted because people do not clearly know what
> they want. It’s not a lack of effort, but a lack of direction.
>
> There are many capable people in the world, but relatively few
> that focus on what matters.
>
> — James Clear
