# Vision without discipline cannot become real

After leaving Apple, Jobs had the vision to change the world but not yet the discipline to carry it out. His failing years at NeXT taught him to focus, trust talented people, and compromise pragmatically.

> Jobs had the vision to change the world. What he did not yet have was the discipline to make that vision real.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project (conversation with Geoffrey Cain)
