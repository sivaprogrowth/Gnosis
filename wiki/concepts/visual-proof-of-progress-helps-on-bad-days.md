# Visual proof of progress helps on bad days

When you feel down or see few results, a visible record of your work reminds you how far you've come and how consistent you've been.

> These visual signals of progress can be particularly powerful on
> a bad day. When you’re feeling down, it’s easy to forget about
> all the progress you have already made.
>
> — James Clear
