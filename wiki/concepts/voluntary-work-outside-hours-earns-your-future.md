# Voluntary work outside hours earns your future

The work you are required to do pays your bills. The extra work you choose to do outside regular hours is what creates opportunity.

> Mandatory work earns you a living. Voluntary work earns you a
> future.
>
> The work you do during regular hours earns you a paycheck, but
> it's what you do (or don't do) outside of those hours that earns
> you opportunity.
>
> I've seen many people fight this insight, and almost none of them
> win.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
