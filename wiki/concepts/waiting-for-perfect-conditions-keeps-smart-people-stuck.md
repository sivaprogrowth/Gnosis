# Waiting for perfect conditions keeps smart people stuck

Intelligent people often stall by waiting for ideal conditions, while those who act and test crude versions move ahead and create the conditions themselves.

> Waiting for perfect conditions is how otherwise intelligent
> people keep themselves stuck.
>
> Two entrepreneurs spot the same opportunity. One builds elaborate
> plans; the other tests crude prototypes. The planner is still
> planning when the tester sells their company.
>
> While you wait for perfect conditions, others are creating them.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
