# Wake up much earlier than you need to

Having one to two unhurried hours in the morning makes you feel far better. Productivity in the morning can come later, if you want it.

> The secret to waking up is to wake up way earlier than you have
> to. When you have 1-2 hours where you can just sit there calmly,
> drinking coffee, and not be rushed, you feel ten times better.
> Later on, you can work on being productive in the morning too, if
> you please.
>
> — Simon Sarris, Twitter
