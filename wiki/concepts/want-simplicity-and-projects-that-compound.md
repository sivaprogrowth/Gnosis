# Want simplicity and projects that compound

Aim for simplicity with no wasted movement or effort. Choose projects that have a long runway and feed one another.

> You want two things:
>
> 1. Simplicity, simplicity, simplicity. No wasted movement. No
> wasted effort.
>
> 2. Compounding. Every project should have a long runway and feed
> the others.
>
> — James Clear
