# Wanting life to be easy makes it complicated

Life is simple but hard. Trying to make it easy rather than accepting the difficulty is what creates complication.

> Life is simple but not easy. The problem is we want life to be
> easy, which makes it complicated.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
