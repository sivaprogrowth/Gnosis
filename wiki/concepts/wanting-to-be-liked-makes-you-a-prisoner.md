# Wanting to be liked makes you a prisoner

If being liked is your main goal, you cannot take risks, disagree, or push boundaries, so you end up trapped by others' expectations.

> When your primary goal is to be liked, you can't take risks. You
> can't disagree. You can't push boundaries. You become a prisoner
> of other people's expectations.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
