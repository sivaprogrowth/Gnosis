# Watching competitors too closely makes you play their game

If you focus too much on your competition, you get pulled into their strategy and lose sight of your own.

> “A risk with watching your competition too closely is that you
> get drawn into playing their game and lose focus on your own.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
