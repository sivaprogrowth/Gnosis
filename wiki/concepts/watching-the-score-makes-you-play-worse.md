# Watching the score makes you play worse

Checking results feels productive but distracts from the only thing that changes them: deciding what to do now.

> The more you watch the score, the worse you play.
>
> Checking the scoreboard feels like doing something, but it pulls
> attention away from the only thing that can change it: What now?
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
