# We avoid simple things because they don't look smart

Smart people skip simple, effective actions because they feel unimpressive, inventing complicated alternatives that do less. Top performers just do basic things better than everyone else.

> We avoid doing simple things that work because they don't make us
> look smart.
>
> Smart people feel stupid doing simple things, so we invent
> complicated alternatives that accomplish less but feel more
> intellectually satisfying.
>
> Meanwhile, the people who dominate their fields are doing
> embarrassingly basic things, but they do them better than
> everyone else.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
