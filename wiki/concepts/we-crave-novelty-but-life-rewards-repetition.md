# We crave novelty but life rewards repetition

Good ideas that work are rare, yet instead of fully exploiting them we chase new ones. We prefer many maybe-ideas over one proven idea.

> We're wired for novelty, but life rewards repetition.
>
> Good ideas are rare. When you find something that works, you've
> found gold. But instead of mining it, we go looking for more
> gold.
>
> We'd rather have ten ideas that might work than one that does.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
