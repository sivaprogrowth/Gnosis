# We judge everyone by our own standard as baseline

People treat their own behavior as the norm, so anyone slower seems foolish and anyone faster seems reckless.

> “Everyone driving slower than you is an idiot, and everyone
> driving faster than you is a maniac!”
>
> — George Carlin
