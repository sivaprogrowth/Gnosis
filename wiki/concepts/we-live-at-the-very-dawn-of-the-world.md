# We live at the very dawn of the world

Viewing our era as ancient times, close to the beginning of history, encourages us to be earnest, ingenious, and brave. It also keeps us from assuming unanswered questions are unknowable and not worth pursuing.

> My own personal theory is that this is the very dawn of the
> world. We're hardly more than an eyeblink away from the fall of
> Troy, and scarcely an interglaciation removed from the Altamira
> cave painters. We live in extremely interesting ancient times.
>
> I like this idea. It encourages us to be earnest and ingenious
> and brave, as befits ancestral peoples; but keeps us from
> deciding that because we don't know all the answers, they must be
> unknowable and thus unprofitable to pursue.
>
> — Teresa Nielsen Hayden, On Time
