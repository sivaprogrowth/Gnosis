# We live forever through what we give

Everyone's success rests on others' support, so we owe it to others to let them stand on our shoulders in turn. Our lasting legacy comes from what we give, not what we take.

> You are where you are today because you stand on somebody’s
> shoulders. And wherever you are heading, you cannot get there by
> yourself. If you stand on the shoulders of others, you have a
> reciprocal responsibility to live your life so that others may
> stand on your shoulders. It’s the quid pro quo of life. We exist
> temporarily through what we take, but we live forever through
> what we give.
>
> — Vernon Jordan, 2002 speech at Howard University
