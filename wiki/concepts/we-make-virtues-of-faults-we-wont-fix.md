# We make virtues of faults we won't fix

People often justify their mistakes as good qualities rather than correcting them.

> We try to make virtues out of the faults we have no wish to
> correct.
>
> — Francois de La Rochefoucauld, Reflections; or Sentences and Moral Maxims
