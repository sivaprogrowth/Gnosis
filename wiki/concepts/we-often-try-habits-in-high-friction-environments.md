# We often try habits in high-friction environments

People sabotage themselves by attempting good habits in settings full of temptations and interruptions, like eating healthy in a house full of cookies.

> Too often, we try to start habits in
> high-friction environments. We try to eat healthy in a house
> filled with chips and cookies.
>
> — James Clear
