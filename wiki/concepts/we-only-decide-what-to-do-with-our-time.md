# We only decide what to do with our time

We cannot choose the hard times we live through; we can only choose how to use the time we are given.

> Frodo: "I wish none of this had happened."
>
> Gandalf: "So do all who live to see such times, but that is not
> for them to decide. All we have to decide is what to do with the
> time that is given to us."
>
> — J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings
