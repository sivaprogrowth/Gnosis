# We regret the things we did not do

At the end of life, mistakes and embarrassments fade in importance; what brings regret is chances not taken. Solace comes from having grabbed opportunities to do something cool.

> It is not the things we do in life that we regret on our death
> bed. It is the things we do not. I assure you I've done a lot of
> really stupid things, and none of them bother me. All the
> mistakes, and all the dopey things, and all the times I was
> embarrassed — they don't matter. What matters is that I can kind
> of look back and say: Pretty much any time I got the chance to do
> something cool I tried to grab for it — and that's where my
> solace comes from.
>
> — Randy Pausch, Carnegie Mellon University commencement speech (2008)
