# We spend our lives seeking love and avoiding rejection

Love is our deepest need and rejection our deepest fear, so much of human behavior is driven by pursuing one and escaping the other.

> Love is our greatest need. Rejection is our greatest fear. We spend our lives seeking love and avoiding rejection.
>
> — Stephen and Mara Klemich
