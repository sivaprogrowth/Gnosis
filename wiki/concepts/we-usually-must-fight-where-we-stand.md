# We usually must fight where we stand

We rarely get to choose when, where, or how we face our struggles. More often we have to deal with them right where we are.

> Sometimes we are blessed with being able to choose the time, and
> the arena, and the manner of our revolution, but more usually we
> must do battle where we are standing.
>
> — Audre Lorde, Sister Outsider: Essays and Speeches
