# Wealth is the power to choose

Wealth comes in several forms, each defined by freedom of choice: financial wealth over money, social wealth over company, time wealth over your day, and mental wealth over your attention.

> Wealth is the power to choose.
>
> Financial wealth is the power to choose how to spend money.
>
> Social wealth is the power to choose who to hang out with.
>
> Time wealth is the power to choose how to spend your day.
>
> Mental wealth is the power to choose how to spend your
> attention.
>
> — James Clear
