# Well-designed lives outlast disciplined ones

Relying on willpower is less durable than shaping your environment and daily schedule. Design your surroundings and routine so following through becomes more likely.

> The disciplined life looks impressive, but the well-designed
> life lasts longer. Before you try to find more willpower, design
> your environment and arrange your day so you're more likely to
> follow through.
>
> — James Clear
