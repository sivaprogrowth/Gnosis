# What advice would you give someone exactly like you?

Imagine meeting a person with your exact experience, resources, and problems. Consider what advice you would give them, as a way to see your own situation more clearly.

> If you met someone exactly like yourself ...
>
> - same experience- same resources- same problems
>
> ... what advice would you give them?
>
> — James Clear
