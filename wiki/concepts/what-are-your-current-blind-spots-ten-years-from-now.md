# What are your current blind spots ten years from now?

Just as you can see past mistaken beliefs from ten years ago, consider what your future self will see as your present blind spots and what you are ignoring now.

> Look back ten years and you can probably identify a few blind
> spots or mistaken beliefs you held at the time. Now, fast forward
> ten years from today... what are likely to be your current blind
> spots? What are you not spending enough time thinking about or
> perhaps even willfully ignoring?
>
> — James Clear
