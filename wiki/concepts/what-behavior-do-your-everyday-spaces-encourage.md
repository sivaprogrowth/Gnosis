# What behavior do your everyday spaces encourage?

Look at the rooms where you spend the most time, notice what behaviors they encourage, and rearrange them to make good habits easier.

> Look around the rooms where you spend the most time.
>
> What behavior does this space encourage? How can you set things
> up to make your good habits easier?
>
> — James Clear
