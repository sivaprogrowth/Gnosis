# What can I create today, and what can I receive?

Alternate between closing yourself off to focus on creating and opening yourself up to relax and receive from the world.

> Eliminate. Focus. Close yourself to the world and ask, "What can
> I create today?"
>
> Relax. Breathe. Open yourself to the world and ask, "What can I
> receive today?"
>
> — James Clear
