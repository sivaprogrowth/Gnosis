# What can I do now that I'll thank myself for later?

Ask which small and big things within your control you could act on now that your future self will be grateful for.

> "Are there small things and big things that are in my control that I could go do now that I will thank myself for doing later?"
>
> — Mark Pincus, The Knowledge Project
