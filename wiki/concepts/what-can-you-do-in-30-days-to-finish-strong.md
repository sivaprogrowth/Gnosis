# What can you do in 30 days to finish strong?

Most people coast through the last month of the year, yet one good month can make the whole year feel successful. The question asks what you can do in the next 30 days to build momentum and end on a high note.

> There is one month left in the year. Most people are ready to
> coast to the finish line, but one good month can make the whole
> year feel like a success.
>
> What can you do in the next 30 days to build momentum and finish
> the year on a high note?
>
> — James Clear
