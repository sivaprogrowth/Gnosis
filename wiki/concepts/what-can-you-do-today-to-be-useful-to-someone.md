# What can you do today to be useful to someone?

Many good things in life follow from being helpful and useful to others. The question asks what concrete help you can offer today.

> Many of the good things in life are downstream from being helpful
> and useful to others. What can you do today to be useful to
> someone else?
>
> — James Clear
