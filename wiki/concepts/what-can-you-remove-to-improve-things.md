# What can you remove to improve things?

A prompt to consider improving life by subtraction, identifying one thing to take away rather than add.

> What is one thing you can remove from your life that would
> improve it?
>
> — James Clear

> Before you ask, "What should I do today?"
>
> Ask yourself, "What should I remove today?"
>
> Create the space you need to succeed.
>
> — James Clear

> What can you eliminate this week?
>
> — James Clear
