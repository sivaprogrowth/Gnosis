# What can your best and worst leaders teach you?

Reflect on the best and the worst coach, teacher, or leader you've had and draw lessons from both.

> Think of the best coach, teacher, or leader you ever had. What
> can you learn from them?
>
> Think of the worst coach, teacher, or leader you ever had. What
> can you learn from them?
>
> — James Clear
