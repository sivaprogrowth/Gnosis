# What do you do today that pays off for years?

A prompt to identify the small, doable action today that would have the greatest impact.

> What is the biggest small thing I could do today?
>
> — James Clear

> What can you work on today that will continue working for you
> years from now?
>
> — James Clear

> What is one small thing I could do today that would make a
> meaningful impact on my future?
>
> — James Clear

> What action can you take today that will contribute to something
> you will be proud of in 10 years?
>
> — James Clear

> What small step can you take today that will put you on the path
> toward something wonderful?
>
> — James Clear

> What are you doing today that will benefit you in a decade?
>
> — James Clear
