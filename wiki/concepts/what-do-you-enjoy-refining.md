# What do you enjoy refining?

To find work you love, look at the areas you can't stop editing and optimizing, because that is where you have a long-term advantage.

> What do you enjoy refining? (It’s the areas you can’t help
> yourself from editing and optimizing where you have a long-term
> advantage.)
>
> — James Clear
