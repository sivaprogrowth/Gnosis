# What do you know you should do right now?

Usually the hard part isn't knowing what to do but having the resolve to do it. Name one thing you already know you should do and simply need to step up and do.

> It's remarkable how uncomplicated it is most of the time. The
> hard part is not knowing what to do, but having the resolve to do
> it. What is one thing you know you should do right now and you
> just need to step up and do it?
>
> — James Clear
