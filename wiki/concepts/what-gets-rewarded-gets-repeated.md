# What gets rewarded gets repeated

Behavior that is rewarded tends to recur and behavior that is punished tends to be avoided. So don't reward behavior you don't want repeated.

> The Cardinal Rule of Behavior Change:
>
> What gets rewarded, gets repeated. What gets punished, gets
> avoided.
>
> Don't reward behavior you don't want to see repeated.
>
> — James Clear

> What is rewarded gets repeated. What is punished is avoided.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
