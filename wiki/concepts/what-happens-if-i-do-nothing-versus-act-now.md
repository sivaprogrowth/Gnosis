# What happens if I do nothing versus act now?

Comparing the outcomes of inaction and immediate action helps you decide whether to let something go or get moving.

> If I do nothing, what will happen? If I act right now, what will
> happen?
>
> Sometimes you need to let go. Sometimes you need to get going.
>
> — James Clear
