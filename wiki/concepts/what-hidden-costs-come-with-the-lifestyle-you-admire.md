# What hidden costs come with the lifestyle you admire?

Wanting someone's results is easy; consider the costs and tradeoffs of their lifestyle that you may be overlooking.

> Think about someone who has the results you want or a lifestyle
> you admire. It's easy to want the results. What are the costs and
> tradeoffs of their lifestyle that you may be overlooking?

> — James Clear
