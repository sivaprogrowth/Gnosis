# What if you did more of what you're great at?

Asks how your daily schedule would change if you shifted a bit more time toward your strengths and away from your weaknesses.

> How would my daily schedule change if I did a little more of what
> I'm great at and a little less of what I'm not great at?
>
> — James Clear
