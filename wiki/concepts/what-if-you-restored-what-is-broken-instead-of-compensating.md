# What if you restored what is broken instead of compensating?

Asks the reader to consider fixing the troubled parts of their life rather than avoiding them and overcompensating in other areas.

> Instead of running from parts of your life and compensating
> elsewhere, what if you began to restore what is broken?
>
> — James Clear
