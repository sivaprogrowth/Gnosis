# What is an inspiring way to live this season?

Rather than chasing the vague goal of a meaningful life, narrow your focus to how you could live inspiringly in your current season of life.

> Forget about living a meaningful life. Narrow your focus. What is an inspiring way to live in this season of life?
>
> — James Clear
