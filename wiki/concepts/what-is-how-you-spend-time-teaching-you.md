# What is how you spend time teaching you?

You are always learning something, even when you don't mean to. Look at how you are spending your time this week and ask what it is teaching you.

> You're always learning, even when you're not intending to learn. Based on how you are spending your time this week, what are you learning?
>
> — James Clear
