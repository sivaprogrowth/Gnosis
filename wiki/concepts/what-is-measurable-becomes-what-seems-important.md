# What is measurable becomes what seems important

Numbers grab our attention and we naturally try to improve them, so measurable things start to feel important simply because they are easy to see. Asking what an inspiring way to live is can be a better question than how to get more.

> What is measurable becomes what is important. Not because it matters more, but because it is easier to see.
>
> Sometimes a new perspective is helpful. Rather than asking, "How can I get more?" Try asking, "What is an inspiring way to live?"
>
> — James Clear
