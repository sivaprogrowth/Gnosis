# What is my highest leverage action right now?

A prompt to look for the most valuable action available right now rather than in some distant future.

> What is the biggest opportunity I could pursue in the next 5
> minutes?
>
> — James Clear

> What is the highest leverage action I can execute on right now?
>
> — James Clear
