# What is the cost of your current pace?

Whether you are moving too slowly or overextending yourself, your pace has tradeoffs. Consider what your current speed is costing you.

> Sometimes we drag our feet. Sometimes we overextend ourselves.
> Either way, there are tradeoffs. What is the cost of your current
> pace?
>
> — James Clear
