# What is the most likely cause of failure?

Identify the most probable way things will fail. Then plan how to prevent it beforehand and how to recover if it happens anyway.

> What is the most likely cause of failure?
>
> Before it happens, how can I prevent it? If it happens, how can I
> recover?
>
> — James Clear
