# What is worth trying even if it fails?

Consider pursuing something whose value lies in what you learn along the way, even if the outcome doesn't work out.

> Let's say it doesn't work out for some reason. What is something
> that is still worth trying because of what you will learn along
> the way?
>
> — James Clear
