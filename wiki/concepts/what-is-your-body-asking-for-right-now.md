# What is your body asking for right now?

Scan your body for tension, breathe deeply, and slow down. Then ask what your body wants to do and what it needs.

> Close your eyes and scan your body. Notice where you are holding
> tension or stiffness. Breathe deeply and slow down. What is your
> body asking to do and what does it need right now?
>
> — James Clear
