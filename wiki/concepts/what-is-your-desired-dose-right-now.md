# What is your desired dose right now?

Almost everything has a useful amount beyond which it harms you, like sun exposure. Ask whether you need a little more or less of work, relationships, hobbies, exercise, or food.

> Nearly everything in life has a useful zone, a desired dose. Ten
> minutes in the sun can energize you. Ten hours in the sun can
> burn you. This concept applies to many things: work,
> relationships, hobbies, exercise, food.
>
> What is your desired dose? What do you need right now? A little
> bit more or a little bit less?
>
> — James Clear
