# What item in your room no longer serves you?

Pick a room where you spend a lot of time and find one item you no longer use or benefit from. Keep what serves or inspires you and remove what distracts you.

> Pick one room where you spend a lot of time. What is one item in this room that you no longer use or do not benefit from?
>
> Keep what serves you or inspires you. Remove what distracts you.
>
> — James Clear
