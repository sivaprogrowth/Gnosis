# What lesson did a past failure teach you?

Recall something you struggled with in the past year and step back to identify one lesson it taught you.

> Think of something you struggled with in the last year. If you
> step back and zoom out, what is one lesson you have learned from
> the experience?
>
> — James Clear

> Think of a time you failed or struggled greatly.
>
> If you were to view that experience in a more useful way, what story would you tell yourself? What did it teach you? How did it prepare you for what came next?
>
> Turn your failures into lessons.
>
> — James Clear
