# What lifestyle do you love 80% of the time?

No lifestyle is perfect all the time, and occasional treats like beach days wouldn't be ideal daily. Identify the lifestyle that feels right most of the time and cultivate it.

> What lifestyle do you love 80% of the time?
>
> No lifestyle is perfect 100% of the time. I love having a few
> days each year to relax on the beach—but I wouldn't want to do
> that every day, or even most days.
>
> What lifestyle feels ideal for you most of the time? Cultivate
> that.
>
> — James Clear
