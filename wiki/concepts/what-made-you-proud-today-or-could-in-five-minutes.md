# What made you proud today, or could in five minutes?

Reflect on something you are proud of today. If nothing comes to mind, consider how to use the next five minutes to create something to be proud of.

> What is something that made you feel proud today? If nothing
> comes to mind, how can you spend the next 5 minutes in a way that
> will make you feel proud?
>
> — James Clear
