# What meaningful thing can you accomplish before year's end?

A prompt to use the final week of the year to accomplish something meaningful rather than coasting, and to end on a high note.

> What is something meaningful you can accomplish in the final week
> of the year?
>
> The year isn't over yet. End on a high note.
>
> — James Clear
