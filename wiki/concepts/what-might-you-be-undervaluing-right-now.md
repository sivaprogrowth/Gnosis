# What might you be undervaluing right now?

We tend to undervalue good things like health, wealth, and kindness until we recently experience their absence, so it's worth asking what we take for granted now.

> We undervalue health if we have not recently experienced illness.
>
> We undervalue wealth if we have not recently experienced poverty.
>
> We undervalue kindness if we have not recently experienced
> cruelty.
>
> What might you be undervaluing right now?
>
> — James Clear
