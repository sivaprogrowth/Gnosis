# What minor thing are you over-emphasizing right now?

Many problems come from the mind inflating small issues into big ones, so it helps to ask what you are blowing out of proportion.

> Half your problems are just your mind making minor things seem
> like major things.
>
> What are you over-emphasizing right now?
>
> — James Clear
