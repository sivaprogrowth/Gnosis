# What needs to be said that isn't being said?

Asking what is going unsaid can prevent future problems. Having the courage to raise issues now avoids headaches later.

> What is not being said right now that needs to be said? What
> problems could we avoid in the future, if only we had the courage
> to talk about them now?
>
> — James Clear
