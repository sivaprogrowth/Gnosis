# What new or different thing can you do this week?

A challenge to break your routine by choosing one thing to do this week that differs from a normal week.

> Challenge yourself to do something out of your normal routine.
> What is one thing you can do this week that is different than
> what you do on a normal week?
>
> — James Clear

> Challenge yourself to have one new experience this week. When everything around you is familiar, the days turn stale and slip by unnoticed. A surprising experience breathes life into tired days and makes the mind fresh again.
>
> What is one new experience you can have this week?
>
> — James Clear
