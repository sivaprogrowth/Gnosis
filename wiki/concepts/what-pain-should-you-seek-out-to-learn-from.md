# What pain should you seek out to learn from?

Some lessons only come through hardship. The question asks what difficult experience you should deliberately pursue over the next year to grow.

> Sometimes you have to pass through the fire. What fire or pain
> should you seek out in your life over the next year so you can
> learn its lessons?
>
> — A reader
