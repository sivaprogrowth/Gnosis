# What season are you in and what does it require?

Life moves through seasons, and habits that work in one may not fit the next. Ask what season you are in and what habits it calls for.

> Life is a series of seasons, and what works in one season may
> not work in the next.
>
> What season are you in right now? What habits does that season
> require?
>
> — James Clear

> Think about something important to you. Maybe it's a project or a
> job. Perhaps a hobby or even a relationship. What season are you
> in right now and what does that season require?
>
> — James Clear
