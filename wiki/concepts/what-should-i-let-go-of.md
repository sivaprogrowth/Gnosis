# What should I let go of?

A prompt to identify what you are holding on to that you need to release this year.

> What am I holding on to that I need to let go of this year?
>
> — James Clear

> What am I holding on to that I need to let go of?
>
> — James Clear

> What do I need to let go of this year to create space for what I
> really want?
>
> — James Clear

> It is common to think about what you would like to accomplish. It
> is less common to think about what must be discarded to make
> space for your new endeavors. The time must come from somewhere.
> What do you need to give up to make space for what you would like
> to achieve?
>
> — James Clear
