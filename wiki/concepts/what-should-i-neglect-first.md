# What should I neglect first?

Instead of asking what to do first, ask what to neglect first. Trimming and cutting things out makes space for better performance.

> Instead of asking yourself, "What should I do first?"
>
> Try asking, "What should I neglect first?"
>
> Trim, edit, cull. Make space for better performance.
>
> — James Clear
