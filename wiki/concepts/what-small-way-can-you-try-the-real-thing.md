# What small way can you try the real thing?

Doing the real thing teaches more than dreaming about the perfect version, so find a small, short experiment to try it now.

> Spend less time dreaming and more time experimenting.
>
> Doing the real thing will usually teach you more than dreaming about the perfect thing. What is one small way you can try out the real thing (even if only for five minutes or at a smaller scale)?
>
> — James Clear
