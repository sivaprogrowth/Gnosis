# What unit should you measure your days by?

Instead of measuring days only by money earned or tasks done, consider other measures like laughter, time outside, or peace of mind, and pick the one that serves you now.

> How should you measure your days? It's easy to measure your days
> in dollars earned or tasks completed, but there are other units
> of measurement to consider:
>
> * What would you do today if you wanted to maximize laughter?
> * What would you do today if you wanted to maximize time outside?
> * What would you do today if you wanted to maximize peace of
> mind?
>
> What unit of measurement serves you best right now?
>
> — James Clear
