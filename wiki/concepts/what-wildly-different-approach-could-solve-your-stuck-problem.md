# What wildly different approach could solve your stuck problem?

Pick something you've been trying to make progress on and consider a completely different line of attack or approach to it.

> Think about something you have been trying to make progress on
> recently. What is a completely different line of attack for
> solving it or a wildly different approach you can explore?
>
> — James Clear
