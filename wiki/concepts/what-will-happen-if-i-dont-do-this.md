# What will happen if I don't do this?

Before starting work, ask what the consequence of skipping a task would be. Many tasks can be eliminated this way.

> What will happen if I don't do this?
>
> It's remarkable how many things can be eliminated from your to-do
> list.
>
> — James Clear
