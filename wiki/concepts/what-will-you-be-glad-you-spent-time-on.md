# What will you be glad you spent time on?

Imagine yourself six months ahead and ask what you will wish you had invested time in today. It helps prioritize present time.

> Six months from now, what you will you wish you had spent time on
> today?
>
> — James Clear

> At this time next year, what will you be thankful you spent time
> on during the next 12 months?
>
> — James Clear
