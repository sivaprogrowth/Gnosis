# What will you focus on enough to ignore everything else?

Often the real talent is obsession with something. The reader is asked what they would focus on so fully that they'd happily ignore nearly everything else.

> In many cases, the real talent is being obsessed with the thing.
> What are you willing to focus on so wholeheartedly that you are
> happy to ignore nearly everything else?
>
> — James Clear
