# What will you improve over the next decade?

Pick an area to improve over ten years and ask how you are working toward it today. Think long-term but act short-term.

> What is an area you'd like to improve over the next decade?
>
> How are you working toward that outcome today?
>
> Think long-term. Act short-term.
>
> — James Clear
