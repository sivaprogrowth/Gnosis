# What would excite you about the worst-case scenario?

Imagine your worst-case scenario actually happens. Beyond what you would dislike, ask which parts of it you might actually be excited about.

> Let's say the worst-case scenario actually occurs. There are
> obviously things you won't like about it. But what aspects of
> that scenario are you excited about?
>
> — James Clear
