# What would make the group excited?

Rather than seeking only personal happiness, ask what would excite the group, since many of life's best moments come from shared fun and energy.

> Instead of asking, "What would make me feel happy?" try asking,
> "What would make the group excited?"
>
> Many of the best moments in life happen when the group is having
> fun. You feed off one another's energy. What can you do to laugh
> or gasp or scream or smile with others?
>
> — James Clear
