# What would you do with half or double the time?

Imagine finishing a project in half the time, then imagine having twice the time. Both constraints reveal different ways to approach the work.

> Faster. If I had to go from start to finish in half the time,
> what would I do?
>
> Slower. If I could afford to spend double the time on it, what
> would I do?
>
> — James Clear
