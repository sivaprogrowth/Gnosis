# What you consume shapes your future thoughts

What you take in each day becomes the raw material from which your later thinking grows. Choosing your inputs is choosing your future thoughts.

> The information you consume each day is the soil from which your
> future thoughts are grown.
>
> — James Clear

> The quality of your thoughts is determined by the quality of
> your reading. Spend more time thinking about the inputs.
>
> — James Clear
