# What you do on bad days matters most

Effort is easy when life is smooth, but your choices in rough patches decide your relationships, health, and emotional control. Consistency through hard days is what sets you apart.

> What you do on your bad days matters more than what you do on
> your good days.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
