# What you envy reveals what you value

You can understand other people by seeing what they reward. You can understand yourself by noticing what you envy.

> To understand others, watch what they reward.
>
> To understand yourself, watch what you envy.
>
> — James Clear

> Who do you secretly envy—and what does that reveal about what you
> truly value?
>
> — James Clear
