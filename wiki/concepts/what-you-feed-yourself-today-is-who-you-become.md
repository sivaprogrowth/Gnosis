# What you feed yourself today is who you become

Your body, mind, and soul adapt to what you eat, consume, and love. Your daily inputs shape who you become.

> Your body adapts to what you eat.
>
> Your mind adapts to what you consume.
>
> Your soul adapts to what you love.
>
> What you feed yourself today is who you become tomorrow.
>
> — James Clear
