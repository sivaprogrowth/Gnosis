# What you have now was once only hoped for

Wanting what you lack can ruin your enjoyment of what you have. Remember that your current possessions were once things you merely wished for.

> Do not spoil what you have by desiring what you have not;
> remember that what you now have was once among the things you
> only hoped for.
>
> — Epicurus, Letters, Principal Doctrines, and Vatican Sayings
