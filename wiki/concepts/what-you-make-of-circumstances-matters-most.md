# What you make of circumstances matters most

Where you were born and how you lived are unimportant; what counts is what you made of the places you have been.

> Where I was born and where and how I have lived is unimportant.
> It is what I have done with where I have been that should be of
> interest.
>
> — Georgia O'Keeffe, Autobiography
