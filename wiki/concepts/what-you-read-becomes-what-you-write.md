# What you read becomes what you write

Your inputs shape your outputs. Be selective about what you read and learn, because that determines what you will write and know.

> "He is careful of what he reads, for that is what he will write.
> He is careful of what he learns, for that is what he will know."
>
> — Annie Dillard
