# What you take pride in is where you're biased

The things you are proud of are the things you are most likely to be biased about. Holding tightly to your current identity makes growing beyond it harder.

> The things you take pride in are the same things you are likely
> to be biased about.
>
> How might the things you take pride in be misleading you?
>
> The tighter you cling to your current identity, the harder it
> becomes to grow beyond it.
>
> — James Clear
