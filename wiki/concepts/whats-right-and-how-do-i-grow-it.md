# What's right and how do I grow it?

Instead of asking what's wrong and how to fix it, ask what's working and how to grow it. This reframe shifts focus to strengths.

> Instead of thinking, "What's wrong and how do I fix it?"
>
> Try exploring, "What's right and how do I grow it?"
>
> — Joe Hudson
