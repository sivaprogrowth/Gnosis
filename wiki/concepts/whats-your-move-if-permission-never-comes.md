# What's your move if permission never comes?

Instead of waiting on external factors or someone's approval, consider what you would do if they never arrived, and act on that.

> Don't wait on external factors. If the thing you're waiting for never arrives or if the permission you're hoping for is never given, what's your move?
>
> — James Clear
