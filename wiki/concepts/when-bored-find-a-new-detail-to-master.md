# When bored, find a new detail to master

Boredom tempts people to jump to new programs and never get results. Instead, stay with the same habit and find a new detail within it to master, like writing better opening sentences.

> The key is once you get bored, you stick with the
> same habit, but find a new detail to master or get interested in.
>
> — James Clear
