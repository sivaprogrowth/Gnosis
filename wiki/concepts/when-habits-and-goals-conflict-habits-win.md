# When habits and goals conflict, habits win

When your daily habits don't line up with your goals, the habits determine the outcome. Results follow what you repeatedly do, not what you aim for.

> Whenever there is a gap between your habits and your goals, your habits will always win.
>
> — James Clear

> When someone's habits don't match their ambitions, trust the
> habits.
>
> The corollary: Do your habits match your ambitions?
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
