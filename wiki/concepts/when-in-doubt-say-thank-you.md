# When in doubt, say thank you

Expressing gratitude has no downside, so when unsure, just thank people. Nobody needs to worry about showing too much gratitude.

> When in doubt, just say thank you. There is no downside. Are you
> honestly worried about showing too much gratitude to the people
> in your life?
>
> — James Clear
