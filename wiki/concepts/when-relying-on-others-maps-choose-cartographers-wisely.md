# When relying on others' maps, choose cartographers wisely

Since we often depend on maps from experts and teachers, we should pick mapmakers who are rigorous, transparent, and open to revision.

> In many areas of life, we are offered maps by other people. We
> are reliant on the maps provided by experts, pundits, and
> teachers. In these cases, the best we can do is to choose our
> mapmakers wisely, to seek out those who are rigorous,
> transparent, and open to revision.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Updated Great Mental Models v1: General Thinking Tools
