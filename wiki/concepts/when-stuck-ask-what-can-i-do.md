# When stuck, ask: What can I do?

When you feel stuck or dwell on what you can't do, regain control by focusing on what is within your power to do.

> When you find yourself feeling stuck or beginning to dwell on what you can't do, regain control with this simple question:
>
> What can I do?
>
> — James Clear
