# When stuck, go smaller and master stage one

When you struggle with an exercise, concept, or habit, scale it down to an easier version. Master that first stage before advancing.

> Go smaller.
>
> Can't learn an exercise? Reduce the range of motion.
>
> Struggling to grasp a new concept? Break it down.
>
> Failing to stick with a habit? Make it easy.
>
> Master stage one, then advance.
>
> — James Clear
