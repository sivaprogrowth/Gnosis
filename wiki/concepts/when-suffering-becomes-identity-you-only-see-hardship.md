# When suffering becomes identity, you only see hardship

Avoid spending too much time with people who live as martyrs, because making suffering your identity narrows your view to only the hard parts of life.

> Don't spend too much time around someone whose primary way of engaging with life is as a martyr. When suffering becomes your identity, you only see the hard parts of life.
>
> — James Clear
