# When the gap is large, focus on the next step

A big goal is useful for direction, but fixating on it gets in the way of progress. Concentrate on the next small action instead.

> When the gap between where you are and where you want to be is large, focus on the next step. One sentence in the book. One workout. One email. While the ultimate goal helps you navigate, focusing on it keeps you from achieving it.
>
