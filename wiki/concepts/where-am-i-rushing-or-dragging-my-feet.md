# Where am I rushing or dragging my feet?

A two-part prompt to find where you are moving too fast when you should be deliberate, and too slow when you should move fast.

> Where am I rushing right now when I should be moving
> deliberately?
>
> Where am I dragging my feet right now when I should be moving
> fast?
>
> — James Clear
