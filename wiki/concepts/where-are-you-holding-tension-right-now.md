# Where are you holding tension right now?

Pause, close your eyes, and scan your body to notice where you're carrying tension, then breathe deeply and relax.

> Slow down for a minute. Close your eyes. Scan your body.
>
> Where are you holding tension right now?
>
> Take a deep breath. Relax.
>
> — James Clear
