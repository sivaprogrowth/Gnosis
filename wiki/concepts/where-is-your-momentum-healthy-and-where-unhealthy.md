# Where is your momentum healthy and where unhealthy?

Momentum can either push you forward or lock you into old choices and habits, so it's worth examining which kind you currently have in each area.

> Momentum is a double-edged sword. It can propel you to new
> heights or keep you locked into previous choices and old habits.
>
> Where do I have healthy momentum right now? Where do I have
> unhealthy momentum?
>
> — James Clear
