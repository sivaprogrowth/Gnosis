# Where will your current life lead in 20 years?

Consider what your life will look like in 20 years if you continue living as you do now; sometimes patience is needed, sometimes action.

> If you keep living the way you are, what will your life look like
> in 20 years?
>
> Sometimes we need patience. Sometimes we need action.
>
> — James Clear
