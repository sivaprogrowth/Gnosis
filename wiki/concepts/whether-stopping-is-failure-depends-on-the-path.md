# Whether stopping is failure depends on the path

On a good path, quitting is the failure and persisting turns setbacks into lessons. On a bad path, persisting is the failure and stopping is the lesson.

> When you're on a good path: It's only a failure if you stop. It's a lesson if you keep going.
>
> When you're on a bad path: It's only a failure if you keep going. It's a lesson if you stop.
>
> — James Clear
