# Which activities should you do more and less?

A prompt to reflect on which activities to increase and which to cut over the next ten years.

> What should you do more of this decade? What should you do less
> of?
>
> — James Clear

> Which activities, if doubled, would make your life meaningfully
> better?
>
> Which activities, if halved, would make your life meaningfully
> better?
>
> — James Clear
