# Which commitment should you finally let go?

Identify a commitment you keep only out of inertia that you would immediately benefit from eliminating if you had the courage.

> What is one commitment you are continuing to do out of inertia,
> but—if you had the courage to eliminate it—you would immediately
> benefit from discarding it?
>
> — James Clear

> Today is a good day to reclaim some of your time and energy. What
> project, commitment, or responsibility have you known for a while
> you should let go of (or wrap up), but still haven't?
>
> — James Clear
