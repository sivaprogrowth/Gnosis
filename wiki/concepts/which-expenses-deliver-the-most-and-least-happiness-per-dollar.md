# Which expenses deliver the most and least happiness per dollar?

Reflect on which spending gives you the least happiness for its cost and which gives you the most. This helps align spending with what actually makes you happy.

> Which expense in your life delivers the least happiness per
> dollar and which delivers the most happiness per dollar?
>
> — James Clear, The Simple Path to Wealth
