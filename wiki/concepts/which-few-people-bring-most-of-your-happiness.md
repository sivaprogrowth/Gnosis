# Which few people bring most of your happiness?

Applying the Pareto Principle to relationships, identify the few people who provide most of your happiness and schedule time with one of them today.

> Apply the Pareto Principle to your relationships. Who are the few
> people that deliver the majority of happiness in your life? Can
> you schedule time with one of them today?
>
> — James Clear
