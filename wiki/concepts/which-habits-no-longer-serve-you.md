# Which habits no longer serve you?

A prompt to identify behaviors you keep doing out of habit even though they no longer help you.

> What am I continuing to do out of habit, but is no longer serving
> me?
>
> — James Clear

> Does this habit still serve me or am I blindly following an old
> routine?
>
> — James Clear

> Life is always progressing to the next stage. As you enter a new
> season, habits that previously served you well may need to be
> refreshed or adjusted. Which one of your current habits have you
> outgrown and is in need of a change?
>
> — James Clear
