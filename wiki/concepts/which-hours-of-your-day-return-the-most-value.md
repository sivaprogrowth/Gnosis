# Which hours of your day return the most value?

Clear finds his early morning hours valuable and his late night hours wasted, so trading one for the other would be worthwhile. He asks readers to identify their highest and lowest value hours.

> Which hours of your day tend to return the most value and which
> ones tend to return the least?
>
> — James Clear
