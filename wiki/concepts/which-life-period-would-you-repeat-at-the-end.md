# Which life period would you repeat at the end?

Imagine choosing one day or phase of your life to relive at the end of it. Your answer may reveal how you should spend your time now.

> Imagine you are at the end of your life and you are granted the
> ability to repeat one day. Which period of your life do you
> choose to repeat? Which phase of life would you want to go back
> to? Does that tell you anything about how you should be spending
> your time today?
>
> — James Clear
