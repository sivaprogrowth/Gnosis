# Which parts of your day align with your goals?

Improvement begins with awareness, so notice which parts of your day feel most and least aligned with where you want to go.

> Improvement often starts with awareness.
>
> Which part of your day currently feels most aligned with where
> you want to go? Which part of your day currently feels least
> aligned?
>
> — James Clear
