# Which price of poor, mediocre, or exceptional work will you pay?

Every level of work quality carries its own cost. The question asks which of those costs you are willing to accept.

> What's the cost of doing a poor job? What's the cost of doing a
> mediocre job? What's the cost of doing an exceptional job?
>
> Which price are you willing to pay?
>
> — James Clear
