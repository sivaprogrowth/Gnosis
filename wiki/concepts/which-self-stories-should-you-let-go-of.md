# Which self-stories should you let go of?

Reflect on which parts of your personal narrative no longer help you and which stories you cling to that you need to release.

> What parts of my story no longer serve me? What stories am I
> attached to that I need to let go of?
>
> — James Clear

> What story about yourself would you have to retire in order to
> grow into the life you want?
>
> — James Clear
