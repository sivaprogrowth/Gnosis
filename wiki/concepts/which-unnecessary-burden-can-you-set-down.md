# Which unnecessary burden can you set down?

Consider which responsibilities, relationships, or projects weigh on you, and whether you can let one go now.

> Which responsibilities, relationships, or projects have been
> weighing heavily on me and can I give myself permission to let
> one of them go right now?
>
> — James Clear

> What weight are you carrying unnecessarily?
>
> — James Clear
