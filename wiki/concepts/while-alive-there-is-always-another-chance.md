# While alive, there is always another chance

Life is a continual cycle of losing and finding, leaving and returning. As long as we live, another chance remains available.

> I know now, after fifty years, that the finding/losing,
> forgetting/remembering, leaving/returning, never stops. The whole
> of life is about another chance, and while we are alive, till the
> very end, there is always another chance.
>
> — Jeanette Winterson, Why Be Happy When You Could Be Normal?
