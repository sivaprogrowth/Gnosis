# Who or what gets your best hours?

Most people have a daily window of peak energy and enthusiasm. The question asks how you spend that time and who or what receives it.

> There is usually a block of time each day when you are at your
> best—peak energy, peak enthusiasm. Let's say it's 2 hours per
> day. How do you spend those 2 hours? Who or what gets your best
> time?
>
> — James Clear
