# Whoever has the most fun wins

Being the best at something is measured by enjoyment rather than achievement. Enjoying the activity is what matters most.

> The best climber in the world is the one who's having the most
> fun.
>
> — Alex Lowe, Outside Magazine

> "Whoever has the most fun, wins."
>
> — James Clear

> Two of my rules for life:
>
> 1. The person who has the most fun wins.
>
> 2. The climb is the fun part.
>
> — James Clear
