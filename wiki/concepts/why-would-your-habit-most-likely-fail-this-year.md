# Why would your habit most likely fail this year?

Imagine a year from now that the habit you hoped to build didn't stick, and identify the most likely reason, so you can anticipate and prevent it.

> It's one year from now. December 2023. The habit you were hoping
> to build during the year didn't stick. What is the most likely
> reason it failed?
>
> — James Clear
