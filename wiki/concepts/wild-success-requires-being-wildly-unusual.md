# Wild success requires being wildly unusual

Extraordinary success means your life won't look like those around you. You can learn from outliers but not copy them; your path to success must fit who you are.

> If you're going to be wildly successful, you have to be wildly unusual.
>
> Your life won't resemble the lives of the people around you. You can learn a lot from Outliers, but you can't copy them.
>
> The style of your success has to fit who you are.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
