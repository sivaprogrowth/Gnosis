# Will to prepare matters more than will to win

Everyone wants to win, so wanting it is not what sets people apart. What matters is being willing to do the preparation that winning requires.

> “The key is not the will to win. Everyone has that. It is the will to prepare to win that is important.”
>
> — Bobby Knight

> "The will to win is not nearly as important as the will to prepare to win. Everyone wants to win, but not everyone wants to prepare to win."
>
> — Bob Knight
