# Willingness matters more than capability

Many people are able to do great things, but fewer are willing to. What separates people is what they choose to do.

> “It’s not what you are capable of; it’s what you are willing to do. Plenty of people are capable. Fewer people are willing.”
>
> — Mike Tomlin
