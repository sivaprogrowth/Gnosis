# Willingness to figure it out is the key qualification

High agency means treating everything as your job; Schwab insisted on learning tasks he fumbled so they never repeated.

> Everything is your job. Les bought his first tire
> shop having never fixed a flat in his life. On day one, a
> customer needs tires mounted. Les fumbles with hand tools on the
> cold concrete, making a complete mess until his employee arrives.
> He insisted on being taught, so the situation never repeated.
> Sometimes, the only qualification you need is the willingness to
> figure it out.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project [Outliers]
