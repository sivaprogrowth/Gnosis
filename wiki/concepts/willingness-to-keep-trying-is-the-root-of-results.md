# Willingness to keep trying is the root of results

Results come from skill, skill from practice, and practice from attitude. The willingness to try and keep trying is what builds ability.

> Everyone wants better results.
>
> Skill precedes results.
>
> Practice precedes skill.
>
> Attitude precedes practice.
>
> It's the willingness to try (and keep trying) that leads to ability.
>
> — James Clear
