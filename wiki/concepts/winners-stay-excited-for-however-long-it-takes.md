# Winners stay excited for however long it takes

Most people sustain enthusiasm for months, a few for years, but winners keep their excitement for decades or as long as it takes to succeed.

> Almost everybody can stay excited for 2 or 3 months. A few
> people can stay excited for 2 or 3 years. But a winner will stay
> excited for 30 years or however long it takes to win.
>
> — Art Williams, Just Do It
