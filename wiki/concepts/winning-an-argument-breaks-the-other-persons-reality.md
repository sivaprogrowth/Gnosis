# Winning an argument breaks the other person's reality

Defeating someone in an argument tears down their view of reality, which is painful, so be kind even when you are right.

> "Always remember that to argue, and win, is to break down the
> reality of the person you are arguing against. It is painful to
> lose your reality, so be kind, even if you are right."
>
> — Haruki Murakami, Colourless Tsukuru Tazaki (Japanese version)
