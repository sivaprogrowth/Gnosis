# Wisdom is choosing right without all the information

Knowledge lets you make the right choice when you have complete information. Wisdom lets you make the right choice when information is incomplete.

> "Knowledge is making the right choice with all the information.
>
> Wisdom is making the right choice without all the information."
>
> — James Clear
