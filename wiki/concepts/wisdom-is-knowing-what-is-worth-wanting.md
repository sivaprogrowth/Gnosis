# Wisdom is knowing what is worth wanting

Intelligence helps you get what you want, but wisdom is knowing which things are actually worth wanting.

> Intelligent people know how to get what they want. Wise people
> know what's worth wanting.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Life comes down to two things: 1. Knowing how to get what you want 2. Knowing what’s worth wanting
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> If intelligence is getting what you want, wisdom is wanting what's worth getting in the first place.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
