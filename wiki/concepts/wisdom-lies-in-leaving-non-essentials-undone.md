# Wisdom lies in leaving non-essentials undone

Knowing what not to do is as valuable as getting things done. A wise life comes from cutting out what is not essential.

> Besides the noble art of getting things done, there is the noble
> art of leaving things undone. The wisdom of life consists in the
> elimination of non-essentials.
>
> — Lin Yutang, The Importance of Living
