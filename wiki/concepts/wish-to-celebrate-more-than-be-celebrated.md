# Wish to celebrate more than be celebrated

Put more weight on finding joy in celebrating things and others than on seeking recognition and status for yourself.

> One should wish to celebrate more than one wishes to be
> celebrated.
>
> — Lucille Clifton, 1992 interview for Poets & Writers Magazine
