# With abundant information, attention becomes the edge

When information is everywhere and easy to get, having it is no longer the edge. The edge comes from choosing what to pay attention to.

> In a world where information is abundant and easy to access, the
> real advantage is knowing where to focus.
>
> — James Clear

> When accessing all information is common, paying attention to
> important information is rare.
>
> — James Clear
