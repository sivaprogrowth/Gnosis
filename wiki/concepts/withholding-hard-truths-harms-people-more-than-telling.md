# Withholding hard truths harms people more than telling

We often see what holds colleagues back but avoid telling them. Telling may hurt, but staying silent does lasting harm.

> “The truth may sting, but silence can leave a scar.
>
> We see the barriers holding back the people we work with, just as
> they see ours. But telling them is hard.
>
> Telling the truth might hurt them, but not telling harms them.”
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
