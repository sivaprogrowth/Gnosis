# Without a known destination, no wind is favorable

If you do not know what you are aiming for, no circumstance can help you. Clarity of purpose is what makes opportunities useful.

> "If you do not know which port you are seeking, no wind is
> favorable."
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius (Epistle 71)
