# Without a personal mission you won't push hard

People often don't push as hard when there's no personal mission behind a goal; that mission matters most for large goals.

> Sometimes you don’t push as hard because there’s no personal
> mission there. There’s no reason to push.
>
> — Rob Fraser, The Knowledge Project podcast
