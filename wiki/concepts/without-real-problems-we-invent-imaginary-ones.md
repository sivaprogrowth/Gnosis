# Without real problems we invent imaginary ones

When people lack genuine problems or meaningful work, they fill the gap by creating imaginary problems and perfecting unimportant things.

> When we lack real problems, we create imaginary ones; when we
> lack meaningful work, we perfect the unimportant.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
