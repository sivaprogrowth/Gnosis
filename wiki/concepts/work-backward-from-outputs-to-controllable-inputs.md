# Work backward from outputs to controllable inputs

You can't directly manage outcomes like stock price, so trace the chain of inputs backward until you reach something you can control, such as reducing defects to lower costs, and focus effort there.

> “What are the inputs to a higher stock price? Okay, well, free
> cash flow and return on invested capital are inputs to a higher
> stock price. Let’s keep working backwards. What are the inputs to
> free cash flow? And you keep working backwards until you get to
> something that’s controllable.”
>
> — Jeff Bezos
