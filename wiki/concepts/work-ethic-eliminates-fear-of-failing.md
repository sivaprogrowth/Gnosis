# Work ethic eliminates fear of failing

If you put in the work, you know what you're capable of, so there is nothing left to fear.

> ethic eliminates fear. So you if you do the work, what are you
> fearing? You know what you’re capable of and what you’re not.”

> — Michael Jordan
