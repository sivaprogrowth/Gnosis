# Work for your own satisfaction, treat praise as bonus

Do work that satisfies you and see others' praise as extra. If you never worked for validation, you won't need it to feel satisfied when done.

> Do things for your own satisfaction. Consider praise from others
> to be a bonus. If you don't work for their validation in the
> first place, you won't need it to feel satisfied once it's done.
>
> — James Clear
