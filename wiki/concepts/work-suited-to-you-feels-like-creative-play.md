# Work suited to you feels like creative play

When you do what you are naturally suited for, work starts to feel like play. That playfulness is what drives creativity.

> When we engage in what we are naturally suited to do, our work
> takes on the quality of play and it is play that stimulates
> creativity.
>
> — Linda Naiman, Creativity and the Meaning of Work
