# Work toward your ideal lifestyle, not retirement

Rather than deferring the life you want until retirement, aim directly at your ideal lifestyle. There is often a way to reach it in years rather than decades.

> Instead of working toward retirement, work toward your ideal
> lifestyle. There is usually a path to get there in a few years
> instead of a few decades.
>
> — James Clear
