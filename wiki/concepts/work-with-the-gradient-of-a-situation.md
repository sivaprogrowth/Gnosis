# Work with the gradient of a situation

Like water flowing downhill, most situations have a natural direction. Positioning yourself to benefit from external forces gets you more results from the same effort than fighting the flow.

> Most situations in life have a tendency—a direction in which
> things want to flow. You can choose to go against the flow (just
> as you can choose to carry water uphill), but your results tend
> to be better when you find a way to work with the gradient of the
> situation.
>
> Position yourself to benefit from the external forces at hand and
> you will get more from the same unit of effort. Energy is
> conserved and results are multiplied.
>
> — James Clear
