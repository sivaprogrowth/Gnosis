# Work without haste, yet without rest

Live and work with calm persistence, like a star orbiting the sun: steady and unhurried but always in motion.

> Without haste, yet without rest.
>
> — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Gedichte. Ausgabe letzter Hand (1827)
