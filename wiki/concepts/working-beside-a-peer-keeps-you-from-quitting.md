# Working beside a peer keeps you from quitting

Alone, a mental block invites distraction or giving up early. With a peer present you don't want to be the first to quit, so you push through and get far more done.

> If I’m with a peer and my mind stalls, I just sit there for a minute or two until the mental block passes. I don’t want to be the lazy one who stops working after a few minutes, and as a result, I get three times as much done as I do on my own.
>
> — James Clear
