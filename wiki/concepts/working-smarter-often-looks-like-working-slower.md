# Working smarter often looks like working slower

Deep thinking can look unproductive in the short term, but its thoroughness pays off in every later decision. Exceptional work comes from depth of thought on the right problems, not hours or fragmented attention.

> The most practical skill you can learn is working smarter. But
> here's what nobody tells you about working smarter: it often
> looks like you're working slower.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
