# Workout and most important task before noon

A simple formula for a good day is to finish a workout and your most important task before lunch, which leaves you feeling ahead of the day.

> If you want a simple formula for having a good day, then get a
> workout done and do your most important task before lunch. Knock
> out those two things by noon and you really feel like you're
> ahead of the day.
>
> — James Clear
