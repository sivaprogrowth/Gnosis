# Worry before committing, then stop worrying

Do your worrying well ahead of time, when you decide whether a goal is worth its risks. Once you commit, further worry only adds another hazard.

> "The time to worry is three months before a flight. Decide then
> whether or not the goal is worth the risks involved. If it is,
> stop worrying. To worry is to add another hazard."
>
> — Amelia Earhart, Last Flight
