# Worry keeps most people from enjoying the present

Like someone in a concert who frets about an unlocked car, most people cannot enjoy what is in front of them because of anxiety about something they cannot act on.

> Think of yourself in a concert hall listening to the strains of
> the sweetest music when you suddenly remember that you forgot to
> lock your car. You are anxious about the car, you cannot walk out
> of the hall and you cannot enjoy the music. There you have a
> perfect image of life as it is lived by most human beings.
>
> — Anthony de Mello, The Way to Love
