# Worrying about missing opportunities wastes the ones you have

Time spent fretting over opportunities you lack is time taken away from making the most of the opportunities you actually have.

> All the time you spend worrying about the opportunities you don't
> have comes at the expense of maximizing the opportunities you do
> have.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
