# Would today's actions repeated for five years help you?

A check on daily behavior: imagine continuing what you're about to do today for five years and ask whether it leads toward more or less of what you want.

> If I keep doing what I am about to do today for the next five
> years, will I end up with more of what I want or less of what I
> want?
>
> — James Clear
