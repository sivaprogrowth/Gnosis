# Would you be happy if no one could see the result?

Judge your results by internal standards: would you be satisfied if only you and your family saw it and you didn't compare it to others' apparent success?

> Would I be happy with this result if no one other than me and my
> family could see it, and I didn't compare the result to the
> appearance of other people's success?
>
> — Morgan Housel
