# Would you fight to keep this employee?

The keeper test asks how hard you would work to keep an employee if they were quitting; if you wouldn't fight for them, let them go and raise the talent bar.

> "If one of your employees were quitting, how hard would you work
> to keep them?"
>
> — Reed Hastings, The Knowledge Project podcast
