# Write a page, then delete all but the best sentences

To clarify your thinking, write a full page about your problem and then cut everything except the one or two sentences that explain it best.

> "The simplest way to clarify your thinking is to write a full
> page about whatever you are dealing with and then delete
> everything except the 1-2 sentences that explain it best."
>
> — James Clear
