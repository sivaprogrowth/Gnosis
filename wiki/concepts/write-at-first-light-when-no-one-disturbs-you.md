# Write at first light when no one disturbs you

Hemingway wrote every morning as early as possible because it was quiet and undisturbed, and he warmed up as he worked.

> “When I am
> working on a book or a story,” he said, “I write every morning as
> soon after first light as possible. There is no one to disturb
> you and it is cool or cold and you come to your work and warm as
> you write.”
>
> — Ernest Hemingway
