# Write to be useful, not to sound smart

Aim your writing at usefulness rather than impressing people. Being useful over a long period makes you look smart anyway.

> Don’t write to sound smart. Write to be useful.
>
> If you’re useful over a long time period, you will end up looking
> smart anyway.
>
> — James Clear
