# Writing exposes gaps in your understanding

Trying to write something down shows you the gaps and flaws in your thinking that you didn't notice before.

> Writing is nature's way of telling us how lousy our thinking
> is.
>
> — Leslie Lamport

> Writing is often the process by which you realize you do not
> understand what you are talking about.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> Writing is the process by which you realize that you do not
> understand what you are talking about.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
