# Writing feedback: shorter, more appealing, more universal

Most writing can be improved by cutting length, making it more appealing, and widening how many people it applies to.

> 1. Make it shorter. 2. Make it more appealing. 3. How could it
> apply to more people?
>
> — James Clear
