# Writing is a way to practice reasoning

Writing is not only for sharing ideas with others; good writing also helps us think through an idea ourselves and practice reasoning.

> While we naturally understand that writing is a good way to
> share ideas with others, we under-appreciate just how much good
> writing helps us think about an idea ourselves. Writing is not
> only a means of communication, it enables us to practice
> reasoning.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
