# Writing is humanity's superpower and truest magic

Writing creates value from nothing at little cost, can change a reader's life, and is the foundation of technology because knowledge must be recorded before it can be built upon. It enriches future generations without making anyone poorer.

> Writing is the superpower of humankind. It is our truest form of
> magic.
>
> Writing allows you to conjure up something of value where nothing
> previously existed. It costs little for you to write down the
> lessons of your life and yet those few minutes spent writing can
> be life-altering for the right reader. As I once saw it put:
> "there is someone out there with a wound in the exact shape of
> your words."
>
> Furthermore, writing is the foundation of nearly every technology
> and innovation because we have to record what we know before we
> can build upon it. And these innovations are passed down from
> generation to generation, allowing our children to inherit a
> richer intellectual fortune than what we were born into. The
> world is richer because we write and nobody is made poorer in the
> process.
>
> — James Clear
