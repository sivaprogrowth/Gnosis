# Writing lets you outlive yourself

Through writing, an author's mental life stays alive and meaningful long after their physical life ends, as old books are still read today.

> Writing is one of the only ways to outlive yourself. People
> still read books from hundreds or even thousands of years ago.
> The author's physical life ended long ago, but their mental life
> remains alive and meaningful even today.
>
> — James Clear
