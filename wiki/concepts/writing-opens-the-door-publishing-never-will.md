# Writing opens the door, publishing never will

Writing itself can make you pay attention, soften, and wake up. Publishing does not give you those things.

> "There is a door we all want to walk through, and writing can
> help you find it and open it. Writing can give you what having a
> baby can give you: it can get you to start paying attention, can
> help you soften, can wake you up. But publishing won’t do any of
> those things; you’ll never get in that way."
>
> — Anne Lamott, Bird by Bird
