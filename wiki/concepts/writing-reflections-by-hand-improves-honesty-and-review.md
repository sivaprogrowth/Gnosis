# Writing reflections by hand improves honesty and review

Answering reflection questions by hand helps you organize your thoughts, encourages honesty, and makes it easier to review your answers later.

> It may seem simple, but writing out your answers by hand will
> help in several ways. First, it will help you reflect and
> organize your thoughts. Second, it promotes honesty. Third, it
> makes periodic review easier.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
