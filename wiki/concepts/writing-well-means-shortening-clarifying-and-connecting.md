# Writing well means shortening, clarifying, and connecting

Good writing turns long sentences into short ones, confusing ideas into clear ones, and unrelated concepts into related ones. It does all of this while keeping the main idea intact.

> The trick to writing well is to...
>
> - take long sentences and make them short
>
> - take confusing ideas and make them clear
>
> - take unrelated concepts and make them related
>
> ...without losing the main idea in the process.
>
> — James Clear
