# You always control and own your response

Many things that happen to you are not your fault, but how you respond to them is always your responsibility.

> You are not the cause of everything that happens to you, but you
> are responsible for how you respond to everything that happens to
> you.
>
> — James Clear

> I can't control the other person's behavior, but I can control
> my response. Their actions may be rude or unacceptable, but I
> still want my response to be measured and thoughtful. Even if
> they aren't doing what is right, I still want to make sure I'm
> doing what is right.
>
> — James Clear
