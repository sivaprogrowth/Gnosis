# You are free to change who you were

You have no duty to stay who you were in the past; your job is to keep shaping who you become.

> You are under no obligation to remain the same person you were a
> year ago, a month ago, or even a day ago. You are here to create
> yourself, continuously.
>
> — Richard Feynman

> You are under no obligation to be the same person you were five
> minutes ago.
>
> — Alan Watts
