# You are not the work you do

Toni Morrison's father taught her to do work well for herself, that she makes the job rather than the job making her, and that her real life is at home. Her worth is not measured by her labor, and job security should never outrank home.

> Listen. You don’t live there. You live here.
> With your people. Go to work. Get your money. And come on home.”
>
> That was what he said. This was what I heard:
>
> 1. Whatever the work is, do it well—not for the boss but for
> yourself.
>
> 2. You make the job; it doesn’t make you.
>
> 3. Your real life is with us, your family.
>
> 4. You are not the work you do; you are the person you are.
>
> — Toni Morrison, The Work You Do, The Person You Are
