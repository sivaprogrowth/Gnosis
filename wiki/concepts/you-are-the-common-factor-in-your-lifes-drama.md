# You are the common factor in your life's drama

After years of blaming others, it is hard to admit that you are the only person present in every scene of your life, so you bear much of the responsibility for how it turns out.

> After a lifetime of blaming others, it is exceedingly difficult
> for us to finally acknowledge that the only person who has
> consistently been in all the scenes of that long-running soap
> opera we call our life is us, and, as a necessary corollary, that
> we bear some large responsibility for how the drama is turning
> out.
>
> — James Hollis, Why Good People Do Bad Things
