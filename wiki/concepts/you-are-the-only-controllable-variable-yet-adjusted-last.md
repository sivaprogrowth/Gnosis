# You are the only controllable variable, yet adjusted last

In any situation the only thing you truly control is yourself. Strangely, people usually try changing everything else before changing themselves.

> The only controllable variable in any situation is you. Oddly,
> it's the last one people adjust.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
