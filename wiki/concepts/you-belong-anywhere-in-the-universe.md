# You belong anywhere in the universe

In space, Jemison felt she had as much right to be anywhere in the universe as any star, comet, or planet.

> Once I got into space, I was feeling very comfortable in the
> universe. I felt like I had a right to be anywhere in this
> universe, that I belonged here as much as any speck of stardust,
> any comet, any planet.
>
> — Mae Jemison, Then & Now: Dr. Mae Jemison
