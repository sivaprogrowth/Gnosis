# You cannot outgrow what you won't release

Growth beyond a habit, belief, or situation requires first being willing to let go of it.

> It's hard to grow beyond something if you won't let go of it.
>
> — James Clear

> You can't outgrow it if you keep clinging to it.
>
> — James Clear
