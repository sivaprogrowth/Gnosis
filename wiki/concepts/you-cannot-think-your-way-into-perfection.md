# You cannot think your way into perfection

Overthinking wastes energy; it is better to trust yourself, decide, and learn from experience since perfection does not exist.

> “Overthinking is the biggest waste of human energy. Trust
> yourself, make a decision, and gain more experience. There is no
> such thing as perfect. You cannot think your way into perfection,
> just take action.”
>
> — Sylvester McNutt III
