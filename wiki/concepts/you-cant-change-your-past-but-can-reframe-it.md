# You can't change your past but can reframe it

Your history is fixed, but the story you tell about it is a choice. Look for the lesson and opportunity in it and share what you learned.

> You can't change your past, but you can reframe it.
>
> Find the lesson in it. Find the opportunity in it. Pull the
> teachable moment out of it and share with others.
>
> You can't choose your history, but you can choose the story you
> tell about it.
>
> — James Clear
