# You can't outperform your attitude

Your beliefs about the work shape how you do it, which determines results. Fix your attitude first and the rest follows.

> You can't outperform your attitude.
>
> What you believe about the work shows up in how you do the work,
> and how you do the work determines your results.
>
> Fix the attitude first. Everything else follows.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
