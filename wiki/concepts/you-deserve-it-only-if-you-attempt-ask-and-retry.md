# You deserve it only if you attempt, ask, and retry

People often get what they deserve, but deserving it implicitly requires courage to attempt, guts to ask, and willingness to try again after failure.

> People frequently get what they deserve, but it doesn't feel
> like it because the unspoken rule is that you only deserve it if
> you have (1) the courage to attempt it, (2) the guts to ask for
> it, and (3) the willingness try again when it doesn't work out
> the first time.
>
> — James Clear
