# You fall to the level of your systems

Results depend less on the goals you set and more on the daily habits that make up your system. Focus on the habits that precede outcomes rather than the outcomes themselves.

> You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level
> of your systems.
>
> Your goal is your desired outcome. Your system is the collection
> of daily habits that will get you there.
>
> This year, spend less time focusing on outcomes and more time
> focusing on the habits that precede the results.
>
> — James Clear, Atomic Habits
