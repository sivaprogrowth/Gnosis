# You have to risk your heart despite being broken

Life will hurt you whether you love or stay alone, so you should risk your heart and feel fully. When you are hurt, remember the sweetness you did taste.

> Life will break you. Nobody can protect you from that, and being
> alone won’t either, for solitude will also break you with its
> yearning. You have to love. You have to feel. It is the reason
> you are here on earth. You have to risk your heart. You are here
> to be swallowed up. And when it happens that you are broken, or
> betrayed, or left, or hurt, or death brushes too near, let
> yourself sit by an apple tree and listen to the apples falling
> all around you in heaps, wasting their sweetness. Tell yourself
> that you tasted as many as you could.
>
> — Louise Erdrich, The Painted Drum
