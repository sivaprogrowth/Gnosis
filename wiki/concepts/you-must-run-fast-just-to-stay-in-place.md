# You must run fast just to stay in place

In competitive environments, constant effort is needed merely to keep your position, and getting ahead requires even more. This is the Red Queen effect.

> "Here we must run as fast as we can, just to stay in place. And
> if you wish to go anywhere you must run twice as fast as that."
>
> — Lewis Carroll
