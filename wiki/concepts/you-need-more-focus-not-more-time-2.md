# You need more focus, not more time

When a problem isn't getting solved, the fix is usually more focused action rather than more hours. Cutting distractions matters more than adding time.

> Before you throw more time at the problem, throw more focused
> action at the problem. You don’t need more time, you need fewer
> distractions.
>
> — James Clear

> The myth is that there isn't enough time. There is plenty of
> time. There isn't enough focus with the time you have. You win by
> directing your attention toward better things.
>
> — James Clear

> Bill Gates once had the radio removed from his car. When asked
> why, he said he didn't want any distractions from thinking about
> Microsoft. This level of single-minded focus is what builds
> empires.
>
> Distractions are the assassins of great work. You don't need more
> time; you need more focus.
>
> Time expands when we eliminate interruptions—our attention, not
> the clock, ultimately limits what we can achieve.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> You don't need more time. You need more focus.
>
> Time isn't the constraint. Your choices are.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]

> You don't need more time, you need more focus.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
