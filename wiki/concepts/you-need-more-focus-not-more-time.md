# You need more focus, not more time

The answer to feeling stretched is fewer projects and commitments. Choose commitments carefully, then go all in on them.

> You don't need more time; you need more focus.
>
> Fewer projects. Fewer commitments. Fewer obligations. Fewer
> responsibilities.
>
> Carefully choose your commitments, then go all in.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
