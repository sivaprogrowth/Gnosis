# You need not correct people committed to being wrong

When someone insists on acting foolishly, you can let them be wrong and move on. Usually it is better to reclaim your time for more productive things than to argue.

> Reminder: If someone is acting like an idiot, you can just move
> on and let them be wrong. You do not have a personal obligation
> to correct people who are committed to being stupid. Sure,
> sometimes you need to attempt to get on the same page, but
> usually you can simply reclaim your time and move on to more
> productive ventures.
>
> — James Clear
