# You need opposite advice at different moments

Each of us sometimes needs more focus, effort, or care and at other times needs the opposite. The key is recognizing which you need right now.

> Some people need more focus. Others need to broaden their
> perspective.
>
> Some people need to try harder. Others need to stress less.
>
> Some people need to care more. Others need to let it go.
>
> The secret is you are both people. The key is to know which one
> you are in this moment.
>
> — James Clear
