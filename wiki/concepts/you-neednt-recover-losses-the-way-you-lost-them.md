# You needn't recover losses the way you lost them

Don't cling to losing efforts or keep chasing what isn't working to win back what you lost. Focus instead on the next best action.

> You don't have to make it back the way that you lost it.
>
> — Warren Buffett, 1994 Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letter
