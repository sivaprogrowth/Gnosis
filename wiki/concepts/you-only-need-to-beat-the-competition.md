# You only need to beat the competition

Sometimes you don't need to be great at something; being better than the competition is enough.

> Sometimes you don’t need to be great, you just need to be
> better than the competition.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], The Knowledge Project episode on Jim Clayton
