# You owe nothing to others' expectations

You are not responsible for living up to what others think you should accomplish. If they expect something else, that is their mistake, not your failure.

> You have no responsibility to live up to what other people think
> you ought to accomplish.
>
> I have no responsibility to be like they expect me to be. It's
> their mistake, not my failing.
>
> — Richard Feynman, Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!

> You have no responsibility to live up to someone else's
> expectation of you.
>
> Spend as little time as possible chasing other people's
> preferences instead of your own.
>
> — James Clear
