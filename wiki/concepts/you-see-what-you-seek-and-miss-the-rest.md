# You see what you seek and miss the rest

When you are fixated on a goal you see only what you seek and miss much else. Finding requires being free and receptive rather than goal-obsessed.

> When someone is seeking, it happens quite easily that he only
> sees the thing that he is seeking; that he is unable to find
> anything, unable to absorb anything, because he is only thinking
> of the thing he is seeking, because he has a goal, because he is
> obsessed with his goal. Seeking means: to have a goal; but
> finding means: to be free, to be receptive, to have no goal...
>
> For in striving towards your goal, you do not see many things
> that are under your nose.
>
> — Hermann Hesse, Siddhartha

> It’s hard to see what you’re not looking for and it’s easy to see what you’re looking for.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
