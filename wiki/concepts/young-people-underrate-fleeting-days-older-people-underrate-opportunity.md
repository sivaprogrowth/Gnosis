# Young people underrate fleeting days, older people underrate opportunity

When young, it is easy to forget how quickly days pass and that each choice matters. When older, it is easy to forget how much opportunity remains; it is never too late to start.

> When you're younger, it's easy to underestimate how fleeting the
> days can be. Each choice matters. Each day matters.
>
> When you're older, it's easy to underestimate how much
> opportunity you still have. Don't talk yourself out of it. It's
> never too late to start.
>
> — James Clear
