# Young, talented underdogs are well positioned to make great things

Being young, talented, and underestimated is a strong combination. You are hungry to prove yourself, unbound by how things have been done, and usually have fewer responsibilities pulling you from the work.

> One of the best positions to be in: young, talented, and the
> underdog.
>
> That's like a cocktail for making something great. You're hungry
> to prove yourself. You're young enough to not care about how it's
> previously been done. And, usually, you have fewer
> responsibilities to pull you away from the work.
>
> — James Clear
