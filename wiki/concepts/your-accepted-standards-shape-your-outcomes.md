# Your accepted standards shape your outcomes

Refusing to settle for less than what you want is a surprisingly effective way to get it. It takes patience and persistence and won't always work, but your standards shape your outcomes.

> A surprisingly effective way to get what you want is to not
> settle for less than what you want.
>
> It doesn't always work—you can't force the world to be a certain
> way—and you may need healthy doses of patience and doggedness,
> but your life bends toward what you accept.
>
> — James Clear

> You won't always get what you strive for, but you will
> definitely get what you settle for. You won't magically
> outperform your standards.
>
> — James Clear
