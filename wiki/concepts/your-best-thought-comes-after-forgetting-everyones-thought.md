# Your best thought comes after forgetting everyone's thought

First thoughts are the conventional ones everyone has; original thinking requires staying with a problem long enough to move past them.

> Your first thought is what everyone else thinks. Your best
> thought comes after you've thought long enough to forget what
> everyone thinks.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
