# Your biggest opportunity hides in what already works

Problems demand attention while successes go quiet, so we chase the loudest thing. The bigger opportunity is often in what is working that we have stopped noticing.

> Problems scream for attention while successes only whisper. We're
> wired to chase whatever's loudest.
>
> Your biggest opportunity isn't hiding in what's broken; it's
> hiding in what's working that you've stopped noticing.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]]
