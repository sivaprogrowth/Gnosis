# Your contribution is what you share, not know

Knowledge kept to yourself is like potential energy that does nothing until it is transformed into something shared. What counts is what you give to others.

> Unshared knowledge is like potential energy. It's nice to have, but it only moves things when you transform it into something else. Your contribution is what you share, not what you know.
>
> — James Clear
