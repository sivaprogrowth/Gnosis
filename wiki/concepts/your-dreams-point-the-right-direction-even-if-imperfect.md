# Your dreams point the right direction even if imperfect

Dreams rarely unfold exactly as expected, but they still guide you the right way. Follow the energy they give you rather than waiting for a perfect vision.

> Chase your dreams. It's unlikely they will play out exactly as you expect, but they know the way. No vision is perfect, but it points in the right direction. Let the energy of your soul lead you.
>
> — James Clear
