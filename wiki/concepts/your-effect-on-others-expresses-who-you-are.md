# Your effect on others expresses who you are

Keep being yourself and surprise an unkind world with kindness. How you affect other people is the highest expression of your own life.

> My wish for you is that you continue. Continue to be who you
> are, to astonish a mean world with your acts of kindness. The
> effect you have on others’ lives is the highest expression of
> your own.
>
> — Maya Angelou, Letter to My Daughter
