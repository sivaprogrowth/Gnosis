# Your existence required an unbroken chain of ancestral luck

Every one of your ancestors survived long enough to reproduce with the right partner at the right moment, making your existence astonishingly improbable and brief.

> Not one of your pertinent ancestors was squashed, devoured,
> drowned, starved, stranded, stuck fast, untimely wounded, or
> otherwise deflected from its life's quest of delivering a tiny
> charge of genetic material to the right partner at the right
> moment in order to perpetuate the only possible sequence of
> hereditary combinations that could result — eventually,
> astoundingly, and all too briefly — in you.
>
> — Bill Bryson, A Short History of Nearly Everything
