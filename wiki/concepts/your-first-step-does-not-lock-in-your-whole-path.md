# Your first step does not lock in your whole path

Fear of choosing the wrong lifelong course can keep you frozen, but a first step is just one of many and there will be more chances to change direction later.

> Your first step is just that: the first of many steps. For so
> long I would stand still, afraid to move in any direction because
> I wasn’t sure which was the course I wanted to follow for the
> rest of my life. Finally, someone explained to me that my first
> steps did not necessarily determine one path for all my
> tomorrows. There would be more decisions, and more opportunities
> to make a shift should I need to do so.
>
> — Ashley C. Ford, In the Company of Women
