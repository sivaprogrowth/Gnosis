# Your habits hold you back more than enemies

Your own habits limit you more than any opponent does. That is good news, because habits can be changed while enemies cannot, so focus on what you control.

> Your habits will hold you back more than your enemies ever will.
>
> Which is good news because you can change your habits. You can't
> change your enemies. Stay focused on what you can control.
>
> — James Clear
