# Your habits reveal whether your days reflect courage or cowardice

Your life is the accumulated record of your days, and your habits make up each day. Ask whether those habits come from small cowardices and laziness or from courage and inventive reason.

> "The history of every day. — What is the history of every day in
> your case? Look at your habits that constitute it: are they the
> product of innumerable little cowardices and lazinesses, or of
> your courage and inventive reason?"
>
> — Friedrich Nietzsche, Die fröhliche Wissenschaft
