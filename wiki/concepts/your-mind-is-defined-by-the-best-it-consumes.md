# Your mind is defined by the best it consumes

The danger is not consuming junk but failing to consume enough excellent work. Regular exposure to genius expands the mind, whose upper limit is set by the best it habitually takes in.

> I’m talking about what you might call the “theory of
> maximum taste.” This theory is based on the idea that exposure to
> genius has the power to expand your consciousness. If you spend a
> lot of time with genius, your mind will end up bigger and broader
> than if you spend your time only with run-of-the-mill stuff. The
> theory of maximum taste says that each person’s mind is defined
> by its upper limit—the best that it habitually consumes and is
> capable of consuming.”
>
> — [[david-brooks|David Brooks]]
