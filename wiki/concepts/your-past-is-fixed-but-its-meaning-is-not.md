# Your past is fixed but its meaning is not

Past events can't change, but the meaning you give them can. Choosing a more useful interpretation of your past makes better action in the present easier.

> The events of your past are fixed. The meaning of your past is
> not.
>
> The influence of every experience in your life is determined by
> the meaning you assign to it.
>
> Assign a more useful meaning to your past and it becomes easier
> to take a more useful action in the present.
>
> — James Clear
