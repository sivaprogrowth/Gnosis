# Your response outweighs talent and luck in outcomes

Talent rarely guarantees success and bad luck rarely guarantees failure. Both matter, but how you respond always shapes the final result.

> Talent is rarely enough to assure victory and bad luck is rarely
> enough to guarantee defeat.
>
> Do they influence the outcome? Of course. But your response will
> always sway the final tally.
>
> — James Clear
