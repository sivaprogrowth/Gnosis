# Your true self lies in your deepest enchantment

You find who you really are in the experiences that have enchanted you most deeply.

> Where is your Self to be found? Always in the deepest
> enchantment that you have experienced.
>
> — Hugo von Hofmannsthal, Buch der Freunde (1922)
