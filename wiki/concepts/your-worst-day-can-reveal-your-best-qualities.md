# Your worst day can reveal your best qualities

Bad days are chances to stand out and learn about yourself. The few people who prepare for how they will act in those moments can turn their worst day into their best.

> Your worst day is a chance to show your best qualities, to stand
> out, and to learn an enormous amount about yourself. Very few
> people plan or prepare for what they’ll do and how they’ll act
> during those times. Those who do might well end up turning their
> worst day into their best.
>
> — [[shane-parrish|Shane Parrish]], You’re Only As Good As Your Worst Day
