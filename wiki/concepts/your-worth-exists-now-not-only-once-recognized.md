# Your worth exists now, not only once recognized

If you will be valued later, you already have value now; wheat is wheat even when others mistake it for grass early on.

> If I am worth anything later, I am worth something now. For
> wheat is wheat, even if people think it is a grass in the
> beginning.
>
> — Vincent van Gogh, The Letters of Vincent van Gogh
