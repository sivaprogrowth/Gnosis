# Youth is defined by play, not age

If childhood is the age of play, then being young depends on whether you play, so some children never feel young and some adults never grow old.

> Childhood may be defined as the age of play; therefore some children are never young, and some adults are never old.
>
> — Will Durant, Fallen Leaves
