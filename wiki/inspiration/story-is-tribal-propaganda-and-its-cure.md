# "Story, then, is both tribal propaganda and the cure for tribal propaganda."

> Story, then, is both tribal propaganda and the cure for tribal propaganda.

— Will Storr, *The Science of Storytelling*, Kindle loc. 2558

## Why it's filed

The single sharpest line on the dual function of narrative. ~80% of hunter-gatherer stories contain behavioural lessons about how to treat in-group members — story is the original tool for enforcing tribal morality. *And yet* story is also the only mechanism humans have for **inhabiting another mind**. It dissolves the boundary between self and other in a way nothing else does. The same instrument that builds tribes is the only one that can let a member of one tribe genuinely understand a member of another.

Useful for:

- **Brand narrative.** A brand that tells only one story is propaganda. A brand that can hold both stories — its own and a credible version of its adversary's — has the rarer move available.
- **Positioning in polarised categories.** The most durable challenger positioning is not "the incumbent is wrong" — it is "here is what the incumbent's story gets right, and here is the part of the picture it cannot see."
- **Internal alignment.** Founder narratives that only function as in-group propaganda burn out the moment the team stops being a tribe of true believers.
