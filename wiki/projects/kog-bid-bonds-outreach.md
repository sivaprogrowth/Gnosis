# KOG Bid Bond Outreach Campaign

ProGrowth's cold-outreach campaign for KOG — the bid bond / surety platform targeting **bonded contractors** in the U.S. construction trades. The deliverables (pain-points research, JTBD analysis, two-stage targeted-prospect/specific-action ads, 2-stage email sequence) are complete; the live phase begins this week.

## Why this exists

The KOG bid bond market is structurally niche: contractors need bid bonds to qualify for public-works projects, but the buying process is split across the contractor (operator pain), the CFO (financial owner), and the insurance agent (carrier relationships). Existing surety marketing is undifferentiated. The campaign is built on the bet that **a campaign aimed precisely at the bonded-contractor segment, with the multi-persona buying triangle addressed explicitly, outperforms generic surety outreach** at the level needed to make a niche-only positioning defensible.

## Campaign assets (built)

- **Pain-points research** — bonded-contractor specific (quoting cycles, multi-carrier coordination, capacity constraints)
- **JTBD analysis** — separate jobs for operator vs CFO vs insurance agent
- **TPSA (targeted prospect / specific action) ads** — for paid channels
- **2-stage email sequence** — first-touch + follow-up; one CTA per stage per [[resurface-kog-bid-bonds-outreach-2026-05-25|the §4.8 resurface guidance]]
