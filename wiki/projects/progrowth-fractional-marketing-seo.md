# ProGrowth Fractional Marketing SEO

A 6–9-month SEO + [[generative-engine-optimization|GEO]] strategy to overtake [[marketri]] on the "fractional marketing" keyword family. The bet is dual-surface — rank for "fractional marketing" in Google **and** become the cited reference in AI engines on the same topic — built on a single pillar piece plus a cluster of 9 supporting articles.

## Why this exists

Per the [[marketri|Marketri competitive analysis]], Marketri's flagship "Fractional Marketing" page ranks #5 for only 1 keyword on the head term. The incumbent's position is **not actually that strong**. ProGrowth is the natural challenger because:

1. **AI-native positioning vs Marketri's advisory-heavy services** — addresses the [[mirage-pmf]] frame directly (fractional marketing in 2026 is AI-leverage marketing or it's services-firm consulting in disguise).
2. **Dual retrieval surface** — content engineered for [[earned-media-bias|AI-engine citation]] and traditional SEO simultaneously. Marketri's content isn't structured for AI retrieval.
3. **Niche-vertical depth** — challenger-brand operators in specific B2B verticals (per [[ai-overview-tool|the AI Overview Tool's]] same positioning).

## Architecture (defined)

- **Pillar piece** — head term: "fractional marketing." Argument-driven (not best-practices summary). Thought-leadership per the Vajre rule — see [[resurface-progrowth-fractional-marketing-seo-2026-05-25]].
- **Cluster** — 9 supporting pieces on adjacent intent (fractional CMO, fractional marketing for B2B SaaS, fractional vs full-time, vertical-specific variants, etc.).
- **Structure** — comparison tables, bolded value props, citable stats, named frameworks. Both retrieval surfaces consume the same shapes per [[justification-attributes]].
