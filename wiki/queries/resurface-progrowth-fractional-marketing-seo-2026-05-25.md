# Resurface — ProGrowth fractional marketing SEO pillar (2026-05-25)

**Work context:** `[[progrowth-fractional-marketing-seo]]` — Phase 1 pillar content for the [[marketri]] overtake. 850–1,200 monthly search opportunity on "fractional marketing." Authority-building pillar + cluster architecture defined; this week's job is to draft the first pillar piece.

**Why these seven:** the pillar is being *written* this week, so the hooks below are decisions about voice, positioning, and structure that have to be made before drafting — not after, when the piece is already in the wrong shape. Source: `mcp__readwise__readwise_search_highlights` vector-similarity pass, re-ranked for applicability per `CLAUDE.md` §4.8.

> "Do some research starting with your single most important keyword phrase you want to rank for and find 9 others that are related to it. This is called creating a topic cluster and it will help you focus your efforts." — Stijn Hendrikse & Mike Northfield, *T2d3*
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** Validates the pillar + cluster architecture you've already defined. The "fractional marketing" head term + 9 supporting cluster pieces is exactly the right structural play. Stick to this discipline; resist adding cluster pieces that aren't directly downstream of the pillar.

> "this style requires that the category is well defined and there's a clear market leader—and you're not it." — April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome*
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** This is **challenger positioning** — and per the [[marketri]] competitive analysis, Marketri's flagship "Fractional Marketing" page ranks #5 for only 1 keyword. The clear market leader's position is *not actually that strong* — you're not the challenger to a fortress, you're the challenger to a paper crown. Write accordingly: confident, specific, with point of view.

> "A thought leader is someone in your industry perceived to be a subject matter expert. Thought leadership is presented through your content. Demonstrating thought leadership in your content is very different than traditional marketing." — Sangram Vajre, *ABM for Dummies*
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** Marketri's content is best-practices summarisation. Yours should be **the specific argument only ProGrowth would make** — e.g., "Fractional marketing in 2026 is AI-leverage marketing or it's services-firm consulting in disguise" ([[mirage-pmf]] framing applied to the fractional-marketing category). Argument, not summary.

> "Dominate a niche, then once you own it, do the same with another and then another. But never do so all at once. Doing so dilutes your message and your marketing power." — Allan Dib, *The 1-Page Marketing Plan*
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** The pillar should be on **"fractional marketing"** specifically, not on "fractional CMO services" generically. The 850–1,200 monthly search opportunity is on that head term. Resist the urge to broaden in Draft 1 to capture adjacent terms — broaden in cluster pieces.

> "Imagine your business showing up when people prompt for Gemini or ChatGPT. That's literally up for grabs right now. … SEO 2.0: AI-search-era content." — @gregisenberg
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** The pillar has a dual job: rank for "fractional marketing" in Google (Marketri overtake) AND get cited by AI engines (per [[earned-media-bias]], [[justification-attributes]]). Structure for both: comparison tables, bolded value props, citable stats, named frameworks. Same piece, two retrieval surfaces.

> "We took a SaaS website from 8k to 80k organic visitors a month. Used SEO 2.0 strategies to 6x their non-branded traffic in 6 months." — @boringmarketer
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** Empirical proof the SEO 2.0 approach works at scale. Useful as an internal conviction anchor when the Phase 1 pillar gets minimal traffic in Months 1–2 (which it will). The cluster compounds; trust the cadence.

> "Your content becomes a reference piece (the equivalent of a wiki article) [is the success signal]." — T2d3
> **Why this matters for the pillar:** Success metric for the pillar isn't traffic or rank position in Month 3 — it's whether the piece becomes **the page Marketri's own prospects cite back to Marketri when they push back on the engagement.** That's the win condition. Write something cite-worthy, even if it ranks #6 initially.
