## Abstract

This article is a personal, curated list of favourite eating and drinking spots in Bangalore (Bengaluru), compiled by blogger Sahil K after completing one year in the city and visiting over 100 places. Organised by meal type — Breakfast & Cafes, Lunch & Dinner, Desserts & Bakes, Bars & Pubs, and Brunch & Meals — the list covers everything from hole-in-the-wall South Indian tiffin joints to fine-dining Kerala and Japanese restaurants. The author tracked visits systematically via an Airtable database and notes specific dishes and ordering tips for each venue. The list is intended to be kept updated over time and invites reader suggestions via social media.

## TL;DR

- A systematically tracked, neighbourhood-annotated guide to ~40 Bangalore restaurants and bars across all meal occasions, with specific dish recommendations for each.
- Covers a wide range of cuisines — South Indian, Kerala, Korean, Japanese, Turkish, Brazilian, North Karnataka, and more — skewed toward Indiranagar and Koramangala.
- Treats craft coffee and craft beer with equal seriousness as food, with strong opinions on specific roasts, brews, and tap offerings.

## Key claims

- Blue Tokai (Koramangala) is cited as the best coffee in Bangalore at time of writing; the iced pour-over in light roast is the recommended order.
- Sour House (Koramangala) is described as an underrated bakery with a mostly delivery clientele; the passionfruit cruffin and honey sea salt croissant are highlighted.
- Naru Noodle Bar operates as an eight-seater ramen bar with weekly reservations opening Mondays at 6 pm, inside The Courtyard events space.
- Harima (Residency Road) offers bento boxes for lunch at ₹575, described as fantastic value for money for Japanese food.
- Bengaluru Oota Company (Cambridge Layout) is called "exceptional" for Mangalorean/Gowda food with reservations mandatory, standing out as the highest-praise brunch recommendation.
- Sri Srinivasa Pork Hotel (Ejipura) offers Coorgi pork chilli masala and chapatis at ₹160 per plate, flagged as seemingly risky on price but personally vouched for.
- Record Room (Ashok Nagar) is noted as being from the team behind Great State Aleworks (Pune) and offers vinyl record listening on quieter days.

## Key passages

> IDC, Residency Road is my preference but has multiple outlets, ghee masala thatte idli and ghee masala dosa, think they let the ghee in the dosas brown just a little bit more to have that nutty flavour making all the difference for me compared to other places

> Naru Noodle Bar, Kengal Hanumanthaiah Road, used to be home-delivery only, now opened as an eight-seater ramen bar at The Courtyard (a very popular events space), reservations for the week open on Mondays at 6pm

> Bengaluru Oota Company, Cambridge Layout, exceptional Mangalorean/Gowda food, incredible how they maintain a sustained level of quality through the meal, reservations mandatory

> Sour House, Koramangala, mostly a delivery clientele so a quiet unassuming spot, excellent breads and other bakes, love the passionfruit cruffin and honey sea salt croissant, always have a sourdough loaf from them in my kitchen

> Sri Srinivasa Pork Hotel, Ejipura, 160rs for a plate for coorgi pork chilli masala and chapatis, pricing makes it look risky but have survived without any issues

> Record Room, Ashok Nagar, from the folks behind Great State Aleworks in Pune, excellent music but gets very loud, listen to records on vinyl players on less busier days
