## Abstract

DataReportal's *Digital 2022: India* report provides a comprehensive snapshot of connected-device and online-service usage across India as of January 2022. With a population of 1.40 billion, India had 658 million internet users (47% penetration), 467 million social media users (33.4%), and 1.14 billion mobile connections, all showing year-on-year growth. The report aggregates advertising-reach data from [[meta|Meta]], [[google|Google]], LinkedIn, Snap, and Twitter alongside mobile-network data from GSMA Intelligence and speed data from Ookla to paint a detailed picture of India's digital landscape. It highlights the scale of remaining offline population (742 million) and significant gender skews in platform audiences, underscoring both the opportunity and inequality embedded in India's digital growth trajectory.

## TL;DR

- India had 658 million internet users in January 2022, but 742 million people (53%) remained offline, representing one of the largest unconnected populations in the world.
- Social media reached 467 million users (33.4% of population), led by YouTube (467M) and [[facebook|Facebook]] (329.7M), with strong male skews across all platforms.
- Mobile connections (1.14 billion) exceeded the total population, and mobile internet speeds improved 55.7% year-on-year to a median of 14.39 Mbps.

## Key claims

- India's total population reached **1.40 billion** in January 2022, growing by 13 million (+1.0%) year-on-year, with a median age of 29.1 and 64.1% living in rural areas.
- Internet users numbered **658 million** (47% penetration), up 34 million (+5.4%) from 2021, leaving 742 million people still offline.
- Median mobile internet connection speed grew by 55.7% year-on-year to **14.39 Mbps**; fixed broadband median speed rose 32.1% to **47.40 Mbps**.
- Social media users totalled **467 million** (33.4% of population), increasing by 19 million (+4.2%) over the prior year.
- YouTube's ad reach of **467 million** covered 71.0% of India's internet user base; [[facebook|Facebook]]'s ad reach of **329.7 million** covered 50.1% of the internet user base.
- [[instagram|Instagram]] reached **230.3 million** users (35.0% of internet users), Snapchat **126 million** (19.1%), Facebook Messenger **122.5 million** (18.6%), LinkedIn **83 million** members (12.6%), and Twitter **23.6 million** (3.6%).
- Male users dominated ad audiences across all platforms: Facebook (76.7% male), YouTube (68.8%), Instagram (73.4%), LinkedIn (71.8%), Facebook Messenger (76.3%); Snapchat was the most gender-balanced at 60.1% male / 38.4% female.
- Cellular mobile connections reached **1.14 billion** (81.3% of population), growing by 34 million (+3.1%) year-on-year.

## Key passages

> There were **658.0 million** internet users in India in January 2022. India's internet penetration rate stood at **47.0 percent** of the total population at the start of 2022.

> These user figures reveal that **742.0 million** people in India did *not* use the internet at the start of 2022, meaning that **53.0 percent** of the population remained offline at the beginning of the year.

> Ookla's data reveals that the median mobile internet connection speed in India **increased** by **5.15 Mbps** (+55.7 percent) in the twelve months to the start of 2022.

> At the start of 2022, **23.3 percent** of Facebook's ad audience in India was **female**, while **76.7 percent** was **male**.

> Data from GSMA Intelligence shows that there were **1.14 billion** cellular mobile connections in India at the start of 2022... mobile connections in India were equivalent to **81.3 percent** of the total population in January 2022.
