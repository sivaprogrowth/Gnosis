## Abstract

Fast Company's 2023 ranking of the ten most innovative AI companies spans the full breadth of the field — from headline-grabbing generative AI labs to quieter, high-impact specialists. [[openai|OpenAI]] leads the list on the strength of DALL-E 2 and ChatGPT, while [[google-deepmind|DeepMind]] earns its place through AlphaFold's protein-structure breakthroughs. The ranking also highlights companies applying AI to narrower but consequential problems: wildfire detection (Pano AI), workplace safety (Voxel), e-commerce fraud (Signifyd), clinical-trial acceleration (Unlearn), and email-based cyberattacks (Abnormal Security). The article argues that AI's transformative impact extends well beyond chatbots, urging readers to recalibrate their sense of what AI looks like in practice.

## TL;DR

- [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[google-deepmind|DeepMind]] top the list for generative AI and protein-structure research respectively, but most of the ten companies solve narrow, high-value real-world problems.
- AI's industrial footprint in 2023 spans fraud detection, wildfire monitoring, clinical trials, speech recognition, workplace safety, and no-code app development.
- [[nvidia|Nvidia]] appears both as an AI computing infrastructure provider (Instant NeRF) and as an enabler cited by several other ranked companies.

## Key claims

- [[openai|OpenAI]]'s DALL-E 2 produces images five times more realistic than its predecessor and introduced "outpainting"; ChatGPT's November 2022 release triggered widespread public experimentation and commentary.
- [[google-deepmind|DeepMind]]'s AlphaFold added more than 200 million protein structures to its public database in 2022, materially accelerating drug and vaccine discovery research.
- Builder.ai claims its AI-powered no-code platform creates software six times faster and 70% cheaper than human development teams.
- Abnormal Security uses AI behavioral analysis to detect email-based attacks (phishing, ransomware), reaching a $4 billion valuation after a $210 million Series C in May 2022; the FBI reported email attacks cost companies $43 billion between 2016 and 2021.
- Speechmatics' speech-to-text AI achieved ~83% accuracy for Black speakers in a Stanford study, outperforming [[google|Google]] and [[amazon|Amazon]] (both ~68%).
- Unlearn's digital-twin technology reduces clinical-trial control group sizes by up to 35%, potentially bringing life-saving drugs to market faster; it has been applied to trials for Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and ALS.
- Pano AI combines AI, cameras, and cloud software to alert fire departments faster than existing methods, now monitoring 5.6 million acres across U.S. states and Australia.
- Voxel's factory-floor video-analysis platform reports a 77% reduction in customer workforce injuries and up to a 90% improvement in monitored safety behaviours.
