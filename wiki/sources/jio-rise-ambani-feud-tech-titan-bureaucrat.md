## Abstract

The article frames Jio's origin story around three unlikely forces: a billionaire family feud (the Ambani brothers), an American tech titan, and a bureaucrat. Published on the day of Jio's trading debut, the piece serves as a special issue contextualising the IPO's historical significance within India's business and political landscape.
