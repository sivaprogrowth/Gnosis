## Abstract

This article presents a practical, step-by-step guide to monetising AI prompts by combining two tools: Prompt Perfect, a prompt-optimisation service, and Promptbase, a marketplace for buying and selling prompts. The author argues that prompts are a form of lightweight, high-value asset — short inputs that guide models like GPT-3, Dall-E, Stable Diffusion, and [[companies/openai|ChatGPT]] to produce accurate, relevant content — and that anyone with an idea can convert it into saleable prompts with minimal effort. The workflow involves using Prompt Perfect to expand a rough idea into a detailed, model-specific prompt, validating it in tools like DreamStudio or the GPT-3 Playground, and then listing it on Promptbase for sale. The piece is aimed at non-technical readers looking for low-barrier income streams enabled by generative AI.

## TL;DR

- Use Prompt Perfect to turn a brief idea into a detailed, optimised prompt for Stable Diffusion, GPT-3, or ChatGPT, then validate it in the respective model's playground.
- List the finished prompt on Promptbase, set a price, and earn passive income once it clears the platform's approval process.
- Prompts are framed as near-zero-effort digital assets that unlock generative AI's commercial potential for non-technical creators.

## Key claims

- Prompts act as steering inputs that give AI models context, improving the accuracy and relevance of generated content for marketing and business use cases.
- Prompt Perfect accepts a brief natural-language idea and outputs an expanded, model-specific prompt optimised for Dall-E, Stable Diffusion, GPT-3, or ChatGPT.
- DreamStudio (powered by Stable Diffusion) and the [[companies/openai|OpenAI]] GPT-3 Playground serve as free validation environments before listing a prompt for sale.
- Promptbase is a two-sided marketplace where prompts can be purchased or traded; it runs an approval process requiring prompts to be unique and consistently performant.
- The author claims the combined Prompt Perfect → Promptbase workflow requires "not much effort," positioning prompts as a passive income source.
- Prompts are described as beneficial to illustrators, writers, and other content creators who face writer's block, by providing a creative starting point.
- The economics depend entirely on pricing decisions made by the seller and Promptbase's curation/approval gate.
