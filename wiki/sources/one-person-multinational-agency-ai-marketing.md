## Abstract

Mahesh Murthy, Managing Director of Pinstorm, argues that AI is triggering the greatest redistribution of marketing services wealth in history — comparable in scale to what the rise of WPP meant for the advertising industry. Drawing on live client engagements, he contends that small, AI-augmented teams can now deliver outputs (global transaction research, consumer surveys, video production, media buying) that previously required hundreds of people or entire consulting firms. The article frames AI not merely as a cost-cutting tool but as the enabler of a fundamentally expanded agency mandate that reaches beyond Promotion into Product, Pricing, and Placement. Murthy concludes that agencies willing to embrace this shift will become more valuable to clients, not less.

## TL;DR

- A 50-person AI-augmented agency can now do the work of a 500-person firm across research, production, and media — and do it faster and cheaper.
- AI expands the agency's role beyond the traditional "Promotion" P into the other three Ps of the marketing mix, directly tying agency output to client P&L.
- Agencies that master AI workflows will gain ground; those that don't will be replaced — not by AI itself, but by smaller rivals who use it better.

## Key claims

- AI represents a wealth redistribution in marketing services larger than Martin Sorrell's creation of [[companies/wpp|WPP]] from a coat-hanger manufacturer.
- The "China moment in manufacturing" analogy: AI is to marketing services what low-cost Chinese manufacturing was to global goods production.
- A modern marketer's needs are now too complex and expensive to be met by conventional agencies at viable cost, creating an opening for AI-native alternatives.
- Redefining the agency mandate beyond Promotion to include Product, Pricing, and Placement is the strategic imperative for post-AI agencies.
- Pinstorm's team of ~3 people delivered a 30,000+ transaction research report across 140 countries in two days using AI, with zero hallucinations — a project no consulting firm would take on.
- AI enabled flavor-acceptance consumer research (surveys, analysis, recommendation) across KSA and UAE within 24 hours of brief receipt.
- For a beauty brand, AI facilitated reel production, media buying for millions of views, real-time attention tracking, and mid-stream video reworking — all within one week.
- Being paid for performance (tied to client P&L) is the agency's stated north star; AI makes this model viable at scale for small teams.

## Key passages

> "I believe AI will trigger the greatest redistribution of marketing services wealth in history. Larger than when Martin Sorrell took a coat hanger manufacturer public and built WPP. The China moment in manufacturing is the AI moment in marketing."

> "A modern marketer's needs are now so complex that no typical agency or set of typical agencies can do everything at a cost clients can afford."

> "Lower costs and faster turnarounds aren't our main advantages: they're the door-breaker. The real gold is in tying ourselves to our clients' P&L by offering services we couldn't have touched without 500 people: business research, global competitive tracking, app creation, video production and media buying across geographies."

> "In a world where agencies complain that AI is decimating their headcount, we're on the other end. It's bolstered our offerings, our importance to marketers, our revenues and turned our people from heroes into superheroes."

> "Using AI is table stakes. Knowing how to use it well will help marketers sell more, by doing a lot more, while spending a lot less. Agencies who get it will, well, get it."
