## Abstract

This article reviews PPV (Pillars, Pipelines and Vaults), a productivity and life-operating system created by August Bradley that gained significant traction in the Notion and productivity community. The review praises the originality of the PPV naming convention over conventional frameworks centred on "Projects and Areas," and explains that PPV aims to organise every aspect of a person's life, not merely work tasks. While Bradley positions PPV as app-agnostic and grounded in [[systems-thinking]], the reviewer notes that the system's deeper functionality is tightly coupled to Notion's unique features, making practical replication elsewhere difficult. The article introduces the three core components — Pillars, Pipelines, and Vaults — with Pillars defined as the foundational key life areas that support everything else.

## TL;DR

- PPV is a "life operating system" designed by August Bradley, structured around Pillars (key life areas), Pipelines (workflows), and Vaults (knowledge/reference stores).
- Despite being framed as app-agnostic through a systems-thinking lens, PPV is in practice heavily dependent on Notion's specific functionality.
- The system distinguishes itself from conventional productivity frameworks by moving beyond standard "Projects and Areas" taxonomies.

## Key claims

- PPV stands for Pillars, Pipelines, and Vaults — a naming convention the reviewer considers more original than standard productivity framework nomenclature.
- August Bradley designed PPV not merely as a productivity system but as a "life operating system" intended to manage every aspect of a user's life.
- Bradley's rationale emphasises systems thinking: the system itself matters more than any specific application.
- In theory, PPV should be replicable in any application or even with pen and paper.
- In practice, much of PPV's functionality relies on features fairly unique to Notion, making faithful replication in other tools difficult without redesigning core workflows.
- Pillars are defined as the key, load-bearing areas of a person's life — the foundations upon which everything else rests.
- August Bradley delivered the system through a large volume of detailed, step-by-step YouTube videos.

## Key passages

> "PPV is a productivity system designed by August Bradley. Actually it is billed as much more than that. This is a life operating system."

> "August Bradley talks a lot about systems thinking and therefore, the system is key and not the specific app."

> "As you watch the videos you will see that a lot of how this system works requires functionality that is fairly unique to Notion. I suspect if you tried to replicate this elsewhere, you would need to take the basic concepts and then design a whole new way of managing the system."

> "Pillars are the KEY areas of your life. These are the things that support everything else. That is what a pillar does!"
