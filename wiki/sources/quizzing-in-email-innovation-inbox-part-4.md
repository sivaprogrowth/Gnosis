## Abstract

This article by Rajesh Jain proposes a concept called **QuizMails** — a daily interactive quiz delivered entirely within an email inbox, combining three prior innovations: Ems (short-form emails), AMP (interactive, app-like emails), and Atomic Rewards (incentivised micro-engagement). The service would deliver three timed, personalised multiple-choice questions per day, answerable without leaving the inbox, and award points (called "Mu") for correct answers. Jain argues that the combination of AMP's interactivity, gamification mechanics (scoring, leaderboards, virality), and Atomic Rewards creates a novel engagement loop that could serve both consumer entertainment and specialised use cases like test preparation. The piece positions QuizMails as a showcase for the broader potential of Email 2.0 and Loyalty 2.0 to help brands reduce AdWaste and deepen customer relationships.

## TL;DR

- QuizMails is a proposed daily quiz-in-email service built on AMP, Ems, and Atomic Rewards — no clickthroughs required.
- Gamification mechanics (timed questions, +4/-2/0 scoring, Mu points, leaderboards) are designed to maximise in-inbox engagement in under 30 seconds.
- The concept is pitched as a proof-of-concept for Email 2.0 and Loyalty 2.0, with potential to cut AdWaste and boost brand profitability.

## Key claims

- AMP technology enables a fully interactive quiz to run inside an email, functioning as an app without requiring a landing page or clickthrough.
- QuizMails would send three personalised, timed multiple-choice questions daily, drawn from a large question bank customised per recipient.
- A scoring system (+4 correct, −2 wrong, 0 skip) denominated in "Mu" points creates incentive structure aligned with Atomic Rewards principles.
- Mu points can be used to unlock additional features: extra questions, extra daily emails, option elimination, and higher difficulty tiers.
- A leaderboard and social-sharing mechanism is intended to drive virality for the QuizMails platform.
- The platform could extend into vertical and specialised use cases such as test preparation or content-comprehension quizzes.
- Jain frames QuizMails as a showcase for the combined power of AMP and Atomic Rewards, advancing the broader Email 2.0 and Loyalty 2.0 theses.
- Consumer adoption of QuizMails could encourage brands to adopt these technologies, reducing AdWaste and increasing profits.

## Key passages

> Think of the AMP email as an app (software) inside an email. It is a gamechanger for driving greater engagement within email.

> Each question is multiple choice with four options – and a fifth "Skip" one. A right answer gets +4, a wrong answer -2, while a skip gets 0. These points can be given in the form of Mu.

> Mu can become the mechanism to unlock additional features: an additional question, an additional email daily, the ability to eliminate one of the wrong options to increase the probability of getting the right answer, and the option to increase the difficulty level of the questions for additional Mu.

> A leaderboard can bring social recognition. The daily score (Mu earned) and leaderboard position can be shared on social media – and could help QuizMails with virality.

> It can also work as a showcase for the richness and immense possibilities of what AMP and Atomic Rewards can do to bring to life the new worlds of Email 2.0 and Loyalty 2.0.
