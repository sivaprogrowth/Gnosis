## Abstract

Terminus, an account-centric platform for B2B revenue growth, announced an industry-leading ad fraud rate of less than 3% — well below the 8–20% fraud rates reported by competing [[concepts/account-based-marketing|ABM]] platforms. The announcement is tied to the release of Terminus' *ABM Benchmark Report: B2B Digital Advertising*, which surfaces best practices in ad copy, creative, format, and channel strategy for B2B marketers. The broader context is significant: ad fraud cost advertisers over $81 billion globally in 2022, with projections reaching $100 billion by 2023. The press release positions Terminus' lower fraud and CPM rates as a compounding advantage for CMOs operating under tighter budget scrutiny.

## TL;DR

- Terminus claims a sub-3% ad fraud rate vs. the 8–20% industry average on other [[concepts/account-based-marketing|ABM]] platforms, representing a meaningful cost-savings edge for B2B advertisers.
- The company's ABM Benchmark Report identifies email signature ads, LinkedIn, and human-photo creatives with blue/black/green backgrounds as top-performing formats.
- Terminus serves 1,000+ customers and has been named a leader in the Forrester Wave for B2B Advertising Solutions (Q3 2022).

## Key claims

- Competing ABM platforms report average ad fraud rates of 8–20%; Terminus claims less than 3%, which it calls the lowest on the market.
- Global ad fraud costs exceeded $81 billion in 2022 and were projected by Statista to reach $100 billion by 2023.
- Email signature ads carry no placement or click costs and achieve the highest click-through rates on the Terminus platform, ranging from 0.50% to 0.80%.
- LinkedIn ads cost up to $30 CPM but deliver highly targeted reach across demographics, industries, and company sizes, with click-through rates up to 0.60%.
- Ad creative featuring photos of people outperforms graphics in click-through rate; blue, black, and green backgrounds perform best.
- Terminus provides access to over 100 ad networks, more than 7,000 private marketplace deals, and premium channels including connected TV (CTV), audio, and video.
- Terminus has been a G2 leader in ABM for 17 consecutive quarters as of the announcement date.
